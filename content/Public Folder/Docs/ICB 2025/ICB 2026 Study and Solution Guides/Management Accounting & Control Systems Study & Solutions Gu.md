4.  Structure of the Exam

Your Exam will be 3.5 hours long and count for a maximum of 180 marks. The paper will consist of
three sections. Section A is compulsory, and will be in the form of 20 multiple-choice questions for a
total of 40 marks. Section B consists of three longer questions, all of which are compulsory. For two of
the three questions in Section B, the topics will vary from exam session to exam session. Section C
consists of two longer questions, only one of which is compulsory. For both of the two choice questions
in Section C, the topics will vary from exam session to exam session.
