SUMMATIVE ASSESSMENT (EXAM)

VERY IMPORTANT EXAM INFORMATION!

You must register for the Summative Assessments (Exams) to receive the Assessment

Envelope, otherwise known as the Portfolio of Evidence (PoE), for that course and

subjects. It is very important that you read the ICB Assessment Policy that was

included in your ICB Assessment Envelope.

Once you have successfully completed your assignments and you are confident you know

all of the relevant work, you will be ready to complete the Summative Assessment (Exam).

ICB Summative Assessments (exams) take place four (4) times per year. Consult with the

College to ensure you register for the exams prior to their closing dates, or contact the ICB

to ensure you have the correct dates.  You can also consult the ICB prospectus found on

the CD.

In your Assessment Envelope (that you will receive from the ICB once you have registered

for your Summative Assessment), you will receive your Portfolio of Evidence for each

course and subjects that you will need to complete. You will need to complete the PoE

and take it with you to the Assessment Centre for your Summative Assessment

(Exam) and hand it in to be assessed.  In each learning area, you will have to complete

a number of Formative Activities and Formative Evaluations.  These will be included in

your PoE for that learning area.

These formative activities and evaluations will count 30% of your final mark. The

Summative Assessment (exam) will count 70% of your final mark. You will need to

achieve an average of 60% to pass.

Please note: There is a section in your PoE where you have to declare that the work in

the PoE is your own. This section requires the signature of a witness who will state that

you have made this declaration. When you are ready to make this declaration, visit

your local police station and request that they witness your declaration. Please

contact your tutor if      you are unsure of how to do this.

If you find that you will not be able to complete your studies in time for the summative

assessment (exam), you should ask for your summative assessment (exam) to be

postponed to a later date. A postponement is only considered upon receiving a

medical certificate or a letter from your employer advising that you will not be able to write

due to work constraints. Should you wish to postpone your assessment for some reason

other than the above mentioned, you will be required to pay a postponement fee per

subject.

To postpone an assessment, the ICB needs to be informed at least one month before the

assessment date. The College can help you with this, but keep in mind that you will

have to let us know even earlier so that we have enough time to contact the ICB.

All the work covered during the course is examinable in the Summative Assessment

(exam).
