2\. The ICB

The ICB was established in 1931. Their role is to improve and maintain the quality of bookkeeping, accounting, office administration, financial management, and entrepreneurship training and qualifications in southern Africa. Their aim is to build the credibility and reputation of these fields.

The QCTO has delegated functions to both Fasset and the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) as Quality Assurance Partners, for the qualifications associated with the ICB. Under this delegation, the ICB is responsible for the assessment of student competency and Fasset/QCTO is responsible for the certification of such achievements, amongst other things.
