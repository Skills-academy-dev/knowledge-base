Understanding Belief, Diversity and Society

Religion shapes identities, communities and conflicts in every part of the world. It influences how people see themselves, how they behave and how they respond to change. To understand today’s societies, you need to understand religion – not just one tradition, but the diversity of beliefs, practices and worldviews that exist side by side.

This Religion textbook introduces you to the study of religion as an academic subject. It explores major religious traditions, their internal differences and their roles in history and in current events. You will also look at how religion interacts with social issues, media, science, human rights and secular worldviews, giving you a balanced and critical understanding of religion in the modern world.

When you study this textbook, you will learn how to:

Understand religious diversity: Explain key concepts in religion and describe both internal differences within religions and the unique features of major world religions. Explore inter-religious relations: Analyse the history and present dynamics of relations between religions, including cooperation, dialogue, tension, and conflict. Connect religion to social issues: Examine how religion engages with current social problems, and how media and public discourse shape religious opinion. Work with doctrines and sources: Identify central doctrines across religions and compare the main normative sources (such as scriptures and traditions) and their roles in communities. Interpret beliefs and worldviews: Interpret normative religious texts, and compare religious perspectives with secular worldviews and modern thought. Relate religion to science and rights: Discuss the relationship between religion and the natural sciences, and explain how religious freedom, human rights, and responsibilities operate in contemporary society.

This textbook encourages informed, respectful and critical engagement with religion. Through explanations, case studies and exercises, you will build the skills to discuss religious questions thoughtfully, understand different perspectives and navigate religious diversity in your classroom, community and future workplace.
