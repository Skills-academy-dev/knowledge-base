HOW TO APPROACH YOUR STUDIES

This is a very important journey and you should be enthusiastic\! After all, this is the first step to

reaching your goals.

Before you get start, take some time to read through these guidelines. How you approach your

studies is just as important as what you do when studying.

This assignment guide contains 2 assignments. You might feel tempted to start on the assignments

as soon as possible. We advise that you work through your textbook and study guide first and

complete the activities that can be found there. Read through your textbook slowly in detail and make

as many notes as possible. This will make the studying process much easier.

Once you have completed the content and the activities in the textbook and study guide, you can

take a shot at the practice test. Practice tests are a great way to prepare for your assignments. So

use the opportunity wisely. Set some time aside to complete this practice test. Please read through

the questions carefully and complete them to the best of your ability. Once completed, use the memo

to see how well you did. If you have not gotten the desired marks, simply revise the sections you

did not fare so well in. Then have a go at the test again\!

Manage your time well so you can give the right amount of attention to the assignments. Once you

have completed these, you must submit them to your tutors to be marked.

Only once you have completed and submitted the assignments to your tutor will you be eligible for

your next round of books. Your books are dispatched in this way to ensure that you do not become

too overwhelmed with your studies.

Remember this journey is yours. So work at your own pace. If you need any assistance, don’t

hesitate to reach out to your tutors. They are there to help you.

Good luck and happy studies\!
