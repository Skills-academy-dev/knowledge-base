Overall Evaluation

The Geskiedenis Handboek mostly meets the QA of Report Structure and Format checklist
standards. It is technically sound, visually accessible, well-structured, and learner-friendly.
The only shortfall is the absence of an ISBN number, which is required for full compliance.

Final Score

98% ✅
