ISINGENISO

Siyakwamukela esiqondisweni sokufunda esizinikele emhlabeni ocebile wezincwadi zesiNgisi. Lesi si-
qondiso sihlanganisa ukukhethwa okuvelele kwezinkondlo, inoveli ehehayo, kanye nomdlalo ojulile.
Siqondiswe ukusiza abafundi, othisha, kanye nabathandi bezincwadi njengoba behlola ukujula no-
buningi bezincwadi zesiNgisi.

Isigaba ngasinye—Izinkondlo, iNoveli, kanye noMdlalo—sinikeza ukuqonda okuhlukile nobuhle be-
zindikimba, kuveza izinkinga eziyinkimbinkimbi zesimo senhlalo namasiko omphakathi okhuluma isiN-
gisi.

Nansi indlela yokusebenzisa isiqondiso sokufunda

Funda Isingeniso Nezingxenye Zokuqala

Qala ngokufunda isingeniso kanye nezingxenye zokuqala zesiqondiso. Lokhu kuzokunika um-
bono jikelele wezindikimba ezizogxilwa kuzo ezihlokweni ezihlukene, okuhlanganisa izinkondlo,
iNoveli, kanye noMdlalo.

Hlaziya inkondlo ngayinye

Lapho ufunda ingxenye yezinkondlo, gxila ezindleleni zokuhlaziya izinkondlo ezihlinzekwe kule-
si siqondiso. Hlola izindikimba, umoya wenkondlo, ulimi olusetshenzisiwe, kanye nezithombe
ezikuyo. Ukuzĳwayeza lezi zici kuzokwenza kube lula ukuqhathanisa izinkondlo ezihlukene.

Hlola Ukuxhumana Kwezingqikithi Ezifanayo

Lapho usuhambele phambili, bheka izixhumanisi phakathi kwezingqikithi ezihlukene kumabali,
izinkondlo, kanye namadrama. Ukuqhathanisa izindikimba ezinjengebuqotho, ukulingwa
kwemvelo, kanye nomlando, kuzokwenza kube lula ukuhumusha izingqikithi ezinhlobonhlobo
nezifundo zazo zamasiko.

Thatha Amanothi Ngesifundo Ngasinye

Sebenzisa indlela yokuthatha amanothi ngezindlela ezikahle ukuze uhlele imibono yakho.
Lokhu kuzokusiza ngesikhathi sokuphindaphinda noma sokulungiselela ukuhlolwa. Amanothi
azokwenza kube lula ukuhlaziya abalingiswa nezimo ezibhekene nabo ngezindlela ezĳulile.

Phinda Uphinde Ukuhlaziya

Sebenzisa siqondiso isikhathi eside njengomthombo wesizukulwane sokufunda. Phinda uhlo-
lisise iziqeshana ezibalulekile ukuze uqonde kangcono izindikimba. Lokhu kuzokwenza ukuthi
ulungele ukuhlolwa futhi uqonde kabanzi izincwadi ezisesiqondisweni.

Hlola Izimpendulo Nezindlela Zokuhlola

Funda izibonelo zezimpendulo ezikulesi siqondiso ukuze ubone ukuthi ungaphendula kanjani
imibuzo yohlobo lwehlolo futhi wazi uhlobo lwemibuzo ongase uhlangabezane nayo.

Ingxenye C: UMdlalo - M.Gcumisa

Kulesi sigaba, abafundi bazobhekana nedrama abakhethe kuyo, engase ibe eyabhalwa ngu-Mlindeni
Gcumisa noma olunye uhlelo olucacisiwe. Abafundi kulindeleke ukuthi baphothule umbuzo owodwa
weNdaba kanye nombuzo owodwa wombhalo. Le misebenzi izohlola ukuqonda kwabo izindikimba
ezibalulekile, izisusa zabalingiswa, kanye nokwakheka kwedrama. Ngemibuzo yabo, abafundi kufanele
bakhombise ikhono lokuhlaziya umbhalo ngokucophelela kuyilapho behlola izici zalo ezibalu- lekile.

Indlela Yokuhlaziya Izinkondlo

Uma uhlaziya izinkondlo kumhloliso wakho, gxila kulezi zici ezibalulekile ezilandelayo:

1. Indikimba: Thola umqondo oyinhloko noma umlayezo wenkondlo.

2. Umoya: Qonda umuzwa noma isimo senhliziyo esivezwa yimbongi.

3. Ulimi Olusetshenzisiwe: Hlaziya ukukhethwa kwamagama nokuthi kunomthelela muni en-
cazelweni ephelele.

4. Izithombe Zenkondlo: Qaphela ulimi olungokomfanekiso (izifaniso, izisho, kanye nokwen-
zasamuntu) olwakha izithombe ezicacile noma imizwa ejulile.

5. Izindlela Zokudlala Ngomsindo: Phawula ukusetshenziswa kwemvumelwano, isivinini
somugqa, ukufana kokuqala kwamagama, nokufana kwamaphimbo okungeza umculo noma umo-
ya enkondlweni.

Isibonelo Sokuhlaziywa: :”UNOXOLO/UNOLUXOLO – KNN Gcumisa”

Indikimba

Le nkondlo igxile endikimbini yoxolo nokuthula enhliziyweni, kanye nokwelapha obuhlungu babantu
abathwele imizwa yokudumazeka nokuhlupheka. Uxolo lufaniswa nemithombo yamanzi apholile ah-
lanzayo, oluphilisa izilonda ezinhliziyweni zabo abahluphekile. Luyindlela yokubuyisela abantu injabu-
lo, uthando, kanye nenjabulo ekuphileni.

Umoya

Umoya wenkondlo unothando, uthobekile, futhi unokuthula. KNN Gcumisa uveza ukubaluleka koku-
thula nokuthokozisa okuqhamuka ekuxolelaneni nasekuthandaneni. Le nkondlo inokuthuliswa, oku-
bonisa ukuzwakalisa intobeko kanye nothando lukaMduduzi (umthombo woxolo).

Ulimi Olusetshenzisiwe

Umbhali usebenzisa ulimi olushukumisa inhliziyo, efaka amagama afana no-“uphola,” “ukuthula,”
kanye no-“umusa” ukwakha umfanekiso wokuthula kanye nenjabulo. Izisho ezifana no-“uphuzisa
bonke abakulangazelelayo” nokuthi “imizimba yethakase” zenza kube lula ukubona izinga lolaka
olushintshwa lwaba ukuthula.

Izithombe Zenkondlo

Izithombe ezicacile emgqeni 2 no-3 ezifana nokuthi “ufana namanz' apholil' ehla entabeni” ziveza um-
fanekiso ohehayo wequla eligeleza, likhuthaza ukuzola nokuphumula. Izithombe ezinjalo zenza ukuthi
umfundi abone ukuthi ukuthula kuhlobene nokubuyiselwa kokujabula. Leli sithombe lezinsiza zezulu
lingumfanekiso wokuthokoza nokuzinza okufika ebantwini abahluphekile.

Izindlela Zokudlala Ngomsindo

Izinsongo ezithile ezifana nokuphindaphinda kwezinhlamvu "U-" ezifana ku-“ufana” no-“uphuzisa”
kudala isigqi esingokwemvelo nokuphumuzayo. Isigqi sanoma imisho efana no-“ubaph' ukujabul' imiz-
imba yethakase” sigcizelela ukuphola kanye nokuthula, ngokugcwalisa umoya wenkondlo wezulu loku-
thula nentokozo.

“THUMELA UZAMCOLO NKOSI! – BT Msimang”

Indikimba

Le nkondlo iyisicelo esicashile kuNkulunkulu, ecela usizo lokubasindisa ebunzimeni nasekuban-
dezelekeni abhekene nakho ngenxa yobubi bomhlaba. Umbhali ucel' uzamcolo, umfanekiso wokuh-
lanzwa noma wokubulala ububi, ukukhulula abantu ezimweni ezinzima. Inkondlo igxile ezinkingeni no-
bunzima abaphila nabo abantu, iphinde ibonise indlela uNkulunkulu obhekwa ngayo njengowukuphela
kwendlela yokuhlenga nokubuyisela ithemba.

Umoya

Umoya wenkondlo ugcwele ukudumazeka kanye nendlela yokuncenga. Umbhali uveza intukuthelo
nosizi lapho ebona umhlaba ugcwele ukungalungi kanye nobunzima, futhi ucula ngenhlonipho eyodwa
kuphela ukuze kusize abantu. Ukuphimisa la mazwi okucela usizo kubonisa inhliziyo esindayo yoku-
fisa ukukhululwa ebubini.

Ulimi Olusetshenzisiwe

Umbhali usebenzisa ulimi oluqondile olujulile. Amagama afana no-"ukucĳa kwameva," "idwala eliyimi
lasala lome," kanye no-"duma langqangqazela" aveza ubunzima, umthwalo, kanye nobumnyama ob-
uhambisana nobunzima. Ukuphindaphindwa kwe-"Wena wedwa" kugcizelela inkolelo yokuthi kukhona
omunye kuphela ongaletha ukusindiswa nokukhululwa kwabantu.

Izithombe Zenkondlo

Umbhali usebenzisa izithombe ezĳulile ezifanayo ezikhombisa isimo sendawo esibukhuni nesiyibhuqu
ngobubi kanye nokubhalela uNkulunkulu. Izithombe ezifana no-"umhlabathi olugagadu" no-"ukucĳa
kwameva" zenza umfundi abone isimo sokuhlupheka okungenakulinganiswa. Iqondo lokubheka uNku-
lunkulu ukuze kutholakale uzamcolo liboniswa ngendlela ye-"imvula yempilo" ekwazi ukusula izono
zomhlaba.

Izindlela Zokudlala Ngomsindo

Ukuphindaphinda kwezinhlamvu ezifana no-"Wena wedwa" kudala isigqi esĳulile futhi esinamandla.
Isigqi senkondlo, kanye nesigqi sokuphawula, sidala umuzwa wobunzima nezinhlekelele eziqhu-
bekayo, okuzwakalisa ukuzinikela kanye nokumemeza ngesicelo. Leli phethini yomzwelo kanye nohle-
lo lomugqa luphefumulela umfundi ukumemeza ngesicelo sokuhlanzwa nokubuyiselwa kokuthula
ezimpilweni zabantu.

NGIZW' UTHANDO – JM Sikakane

Indikimba

Indikimba yale nkondlo igxile kubunjalo bothando olujulile kanye nomthelela onoju enhliziyweni
yomuntu. Lolu thando lumunxa imbongi, lumhlanganisa futhi lumqhuba ezimweni ezihlukene zobunjalo
bomuntu. Inkondlo iveza imizwa yokunxanelwa yothando nokuzinikela ngothando oluzuzisa kakhulu.

Umoya

Umoya wenkondlo uthakazelisa futhi unothando olujulile, ubonisa isifiso esikhulu nokuzinikela
ngenhliziyo yonke. Uthando lwethusa umfundi kodwa lumthokozisa kakhulu, lwakha umuzwa ojulile
wokuthokoza nokuthuthumela. Imbongi ibonisa ukuthi uthando lunamandla angaphezu kokulawula
okuthile futhi lumhlanganisa ngokugcwele.

Ulimi Olusetshenzisiwe

Ukukhethwa kwamagama kuveza ukuthi lolu thando lujulile futhi luyintokothi. Amagama afana no-
“sanganisayo,” “umzwangedwa,” kanye no-“ngizw' uthando” aveza izinga lothando olunzulu kanye
nomuzwa wokungenakucashelwa onikwe umfundi. Ukuphindaphinda kwamagama afana no-“ngizw'
uthando” kuphakamisa imizwa, kwenza ukuthi umfundi avuswe isasasa lothando olungenakuvinjelwa.

Izithombe Zenkondlo

Izithombe ezibekwe kule nkondlo ziveza ukushukumisa okunjengamandla othando. Izisho ezifana no-
“ngiyazumeka ngingazumeki” kanye no-“lungintofoza lunginwayanwaya” zidala umfanekiso oqinile
wokuphazamiseka kwenhliziyo nezinzwa ngenxa yothando. Izithombe ezinjalo zibeka umfundi
endaweni lapho engaphila khona lolu thando njengento eyimpumelelo futhi ethokozisa kakhulu.
