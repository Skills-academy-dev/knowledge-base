Izindlela Zokusebenzisa Umhlahlandlela Wokufunda
Wesifundo SesiZulu
1. Qala Ngomdwebo Wesifundo
● Bheka igama le-Module ukuze wazi ukuthi isifundo sigxile kuphi.
● Isihloko sikusiza ukuthi ulungiselele lokho ozokufunda.
2. Funda Isingeniso
● Isingeniso sichaza ukuthi uzofunda ini kulowo Module.
● Funda ngokucophelela ukuze uqonde inhloso yesifundo.
3. Landela Okuqukethwe Ngokulandelana
● Okuqukethwe kulotshwe ngendlela elandelanayo njengezihloko ezincane
(subheadings).
● Fundisa ngamunye ngawo, bese ubheka ikhasi elikhonjisiwe encwadini yakho.
● Lokhu kukusiza uqonde umqondo ngamunye ngokujulile.
4. Qaphela Izikhombo Zamakhasi
● Ekugcineni kwesigaba ngasinye, kukhona inkomba ethi “Bheka ikhasi XX lencwadi
yakho yezifundo”.
● Vula lelo khasi encwadini yakho ukuze uthole imininingwane eyengeziwe.
5. Sebenzisa Izifinyezo
● Izifinyezo ekugcineni kwe-Module ngayinye zibuyekeza amaphuzu abalulekile.
● Phinda ufunde izifinyezo ngaphambi kokuhlolwa noma izivivinyo.
6. Yenza Imisebenzi
● Imisebenzi itholakala ekugcineni kwe-Module ngayinye.
● Zama ukuyenza ngaphandle kosizo lwezincwadi, bese ubheka impendulo yakho
encwadini.
● Lokhu kuzosiza ukuzilungiselela izivivinyo.
7. Hlola Ngohlu Lokuhlola
● Ekugcineni kwe-Module ngayinye kukhona itafula lohlu lokuhlola.
8

● Phendula imibuzo ngokuthi Yebo noma Cha.
● Uma impendulo yakho iyi-Cha, buyela lapho ku-Module ukuze ufunde kabusha.
8. Zilungiselele Izivivinyo
● Sebenzisa izifinyezo, imisebenzi, kanye nohlu lokuhlola ukuze uphinde uhlolisise
ulwazi lwakho.
● Dala imibuzo yakho usebenzisa izinto ozifundile bese uzama ukuyiphendula.
9. Fundisa Omunye Umuntu
● Uma uchaza umqondo kumngane noma kumndeni, kusho ukuthi wena uqonde kahle.
● Sebenzisa le ndlela uma uzilungiselela izivivinyo.
10. Hamba Ngomdwebo Wesikhathi
● Hlela isikhathi sokufunda nsuku zonke, uhlanganise wonke ama-Module kancane
kancane.
● Qala ngezihloko ozithola zinzima bese uqhubeka ngalezo ozithola zilula.
9

Okulindele Usuku Lokuhlolwa
● Hamba kusenesikhathi – ukuze ufinyelele endaweni yokuhlolwa ngaphandle
kokuphazamiseka.
● Yizisa izinto ezidingekayo – njengepeni, ipensela, iraba, kanye nezinto ezivunyelwe
kuphela.
● Lalela imiyalelo yomhloli – qiniseka ukuthi uyayiqonda imithetho ngaphambi kokuqala.
● Bheka yonke iphepha kuqala – hlola ukuthi zingaki izimibuzo futhi zihlanganaphi.
● Hlela isikhathi sakho – ungachithi isikhathi esiningi embuzweni owodwa.
● Qala kalula – phendula imibuzo elula kuqala ukuze uzuze amamaki asheshayo.
● Phinda uhlole – uma isikhathi sisekhona, buyela emuva uphinde uhlole izimpendulo zakho.
10

Uhlolojikelele Lwezivivinyo ZesiZulu
1. Injongo Yezivivinyo
● Ukuhlola ukuthi umfundi ukwazi kanjani ukufunda, ukuhumusha, ukubhala,
nokuhlaziya ulimi lwesiZulu.
● Ukuhlola amakhono okukhuluma, okubhala, kanye nokuhlaziya izincwadi ezifana
nomdlalo, inoveli nezinkondlo.
2. Izingxenye Eziyinhloko
Izivivinyo zihlukaniswa ngezigaba ezilandelayo:
1. Ulimi (Izakhiwo Nemithetho Yolimi)
● Imibuzo ngemithetho yolimi, amagama, izisho, izaga, nokwakheka kwemisho.
● Ukusebenzisa ulimi ngendlela efanele.
2. Ukufunda Nokuzwisisa
● Umbhalo onikeziwe, bese kufakwa imibuzo ethinta ukuqonda umongo,
amagama, izisho, nencazelo ejulile.
3. Ukubhala
● Ukubhala izinhlobo ezahlukene: izincwadi, izindatshana, izimemo, imibiko, i-CV, noma
izinkulumo.
● Kubhekwa uhlaka, isitayela, amagama, nokuqukethwe.
4. Uhlaziyo Lwezincwadi
● Imibuzo ephathelene ne-noveli, umdlalo, izinkondlo ezifundwe esifundweni.
● Ukuhlaziywa kwezindikimba, abalingiswa, ulimi, nomlayezo.
3. Uhlobo Lwemibuzo
● Imibuzo Yokukhetha Impendulo – khetha impendulo efanele.
● Yiqiniso/Namanga – ukuhlola ukuqonda okuyisisekelo.
● Imibuzo Emifushane – ukuphendula ngamagama ambalwa noma imisho emifushane.
11

● Imibuzo Ede – ukuphendula ngokuningiliziwe, ikakhulukazi ekubhaleni nasekuchazeni
izincwadi.
4. Ukwabiwa Kwamamaki
Amamaki ahlukaniswe kanje:
● Ulimi: cishe u-30%
● Ukufunda Nokuzwisisa: cishe u-25%
● Ukubhala: cishe u-25%
● Uhlaziyo Lwezincwadi: cishe u-20%
(Leli phesenti lingashintsha kuye ngezinga lokuhlolwa).
5. Okulindelekile Kubafundi
● Ukuqonda kahle imithetho yolimi.
● Ukuhlaziya imibhalo nokuthola umongo ojulile.
● Ukubhala imibhalo ehlukahlukene ngendlela efanele.
● Ukuhlaziya izincwadi – izindikimba, abalingiswa, indawo nencazelo.
6. Amasu Okuphumelela
● Phinda ufunde imithetho yolimi njalo (amabizo, izenzo, izisho).
● Zĳwayeze ukufunda imibhalo emisha bese uphendula imibuzo.
● Hlola izincwadi ozifundile, uqaphele izindikimba nabalingiswa.
● Bhala izinkulumo, izincwadi, nezindatshana ezahlukene ukuze uzĳwayeze isitayela.
● Sebenzisa imisebenzi namashejuli wokuhlola (checklist) ekugcineni kwe-Module
ngayinye njengendlela yokuzilungiselela.

ISAHLUKO 1: IZAKHIWO
NEMITHETHO YOLIMI
Izinhloso Zokufunda:
1. Qonda izingxenye eziyisisekelo zolimi kanye neqhaza lazo emishweni.
2. Hlukanisa imisho ngokwesigaba sayo nesakhiwo sayo.
3. Qaphela futhi wakhe imisho elula, eyezinto ezimbili, eyezinto eziyinkimbinkimbi, kanye
nemisho ezishintshashintshayo.
4. Qonda ukuhlelwa kolwazi emishweni, kufaka phakathi imisho ethile, engapheli,
neyixubile.
5. Hlukana phakathi kokusetshenziswa kwezwi elisebenzayo nelingasebenzi, kanye
nokukhuluma okuqondile nokungaqondile.
6. Sebenzisa imithetho yokuhleleka kwamagama okulungile kanye nokuhambisana
kwemisho esiNgisini.
7. Qonda futhi usebenzise izimpawu zokuloba ezahlukene ngendlela efanele.
8. Qaphela futhi usebenzise izifinyezo nezifinyeziso ngendlela efanele.
9. Sebenzisa amagama okuhlukanisa nezihlanganisi ezifanele ekubhaleni kwabo.
10. Qaphela futhi usebenzise izinhlobo ezahlukene zolimi olugqamile nezindlela
zokukhuluma ezihambisana nesimo.
11. Qonda umehluko phakathi kwezinhloso kanye nemizwa.
12. Landela imithetho yokupela okujwayelekile futhi bahlele umsebenzi wabo ngokunemba
nangokucacile.
13

Isingeniso
Kulesi sahluko abafundi bazofunda ngezindlela ezahlukene zokufunda imibhalo ukuze baqonde
kahle amagama, izisho, nemisho. Bazobona umehluko phakathi kwezindaba, imibhalo
echazayo, nemibhalo yolwazi. Abafundi bazofundiswa amasu okufunda ngokushesha ukuze
bathole umqondo oyinhloko (skimming) nokufunda ngokucophelela ukuze bathole
imininingwane (scanning). Lawa masu azosiza abafundi ukuthi babe namakhono okuhlaziya
imibhalo ngendlela ebanzi.
Bheka ikhasi 8 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Izingxenye Zolimi
● Amabizo, Izichasiso, Izandiso, Izihlanganiso, Izabizwana, Izihlomelo
Lezi yizakhi eziyinhloko zolimi olusetshenziselwa ukwakha imisho. Isibonelo: umfundi,
uyahamba, njalo.
● Izenzo neZikopulativa
Izenzo zikhombisa isenzo noma isimo, kanti izikopulativa (isb. ukuba, ukubonakala)
zixhumanisa isihloko neminye imisho.
● Izikhathi Zesenzo
Kubhekwa izikhathi ezedlule, zamanje, nezizayo, kuhlanganise nezikhathi ezilula,
eziqhubekayo, eziphelele, nezihlanganisayo.
Bheka ikhasi 8–14 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Imithetho Yokubhala Nokusetshenziswa Kwezimpawu
Abafundi bafunda:
● Ukusebenzisa izinhlamvu ezinkulu ngendlela efanele.
● Ukuloba amagama ngendlela enembile.
● Ukusebenzisa izimpawu zokubhala ezifanele njengamaphoyinti, iziphumlisi, nezimpawu
zokucaphuna.
Bheka ikhasi 14–34 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
14

Izisho, Izaga, Nezinye Izindlela Zokukhuluma
Lesi sigaba sibheka izisho, izaga, nokusetshenziswa kolimi olufanekisayo ukuze kwenziwe ulimi
lube mnandi futhi lunothile. Kuphinde kugqanyiswe ukubaluleka kokuqonda izincazelo
zamagama, amagama ahlanganisiwe, nokusebenzisa kahle isilulumagama.
Bheka ikhasi 35–41 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Isifinyezo
Kulesi sahluko abafundi bazobe sebenamakhono alandelayo:
● Ukuqonda izakhi eziyinhloko zolimi.
● Ukuqonda izikhathi zesenzo nezindlela ezahlukene zokuzisebenzisa.
● Ukusebenzisa imithetho yokubhala ngendlela efanele.
● Ukuqonda nokusebenzisa izisho nezaga.
● Ukwandisa isilulumagama ukuze bakhulume futhi babhale kahle.
Bheka ikhasi 41 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Umsebenzi
Imisebenzi ehambisana nalesi sahluko itholakala ekhasini 42. Abafundi bayacelwa ukuthi
bayenze ukuze baqiniseke ukuthi baqonde kahle okuqukethwe kulesi sahluko.
Bheka ikhasi 42–51 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi. Ngempendulo
zemisebenzi, bheka ikhasi 164 lencwadi yezifundo.

ISAHLUKO 2: AMANDLA
OKUFUNDA NOKUZWISISA
Izinhloso Zokufunda:
1. Qonda izigaba ezahlukene zokufunda, kusukela ekubukezeni ngaphambi kokufunda
kuze kube sekupheleni kokufunda.
2. Hlola impahla bese uvuselela ulwazi lwangaphambilini mayelana nesihloko
ngaphambi kokufunda.
3. Dlulisa amehlo umbhalo ngokushesha ukuze uthole umqondo ojwayelekile noma
ulwazi oluthile.
4. Funda umbhalo ngokucophelela, uqonde amaphuzu amqoka futhi wenze amanothi.
5. Buyekeza bese ufingqa umbhalo ngemuva kokufunda.
6. Buza imibuzo ngomkhiqizo futhi ucabangele umbono nesifiso somlobi.
7. Thola amaphuzu amqoka kanye nemininingwane emincane exhasa lezi zinto
embhalweni.
8. Bheka izindikimba eziphindaphindayo kanye nokubaluleka kwazo embhalweni.
9. Sebenzisa izinkomba embhalweni ukwenza ukuqagela noma ukubikezela ukuthi yini
engahle yenzeke ngokulandelayo.
10. Sebenzisa amathuluzi afana nokugqamisa kanye nokuthatha amanothi ukuze uqonde
kangcono futhi ukhumbule umbhalo.
11. Sebenzisa izinkomba embhalweni ukuze uqonde amagama noma imishwana
ongajwayele.Qonda ukuthi kungani umbhali abhale umbhalo nokuthi uveza kanjani
umbono wakhe.
17

Isingeniso
Kulesi sahluko, abafundi bazofunda izindlela zokufunda imibhalo ngokushesha,
ngokucophelela, futhi ngendlela enolwazi. Kuzobhekwa amasu azokwenza kube lula ukuthola
amagama abalulekile, ukuhlanganisa imibono, nokuhlaziya imiyalezo ebalulekile embhalweni.
Lokhu kuzokwenza abafundi bakwazi ukuqonda umongo nombono womlobi ngendlela ecacile
futhi ebanzi.
Bheka ikhasi 53 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Inqubo Yokufunda
● Ngaphambi Kokufunda
Kubalulekile ukubuka umbhalo kuqala ukuze ubikezele ukuthi ungaba ngani. Lokhu
kufaka ukuhlola ulwazi lwangaphambilini ukuze uqonde umbhalo kangcono.
● Ngesikhathi Ufunda
Dlulisa amehlo umbhalo ukuze uthole umqondo ojwayelekile, bese ufunda
ngokucophelela ukuze uqonde amaphuzu abalulekile. Qaphela amagama
aphindaphindiwe, izindikimba, nezindlela ezisetshenziswe umlobi
● Ngemuva Kokufunda
Kufanele wenze isifinyezo, uhlukanise imibono emikhulu nemininingwane, bese ubhala
amanothi ukuze ulwazi luhlale isikhathi eside.
Bheka ikhasi 53–55 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Ukuqonda Izikhombisi Zomongo
Lokhu kusho ikhono lokusebenzisa amagama nemisho ezizungezile ukuze uqonde incazelo
yamagama amasha. Ngokuhlaziya izisho eziseduze, umfundi angathola incazelo efanele
ngaphandle kokusebenzisa isichazamazwi.
Bheka ikhasi 56 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
18

ngaleso sihloko. Ngokuhlaziya le nhloso, umfundi angabona ukuthi umbhalo uhlose ukuchazela,
ukukholisa noma ukukhuthaza abafundi.
Bheka ikhasi 56–57 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Izibonelo Zokusebenza (Isimo sikaSipho)

wesikhashana. Incwadi inemininingwane ngamaholo, izinsuku zokusebenza, nezibopho.
USipho kufanele aqonde amaphuzu ayinhloko kodwa futhi asebenzise amasu okuhumusha
ukuze aqonde izincazelo ezĳulile ezikhona embhalweni.
Bheka ikhasi 52–53 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.

Bheka ikhasi 59 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.

ISAHLUKO 3: AMAKHONO
OKUFINGQA
Izinhloso Zokufunda:
1. Khomba ulwazi olubalulekile embhalweni.
2. Hlukanisa bese ushiya ngaphandle imininingwane engabalulekile.
3. Susa ukuphindaphinda nokwenza inkulumo yeluleke kakhulu.
4. Khipha imibono emikhulu, ushiye imininingwane, izibonelo, nezithombe.
5. Phinda umbhalo usebenzisa amagama akho.
6. Landela imiyalelo mayelana nesitayela, isakhiwo, ubude/isisindo
samagama, nokukhethwa kwesihloko.
7. Phendula imibuzo yokufingqa emvavanyweni.
21

Isingeniso
Kulesi sahluko abafundi bazofunda ukuthi ukufingqa kusho ukuthatha umbhalo omude bese
bewunciphisa ngendlela ecacile, ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa umqondo oyinhloko. Lokhu
kudinga ukuthi umfundi akwazi ukuhlonza ulwazi olubalulekile, asuse imininingwane
engabalulekile noma ephindaphindwayo, futhi akwazi ukubhala kabusha umqondo wokuqala
ngamagama akhe. Ukufingqa kusiza umfundi ukuthi aqonde umbhalo ngokujulile futhi akwazi
ukuwuchaza kwabanye ngendlela elula.
Bheka ikhasi 61 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Izinyathelo Zokufingqa
● Khomba ulwazi olubalulekile: Lokhu kufaka imibono emikhulu nomongo oyinhloko
wombhalo.
● Shiya imininingwane engabalulekile: Susa izibonelo ezingaphezu kwamandla
noma imininingwane engasizi ekuqondeni umbhalo.
● Susa ukuphindaphinda: Uma imisho iphinda into efanayo, kufanele ikhishelwe
ngaphandle.
● Sebenzisa amagama akho: Bhala kabusha umbhalo ngamagama akho ukuze
ubonise ukuqonda kwakho.
● Landela isitayela esifanele: Qinisekisa ukuthi ukufingqa kuyahambisana
nezimfuneko zesikole noma zomsebenzi.
Isibonelo
Umbhalo omude ungachaza izinguquko zesimo sezulu ezithinta ulwandle. Uma umfundi
efingqa, kufanele akhiphe amaphuzu amakhulu kuphela: ukukhuphuka kwezinga lokushisa,
ukwanda kwe-asidi, kanye nemiphumela kubantu nezilwane zasolwandle. Lokhu kufingqa
kugxile kuphela kulokho okubalulekile ngaphandle kokusebenzisa imininingwane eminingi.
Bheka ikhasi 61–63 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
22

Isifinyezo
Kulesi sahluko abafundi bafunde ukuthi ukufingqa kuyikhono elibalulekile elisiza ekufundeni
nasekubhaleni. Kubalulekile ukuhlonza amaphuzu amakhulu, ukushiya imininingwane
engabalulekile, ukususa ukuphindaphinda, nokusebenzisa amagama akho. Ukufingqa kusiza
ukufunda ngokujulile, ukuqonda kahle imiyalezo, nokwethula imibono ngendlela elula
nehlanzekile.
Bheka ikhasi 64 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Umsebenzi
Imisebenzi ehambisana nale module itholakala ekhasini 65. Lapha abafundi bayacelwa ukuthi
bafingqe umbhalo omude ngamazwi angaba ngu-100, bagcine ithoni efanele futhi bafomethise
ngendlela ehlelekile.
Bheka ikhasi 65 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi. Ngempendulo
zemisebenzi, bheka ikhasi 166 lencwadi yezifundo.

ISAHLUKO 4: UKUFUNDA
NGOKUBONAKALAYO
NOKWABEZINDABA
Izinhloso Zokufunda:
1. Qonda futhi uhlaziye izikhangiso, amakhathuni, namagrafu.
2. Qaphela futhi uchaze amagama ezobuchwepheshe okufunda ngokubonakalayo
nowabezinda ba.
3. Qonda indima kanye nokusetshenziswa kolimi ezikhangisweni nakumakhathuni.
4. Khomba futhi uchaze izinganekwane ezikhangisweni.
5. Qonda futhi uhlaziye izindlela zokukhangisa eziku-inthanethi.
25

Isingeniso
Kulesi sahluko, abafundi bazofunda ukuthi singaqonda kanjani izithombe nezikhangiso
esizibona nsuku zonke. Izithombe ezinjengalezi ezikuMabonakude, emaphephandabeni,
kumabhodi ezikhangiso, noma ku-inthanethi ziba nemiyalezo efihlekile. Abafundi bazobona
ukuthi amagama nezithombe kuxhumana kanjani ukuze kudluliselwe imiqondo ebalulekile.
Ekupheleni kwesahluko, umfundi uzokwazi ukuhlaziya izikhangiso, amahlaya, namashadi
ngendlela ecacile.
Bheka ikhasi 67 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Ukuhumusha Imibhalo Yezinto Ezibonakalayo
Imibhalo yezinto ezibonakalayo ifaka izithombe, amamephu, imigrafu, noma imidwebo edlulisa
ulwazi. Abafundi bayakhuthazwa ukuthi bafunde ukusebenzisa le mibhalo ukuze bathole
umqondo ojulile, njengoba ivame ukudlulisa imilayezo ngokushesha nangokucacile.
Bheka ikhasi 67 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Ukufunda Izindaba Ezĳulile Ezinemiyalezo Ebalulekile
Abafundi bayahlaziywa indlela amahlaya, izithombe, noma izindaba ezimfishane ezingadlulisa
ngayo umqondo ongaphezulu kobuso obubonakalayo. Lokhu kufundisa ukuthi ulimi lomzimba,
ukusetshenziswa kwezimpawu zobuso, nokuhleka usulu kuyindlela yokuxhumana efanele futhi
enezimfundiso ezĳulile.
Bheka ikhasi 68 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Ukuhlaziya Izikhangiso
Izikhangiso zifundiswa njengendlela yokudlulisa imiyalezo. Kuhlaziywa amagama
asetshenziswa, izithombe, imibala, kanye nendlela izikhangiso ezihlosela ngayo ukuthonya
izethameli. Abafundi bazobona ukuthi ulimi olusetshenziswa kwezikhangiso luhlale lugxile
ekukholiseni nasekuthonyeni.
Bheka ikhasi 69–70 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
26

Ulimi Olusetshenziswa Ezikhangisweni
Lolu limi luvame ukusebenzisa izisho ezinamandla, izikhangiso eziphoqayo, kanye nemibono
ethinta imizwa. Lokhu kusiza ukuqinisekisa ukuthi izethameli ziyakhuthazeka noma zikholelwe
kulokho okukhangiswayo.
Bheka ikhasi 70–72 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Amasu Okukhangisa Aku-Inthanethi
Abafundi bazofunda ngezindlela zesimanje zokukhangisa ezisebenzisa inthanethi,
okuhlanganisa izikhangiso ezizenzakalelayo, ama-pop-up, kanye nezinye izindlela
zokukhangisa eziku-inthanethi. Lokhu kubalulekile ngoba izindlela zokuxhumana zikhula
kakhulu emhlabeni wanamuhla.
Bheka ikhasi 72–73 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
ISathaya (Satire)
Lesi sigaba sichaza indlela amahlaya noma izikhangiso ezisebenzisa ngayo ukuhleka usulu,
ukugxeka, noma ukuhlanganisa iqiniso nokuhleka ukuze kudluliselwe imiyalezo ebalulekile
emphakathini. Lokhu kusiza abafundi baqonde ukuthi ukuhlazisa kungadluliswa ngendlela
emnandi kodwa enamandla.
Bheka ikhasi 74–76 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Ukufunda Amashadi Nemigqa Namashadi Wezinsika
Abafundi bayakhuthazwa ukuthi bafunde ukuhlaziya amashadi ahlukahlukene: imigqa, izinsika,
namanye amashadi wedatha. Lokhu kufundisa ukuhlaziya izihloko, ukubona izitayela,
nokwenza iziphetho kususelwa kulwazi olukhonjisiwe.
Bheka ikhasi 77–78 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
27

Isifinyezo
Kulesi sahluko abafundi bafunde:
● Ukuhumusha imibhalo yezinto ezibonakalayo.
● Ukuqonda izindaba ezĳulile ezinemiyalezo efihlekile.
● Ukuhlaziya izikhangiso, amagama nezithombe.
● Ukusebenzisa ulimi olubonakalayo nolukhuthazayo ezikhangisweni.
● Ukuqonda amasu okukhangisa aku-inthanethi.
● Ukuqonda indima ye-sathaya emphakathini.
● Ukuhlaziya nokufunda amashadi nemishadi yezinsika.

oluphoqayo.
Bheka ikhasi 80–81 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi. Ngempendulo
zemisebenzi, bheka ikhasi 168–169 lencwadi yezifundo.

ISAHLUKO 5: UKUBHALA
INKULUMO
Izinhloso Zokufunda kumakhono:
1. Ukuqonda izinhlobo ezahlukene zezindatshana.
2. Ukusebenzisa isu lokubhala elibizwa nge-5 S ekubhaleni izindatshana.
3. Ukusebenzisa indlela yokubhala ye-prosesi ekubhaleni izindatshana
4. Ukusebenzisa izakhiwo zolimi kanye nemithetho kahle ekubhaleni izindatshana.
5. Ukubhala indatshana ehlelwe kahle futhi ehlanganisiwe.
6. Ukufunda kabusha nokuhlela izindatshana zakho ngokucophelela.
30

Isingeniso
Kulesi sahluko abafundi bazofunda indlela yokubhala inkulumo ngendlela ehlelekile, ecacile
futhi ephumelelayo. Ukubhala inkulumo kudinga ukuhleleka, ukusebenzisa ulimi olucacile,
kanye nokuhlonza izethameli. Inkulumo enhle kufanele ibambe ukunaka, ibe nesakhiwo esihle,
futhi ibe nomthelela ezethamelini.
Bheka ikhasi 83 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Isakhiwo Senkulumo
Inkulumo ehlelekile ihlukaniswe yaba izingxenye ezintathu:
● Isingeniso – sibamba ukunaka kwezethameli futhi sikhombisa inhloso yen kulumo.
● Umzimba – wabelana ngemibono emikhulu, izibonelo, kanye nezincomo.
● Isiphetho – sigcizelela iphuzu eliyinhloko futhi siqeda inkulumo ngendlela enamandla.
Bheka ikhasi 84 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Isitayela Senkulumo
Inkulumo kufanele isebenzise ulimi oluhambisana nezethameli. Lokhu kufaka:
● Ukusebenzisa amagama acacile, alula.
● Ukusebenzisa imishwana emifushane ukuze kube lula ukuyilandela.
● Ukusebenzisa izingathekiso, izisho noma izifanekiso ukuze inkulumo ibe nomdlandla.
● Ukugwema amagama ayinkimbinkimbi adida izethameli.
Bheka ikhasi 85 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Isu Lokubhala – 5 S
Isu elibizwa nge-5 S lisiza ukubhala inkulumo ephumelelayo. Lihlanganisa:
1. Amandla – inkulumo kufanele ibe namandla okubamba ukunaka.
2. Isakhiwo – hlela inkulumo ibe nesingeniso, umzimba nesiphetho.
3. Izinzwa – sebenzisa imininingwane ehlanganisa ukubona, ukuzwa, nokuzwa imizwa.
4. Isitayela – qinisekisa ukuthi ulimi luyahambisana nezethameli.
31

5. Ukuhlola – funda kabusha, ulungise futhi uqinisekise ukuthi akukho phutha.
Bheka ikhasi 85 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
Ukubhala Ngokwenqubo
Inkulumo ibhalwa ngokulandela lezi zinyathelo:
● Ukuhlelela umbhalo kusengaphambili.
● Ukubhala okokuqala ngokulandela imiqondo emikhulu.
● Ukulungisa nokuhlela inkulumo.
● Ukufunda kabusha ukuze uqinisekise ukucaca nokuhleleka.
Bheka ikhasi 86–87 lencwadi yakho yezifundo ukuze uthole olunye ulwazi.
