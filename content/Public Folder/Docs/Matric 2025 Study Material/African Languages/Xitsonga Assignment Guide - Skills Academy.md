HOW TO APPROACH YOUR STUDIES

This is a very important journey and you should be enthusiastic\! After all, this is the

first step to reaching your goals.

Before you get start, take some time to read through these guidelines. How you

approach your studies is just as important as what you do when studying.

This assignment guide contains 2 assignments. You might feel tempted to start on the

assignments as soon as possible. We advise that you work through your textbook and

study guide first and complete the activities that can be found there. Read through

your textbook slowly in detail and make as many notes as possible. This will make the

studying process much easier.

Once you have completed the content and the activities in the textbook and study

guide, use the memo to see how well you did. If you have not gotten the desired

marks, simply revise the sections you did not fare so well in. Then have a go at the

test again\!

Manage your time well so you can give the right amount of attention to the

assignments. Once you have completed these, you must submit them to your tutors to

be marked.

Only once you have completed and submitted the assignments to your tutor will you

be eligible for your next round of books. Your books are dispatched in this way to

ensure that you do not become too overwhelmed with your studies.

Remember this journey is yours. So work at your own pace. If you need any

assistance, don’t hesitate to reach out to your tutors. They are there to help you.

Good luck and happy studies\!

● Tsala hi nkarhi lowu nga hundza.

● Tsala vuxokoxoko lebyi faneleke byo fana na:

● Vito na xivongo swa mufi.

● Adirese ya yena.

● Siku ro velekiwa.

● Vatswari va mufi na leswaku a a ri n’wana.

● Siku ro hundza emisaveni na xivangelo xa rifu.

● Leswi a swi fikeleleke/laha a nga fika emaninginingini/tidyondzo/xiave

eka migingiriko ya muganga kumbe tiko.

● Switori swo koma mayelana na leswi a a hanyisa xiswona. Leswi maxaka na

vandyangu va n’wi tsundzukaka ha swona.

● Lava a va siyeke na ku n’wi navelela ku etlela hi kurhula kumbe ku n’wi phata
