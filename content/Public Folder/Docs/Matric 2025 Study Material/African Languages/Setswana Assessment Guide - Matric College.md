HOW TO APPROACH YOUR STUDIES
We are sure that you are excited to dive right into your studies. This is a very important
journey and you should be enthusiastic! After all, this is the first step to reaching your
goals.
Before you get started, take some time to read through these guidelines. How you
approach your studies is just as important as what you do when studying.
This study guide includes practice assessments, memos and assignments. You might feel
tempted to start on the practice assessment as soon as possible. We advise that you work
through your textbook first and complete the activities that can be found there. Read
through your textbook slowly and make as many notes as possible. This will make the
studying process much easier.
Once you have completed the content and the activities in the textbook, you can take a
shot at the practice test. Practice tests are a great way to prepare for your assessments.
So use the opportunity wisely.
Set some time aside to complete this practice test. Read through the questions carefully
and complete it to the best of your ability. Once completed, use the memo to see how well
you did. If you find that you have not gotten the marks you desire, then simply revise the
sections you did not fare so well in. Then have a go at the test again!
If you are happy with your results for the practice test then you can move onto the
assignments provided by us. Manage your time well so you can give the right amount of
attention to the assignments. Once you have completed these, you must submit them to
your tutors to be marked.
Only once you have completed and submitted the assignments to your tutor will you be
eligible for your next round of books. Your books are dispatched in this way to ensure that
you do not become too overwhelmed with your studies.
Remember this journey is yours. So work at your own pace. If you need any assistance,
don’t hesitate to reach out to your tutors. They are there to help you.
Good luck and happy studies!

SETLHANGWA C
GO REKA SEJANAGA KWA FANTISING
Batho ba bantsi ga ba nke ba nagana ka ditsela tse dingwe tsa go reka sejanaga. Ba
nagana fela ka tsela e e tlwaelegileng ya go ikela kwa lebenkeleng la dĳanaga kgotsa go
reka mo mothong yo mongwe. O ka tlhopha go iponela sejanaga ka go reka kwa fantising.
Fantisi ke sengwe se se ka go itumedisang. Ga se batho ba bantsi ba ba ka lemogang
gore ba ka boloka madi a mantsi go bona dĳanaga tsa ditoro tsa bona ka mokgwa o.
Tlhokomela gore go ka nna le dilo dingwe tse dintsi tse di ka se tsamayeng sentle.
Fa o reka sejanaga kwa fantising, netefatsa gore setheo seo se kwadisitswe le molao wa
tshireletso ya bareki. Setheo se, se bolela fa fantisi e sireletsa bareki ka ditsela tse tharo
tse di latelang: go kwala diphoso tsotlhe tsa sejanaga, mofantisi o tshwanetse go neela
batho nako go lekola sejanaga pele fantisi e simolola, mme ga a tshwanela go bua bobe
ka, kgotsa go tlhagisa maemo a sejanaga. Itse gore dĳanaga di rekisiwa ka maemo ao di
leng mo go ona. Se se kaya gore o ka se ka wa se kgweetsa pele o se reka. Ka letsatsi la
fantisi, o tshwanetse go duela madi a peeletso, mme o bone nomoro ya go ikwadisetsa
theko.

Mokgwa wa go tshwaya Tekatlhaloganyo
 Motlhatlhojwa a se sokelwe dirope fa dikarabo di na le mopeleto o o fosagetseng
le diphoso tsa puo ntle le fa diphoso tseo di fetola bokao gonne go tobilwe go
tlhaloganya. (Diphoso di nne di supiwe.)
 Fa motlhatlhojwa a dirisa mafoko a puo esele, ikgatholose mafoko ao, mme fa
karabo e santse e tlhaloganyega, o se ka wa mo jela maduo. Fela, lefoko le le
tswang mo puong esele le a amogelesega fa le dirisitswe mo setlhangweng e bile
le tlhokega mo karabong.
 Mo dipotsong tse di bulegileng, dikarabo tsa EE/NNYAYA kgotsa KE A DUMELA/
GA KE DUMELANE di se abelwe maduo. Lebaka/Tshegetso ke yona e e
tshwanetseng go tsewa tsia.
 NNETE/FOSAGETSE kgotsa NTLHA/KAKANYO e se abelwe leduo lepe.
Lebaka/Tshegetso ke yona e e tshwanetseng go tsewa tsia.
 Fa go tlhokega karabo ya lefoko le le lengwe, mme motlhatlhojwa a neetse polelo
yotlhe, mo tshwae ka nepagalo fa e le gore lefoko le le nepagetseng le thaletswe/
le ntshofaditswe.
 Fa go tlhokiwa dintlha di le pedi/tharo, mme go tlhagisitswe tse dintsi, go
tshwaiwe fela tse pedi/tharo tsa ntlha.
 Amogela dipharologano tsa diteme.
 Mo dipotsong tse go tlhophiwang karabo e e nepagetseng mo go tsona, amogela
