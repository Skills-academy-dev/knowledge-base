HOW TO APPROACH YOUR STUDIES

We are sure that you are excited to dive right into your studies. This is a very important journey

and you should be enthusiastic\! After all, this is the first step to reaching your goals.

Before you get started, take some time to read through these guidelines. How you approach

your studies is just as important as what you do when studying.

This study guide includes practice assessments, memos and assignments. You might feel

tempted to start on the practice assessment as soon as possible. We advise that you work

through your textbook first and complete the activities that can be found there. Read through

your textbook slowly and make as many notes as possible. This will make the studying process

much easier.

Once you have completed the content and the activities in the textbook, you can take a shot at

the practice test. Practice tests are a great way to prepare for your assessments. So use the

opportunity wisely.

Set some time aside to complete this practice test. Read through the questions carefully and

complete it to the best of your ability. Once completed, use the memo to see how well you did.

If you find that you have not gotten the marks you desire, then simply revise the sections you

did not fare so well in. Then have a go at the test again\!

If you are happy with your results for the practice test then you can move onto the assignments

provided by us. Manage your time well so you can give the right amount of attention to the

assignments. Once you have completed these, you must submit them to your tutors to be

marked.

Only once you have completed and submitted the assignments to your tutor will you be eligible

for your next round of books. Your books are dispatched in this way to ensure that you do not

become too overwhelmed with your studies.

Remember this journey is yours. So work at your own pace. If you need any assistance, don’t

hesitate to reach out to your tutors. They are there to help you.

Good luck and happy studies\!

UMBHALO C

INCWADI YOKUZICHAZA EBHALWE KAHLE

Kubalulekile ukuba ubhale kahle incwadi yakho yokuzichaza ('CV') ukuze ube sethubeni elihle lokubizelwa inhlolokhono nokuqashwa. Imvamisa incwadi yokuzichaza iyona equkethe ubunjalo bakho obungakubeka ngisho nasengcupheni yokungabizwa nhlobo uma ingabhalekanga kahle. Uma ufuna umsebenzi, incwadi yakho yokuzichaza iyona efika kuqala kumqashi engakakuboni wena siqu. Bhala incwadi yokuzichaza engathemelezi ngemininingwane engaqondene nalowo msebenzi owufunayo. Mayibe mfushane ukuze ongakuqasha akwazi ukuyifunda ngokushesha angazichitheli isikhathi ngemininingwane engenasidingo. Ikhasi lokuqala libaluleke kakhulu ngoba liyindawo lapho amehlo egĳimela khona kuqala ukuthola imininingwane ebalulekile. Lokhu kuhlanganisa ulwazi ngawe, indlela abangakuthola ngayo, imfundo yamabanga aphansi naphezulu neziqu ozizuzile.

Kungaba umqondo omuhle uma ungafaka izibonelo zemisebenzi osuyenzile ikakhulukazi emikhakheni ephathelene nobuciko. Izinto osuke wazenza emphakathini, izinto osuhlomulile kuzo nezinto onamakhono okuzenza nazo zibalulekile. Kungaba izitifiketi zokuthamela imicimbi noma izifundo ezithile. Yenaba kabanzi ngolwazi oluzuzile eminyakeni edlule kweminye imisebenzi oseke wasebenza kuyo. Makuvele nokuthi ungumuntu okungathenjelwa kuye, oneqiniso, okwazi ukusebenza nabanye abantu nangaphansi kwengcindezi nozimiselayo ngomsebenzi wakhe.

Zinike isineke nesikhathi sokubhala incwadi yakho yokuzichaza. Lokho kuzokusiza ngokuthi ukwazi ukubona izinto ezingakulahlekisela ngamathuba. Uzokwazi nokwenza ucwaningo ngomsebenzi nenkampani ofaka kuyo isicelo. Sebenzisa le ncwadi yokuzichaza ukuzidayisa kabanzi ngokuthi kungani ungumuntu okufanele leyo nkampani imqashe. Incwadi yokuzichaza mayihambisane nokudingwa inkampani.
