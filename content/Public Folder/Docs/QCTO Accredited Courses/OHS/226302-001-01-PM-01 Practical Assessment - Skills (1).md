PM-01-PS01 CONDUCT MEETINGS WITH EMPLOYEES
TO DETERMINE SPECIFIC NEEDS RELATING TO
OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY PRACTICES IN
THE WORKPLACE
Complete the following activities in the presence of your facilitator/instructor who must observe the
activity and complete the attached observation evaluation form.
Activity 1
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to conduct meetings
with employees to determine specific needs relating to Occupational Health and Safety practices
in the workplace in your role as an Occupational Health and Safety Practitioner.
This practical is part of learning whose focus is on providing the learner an opportunity to practice
the skills required to consult with employees, identify their OHS needs and represent them at the
various forums as an advocate and facilitator to ensure that these needs are adequately considered
and that the issues underpinning these needs are resolved.
You will be provided with a simulated or controlled working environment with all the tools,
equipment and physical requirements to execute the skill.
Scenario-Based Practical Task: Conducting Meetings to Determine Specific OHS Needs
Overview:
This practical task evaluates the learner’s ability to conduct effective meetings with employees to
determine and address Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) needs in the workplace. The task
involves setting up and facilitating meetings, probing issues, summarizing and prioritizing needs,
and recording minutes.
11

Scenario Description:
You are an Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Practitioner tasked with organizing and conducting a
meeting to gather feedback and identify OHS needs from employees. You will be using roleplay with fellow
learners acting as employees with varying perspectives and concerns. Your role is to facilitate the meeting,
guide discussions, and ensure all concerns are addressed and documented.
Staging for the Task:
Simulated Environment:
● Meeting Room Setup: Arrange a meeting room with a table, chairs, a whiteboard or flip chart, and
necessary stationery (e.g., pens, paper).
● Participants: Fellow learners will roleplay as employees with diverse OHS concerns. E a c h
participant will have a specific role and concerns to express during the meeting.
● Equipment: Laptop or projector for displaying the agenda if needed, and recording tools for capturing
minutes.
Roleplay Details for Participants:
● Participant 1: Concerned about inadequate ventilation in the painting area. Raises issues about
unstable scaffolding and its safety.
● Participant 2: Worried about unsafe handling of hazardous chemicals. Reports frequent near-
misses with heavy machinery.
● Participant 3: Frustrated with lack of training on new equipment. Complains about insufficient
personal protective equipment (PPE).
● Participant 4: Expresses general dissatisfaction with current safety procedures. Feels that
emergency procedures are not well communicated or practiced.
Resources Required:
● Simulated Meeting Room: Table, chairs, whiteboard/flip chart, and stationery.
● Roleplay Participants: Fellow learners acting as employees with specific concerns.
● Recording Tools: Laptop or paper for recording minutes.
● Agenda Template: Pre-prepared agenda for the meeting.
12

Tasks to Be Completed:
PA01-01: Appropriately Convene a Meeting with Employees and Set a Basic Meeting Purpose and
Agenda
● Objective: Set up and initiate a meeting to discuss OHS needs with a clear purpose and agenda.
● Steps:
1. Schedule the Meeting: Send out meeting invitations to all participants, specifying the date,
time, and location of the meeting.
2. Prepare the Agenda: Develop a clear agenda outlining the meeting’s purpose, topics for
discussion, and objectives. Include items such as reviewing current OHS practices,
identifying issues, and discussing potential improvements.
3. Distribute the Agenda: Share the agenda with participants prior to the meeting, allowing
them to prepare any relevant information or questions.
4. Set Up the Room: Arrange the meeting room to facilitate discussion, ensuring all
participants can see and hear each other.
PA01-02: Guide and Facilitate Discussions to Identify the Issues, Needs, and Feelings of the
Group
● Objective: Lead the discussion to uncover and understand the issues, needs, and feelings of the
participants.
● Steps:
1. Open the Meeting: Welcome participants, introduce the purpose of the meeting, and
outline the agenda.
2. Facilitate Discussion: Use open-ended questions to encourage participants to share their
OHS concerns and needs. For example, “Can you describe any safety issues you’ve
encountered recently?” or “What improvements would you like to see in our OHS
practices?”
3. Encourage Participation: Ensure all participants have an opportunity to speak and share
their perspectives. Manage dominant voices to give others a chance to contribute.
4. Acknowledge Feelings: Recognize and validate participants' feelings and concerns to
build trust and ensure open communication.
13

PA01-03: Probe the Issues and Identify the Real Issues and the Underpinning Needs and
Potential Consequences of These Needs
● Objective: Delve deeper into the issues to uncover underlying needs and potential
consequences.
● Steps:
1. Ask Probing Questions: Follow up on initial comments with probing questions to get to the
root of the issues. For example, “What specific problems are you facing with the current
safety protocols?” or “How does this issue impact your daily work?”
2. Analyse Needs: Identify and document the underlying needs driving the issues
discussed. Consider factors such as training gaps, equipment deficiencies, or procedural
improvements.
3. Discuss Consequences: Explore the potential consequences of not addressing these
needs. Discuss how unresolved issues might impact safety, productivity, and employee
well-being.
PA01-04: Summarize and Prioritize the Needs
● Objective: Summarize the identified needs and prioritize them based on urgency and impact.
● Steps:
1. Summarize Needs: Recap the key issues and needs identified during the discussion,
ensuring all points are covered.
2. Prioritize Needs: Use a prioritization method (e.g., high, medium, low) to rank the needs
based on their severity and potential impact on workplace safety.
3. Discuss Prioritization: Share the summary and prioritization with participants to confirm
that the key concerns are accurately reflected and agreed upon.
PA01-05: Commit to a Process for Raising the Needs with the Relevant Decision-Makers
● Objective: Develop a plan to present the identified needs to decision-makers for action.
● Steps:
1. Develop a Plan: Outline a process for escalating the identified needs to management or
relevant decision-makers. Include steps for documentation, communication, and follow-
up.
2. Communicate the Process: Inform participants about how their concerns will be
addressed, including who will be contacted and what actions will be taken.
14

3. Set Timelines: Establish timelines for presenting the needs and receiving feedback
from decision-makers.
PA01-06: Capture/Record the Minutes of the Discussion
● Objective: Accurately document the discussion, including key points, decisions, and action
items.
● Steps:
1. Take Notes: Record key points from the discussion, including issues raised, needs
identified, and any agreed-upon actions or decisions.
2. Format Minutes: Organize the minutes in a clear and structured format, including the
meeting date, participants, agenda items, discussion points, and action items.
3. Distribute Minutes: Share the minutes with all participants and relevant stakeholders
to ensure transparency and accountability.
Examination Details:
The learner will be assessed on their ability to:
● IAC0101: Appropriately set up and convene the meeting, including providing an agenda and
invitations.
● IAC0102: Effectively guide and facilitate discussions, ensuring that all participants’ issues,
needs, and feelings are identified.
● IAC0103: Probe issues to uncover real needs and potential consequences, demonstrating
analytical skills.
● IAC0104: Summarize and prioritize needs clearly and accurately, showing organizational
skills.
● IAC0105: Commit to a process for addressing needs with decision-makers, demonstrating
planning and communication skills.
● IAC0106: Capture and record minutes accurately, ensuring all relevant information is
documented.
Tangible Outputs Required:
● Meeting agenda and invitations.
● Facilitated discussion and summarized needs.
● Prioritized list of OHS needs.
● Documented plan for raising needs with decision-makers.
15

● Recorded minutes of the meeting.
Assessment Method:
● Direct Observation: The learner’s performance in organizing, facilitating, and documenting the
meeting will be observed.
● Review of Documentation: Accuracy and completeness of meeting minutes, agenda, and action
plans will be reviewed.
● Roleplay Performance: Evaluation of the learner’s interaction with roleplay participants and their
effectiveness in addressing concerns.
This detailed scenario ensures that learners are tested on their ability to manage meetings effectively,
address OHS needs, and communicate solutions in a simulated but realistic setting.
Note: The facilitator/assessor must be present and he or she must complete the attached evaluation
checklist.

Scenario-Based Practical Task: Investigating Employee Needs
Overview:
This practical task is designed to test a learner’s ability to investigate stated employee needs related to
Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) in the workplace. The task involves checking company policies,
researching the history of stated needs, discussing practicality and cost implications, and providing feedback to
employees.
Scenario Description:
You are an Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Practitioner tasked with investigating and addressing specific
employee OHS needs within a simulated workplace environment. The scenario involves roleplay with fellow
learners acting as employees with specific concerns. Your role is to thoroughly investigate these concerns,
communicate with supervisors and OHS staff, and provide feedback and alternative solutions to employees.
17

Resources Required:
● Simulated Office/Meeting Room: Table, chairs, computers, file cabinets, whiteboard or flip chart.
●
Documentation: Company policies, procedures, SOPs, historical records, and previous OH
S reports.
● Roleplay Participants: Fellow learners acting as employees, supervisors, and OHS staff.
● Recording Tools: Laptop or paper for documenting feedback and meeting outcomes.
Staging for the Task:
Simulated Environment:
● Workspace Setup: Create a simulated office or meeting room environment with necessary
equipment including computers, file cabinets, and a whiteboard or flip chart.
● Roleplay Participants: Fellow learners will act as employees with specific OHS needs, as well as
supervisors and OHS staff.
● Documents: Prepare relevant company policies, procedures, and standard operating procedures
(SOPs) for review. Include historical records of previous OHS issues and needs.
Roleplay Details for Participants:
1. Employees:
● Participant 1: Complains about insufficient ergonomic support leading to musculoskeletal issues.
Concerned about previous requests being ignored.
● Participant 2: Reports inadequate emergency exits and safety signage in the warehouse.
Expresses frustration over the slow response to previous safety concerns.
● Participant 3: Expresses concern about outdated training materials and lack of refresher courses.
Believes previous requests for training updates were not taken seriously.
● Participant 4: Notes issues with the ventilation system causing discomfort and health problems.
Feels that past concerns were dismissed due to cost issues.
2. Supervisors and OHS Staff:
● Supervisor 1: Provides insight into budget constraints and operational challenges affecting the
implementation of ergonomic improvements.
● Supervisor 2: Discusses the reasons behind delays in addressing emergency exit and signage
issues, citing past budget limitations and prioritization of other issues.
● OHS Staff 1: Gives feedback on why training materials were not updated previously, including
reasons related to resource allocation and administrative delays.
18

● OHS Staff 2: Explains previous decisions regarding the ventilation system and discusses possible cost
implications for upgrades.
Tasks to Be Completed:
PA02-01: Check the Relevant Company Policies, Procedures, and SOPs and Compare Them with the
Stated Needs
● Objective: Ensure that the stated employee needs are in line with existing company policies,
procedures, and SOPs.
● Steps:
1. Obtain Documentation: Gather all relevant company policies, procedures, and SOPs related to
OHS.
2. Review Stated Needs: Review the specific OHS needs stated by employees.
3. Compare and Analyse: Compare the stated needs with the existing policies and procedures to
identify any gaps or areas of non-compliance.
4. Document Findings: Prepare a summary of how the stated needs align or conflict with current
policies and procedures.
PA02-02: Research the History of the Stated Need and Investigate Reasons Why This Need Was Not
Addressed Previously
● Objective: Understand the background of the stated needs and why they may not have been addressed
in the past.
● Steps:
1. Access Historical Records: Review historical records, previous reports, and meeting minutes
related to the stated OHS needs.
2. Conduct Interviews: Interview relevant personnel, including past OHS practitioners, to gather
information on why the needs were not previously addressed.
3. Analyse Reasons: Identify common reasons for the lack of resolution, such as budget
constraints, lack of awareness, or procedural gaps.
4. Prepare a Report: Document the history of the needs and reasons for their prior inaction.
PA02-03: Discuss with Supervision and OHS Staff Regarding the Practicality and Cost Implications of
Meeting the Needs
● Objective: Evaluate the feasibility and financial impact of addressing the stated OHS needs.
19

● Steps:
1. Schedule Meetings: Arrange meetings with supervisors and OHS staff to discuss the stated
needs.
2. Present Findings: Present the results of your comparison and historical research.
3. Discuss Practicality: Evaluate the practicality of addressing the needs, considering factors
such as resources, time, and operational impact.
4. Assess Cost Implications: Analyse and discuss the financial implications of implementing
changes or solutions.
5. Document Discussions: Prepare a summary of the discussions, including key points on
feasibility and costs.
PA02-04: Give Feedback to Constituency and Discuss Alternative Ways of Dealing with the Needs
● Objective: Provide feedback to employees and explore alternative solutions for addressing the
OHS needs.
● Steps:
1. Prepare Feedback: Develop a clear and concise feedback report summarizing the
investigation results, discussions with supervisors, and proposed alternatives.
2. Conduct Meeting: Arrange a meeting with the employees to discuss the findings, feedback,
and potential alternative solutions.
3. Discuss Alternatives: Explore and discuss different approaches to addressing the needs,
including both immediate and long-term solutions.
4. Document Outcomes: Record the feedback provided and decisions made during the
meeting, including any agreed-upon actions or follow-ups.
Examination Details:
The learner will be assessed on their ability to:
● IAC0201: Check and comply with relevant company policies, procedures, and SOPs when
addressing OHS needs.
● IAC0202: Investigate and document the history of the stated OHS needs and reasons for previous
inaction.
● IAC0203: Effectively discuss practicality and cost implications with supervisors and OHS staff.
● IAC0204: Provide clear feedback and discuss alternative solutions with employees.
20

Tangible Outputs Required:
● Summary of policy and procedure comparison.
● Report on the history of the OHS needs and reasons for prior inaction.
● Summary of discussions with supervisors and OHS staff.
● Feedback report and documented alternative solutions discussed with employees.
Assessment Method:
● Direct Observation: Evaluation of the learner’s ability to facilitate discussions, investigate needs,
and provide feedback.
● Review of Documentation: Assessment of the accuracy and completeness of policy
comparisons, historical reports, and feedback documentation.
● Roleplay Performance: Evaluation of the learner’s interaction with roleplay participantsand
effectiveness in addressing OHS concerns.
This detailed scenario ensures that learners are tested on their ability to investigate andaddress
employee OHS needs thoroughly and effectively in a realistic and controlled environment.

Simulated Scripting for Meetings
1. Meeting to Discuss Outstanding Issues with Decision-Makers
Participants:
● Learner (OHS Practitioner)
● Decision-Maker 1 (Senior Manager)
● Decision-Maker 2 (Operational Manager)
Setting: Formal meeting room with a table and presentation setup.
Learner: "Good morning/afternoon. Thank you both for meeting with me today. As part of our ongoing
efforts to address employee needs identified in our recent safety meeting, I’ve reviewed the minutes and
identified several outstanding issues that require our attention. I’d like to go over these with you and
