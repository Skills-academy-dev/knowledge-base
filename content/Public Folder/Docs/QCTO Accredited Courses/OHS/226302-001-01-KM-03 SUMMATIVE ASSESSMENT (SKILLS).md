Question 8
IAC0108 Given a range of individual and group communication, conflict and understanding gap
scenarios be able to:
a. Indicate how the model of communication applies to solving communication and conflict problems;
b. Draw up appropriate coaching plans for subordinates;
c. Indicate the process of effectively influencing people to work according to standards. [15]
Scenario: At a marketing firm, the team has been facing issues with meeting project deadlines. Emily, a
project manager, notices that the team’s communication has become increasingly strained. There is
tension between team members, particularly between Alex, a senior designer, and Jordan, a junior
designer. Alex believes Jordan’s work quality is affecting the project’s success, while Jordan feels that
Alex’s feedback is overly critical and unconstructive. The team has started to show signs of frustration
and disengagement.
Emily, the project manager, observes a decline in team morale and efficiency due to communication
issues and conflicting views between Alex and Jordan. To address this, she initiates a team meeting to
discuss the project’s progress and the challenges being faced. During the meeting, Emily uses the
communication model to clarify expectations, address concerns, and set actionable goals.
In a follow-up one-on-one meeting with Alex, Emily provides specific examples of how his feedback
could be more constructive and less critical. She helps Alex develop a plan for providing positive and
actionable feedback to Jordan. Similarly, Emily coaches Jordan on how to better receive and implement
feedback, offering support and setting clear personal goals for improvement.
Emily also introduces regular feedback sessions and team-building activities to improve communication
and collaboration. By setting clear expectations, offering support, and leading by example, Emily
effectively influences Alex and Jordan to align with project standards and foster a more collaborative and
productive work environment.
