Activity 1

In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to conduct meetings with

employees to determine specific needs relating to Occupational Health and Safety practices in the

workplace in your role as an Occupational Health and Safety Practitioner.

This practical is part of learning whose focus is on providing the learner an opportunity to practice

the skills required to supervise the execution of the activities of an OHS function.

You will be provided with a simulated or controlled working environment with all the tools,

equipment and physical requirements to execute the skill.

Detailed Scenario-Based Practical Task for Conducting OHS Needs Assessment Meetings

Scenario Overview:

As an Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Practitioner, you are tasked with conducting

meetings with employees to determine their specific needs regarding OHS practices in the

workplace. This practical task will test your ability to plan and manage OHS-related meetings, set

objectives, align activities with performance targets, establish review processes, and contribute to

budget development.

Controlled/Simulated Environment:

You will conduct this practical task in a controlled environment that simulates a workplace setting.

This may include a conference room for meetings, access to relevant documentation, and roleplay

participants acting as employees. You will use roleplay scenarios to simulate interactions and

gather information.

Tasks and Detailed Instructions

Task PA01-01: Set Planning Objectives, Develop Key Performance Areas (KPAs), and Key

Success Indicators (KSIs)

Objective: Establish clear planning objectives for improving OHS practices, identify key

performance areas, and define key success indicators.

Steps:

1\. Review Background Information:

o Study the provided background information on current OHS practicesand previous

performance reports.

o Understand the specific needs of the organization and employees.

2\. Set Planning Objectives:

o Define specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART)

objectives. For example: "Reduce workplace accidents by 20% within the next

year."

3\. Develop Key Performance Areas (KPAs):

o Identify critical areas to focus on, such as training effectiveness, incident

reporting, and hazard identification.

o Example KPAs:

▪ Training: Percentage of employees trained in OHS practices.

▪ Incident Reporting: Number of incidents reported and resolved.

▪ Hazard Identification: Frequency and effectiveness of hazard

assessments.

4\. Define Key Success Indicators (KSIs):

o Establish metrics to measure success in achieving objectives. For example:

▪ Training: At least 90% of employees complete OHS training.

19

▪ Incident Reporting: Reduce the number of unreported incidents by 30%.

▪ Hazard Identification: Increase the identification of potential hazards by

50%.

5\. Document Your Plan:

o Prepare a detailed planning document outlining objectives, KPAs, and KSIs.

Task PA01-02: Align the Various Activities with the Performance Targets

Objective: Ensure that all planned activities are aligned with the performance targets set in the

previous task.

Steps:

1\. Review Planned Activities:

o List all planned OHS activities, including training sessions, safety drills, and

policy updates.

2\. Align Activities with Performance Targets:

o Match each activity with the relevant KPAs and KSIs.

o For example:

▪ Training Sessions: Align with the training KPA to improve employee

knowledge and skills.

▪ Safety Drills: Align with the incident reporting KPA to enhance emergency

preparedness.

3\. Develop an Activity Schedule:

o Create a timeline for implementing activities and achieving performance targets.

o Example Schedule:

▪ Training Sessions: Monthly, starting next month.

▪ Safety Drills: Quarterly.

4\. Document Alignment:

o Prepare a document showing how each activity aligns with performance targets

and KPAs.

Tasks and Guidelines:

Task PA02-01: Identify the Availability of the Required Resources

1\. Resource Inventory:

o Review Resource Needs:

▪ List all resources required for the OHS function, including personnel,

equipment, tools, and materials.

▪ Example resources: Safety equipment (PPE), training materials,

inspection tools, safety signage.

o Conduct Resource Assessment:

▪ Check current inventory levels for all listed resources.

▪ Identify any shortages or surpluses in resources.

▪ Use resource management software or inventory records to track

availability.

2\. Assess Resource Providers:

o Internal Resources:

▪ Review internal records to determine available resources within the

organization.

▪ Consult with relevant departments or teams to confirm availability.

o External Resources:

▪ Identify external suppliers or service providers who can supply the

required resources.

▪ Contact suppliers to confirm availability and delivery timelines.

3\. Documentation:

o Resource Availability Report:

▪ Prepare a report documenting the availability of required resources,

including any gaps or issues.

29

▪ Include details such as resource quantities, provider names, and

availability status.

Task PA02-02: Set Up Appropriate Integrated Networks to Obtain Shared Resources

1\. Identify Shared Resources:

o Determine Requirements:

▪ Identify resources that are shared among different departments or

functions.

▪ Examples: Safety training programs used by multiple teams, shared

inspection tools.

2\. Establish Integrated Networks:

o Network Setup:

▪ Develop a plan for integrating resources across departments or teams.

▪ Identify key stakeholders and departments involved in the sharing

process.

o Coordination:

▪ Set up meetings or communication channels with stakeholders to discuss

resource sharing.

▪ Use collaboration tools or software to facilitate resource coordination and

scheduling.

3\. Create Resource Sharing Agreements:

o Draft Agreements:

▪ Develop agreements or protocols outlining how shared resources will be

accessed and managed.

▪ Include details on scheduling, usage policies, and responsibilities.

o Approval and Implementation:

30

▪ Obtain approval from relevant departments or teams for the resource

sharing agreements.

▪ Implement the agreements and monitor adherence.

Task PA02-03: Set Up Service Level Agreements (SLAs) with Internal and External Clients

1\. Define SLA Requirements:

o Internal Clients:

▪ Identify internal departments or teams requiring OHS services or

resources.

▪ Determine the specific services or support they need, such as training,

inspections, or compliance assistance.

o External Clients:

▪ Identify external clients, such as suppliers or contractors, who provide

resources or services.

▪ Determine the expectations and requirements for these services, such as

delivery times, quality standards, or reporting.

2\. Draft SLAs:

o Internal SLAs:

▪ Prepare SLA documents outlining the agreed-upon services, response

times, performance metrics, and responsibilities.

▪ Include terms for regular review and updates.

o External SLAs:

▪ Prepare SLA documents for external clients, including service

descriptions, performance expectations, and compliance requirements.

▪ Ensure terms are clear and enforceable.

3\. Negotiate and Finalize SLAs:

31

o Internal Negotiations:

▪ Meet with internal stakeholders to discuss and finalize SLA terms.

▪ Obtain formal approval and signatures if necessary.

o External Negotiations:

▪ Engage with external clients to negotiate SLA terms.

▪ Finalize agreements and ensure both parties understand and accept the

terms.

4\. Monitor and Review SLAs:

o Implementation:

▪ Implement the SLAs and monitor performance against the agreed terms.

▪ Address any issues or deviations promptly.

o Review and Update:

▪ Regularly review SLA performance and update agreements as needed to

reflect changes in requirements or conditions.
