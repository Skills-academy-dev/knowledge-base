Resources for Conducting OHS NeedsAssessment Meetings
1. Roleplay Scripts and Instructions
Roleplay Scenario: Conduct Meetings with Employees to Gather OHS Needs
Scenario Description: The learner, in their role as an Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)
Practitioner, is tasked with conducting meetings to gather input from employees about their
specific needs and concerns related to OHS practices.The goal is to identify areas for
improvement and develop actionable plans based on employee feedback.
Participants:
● Learner (OHS Practitioner): Responsible for preparing and conducting the meetings,
gathering information, and documenting needs.
● Employees (Role-players):Act as employees providing feedback, raising concerns,
and suggesting improvements.
Instructions for Participants:
● Learner (OHS Practitioner):
1. Preparation:
▪ Review background information and current OHS practices.
▪ Develop a meeting agenda that includes key topics to be discussed.
▪ Prepare questions and prompts to facilitate discussion.
2. Conducting the Meeting:
▪ Start the meeting by introducing the purpose and agenda.
▪ Facilitate a discussion where employees can share their feedback and
concerns.
▪ Take detailed notes on the feedback received.
▪ Ensure that all relevant topics are covered and questions are answered.
3. Post-Meeting:
