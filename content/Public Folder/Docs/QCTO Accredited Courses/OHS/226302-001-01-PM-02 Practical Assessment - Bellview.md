PM-02-PS01 IDENTIFY THE LEGAL AND REGULATORY
REQUIREMENTS RELATING TO CONTINUOUS
HAZARD AND RISK IDENTIFICATION IN WORKPLACES
Complete the following activities in the presence of your facilitator/instructor who must observe the
activity and complete the attached observation evaluation form.
Activity 1
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to identify the legal and
regulatory requirements relating to continuous hazard and risk identification in workplaces in your
role as an Occupational Health and Safety Practitioner.
This practical is part of learning whose focus is on providing the learner an opportunity to practice
t h e
skills required to inspect workplaces against specific workplace standards and initiate actio
ns to prevent injury and harm to people and equipment.
You will be provided with a simulated or controlled working environment with all the tools,
equipment and physical requirements to execute the skill.
Scenario-Based Practical Task: Identifying Legal and Regulatory Requirements for
Continuous Hazard and Risk Identification
Overview:
This practical task tests the learner's ability to identify and understand legal and regulatory
requirements related to continuous hazard and risk identification in workplaces. As an Occupational
Health and Safety (OHS) Practitioner, the learner will be required to inspect and verify that
workplace standards are adhered to and that effective controls are in place to prevent injury and
harm.
The scenario will take place in a simulated or controlled working environment arranged by the
training provider. This environment will include a variety of tools, equipment, and staged hazards to
provide a realistic experience for the learner.
11

Scenario Description:
You are assigned to review and ensure compliance with OHS standards and regulatory requirements
for a simulated workplace. The environment will include different work areas, equipment, and potential
hazards. Your goal is to access and interpret SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures) and
regulatory documents, evaluate the risk profile, confirm regulatory compliance, and ensure that
appropriate controls are in place.
Resources Required:
● Simulated Worksite Environment: Set up with various work areas, equipment, and staged
hazards.
● Regulatory and SOP Documents: Access to relevant SOPs and regulatory guidelines.
● Risk Assessment Tools: Risk matrices, checklists, and hazard identification forms.
● Safety Signage and PPE: Proper signage and personal protective equipment for demonstration.
Staging for the Task:
The controlled worksite will include:
● Simulated Work Areas: Various workstations representing different functions and types of work
(e.g., manufacturing, warehousing, administrative).
● Regulatory and SOP Documents: A collection of SOPs and regulatory guidelines relevant to the
simulated worksite.
● Hazardous Conditions: Staged hazards such as unsafe equipment, improper storage, and
potential risk areas.
● Safety Signage and Controls: Proper signage indicating hazards, safety procedures, and
required PPE.
Tasks to Be Completed:
PA01-01: Access the SOPs and Regulatory Requirements for the Workplace
● Objective: Obtain and review the relevant SOPs and regulatory documents for the workplace.
● Steps:
1. Locate Documents: Access the folder or digital repository where SOPs and regulatory
requirements are stored. Ensure you have access to the latest versions.
12

2. Review SOPs: Read through the SOPs provided. These should cover procedures for
various tasks and operations within the workplace.
3. Review Regulatory Requirements: Study the relevant local, national, and
industry-specific regulations that apply to the workplace. This may include health and
safety regulations, environmental laws, and industry standards.
4. Document Access: Take notes on key aspects of the SOPs and regulations, focusing
on requirements related to hazard and risk management.
PA01-02: Interpret the Risk Profile for the Workplace
● Objective: Analyse and interpret the risk profile for the simulated workplace based on available
data and observations.
● Steps:
1. Review Risk Assessments: Examine any existing risk assessments and hazard
identification documents provided for the simulated workplace.
2. Identify Risks: Identify and categorize the hazards and risks present in each work area.
Consider factors such as equipment, processes, and work environment.
3. Evaluate Risk Levels: Assess the severity and likelihood of each identified risk. Use
tools like risk matrices or scoring systems to determine the risk levels.
4. Document Findings: Record your interpretation of the risk profile, noting high, medium,
and low-risk areas and any specific concerns or priorities.
PA01-03: Confirm the Regulated Requirements
● Objective: Verify that all regulated OHS requirements are met within the simulated workplace.
● Steps:
1. Cross-Check Regulations: Compare the requirements outlined in the regulatory
documents with the practices and conditions observed in the workplace.
2. Verify Compliance: Check that all applicable regulations are being followed. This
includes proper documentation, employee training, and adherence to safety standards.
3. Identify Non-Compliance: Note any areas where regulatory requirements are not being
met. Document specific instances and provide evidence.
4. Report Findings: Prepare a report summarizing your findings, including compliance
status and any gaps or deficiencies.
13

PA01-04: Check That All Requirements Are Covered by the SOPs
● Objective: Ensure that the SOPs adequately address all required safety and operational
standards.
● Steps:
1. Review SOP Content: Examine the SOPs to ensure they cover all necessary
procedures and safety measures for identified hazards and risks.
2. Match with Regulatory Requirements: Cross-reference the SOPs with regulatory
requirements to ensure that all aspects are covered.
3. Identify Gaps: Look for any missing elements in the SOPs that should be included
based on the regulatory requirements and risk profile.
4. Update SOPs: If gaps are identified, recommend updates or revisions to the SOPs to
ensure comprehensive coverage.
PA01-05: Check That Appropriate Controls Are in Place
● Objective: Verify that appropriate controls are implemented to manage identified risks
effectively.
● Steps:
1. Inspect Controls: Examine the controls in place for managing identified hazards. This
may include engineering controls, administrative controls, and personal protective
equipment (PPE).
2. Assess Effectiveness: Evaluate the effectiveness of the controls based on their
implementation and performance in mitigating risks.
3. Identify Improvements: Note any areas where controls are insufficient or could be
improved. Consider factors such as adequacy, maintenance, and employee
compliance.
4. Document and Report: Prepare a report detailing the effectiveness of controls, any
identified issues, and recommendations for improvement.
14

Examination Details:
The learner will be assessed on their ability to:
● IAC0101: Access and review OHS SOPs and regulatory requirements, demonstrating an
understanding of the documentation.
● IAC0102: Interpret the OHS risk profile for the workplace, showing the ability to identify
and assess risks effectively.
● IAC0103: Confirm that regulated OHS requirements are met, identifying areasof
compliance and non-compliance.
● IAC0104: Ensure that SOPs cover all required aspects, recommending updates
where necessary.
● IAC0105: Verify that appropriate controls are in place, assessing their effectiveness and
suggesting improvements.
Tangible Outputs Required:
● Detailed reports on SOPs and regulatory requirements, including access and review notes.
● Risk profile interpretation documentation, including identified risks and assessments.
● Compliance confirmation report, highlighting regulatory adherence and gaps.
● SOP coverage report, noting any deficiencies and recommendations for updates.
● Controls assessment report, documenting effectiveness and proposed improvements.
Assessment Method:
● Direct Observation: The learner’s ability to access, interpret, and verify documentation and
controls will be observed in real-time.
● Review of Documentation: The learner’s reports and notes will be reviewed for
accuracy, completeness, and adherence to OHS standards.
● Practical Application: The learner’s ability to apply their findings and recommendations in a
simulated environment will be critically assessed.
Note: The facilitator/assessor must be present and he or she must complete the
attached evaluation checklist.

PM-02-PS02 PREPARE FOR AND CONDUCT CONTINUOUS
RISK AND ASSESSMENT FOR DIFFERENT WORKPLACES
Complete the following activities in the presence of your facilitator/instructor who must observe the activity
and complete the attached observation evaluation form.
Activity 2
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to prepare for and conduct continuous
risk assessment for different workplaces in your role as an Occupational Health and Safety Practitioner.
This practical is part of learning whose focus is on providing the learner an opportunity to practice the skills
required to inspect workplaces against specific workplace standards and initiate actions to prevent injury
and harm to people and equipment.
You will be provided with a simulated or controlled working environment with all the tools, equipment and
physical requirements to execute the skill.
Scenario-Based Practical Task: Continuous Risk Assessment for Different Workplaces
Overview:
This practical task tests the learner’s ability to prepare for and conduct continuous risk assessments
across different workplaces. The task will involve two distinct simulated work environments: a car garage/
body shop and a construction site. The learner will need to demonstrate their skills in assessing risks,
preparing for assessments, and implementing actions to manage identified risks.
Scenario Description:
You are an Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Practitioner assigned to perform continuous risk
assessments in two distinct simulated environments: a car garage/body shop and a construction site. Your
objective is to evaluate the risk profiles of both workplaces, prepare and use appropriate equipment and
PPE, communicate the assessment process, conduct detailed risk assessments, and initiate necessary
actions based on your findings.
16

Resources Required:
● Simulated Worksite Environment: Two distinct areas set up with realistic hazards
andwork conditions (car garage/body shop and construction site).
● Risk Assessment Tools: Noise meters, gas detectors, illumination meters, measuring tapes,
and any other relevant tools.
● PPE: Safety helmets, gloves, goggles, ear protection, respiratory protection, and other
necessary PPE.
● Documentation Forms: Risk assessment report forms, measurement logs, and actionplan
templates.
Staging for the Task:
1. Car Garage/Body Shop:
● Work Areas: Vehicle repair bays, painting areas, storage for hazardous substances(e.g.,
chemicals), and administrative areas.
● Hazards: Chemical spills, moving vehicle parts, mechanical hazards, high noise levels, and
poor ventilation.
● Safety Signage: Hazard signs, PPE requirements, and emergency procedures.
2. Construction Site:
● Work Areas: Active construction zones, scaffoldings, material storage areas,
andequipment maintenance areas.
● Hazards: Falling objects, scaffold instability, heavy machinery operation, electrical hazards,
and dust.
● Safety Signage: Safety barriers, hazard warnings, PPE requirements, and emergency exits.
Tasks to Be Completed:
PA02-01: Study the Risk Profile for the Workplace
● Objective: Review and understand the risk profile for each workplace.
● Steps:
1. Access Risk Profiles: Retrieve existing risk assessments, hazardidentification reports,
and safety data sheets (SDS) for both the car garage/body shop and the construction
site.
2. Review Documents: Examine the risk profiles for each environment, focusing on
identified hazards, risk levels, and previous incidents.
3. Analyse Risk Factors: Identify key risk factors for each environment, such asspecific
hazards related to equipment, processes, and materials used.
17

4. Document Insights: Note your understanding of the risk profiles, highlighting any significant
hazards or areas of concern.
PA02-02: Prepare Equipment and PPE for Risk Assessment
● Objective: Ensure that all necessary equipment and PPE are available and suitable for conducting
the risk assessment.
● Steps:
1. List Equipment and PPE: Identify and list the equipment and PPE required for the risk
assessment (e.g., measuring tools, gas detectors, noise meters, safety helmets, gloves,
goggles).
2. Inspect and Prepare: Check the condition and functionality of the equipment and PPE.
Ensure they are clean, operational, and suitable for the specific hazards of each workplace.
3. Gather Resources: Assemble all necessary tools and protective gear for use during the
assessment.
4. Verify Readiness: Confirm that all equipment and PPE are in place and ready for use, and
that you are familiar with their operation.
PA02-03: Communicate That Risk Assessment Is Taking Place and Remove People if Required
● Objective: Effectively communicate the risk assessment process to relevant personnel and ensure
safety by removing people if needed.
● Steps:
1. Notify Personnel: Inform all employees and visitors about the upcoming risk assessment.
Provide clear instructions about the process and any necessary safety precautions.
2. Issue Warnings: Use appropriate communication methods (e.g., announcements, signs) to
alert people to stay clear of the assessment areas.
3. Evacuate Areas: If required, evacuate specific areas where the risk assessment is being
conducted to ensure safety.
4. Confirm Evacuation: Ensure that all personnel are safely removed from the assessment
areas before proceeding.
18

PA02-04: Conduct Risk Assessments, Take Measurements, and Complete Required Documentation
● Objective: Perform detailed risk assessments, take necessary measurements, and document
findings accurately.
● Steps:
1. Conduct Visual Inspections: Inspect both workplaces for visible hazards and unsafe
conditions. Note any discrepancies from standard safety practices.
2. Take Measurements: Use appropriate tools to measure environmental factors such as noise
levels, air quality, and illumination levels.
3. Assess Risk Levels: Evaluate the severity and likelihood of each identified hazard, classifying
them based on risk levels (e.g., high, medium, low).
4. Complete Documentation: Record all findings, measurements, and observations in the risk
assessment report forms. Ensure all data is accurate and complete.
PA02-05: Initiate Actions to Deal with the Identified and Classified Risks
● Objective: Implement actions to manage and mitigate the risks identified during the assessment.
● Steps:
1. Develop Action Plans: Based on your findings, create detailed action plans to address
identified risks. Include corrective actions, responsible persons, and deadlines.
2. Prioritize Actions: Prioritize the actions based on the severity and likelihood of the risks.
Address high-risk issues immediately.
3. Communicate Actions: Inform relevant personnel about the actions to be taken, providing
clear instructions and timelines.
4. Monitor Implementation: Oversee the implementation of the action plans, ensuring that
corrective measures are applied effectively.
Examination Details:
The learner will be assessed on their ability to:
● IAC0201: Study and interpret the risk profiles for both the car garage/body shop and construction
site, demonstrating a thorough understanding of workplace risks.
● IAC0202: Prepare and verify the readiness of equipment and PPE for risk assessment, ensuring all
tools are functional and suitable.
● IAC0203: Communicate the risk assessment process effectively and evacuate personnel from
hazardous areas, if necessary.
19

● IAC0204: Conduct risk assessments, take accurate measurements, and
complete documentation, demonstrating attention to detail and thoroughness.
● IAC0205: Initiate and oversee actions to manage and mitigate identified risks, showing
effective planning and implementation skills.
Tangible Outputs Required:
● Risk profile analysis notes and findings.
● Prepared equipment and PPE checklist.
● Communication logs and evacuation records.
● Completed risk assessment reports with measurements and observations.
● Action plans with recommendations for risk mitigation.
Assessment Method:
● Direct Observation: The learner’s performance during the risk assessment, including
equipment preparation, communication, and evacuation, will be observed in real-time.
● Review of Documentation: Accuracy and completeness of risk assessment
reports, measurements, and action plans will be reviewed.
● Practical Application: The effectiveness of actions initiated to address risks will be
evaluated based on implementation and follow-up.
Note: The facilitator/assessor must be present and he or she must complete the attached evaluation
checklist.
