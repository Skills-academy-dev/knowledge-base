Tasks and Guidelines
Task PA01-01: Oversee the Selection Processes as per Legislative Requirements
Objective:EnsurethattheselectionofOHSrepresentativesisconductedfairly,transparently,and
in accordance with the relevant legislative requirements.
Steps:
1. Review the Legislation: Begin by reviewing the relevant OHS legislation that governs the
