PS0401 ACT AS CUSTODIAN OF HRM RECORDS.

Activity 1

In  this  practical  task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to act as custodian of HRM

records  in  your  role  as  an  HR  Administrator.  You  will  be  provided  with a simulated or real-world

situation with details and facts regarding the internal and external environment of an organisation

as  well  as  the  organisations  vision,  mission  and  strategic  objectives  and  some  data  relating  to

current performance and HRM dynamics.

Scenario: Managing Employee Records

Context:

You are an HR Administrator at a growing software development company. You are responsible for

maintaining  accurate  and  secure  employee  records,  ensuring  appropriate  access  control,  and

responding to inquiries in accordance with data privacy regulations.

Resources:

●  Learner acting as the HR Administrator

●  Assessor acting as a manager or another employee

●  Access to a computer with HR record management software (simulated or real, depending

on availability)

●  Employee record templates or sample data (if using a simulated system)

●  Company data privacy policy

Tasks:

The  learner  will  complete  the  following  tasks  to  demonstrate  their  ability  to  manage  employee

records:

PA0101: Compile and Maintain Employee Records

1.  New Employee Onboarding:

o  The assessor acts as a new employee who has recently completed the onboarding

process.

o  The  learner  demonstrates  how  they  would  collect  and  file  the  new  employee's

essential information (name, contact details, emergency contact, qualifications, etc.)

using the HR record management system.

o  The learner explains the purpose of each data field being collected and obtains the

employee's consent for data storage according to the company's privacy policy.

2.  Updating Existing Records:

o  The learner retrieves a sample employee record and demonstrates how they would

update  it  with  any  changes,  such  as  a  change  of  address,  marital  status,  or

emergency contact information.

o  The  learner  emphasizes  the  importance  of  maintaining  accurate  and  up-to-date

employee records.

PA0102: Control Access to Information

1.  Access Levels:

o  The  learner  explains  the  different  access  levels  assigned  to  various  user  roles

within  the  HR  record  management  system.  For  example,  managers  may  have

access to more detailed employee information compared to team leads.

2.  Access Request Process:

o  The  learner  outlines  the  process  for  requesting access to specific employee data.

This may involve a formal request form and approval from a designated authority.

14

PA0103: Manage Employee Record Archive

1.  Retention Policy:

o  The learner explains the company's policy on employee record retention. This may

involve  keeping  certain  records  for  a  specific  period  after  employment  ends,

depending on legal requirements or internal policies.

2.  Archiving Procedures:

o  The  learner  demonstrates  how  they  would  archive  inactive  employee  records

according to the organization's policy. This may involve transferring data to a secure

offline storage system or anonymizing data for statistical purposes.

PA0104: Respond to Information Requests

1.  Verify Requestor Identity:

o  The assessor acts as a manager requesting information from an employee record.

The  learner  verifies  the  manager's  identity  and  confirms  their  access  level before

proceeding.

2.  Identify and Provide Information:

o  Based  on  the  manager's  request  and  their  access  level,  the  learner  retrieves the

relevant information from the employee record and provides it in a secure manner.

o  The  learner  explains  any  limitations  on  data  disclosure  based  on  data  privacy

regulations and the employee's consent.

3.  Escalate Requests:

o  The  learner  explains  the  process  for  escalating  requests  for  information that may

not  be  accessible  due  to  access  restrictions  or  data  privacy  concerns.  This  may

involve seeking approval from a higher authority or the employee themselves.

Evaluation (IAC0101 - IAC0104):

●  The  assessor  will  observe  and  evaluate  the  learner's  performance  throughout  the  tasks,

focusing on:

o  Accuracy and completeness of data entry and record updates (PA0101).

o  Ability to explain access control procedures and user roles (PA0102).

o  Understanding of record retention policies and archiving procedures (PA0103).

o  Effective  verification  of  identity  and  adherence  to  data  privacy  regulations  when

responding to information requests (PA0104).

o  Ability to escalate requests appropriately when necessary.

Overall, this scenario-based practical task allows the learner to demonstrate their understanding of

HR  record  management  principles.  It  assesses  their  skills  in  maintaining  accurate  and  secure

employee information, controlling access according to user roles, and responding to inquiries in a

professional and compliant manner.
