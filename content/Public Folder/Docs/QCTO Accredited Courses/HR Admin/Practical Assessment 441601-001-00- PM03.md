PM0101 ADMINISTER ORGANISATIONAL COMMUNICATIONS.

Activity 1

In  this  practical  task,  you  will  be  required  to  demonstrate  your  ability  to  administer  organisational

communications  in  your  role  as  an  HR  Administrator.  You  will  be  provided  with  a  simulated  or

real-world  situation  with  details  and  facts  regarding  the  internal  and  external  environment  of  an

organisation  as  well  as  the  organisations  vision,  mission  and  strategic  objectives  and  some  data

relating to current performance and HRM dynamics.

The  focus  of  the  learning  in  this  module  is  to  provide  learners  the  opportunity to practice the skills

required  to  execute the specific occupational task of coordinating HRM communications to enhance

employment relations.

Scenario: Implementing a New Paid Parental Leave Policy

Context:

You  are  an  HR  Administrator  at  a  mid-sized  manufacturing  company.  The  company  has  recently

approved  a  new  paid  parental  leave  policy  that  is  more  generous than the statutory minimum.  The

policy offers additional benefits to new parents, including extended leave and financial assistance.

Resources:

●  Learner acting as the HR Administrator

●  Assessor acting as a company manager or employee

●  Access to a computer with word processing software and internet access (optional)

●  Templates for HR communication materials (optional)

Tasks:

The learner will complete the following tasks to demonstrate their ability to administer organizational

communications related to the new parental leave policy:

11

PA0101 & PA0102: Update Records and Guidelines

1.  Update Policy Filing System:

o  The learner retrieves the existing parental leave policy document.

o  The  learner  highlights  or  inserts  the  revisions  reflecting  the  newly  approved  policy

details (extended leave duration, financial assistance details).

o  The learner saves the updated policy document with a clear version control reference

(e.g., "Parental Leave Policy v2.0").

o  The  learner  stores  the  updated  policy  document  in  the  designated  electronic  filing

system or a secure physical location for HR policies.

2.  Prepare Implementation Guidelines:

o  The  learner  drafts  a  clear  and  concise  document  outlining  the key points of the new

parental  leave  policy  (eligibility  criteria,  leave  duration,  application  process,  financial

assistance details).

o  The learner includes relevant visuals or examples to enhance understanding (if using a

word processing software with such capabilities).

o  The learner ensures the document adheres to the organization's communication style

guide (tone, formatting).

PA0103 & PA0104: Coordinate Communication

1.  Schedule Announcement Meeting:

o  The  learner  collaborates  with  the  company  manager  to  schedule  an  all-employee

meeting to announce the new parental leave policy.

o  The  learner  selects  a  suitable  date  and  time  that  minimizes  disruption  to  work

schedules.

o  The  learner  sends  a  meeting  invitation  to  all  employees  via  email  or  the  company's

internal  communication  platform,  clearly  stating  the  meeting  purpose  and  agenda

(announcement of new parental leave policy).

2.  Prepare Meeting Materials:

o  The learner prepares a presentation or talking points for the manager to use during the

announcement  meeting.  This  should  highlight  the  key  benefits  of the new policy and

provide a brief overview of the application process.

12

PA0105 & PA0106: Address Employee Inquiries

1.  Respond to Policy Questions:

o  The  learner  anticipates  potential  employee  questions about the new policy (eligibility,

leave calculations, financial assistance details).

o  The  learner  prepares  a  Frequently  Asked  Questions  (FAQ)  document with clear and

concise answers based on the policy guidelines.

o  The learner role-plays with the assessor, acting as an employee with questions about

the  new  policy.  The  learner  demonstrates their ability to answer questions accurately

and professionally, referring to the FAQ document or policy guidelines as needed.

Direct Contractual and Conditions Inquiries:

o  The  learner  acknowledges  that  some  employee  questions  may  relate  to  contractual

details  or  specific conditions of employment (e.g., impact on probation period, benefit

calculations).

o  The  learner  explains  that  they will direct such inquiries to the HR Manager or Payroll

department for further clarification.

PA0107 & PA0108: Develop Communication Channels

1.  Create an HR Newsletter Announcement:

o  The  learner  drafts  a  clear  and  engaging  announcement  for  the  company's  HR

newsletter about the new parental leave policy.

o  The  announcement  should  summarize  the  key  benefits,  highlight the implementation

timeline, and direct employees to access the full policy document and FAQ resources.

2.  Social Media Communication (Optional):

o  The  learner  may  be  asked  to  consider  how  the  new  policy  announcement  could  be

made  on  the  company's  internal  social  media  platform  (if  applicable).  This  could

involve creating a post with a brief explanation, a link to the full policy document, and

encouraging employees to reach out to HR with any questions.

Evaluation (IAC0101 - IAC0104):

●  The  assessor  will  observe  and  evaluate  the  learner's  performance  throughout  the  tasks,

focusing on:

o  Accuracy and completeness of updates to policy documents and guidelines (PA0101 &

PA0102).

o  Clarity,  structure, and professionalism of communication materials (meeting invitation,

presentation, FAQ document, newsletter announcement) (PA0103 & PA104).

o  Ability to answer policy-related questions accurately and confidently (PA0105).

o  Understanding of when to direct inquiries to other HR personnel (PA0107 & PA0108)

13

Evaluating Message Understanding (Optional - IAC0108):

●  Depending  on  the  assessment  resources  available,  there  could  be  an  optional  step  to

evaluate if the messages were heard and understood. This could involve:

o  Short  survey:  After  the  announcement  meeting  or  newsletter  distribution,  a  brief

anonymous  survey  could  be  sent  to  employees  to  gauge  their  understanding  of  the

key points of the new policy.

o  Focus  group  discussion:  A  small  group  of  employees  could  be  invited  to  a  focus

group discussion to share their feedback on the communication materials and whether

they have any remaining questions about the policy.

Overall, this scenario-based practical task allows the learner to demonstrate their ability to effectively

communicate  HR  policy  changes  through  various  channels.  It  assesses  their  skills  in  maintaining

accurate records, developing clear communication materials, and addressing employee inquiries in a

professional and timely manner.

The tangible outputs from this practical that the learner will be expected to produce are:

●  Updated  Parental  Leave  Policy  Document:  This  will  be  the  original  policy  document  with

clear  revisions  or  annotations  reflecting  the  newly  approved  details  (e.g.,  extended  leave

duration,  financial  assistance details). It should be saved with a version control reference for

easy identification. (PA0101)

●

Implementation  Guidelines  Document:  This  will be a separate document outlining the key

points of the new parental leave policy in a clear and concise manner for employee reference.

It may include visuals or examples for better understanding. (PA0102)

●  Meeting Invitation: This will be an electronic invitation sent to all employees via email or the

company's  internal  communication  platform.  It  will  clearly  state  the  meeting  purpose  and

agenda (announcement of new parental leave policy) and details like date and time. (PA0103)

●  Presentation  or  Talking  Points  (Optional):  This  could  be  a  slide  deck  presentation  or  a

document  outlining  key  points  for  the  manager  to  use  during  the announcement meeting. It

should highlight the benefits of the new policy and provide a brief overview of the application

process. (PA0104)

●  Frequently  Asked  Questions  (FAQ)  Document:  This  will  be  a  document  listing  potential

employee  questions  about  the  new  policy  (eligibility, leave calculations, financial assistance)

with clear and concise answers based on the policy guidelines. (PA0105)

●  HR Newsletter Announcement: This will be a clear and engaging announcement drafted for

the  company's  HR  newsletter.  It  should  summarize  the  key  benefits,  highlight  the

implementation  timeline,  and  direct  employees  to  access  the  full  policy  document  and  FAQ

resources. (PA0107)

14

Optional Outputs:

●  Social Media Announcement (if applicable): This could be a post created for the company's

internal social media platform explaining the new policy briefly, including a link to the full policy

document and encouraging employees to reach out to HR with questions. (PA0107)

●  Employee  Survey  or  Focus  Group  Discussion  Results  (if  applicable):  These  would  be

additional  outputs  depending  on  the  resources  available  to  assess  if  the  messages  were

understood by employees. (PA0108)
