PS0201PROVIDE ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES FOR WORKFORCE
PLANNING.
Activity 1
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to provide
administrative services for Workforce Planning in your role as an HR Administrator. You will
be provided with a simulated or real-world situation with details and facts regarding the
internal and external environment of an organisation as well as the organisations vision,
mission and strategic objectives and some data relating to current performance and HRM
dynamics.
The focus of the learning in this module is to provide learners the opportunity to practice the
skills required to execute the specific occupational task of providing administrative services
for a full range of HRM services and processes.
Practical Task Scenario for HR Administrator: Workforce Planning Administration
Scenario Overview:
You are an HR Administrator at ABC Company, tasked with providing administrative support
for workforce planning initiatives. Your role involves data analysis, report preparation, record
maintenance, event coordination, and stakeholder communication. You will be evaluated on
your ability to execute these tasks effectively within the specified timeframe.
Resources and Personnel Required:
● Computer with Microsoft Office suite (Excel, Word, Outlook)
● Access to company HR systems and databases
● Sample data sets for workforce planning analysis
● Templates for reports, meeting minutes, and regulatory submissions
● Calendar for scheduling events
● List of stakeholders and contact information
● Supervisor or evaluator to oversee the task

Task Instructions:
PA0101: Prepare, Analyse, and Present Data
1. Data Collection: Access HR systems and databases to retrieve relevant workforce
data, including employee demographics, turnover rates, and recruitment metrics.
2. Data Analysis: Use Excel or similar software to analyse the data sets. Calculate key
metrics such as turnover rate, retention rate, and diversity indices.
3. Data Presentation: Create visualizations (charts, graphs) to present the analysed
data effectively. Ensure clarity and accuracy in your presentations.
PA0102: Prepare Reports
1. Report Structure: Use the provided template to structure your report. Include
sections for executive summary, findings, recommendations, and appendices.
2. Content Creation: Compile your analysis findings into the report. Provide insights
and recommendations based on the data analysis.
3. Formatting: Ensure consistency in formatting, font styles, and alignment throughout
the report. Use headings and subheadings for easy navigation.
14

PA0103: Maintain Records
1. Record Organization: Create a system for organizing and storing workforce
planning documents. Use folders or electronic filing systems to maintain records
systematically.
2. Version Control: Keep track of document versions and updates. Use naming
conventions to distinguish between different versions of documents.
3. Document Retrieval: Ensure easy accessibility of records for future reference. Label
documents accurately and categorize them based on relevance.
PA0104: Coordinate Events
1. Event Planning: Identify upcoming workforce planning meetings or events.
Coordinate logistics such as venue booking, catering, and equipment setup.
2. Communication: Send invitations to participants and stakeholders. Confirm
attendance and provide necessary pre-meeting materials.
3. Event Execution: Oversee the smooth running of the event. Take note of any action
items or decisions made during the meeting.
PA0105: Take Minutes
1. Meeting Documentation: Attend workforce planning meetings and take accurate
minutes. Record key discussion points, decisions, and action items.
2. Format: Follow the standard format for meeting minutes. Include meeting date,
attendees, agenda items, and a summary of discussions.
3. Distribution: Share the meeting minutes with participants promptly after the meeting.
Seek feedback or corrections if necessary.
PA0106: Complete Regulatory Reports
1. Regulatory Requirements: Review the regulatory guidelines for workforce reporting.
Identify the required data elements and reporting deadlines.
2. Data Compilation: Gather the necessary workforce data to fulfil regulatory
obligations. Ensure accuracy and completeness in data collection.
3. Report Submission: Prepare and submit the regulatory reports within the specified
timeframe. Keep records of submissions for compliance purposes.
PA0107: Update Employee Records
1. Data Verification: Review employee records for accuracy and completeness.
Update personal information, job titles, and other relevant details as needed.
2. System Entry: Enter the updated information into the HR database or personnel
management system. Ensure data integrity and consistency across all records.
3. Documentation: Maintain an audit trail of record updates. Document any changes
made and the reasons for those changes.
15

PA0108: Schedule Events
1. Event Coordination: Use Outlook or a similar calendar tool to schedule workforce
planning meetings and events. Consider participants' availability and preferences.
2. Confirmation: Send meeting invitations to participants. Include details such as date,
time, location, and agenda.
3. Follow-Up: Send reminders to participants closer to the event date. Confirm
attendance and make any necessary adjustments to the schedule.
PA0109: Provide Stakeholder Feedback
1. Feedback Collection: Solicit feedback from stakeholders on workforce planning
initiatives and processes. Use surveys or feedback forms to gather input.
2. Analysis: Analyse the feedback received to identify trends and areas for
improvement. Consider both quantitative and qualitative feedback.
3. Action Plan: Develop an action plan based on stakeholder feedback. Implement
changes or enhancements to improve workforce planning effectiveness.
Assessment Criteria:
IAC0101: Compliance with Processes
● Checklist: Evaluate adherence to task instructions using a predefined checklist.
● Observation: Supervisor or evaluator observes the learner's execution of each task
step.
IAC0102: Time Management
● Time Tracking: Monitor the time taken to complete each task.
● Deadline Adherence: Assess whether tasks are completed within the specified time
limits.
IAC0103: Competence in Techniques
● Technical Skills: Evaluate proficiency in using software tools for data analysis,
report preparation, and record maintenance.
● Problem-Solving: Assess the learner's ability to overcome challenges encountered
during task execution.
IAC0104: Demonstration of Key Attributes
● Attention to Detail: Evaluate the accuracy and thoroughness of work produced.
● Communication Skills: Assess clarity and effectiveness in written communication
(reports, meeting minutes, emails).
16

Examination Process:
1. Simulated Environment: Create a controlled setting with access to necessary
resources and tools (e.g., computer, sample data, templates).
2. Task Execution: The learner performs each task step-by-step under the supervision
of an evaluator.
3. Documentation Review: Evaluate the quality of documentation produced (reports,
meeting minutes, record updates).
4. Feedback Session: Provide constructive feedback to the learner based on their
performance against the assessment criteria.

PS0202 PROVIDE ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES FOR STAFF
RECRUITMENT, SELECTION AND PLACEMENT.
Activity 2
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to provide
administrative services for Staff Recruitment, Selection and Placement in your role as an HR
Administrator. You will be provided with a simulated or real-world situation with details and
facts regarding the internal and external environment of an organisation as well as the
organisations vision, mission and strategic objectives and some data relating to current
performance and HRM dynamics.
The focus of the learning in this module is to provide learners the opportunity to practice the
skills required to execute the specific occupational task of providing administrative services
for a full range of HRM services and processes.
Practical Task Scenario for HR Administrator: Staff Recruitment, Selection, and
Placement
Scenario Overview:
You are an HR Administrator at XYZ Corporation, responsible for managing the recruitment,
selection, and placement process for new staff members. In this simulated environment, you
will demonstrate how you would execute various administrative tasks involved in this
process. You will utilize real-world documentation from previous job applications to guide
your actions.
Resources and Personnel Required:
● Computer with access to previous job application documents
● Sample job advertisement templates
● Email or communication platform for candidate correspondence
● Interview evaluation forms or templates
● Access to HR systems for record-keeping (simulated environment)
● Supervisor or evaluator to oversee the task

Task Instructions:
PA0201: Prepare and Distribute Advertisements
1. Job Advertisement Review: Review previous job advertisements to understand the
format and content.
2. Adapt Template: Modify the job advertisement template to fit the current vacancy.
Include necessary details such as job title, responsibilities, qualifications, and
application instructions.
3. Distribute Advertisement: Choose appropriate channels for distributing the job
advertisement (e.g., company website, job boards, social media). Follow company
guidelines for posting job openings.
PA0202: Receive and Screen Applications
1. Application Collection: Create a system for receiving job applications, whether
through email, online portals, or postal mail.
2. Application Review: Screen received applications against the job requirements and
qualifications. Use predetermined criteria to shortlist candidates for further
consideration.
3. Document Review: Refer to resumes, cover letters, and other application
documents to assess candidate suitability.
PA0203: Acknowledge Receipt of Applications
1. Acknowledgment Email: Send automated or personalized acknowledgment emails
to all applicants upon receipt of their application.
2. Confirmation Record: Maintain a record of acknowledgment emails sent for tracking
purposes.
PA0204: Verify Documents
1. Document Verification Checklist: Develop a checklist of required documents (e.g.,
academic transcripts, certificates) for each position.
2. Document Verification Process: Cross-reference submitted documents with the
checklist to ensure completeness and authenticity.
3. Document Storage: Organize verified documents in a secure location for future
reference.
19

PA0205: Conduct Reference Checks
1. Reference Check Protocol: Determine the reference check process, including the
type of references to contact (e.g., previous employers, academic institutions).
2. Reference Contact: Reach out to provided references via phone or email to gather
feedback on the candidate's qualifications and work ethic.
3. Reference Documentation: Record the outcomes of reference checks for each
candidate.
PA0206: Develop Long Lists
1. Long List Criteria: Establish criteria for including candidates on the long list, based
on their suitability for the position.
2. Candidate Evaluation: Evaluate each candidate against the established criteria to
create a preliminary long list.
3. Long List Review: Review the long list to ensure it includes candidates who meet
the minimum requirements for the position.
PA0207: Arrange Logistics for Candidate Testing and Interviews
1. Test and Interview Scheduling: Coordinate schedules for candidate assessments,
tests, and interviews. Ensure availability of interviewers and testing facilities.
2. Logistical Arrangements: Arrange necessary logistics such as meeting rooms,
equipment, and materials for candidate assessments.
3. Confirmation Communication: Confirm details with candidates regarding test and
interview arrangements.
PA0208: Record Findings of Interview Panels
1. Interview Evaluation Forms: Prepare interview evaluation forms or templates to
record panellists’ observations and ratings.
2. Interview Panel Coordination: Coordinate interview panels and ensure all panellists
are briefed on evaluation criteria and processes.
3. Findings Documentation: Record interview panel findings, including candidate
performance, strengths, weaknesses, and overall suitability for the position.
PA0209: Communicate with Candidates for Required Additional Information
1. Candidate Communication Protocol: Establish guidelines for communicating with
candidates regarding additional information requests.
2. Information Request Template: Prepare a template for requesting additional
information from candidates, if needed.
3. Follow-Up Communication: Reach out to candidates via email or phone to request
any missing or clarification on submitted documents.
20

PA0210: Develop Shortlists
1. Shortlist Criteria: Define criteria for shortlisting candidates based on interview
performance, reference checks, and alignment with job requirements.
2. Candidate Evaluation: Evaluate candidates against the established criteria to create
a shortlist of top candidates.
3. Shortlist Review: Review the shortlist to ensure it includes candidates who best fit
the position requirements and organizational needs.
PA0211: Notify Candidates of Results of Applications
1. Notification Template: Prepare a template for notifying candidates of the outcome of
their application (e.g., invitation for interview, rejection).
2. Candidate Notification: Communicate application outcomes to candidates promptly
and professionally via email or postal mail.
3. Feedback Provision: Offer constructive feedback to candidates, if applicable,
regarding the outcome of their application.
PA0212: Facilitate Contracting
1. Contract Preparation: Prepare employment contracts or offer letters for selected
candidates based on organizational templates and guidelines.
2. Contract Review: Review contracts for accuracy and completeness, ensuring all
necessary terms and conditions are included.
3. Contract Distribution: Distribute contracts to selected candidates and facilitate the
signing process.
PA0213: Facilitate Onboarding
1. Onboarding Plan: Develop an onboarding plan outlining the steps and activities for
integrating new employees into the organization.
2. Onboarding Documentation: Prepare necessary documents and materials for new
employee orientation, including employee handbooks, policies, and training
materials.
3. Onboarding Coordination: Coordinate logistics for new employee onboarding
sessions, including scheduling, venue booking, and facilitator arrangements.
PA0214: Facilitate Induction
1. Induction Program: Design an induction program to introduce new employees to the
organization's culture, policies, procedures, and key personnel.
2. Induction Materials: Prepare induction materials such as presentations, handouts,
and videos to support the induction program.
3. Induction Delivery: Deliver the induction program to new employees in a structured
and engaging manner, ensuring they feel welcomed and informed.
21

Simulated Documentation
Sample Training Needs Analysis Report
Introduction:
This training needs analysis report identifies a skills gap in safety procedures among
production floor employees at XYZ Company. The report recommends a safety training
program to address this gap and ensure employee compliance with safety regulations.
Methodology:
● Review of incident reports
● Observation of work practices on the production floor
● Interviews with production floor employees and supervisors
Findings:
● Several near-miss incidents have occurred due to a lack of awareness of safety
procedures.
● Employees were unsure of proper equipment operation and safety protocols.
● Supervisors expressed a need for additional resources to support safety training.
Recommendations:
● Implement a comprehensive safety training program for all production floor
employees.
● The program should cover topics such as:
o Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) usage
o Ergonomics and safe lifting practices
o Machine operation and safety protocols
o Hazard identification and risk assessment
o Emergency procedures
● The program should be interactive and include practical exercises.
● Supervisors should be provided with training on how to deliver safety training to their
teams.
Estimated Costs:
● Trainer fees: R 15,000 per day (2 days) = R 30,000
● Training materials: R 500 per employee (100 employees) = R 50,000
● Venue rental: R 2,000 per day (2 days) = R 4,000
● Catering: R 100 per person per day (2 days) (100 employees) = R 40,000
● Total Estimated Cost: R 124,000

Investing in a safety training program is essential to ensure the well-being of employees,
minimize workplace incidents, and comply with safety regulations. This training program will
equip employees with the necessary knowledge and skills to work safely and efficiently.
