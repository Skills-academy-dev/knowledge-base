PS0101 USE APPROPRIATE INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY TO
COLLECT REQUIRED HRM DATA.
Activity 1
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to use appropriate
information technology to collect required HRM data in your role as an HR Administrator. You
will be provided with a simulated or real-world situation with details and facts regarding the
internal and external environment of an organisation as well as the organisations vision,
mission and strategic objectives and some data relating to current performance and HRM
dynamics.
The focus of the learning in this module is to provide learners the opportunity to practice the
skills required to execute the specific occupational task of collecting and processing HRM data.
Scenario: Analysing Employee Turnover Data
Context:
You are an HR Administrator at a retail clothing company. The company has been experiencing
higher than usual employee turnover rates in recent months, particularly within the sales
department. Your manager has tasked you with using the company's HR information system
(HRIS) to collect and analyse relevant data to understand the reasons behind this trend.
Resources:
● Learner acting as the HR Administrator
● Assessor acting as the HR Manager
● Access to a computer with a simulated or real HRIS (depending on availability) with
sample employee data
● Data analysis tools within the HRIS (if available) or a separate spreadsheet program

Tasks:
The learner will complete the following tasks to demonstrate their ability to use IT to collect and
analyse HRM data:
PA0101: Prepare to Execute the Skill
● Define Data Requirements:
○ The learner discusses with the assessor (acting as the HR Manager) the specific
data points needed to analyse employee turnover. This may include information
on:
■ Department
■ Job Title
■ Date of Hire
■ Date of Termination
■ Reason for Leaving (if available)
■ Exit Interview Data (if available)
PA0102: Check and Verify Information
● HRIS Access and Functionality:
o The learner verifies their access to the HRIS and confirms they have the
necessary permissions to run reports and extract relevant data.
o The learner familiarizes themselves with the specific features of the HRIS used for
data extraction and analysis (e.g., report templates, data filters).
13

PA0103: Execute the Skill Using Specific Processes
● Data Extraction:
o The learner utilizes the HRIS to extract employee data based on the defined
requirements (department, job title, date range).
o The learner may use filters to focus on specific demographics or departments
experiencing high turnover.
o The learner downloads or exports the extracted data into a format suitable for
further analysis (e.g., CSV file).
PA0104: Validate the Results
● Data Quality Checks:
o The learner performs basic checks on the extracted data to ensure accuracy and
completeness. This may involve checking for missing values or inconsistencies.
PA0105: Report the Results
● Data Analysis:
○ The learner may use basic data analysis tools within the HRIS (if available) or
import the data into a spreadsheet program for further analysis.
○ The learner focuses on identifying patterns or trends in the data, such as:
■ High turnover rates in specific departments or job titles.
■ Common reasons for leaving mentioned in exit interviews (if available).
○ The learner creates visuals such as charts or graphs to represent the key findings.
● Reporting:
○ The learner prepares a concise report for the HR Manager summarizing the
findings from the data analysis.
○ The report should highlight key trends, potential reasons for turnover, and any
recommendations for further investigation.
PA0106: Modify and Improve
● Continuous Improvement:
○ The learner may discuss with the assessor (acting as the HR Manager) how the
data collection and analysis process could be improved in the future. This may
involve:
■ Identifying additional data points to analyse.
■ Exploring more advanced data analysis techniques.
14

Evaluation (IAC0101 - IAC0104):
● The assessor will observe and evaluate the learner's performance throughout the tasks,
focusing on:
o Ability to define the necessary data points for analysing employee turnover
(PA0101).
o Verification of access and understanding of HRIS functionalities (PA0102).
o Competence in extracting data from the HRIS using relevant filters (PA0103).
o Attention to data quality and implementation of basic data validation checks
(PA0104).
o Skill in generating reports and presenting key findings in a clear and concise
manner (PA0105).
o Ability to discuss potential improvements to the data collection and analysis
process (optional - PA0106).
Overall, this scenario-based practical task allows the learner to demonstrate their ability to
utilize HR information technology for data collection and analysis. It assesses their skills in
defining data requirements, extracting data from HR systems, and interpreting the results to
gain valuable insights into workforce dynamics.
Note: The facilitator/assessor must be present and he or she must complete the attached
evaluation checklist.

PS0104 MAINTAIN APPROPRIATE DATABASES OF INFORMATION AND
EXTRACT REPORTS IN RESPONSE TO SPECIFIC AUTHORISED
REQUESTS.
Activity 4
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to maintain appropriate
databases of information and extract reports in response to specific authorised requests in your
role as an HR Administrator. You will be provided with a simulated or real-world situation with
details and facts regarding the internal and external environment of an organisation as well as
the organisations vision, mission and strategic objectives and some data relating to current
performance and HRM dynamics.
The focus of the learning in this module is to provide learners the opportunity to practice the
skills required to execute the specific occupational task of collecting and processing HRM data.
Scenario Overview:
You are an HR Administrator at XYZ Corporation, responsible for maintaining the company's HR
database and generating reports based on specific requests from authorized personnel. The HR
database includes employee details such as personal information, job roles, performance data,
and leave records. Your task is to demonstrate your ability to manage this database and provide
accurate reports upon request.
Resources and Personnel Required:
● Computer with HR database software (e.g., Microsoft Access, HRIS system)
● Sample HR database with fictitious employee data
● Instruction manual for the database software
● List of authorized requests for reports
● Supervisor or evaluator to oversee the task
● Spreadsheet software (e.g., Microsoft Excel) for additional data manipulation if required
Task Instructions:
PA0401: Prepare to Execute the Skill
1. Read the Task Brief: Review the instructions and understand the tasks you are required
to complete.
2. Familiarize with the Database: Open the HR database software and explore the
interface. Check the sample data for accuracy and completeness.
3. Gather Required Resources: Ensure you have access to all necessary tools, including
the database, manuals, and reporting templates.
27

PA0402: Check and Verify All Information
1. Verify Data Accuracy: Cross-check a sample of employee records against the provided
data to ensure accuracy. Note any discrepancies.
2. Update Records if Necessary: Make corrections in the database where discrepancies
are found. Document any changes made.
3. Confirm Data Completeness: Ensure all necessary fields (e.g., personal details, job
roles, performance metrics) are filled out for each employee.
PA0403: Execute the Skill Following Specific Processes
1. Request Analysis: Review the specific report requests provided by authorized
personnel. Understand the scope and data required for each report.
2. Data Extraction: Use the database tools to extract the required data. Follow
step-by-step processes to filter and sort the data as needed.
● Example: For a report on employee performance ratings, filter by department and
time period.
3. Generate Reports: Create the reports in the requested format (e.g., PDF, Excel).
