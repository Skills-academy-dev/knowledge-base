PM0201 CONTRIBUTE TOWARDS THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE
ORGANISATIONAL TALENT MANAGEMENT STRATEGY AND OVERSEE
IMPLEMENTATION OF THE STRATEGY.
Activity 1
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to contribute towards the
development of the organisational talent management strategy and oversee implementation of the
strategy in your role as an HR Officer. You will be provided with a simulated or real-world situation
where oversight must be provided on the implementation of Learning and Development plans.
The focus of the learning in this module is on providing learners the opportunity to practice and
master the skills required to Talent Management plans, policies, and procedures. Learners will
receive instruction in the methods, procedures, and techniques applicable to this specific discipline
and they will be required to select appropriate methods to resolve problems and/or facilitate
appropriate change. Learners will be required to demonstrate their application of the required skills
in an ethical way that is aligned to the professional practice standards.
Title: Talent Management Strategy Implementation Practical Task
Scenario: You, as an HR Officer, have been tasked with contributing towards the development of
the organizational talent management strategy and overseeing its implementation. Your
organization, a medium-sized tech company, is experiencing rapid growth and needs a robust
talent management strategy to ensure continued success. As part of your role, you are responsible
for investigating current talent, determining future talent requirements, and implementing the talent
management strategy effectively.
Resources Needed:
● Access to HR database/system for employee records
● Access to organizational performance data and forecasts
● Collaboration with department heads and managers
● Access to training and development resources
● Time allocation for meetings and analysis
11

Task Guidelines:
1. Investigate and Determine Current Talent in the Organization (PS0101):
o Utilize the HR database/system to gather comprehensive data on current
employees.
o Review employee performance evaluations, skills assessments, and career
development plans to identify key talents within the organization.
o Conduct interviews or surveys with department heads and managers to gather
insights into their teams' strengths and weaknesses.
o Analyse turnover rates, retention data, and succession plans to identify potential
talent gaps.
o Document findings in a detailed report outlining the current talent landscape,
including strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.
2. Investigate and Determine Future Talent Requirements (PS0102):
o Review organizational goals, growth projections, and strategic plans to understand
future talent needs.
o Conduct competency gap analysis to identify skills and capabilities required for
future roles.
o Collaborate with department heads and managers to forecast workforce needs
based on upcoming projects, expansions, or changes in business direction.
o Research industry trends and advancements to anticipate emerging skill
requirements.
o Develop a comprehensive report outlining future talent requirements, including a
roadmap for acquiring, developing, and retaining talent to meet organizational
objectives.
3. Implement Talent Management Strategy (PS0103):
o Develop a talent management strategy based on the findings from steps 1 and 2.
o Collaborate with department heads and managers to align the strategy with
departmental goals and objectives.
o Identify key initiatives such as training programs, succession planning, performance
management systems, and recruitment strategies.
o Establish metrics and KPIs to measure the effectiveness of the talent management
strategy.
o Create a timeline and action plan for implementation, assigning responsibilities to
relevant stakeholders.
o Monitor progress regularly, make adjustments as needed, and communicate
updates to senior management.
12

o Evaluate the impact of the talent management strategy on organizational
performance and make recommendations for continuous improvement.
Examination Guidelines:
● Compliance with Processes and Steps (IAC0101): Assess the completeness and
accuracy of the reports and plans developed during each step. Ensure that all necessary
data sources are utilized, and the analysis is thorough.
● Execution within Time Limits (IAC0102): Monitor the learner's ability to complete each
task within the allocated time frame. Evaluate their time management skills and prioritize
tasks effectively.
● Competence in Application of Prescribed Techniques (IAC0103): Evaluate the
learner's proficiency in conducting talent investigations, analysing data, and developing
strategic plans. Assess their ability to apply relevant HR concepts and methodologies.
● Demonstration of Key Attributes (IAC0104): Observe the learner's communication skills,
teamwork, problem-solving abilities, and strategic thinking throughout the practical task.
Evaluate their professionalism, attention to detail, and adaptability in handling various
challenges.
Feedback and Assessment: Provide constructive feedback to the learner based on their
performance in each assessment criterion. Highlight strengths and areas for improvement, and
offer recommendations for further development. Use a scoring rubric to quantify the learner's
proficiency level in each skill area.
Note: The facilitator/assessor must be present and he or she must complete the attached
evaluation checklist.

PM0202 FACILITATE AND GUIDE STAFF PROCUREMENT PROCESSES.
Activity 2
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to facilitate and guide staff
procurement processes in your role as an HR Officer. You will be provided with a simulated or
real-world situation where Performance Management processes must be implemented.
The focus of the learning in this module is on providing learners the opportunity to practice and
master the skills required to Talent Management plans, policies, and procedures. Learners will
receive instruction in the methods, procedures, and techniques applicable to this specific discipline
and they will be required to select appropriate methods to resolve problems and/or facilitate
appropriate change. Learners will be required to demonstrate their application of the required skills
in an ethical way that is aligned to the professional practice standards.
Practical Task: Demonstrating Staff Procurement Processes
Scenario:
You are an HR Officer in a growing company with 50 employees. The company is experiencing
rapid expansion and needs to hire several new employees across various departments. You will
demonstrate your understanding of the key steps involved in facilitating and guiding staff
procurement processes, including:
● Planning and conducting recruitment, selection, and placement processes.
● Facilitating appointment decisions and finalizing contracts.
● Planning and executing onboarding activities.
● Planning and executing employee induction.
● Planning and executing termination processes.
14

Demonstration:
1. Planning and conducting recruitment, selection, and placement processes in line with
organisational requirements and policies:
● Explain:
o Describe the importance of aligning recruitment efforts with the company's strategic
needs and talent management objectives.
o Outline the various recruitment channels (e.g., job boards, social media, employee
referrals) and discuss the factors influencing their selection.
o Explain the steps involved in the recruitment process, including:
▪ Job analysis and development of accurate job descriptions.
▪ Advertising open positions effectively.
▪ Screening resumes and applications.
▪ Conducting interviews and assessments to evaluate candidate skills and
suitability.
▪ Maintaining accurate records and documentation throughout the process.
● Demonstration:
o Provide examples of well-written job descriptions that clearly outline essential
duties, responsibilities, and required qualifications.
o Showcase your ability to utilize an applicant tracking system (ATS) or other relevant
tools to manage the recruitment process.
2. Facilitating appointment decisions and finalizing contracts:
● Explain:
o Describe the importance of involving relevant stakeholders in the selection process
and obtaining their input.
o Explain the factors to consider when making appointment decisions, including
candidate qualifications, performance during interviews, and cultural fit.
o Outline the key elements of an employment contract, including start date, salary,
benefits, and probationary period.
● Demonstration:
o Role-play a scenario where you present shortlisted candidates to a hiring manager
and facilitate a discussion to reach a consensus on the best candidate.
o Explain the contract negotiation process and how you would ensure compliance
with legal requirements.
15

3. Planning and executing onboarding activities:
● Explain:
o Describe the importance of a well-structured onboarding program to welcome new
employees and help them integrate into the organization.
o Outline the key components of an onboarding program, such as:
▪ Completing necessary paperwork and administrative tasks.
▪ Providing company orientation and familiarizing new employees with the
work environment.
▪ Introducing new employees to colleagues and their teams.
▪ Setting clear expectations and performance goals.
● Demonstration:
o Share an example of an onboarding checklist or plan that outlines the key activities
and timeline for new hires.
o Discuss strategies for ensuring effective communication and support for new
employees during the onboarding process.
4. Planning and executing employee induction (Covering different types of contracts):
● Explain:
o Describe the purpose of employee induction, which is to provide specific job-related
training and familiarize new employees with their roles and responsibilities.
o Explain how induction programs can be tailored to different types of contracts
(full-time, part-time, contractors) and highlight the key differences in each approach.
● Demonstration:
o Provide an example of an induction program tailored for a specific role, outlining the
training content and delivery methods.
o Discuss strategies for ensuring that contractors receive the necessary information
and support to perform their jobs effectively.
16

5. Planning and executing termination processes:
● Explain:
o Describe the importance of adhering to fair and legal procedures when terminating
an employee's contract.
o Outline the different types of terminations (e.g., redundancy, dismissal) and the
associated processes.
o Explain the importance of providing clear communication and support to employees
throughout the termination process.
● Demonstration:
o Role-play a scenario where you explain the termination process to an employee
and answer their questions in a professional and empathetic manner.
o Discuss strategies for ensuring compliance with company policies and legal
requirements related to employee termination.
Assessment Criteria:
● IAC0201 Observe and evaluate the extent to which learners comply with the
processes and steps required to apply the skill.
o The learner demonstrates a clear understanding of the key steps involved in each
stage of the staff procurement process.
o They explain the rationale behind different approaches and highlight the importance
of adhering to company policies and legal requirements.
● IAC0202 Evaluate the extent to which learners execute the tasks within the required
time limits.
o The learner demonstrates effective time management skills and explains how they
would manage the different stages of the staff procurement process efficiently.
● IAC0203 Evaluate the level of competence in the application of the prescribed
techniques.
o The learner demonstrates a strong understanding of best practices in recruitment,
selection, onboarding, and employee relations.
o They provide clear and concise explanations of the different steps involved and
highlight the importance of using appropriate tools and techniques.
● IAC0204 Observe and evaluate the ability to demonstrate the key attributes as set
out in the applied knowledge section.
o The learner exhibits strong communication and interpersonal skills when explaining
the processes and procedures.
o They demonstrate problem-solving skills and critical thinking when addressing
challenges related to staff procurement.
o They display a professional and ethical approach to all aspects of the staff
procurement process.
17

Resources and Personnel:
● Job descriptions
● Recruitment policies and procedures
● Applicant Tracking System (ATS) (if applicable)
● Hiring managers
● Line managers
● Legal counsel (for consultation)
Examination:
● The learner will be assessed based on their ability to clearly and comprehensively explain
the key steps involved in each stage of the staff procurement process.
● They will be evaluated on their understanding of best practices, relevant policies, and legal
requirements.
● The learner's ability to demonstrate effective communication, problem-solving, and ethical
conduct will also be considered.
Note:
This practical task is designed to be a demonstration rather than an actual hiring process due to
ethical and legal considerations. The learner should focus on explaining the different steps
involved in each stage and showcasing their knowledge of best practices and relevant procedures.
