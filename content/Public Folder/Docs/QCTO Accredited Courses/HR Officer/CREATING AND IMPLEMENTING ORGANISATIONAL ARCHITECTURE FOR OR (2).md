Welcome to your Creating and Implementing Organisational Architecture for

Organisational Success and Sustainability study guide. This guide has been designed to

support you as a distance learning student, helping you engage more effectively with your

learner manual and prepare confidently for assessments — even when studying

independently from home.

Studying Creating and Implementing Organisational Architecture for Organisational

Success and Sustainability equips you with the knowledge and practical understanding

needed to design, structure, and manage organisations in ways that support long-term

performance and stability. The module focuses on how organisational structures, job design,

productivity systems, technology, organised labour, and human resource practices work

together to support organisational goals and sustainability in real workplace contexts.

This study guide is your learning companion throughout the course. It is designed to:

●  Explain key concepts from the learner manual in a clear, structured, and

learner-friendly manner.

●  Provide concise overviews of each module, unit, and key topic.

●  Support revision and preparation for assignments, assessments, and practical

workplace tasks.

●  Build your confidence in applying organisational architecture principles to real

organisational challenges.

Each module in this study guide follows the structure of your prescribed learner manual,

breaking the content into manageable and logical sections. The summaries highlight

essential concepts and frameworks using clear academic language to support independent

learning and understanding.

As a distance learner, you may not attend face-to-face classes, but this guide will help you

understand what is expected of you at each stage of the course. Use it to review your

learning, check your progress, and identify areas that may require further study or practice.

Remember, successful learning requires consistency and self-discipline. Stay organised,

manage your study time effectively, and reach out to your tutor whenever you need support

or clarification.

Let’s begin exploring how effective organisational architecture contributes to organisational

success, adaptability, and long-term sustainability — one structured step at a time.

How to Use the CREATING AND IMPLEMENTING

ORGANISATIONAL ARCHITECTURE FOR ORGANISATIONAL

SUCCESS AND SUSTAINABILITY Study Guide

This study guide is designed to make your learning journey easier, especially as a distance

learning student. Here are some helpful tips on how to use this guide effectively:

structured way. These summaries highlight the most important concepts, processes,

and responsibilities involved in delivering effective HR services.

●  Link theory to workplace practice: As you study, think about how the content

applies to real HR environments.

●  Highlight key concepts and terms: Identify and highlight important ideas,

definitions, and HR practices, such as service delivery models, professional

standards, SLAs, people analytics, compliance, and ethical HR behaviour.

●  Use It for Test and Exam Preparation: Use the summaries to revise important

points before assessments instead of re-reading the entire textbook.

●  Refer back to the learner manual when needed: The learner manual provides

detailed explanations, workplace scenarios, activities, and examples. Use it to

deepen your understanding where the study guide highlights key points.

●  Manage Your Time Wisely: Create a study schedule that allows you to work through

one module at a time.

●  Reach out for support: If you are unsure about any topic, process, or assessment

requirement, contact your tutor for guidance.

Using this guide alongside your manual will give you a clear, practical understanding of

Delivering Human Resource Management Services. Study at your own pace, stay motivated,

and keep focused on your goals. You’ve got this!

7 Study Tips

1.  Break down big topics into smaller parts: Do not try to master everything at once.

Make sure you understand each topic clearly before moving on to the next.

2.  Practise applying HR service delivery processes: Many assessment questions

require you to apply theory to realistic workplace situations.Work through activities,

case studies, and examples until you can explain each process confidently and

accurately.

terms. Review these regularly to strengthen your understanding and recall of

essential Human Resource Management terminology.

4.  Link theory to real-life examples: Think about how the content applies in real

organisations. Consider everyday HR activities such as handling employee queries,

maintaining accurate records, supporting managers, using HR technology systems,

analysing workforce data, or communicating HR policies to employees. Linking

theory to practice will make the content more meaningful and easier to remember.

5.  Test yourself often: Use end-of-module exercises, reflection questions, and

scenario-based tasks to test your understanding without referring to your notes.

6.  Use the study guide for quick revision: Before starting a new module in your

learner manual, read the relevant study guide section first. The summaries give you a

clear overview of what to expect and highlight key concepts, processes, and

responsibilities, helping you revise more efficiently.

7.  Stick to a study schedule: As a distance learner, good time management is

essential. Create a realistic study timetable and set small weekly goals. Even one

focused study session a day can make a significant difference. Stay consistent, avoid

last-minute studying, and plan your learning around your daily responsibilities so

studying becomes part of your routine.

Module 1:

KM0101 – Fundamentals of

Business and Strategic HRM

Learning Objectives

After completing this module, you should be able to:

1.  Define what an organisation is and explain its main purpose.

2.  Identify the key functions that help organisations achieve their goals.

3.  Describe different types of organisations and how they operate.

4.  Explain various organisational structures and how they support productivity.

5.  Discuss the role of management and leadership in achieving organisational success.

6.  Understand the basics of strategic Human Resource Management (HRM).

7.  Recognise how economic, social, and sustainability factors influence organisations.

9

Introduction

Every organisation, whether a small shop, a school, or a large company, exists for a reason

— to meet needs and achieve goals. To do this effectively, organisations rely on structure,

teamwork, leadership, and good management practices. This module will help you

understand how organisations function and how people within them work together to reach

shared objectives.

In today’s fast-changing world, businesses face many challenges, including competition,

globalisation, and technology. Learning about the fundamentals of business and HRM will

help you understand how organisations stay strong and adapt to change. You will also

explore how Human Resource Management plays a vital role in helping organisations

succeed by managing people effectively.
