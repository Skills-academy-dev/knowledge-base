PM0401 COORDINATE THE IMPLEMENTATION OF WORKPLACE
DISCIPLINARY AND GRIEVANCE PROCEDURES.
Activity 1
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to coordinate the
implementation of workplace disciplinary and grievance procedures in your role as an HR Officer.
You will be provided with a simulated or real-world situation where workplace disciplinary and
grievance cases must be handled.
The focus of the learning in this module is on providing learners the opportunity to practice and
master the skills required to Operationalise Employment Relations strategies and plans. Learners
will receive instruction in the methods, procedures, and techniques applicable to this specific
discipline and they will be required to select appropriate methods to resolve problems and/or
facilitate appropriate change. Learners will be required to demonstrate their application of the
required skills in an ethical way that is aligned to the professional practice standards.
Practical Task: Coordinating Workplace Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures
Scenario:
You are an HR Officer at XYZ Company, a large retail chain with over 1,000 employees. You have
been notified of two separate situations requiring your attention:
1. Disciplinary Case: A manager has reported an employee for repeated instances of
lateness and insubordination. The manager has gathered witness statements and
documented the incidents.
2. Grievance Case: An employee has filed a formal grievance alleging discrimination based
on their age. They claim they were passed over for a promotion in favour of a younger
colleague.

Task:
As the HR Officer responsible for coordinating workplace disciplinary and grievance procedures,
you are required to:
1. PS0101 Arrange disciplinary hearings and grievance consultations:
● Disciplinary Case:
o Schedule a formal disciplinary hearing with the employee and the manager who
reported the misconduct.
o Ensure the employee receives written notification of the hearing, including the date,
time, location, and the allegations against them.
o Inform the employee of their right to be accompanied by a representative.
● Grievance Case:
o Schedule a confidential grievance consultation with the employee to discuss their
concerns and gather details of the alleged discrimination.
o Ensure the employee receives written confirmation of the consultation date, time,
and location.
o Inform the employee of the grievance procedure and their right to be accompanied
by a representative.
2. PS0102 Provide information and advice regarding disciplinary hearing processes and the
grievance procedure:
● Explain the purpose and format of the disciplinary hearing to both the employee and the
manager.
● Outline the steps involved in the grievance procedure and the potential outcomes.
● Provide the employee with relevant company policies and procedures related to
disciplinary action and grievance handling.
● Ensure both parties understand their rights and responsibilities throughout the process.
13

3. PS0103 Collate and distribute all required documents for the conducting of a disciplinary
hearing and grievance consultations:
● Disciplinary Case:
o Gather all relevant documentation, including witness statements, performance
reviews, and company policies on disciplinary action.
o Prepare a hearing agenda and ensure all necessary materials are available for the
hearing.
● Grievance Case:
o Collect any documentation related to the alleged discrimination, such as job
descriptions, performance reviews, and communication records.
o Prepare a grievance form for the employee to complete and ensure they have
access to relevant company policies on non-discrimination.
4. PS0104 Oversee the maintenance and safekeeping of employment relations records:
● Maintain accurate and confidential records of all disciplinary and grievance cases, including
the dates, participants, and outcomes.
● Ensure compliance with data privacy regulations and company policies regarding the
storage and security of employee information.
● File all documentation securely and retain it for the required period as per company policy.
Assessment Criteria:
The learner will be assessed on the following criteria:
● Compliance with processes and steps:
o The learner demonstrates a clear understanding of the steps involved in
coordinating disciplinary hearings and grievance consultations.
● Time management:
o The learner schedules and conducts the hearings and consultations efficiently
within the allocated timeframe.
● Competence in applying techniques:
o The learner effectively applies the required skills and techniques for each task,
including communication, information provision, document management, and
adherence to company policies and procedures.
● Demonstration of key attributes:
o The learner exhibits the key attributes of an HR professional, such as
professionalism, impartiality, confidentiality, and sensitivity.
14

Roleplay Guidelines:
● The learner will act as the HR Officer and interact with two other individuals:
o Manager: Representing the manager who reported the employee for misconduct in
the disciplinary case.
o Employee: Representing the employee involved in both the disciplinary case and
the grievance case.
● The roleplay should be conducted in a private and confidential setting.
● The learner should use clear and concise language, demonstrating active listening and
empathy.
● The learner should maintain a professional and impartial demeanour throughout the
interactions.
Resources:
● Company disciplinary policy and procedure
● Company grievance policy and procedure
● Hearing room and meeting space
● Relevant documentation (prepared in advance)
Examination:
The learner's performance will be evaluated through a combination of:
● Observation of the learner's interactions with the manager and employee
● Review of the learner's explanations and responses regarding the disciplinary hearing
process and grievance procedure
● Assessment of the learner's ability to manage the roleplay scenario effectively and
professionally
This scenario-based practical task allows learners to demonstrate their ability to coordinate the
implementation of workplace disciplinary and grievance procedures in a simulated environment
that reflects the real-world challenges and responsibilities of an HR Officer.
Note: The facilitator/assessor must be present and he or she must complete the attached
evaluation checklist.

PM0402 FACILITATE AND GUIDE ORGANISED LABOUR RELATIONSHIPS.
Activity 2
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to facilitate and guide
organised labour relationships in your role as an HR Officer. You will be provided with a simulated
or real-world situation where fit for purpose work profiles must be created. Include details and facts
regarding the internal and external environment of an organisation as well as the organisations
vision, mission and strategic objectives and some data relating to current performance and HRM
dynamics.
The focus of the learning in this module is on providing learners the opportunity to practice and
master the skills required to Operationalise Employment Relations strategies and plans. Learners
will receive instruction in the methods, procedures, and techniques applicable to this specific
discipline and they will be required to select appropriate methods to resolve problems and/or
facilitate appropriate change. Learners will be required to demonstrate their application of the
required skills in an ethical way that is aligned to the professional practice standards.
Detailed Scenario-Based Practical Task: Facilitating and Guiding Organized Labour
Relationships
Scenario Overview:
You are an HR Officer at GreenFuture Manufacturing, a company specializing in eco-friendly
consumer products. The company has a diverse workforce, and maintaining strong labour
relations is crucial for organizational harmony and productivity. Recently, there have been several
issues requiring your attention:
1. Workplace Forum Coordination:
o Issue: Employee feedback indicates a need for more effective communication
channels and involvement in decision-making processes.
o Action: Coordinate and facilitate a Workplace Forum meeting to address employee
concerns and enhance communication.
2. Engagement with Shop Stewards:
o Issue: Shop stewards have raised concerns about the company's compliance with
employee rights and responsibilities.
o Action: Engage with shop stewards to address their concerns and ensure
compliance with their rights and responsibilities.
16

Step-by-Step Guidelines:
Task 1: Coordinate and Facilitate Appropriate Forums and Committees (PS0101)
Workplace Forum Coordination:
1. Preparation:
o Review Policies: Ensure you are familiar with the company's policies on workplace
forums and relevant legal requirements.
o Set Objectives: Define the goals of the meeting, such as improving
communication, addressing employee concerns, and enhancing participation in
decision-making.
o Schedule the Meeting:
▪ Choose a suitable date and time considering the availability of participants
(employees, management representatives, and facilitators).
▪ Book a suitable venue or set up a virtual meeting platform.
2. Invite Participants:
o Send formal invitations to all relevant stakeholders, including employee
representatives, management, and facilitators.
o Provide an agenda outlining the topics to be discussed and the objectives of the
meeting.
3. Prepare Materials:
o Gather relevant documents, such as employee feedback, policy documents, and
previous meeting minutes.
o Prepare presentation materials to communicate key points effectively.
4. Facilitate the Meeting:
o Introduction: Welcome participants, outline the objectives, and explain the agenda.
o Discussion: Encourage open discussion on each agenda item, ensuring all voices
are heard.
o Resolution: Summarize key points, propose solutions, and seek agreement on
action items.
o Documentation: Record minutes of the meeting, capturing decisions, action items,
and responsibilities.
5. Follow-Up:
o Distribute the minutes to all participants and relevant stakeholders.
o Monitor the implementation of agreed actions and provide updates in subsequent
meetings.
17

Task 2: Engage with Shop Stewards and Coordinate Compliance (PS0102)
Engagement with Shop Stewards:
1. Preparation:
o Review Rights and Responsibilities: Familiarize yourself with the legal rights and
responsibilities of shop stewards and the company's policies on labour relations.
o Set Objectives: Define the goals of the engagement, such as addressing
concerns, ensuring compliance, and fostering positive relationships.
2. Schedule Meetings:
o Arrange regular meetings with shop stewards to discuss ongoing issues and
concerns.
o Ensure meetings are scheduled at convenient times for all participants.
3. Prepare Materials:
o Gather relevant documents, such as labour laws, company policies, and records of
previous engagements.
o Prepare an agenda for each meeting to structure the discussion effectively.
4. Engage in Dialogue:
o Introduction: Welcome participants, outline the objectives, and explain the agenda.
o Discussion: Listen to the concerns of shop stewards, provide information, and
discuss compliance issues.
o Resolution: Propose solutions, seek agreement on actions, and ensure mutual
understanding.
o Documentation: Record minutes of the meeting, capturing decisions, action items,
and responsibilities.
5. Follow-Up:
o Distribute the minutes to shop stewards and relevant stakeholders.
o Monitor the implementation of agreed actions and provide updates in subsequent
meetings.
o Ensure ongoing compliance with the rights and responsibilities of shop stewards.
Resources and Personnel Required:
● Resources:
o Meeting room or virtual meeting platform
o Computers and access to HR information systems
o Company policy documents and templates
o Recording equipment (if required)
o Presentation materials (projector, slides, handouts)

o HR Officer (Learner)
o Employees (Workplace Forum participants)

o Shop stewards (Employee representatives)
o Neutral facilitator (if required)
Examination Details:
● Observation:
o Evaluators will observe the learner’s preparation and execution of each task.
o Assess compliance with procedures and timelines (IAC0201, IAC0202).
● Documentation:
o Learner will submit written invitations, agendas, minutes, and follow-up records.

o The learner will conduct role-play scenarios for the Workplace Forum and shop

o Evaluators will assess communication skills, adherence to procedures, and ethical

Detailed Scenario for Role Play:
Workplace Forum Coordination:
● Setting: Meeting room or virtual meeting platform.
● Participants: HR Officer (Learner), Employees, Management Representatives, Neutral
Facilitator.
● Role Play:
o HR Officer introduces the purpose of the forum and outlines the process.
o Employees present their concerns and suggestions.
o Management representatives provide their responses and perspectives.
o Facilitator guides the discussion to ensure constructive dialogue and resolution.
o HR Officer records minutes and ensures all procedures are followed.

● Participants: HR Officer (Learner), Shop Stewards, Management Representatives.

o HR Officer and management representatives provide responses and discuss
