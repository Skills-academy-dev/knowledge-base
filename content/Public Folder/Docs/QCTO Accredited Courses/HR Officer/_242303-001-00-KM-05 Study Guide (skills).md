Introduction to the Delivering Human Resource

Management Services Study Guide

Welcome to your KM-05: Delivering Human Resource Management Services Study Guide.

This guide has been developed to support you as a distance learning student, helping you

engage with your learner manual more effectively and prepare confidently for your assessments

— even if you are studying independently from home.

Studying Delivering Human Resource Management Services equips you with the knowledge

and practical understanding needed to support people and organisations through effective,

ethical, and professional HR practices. The module focuses on how HR services are delivered

in real workplace contexts, including recruitment and selection support, employee

administration, performance support, training coordination, HR technology, people analytics, and

the implementation of HR policies and procedures.

This guide is your companion as you progress through the course. It is designed to:

● Explain key content from the manual in a clear, structured, and learner-friendly format.

● Provide concise summaries of each module, unit, and key topic.

● Help you revise and prepare for assignments, assessments, and practical tasks.

● Build your confidence in applying HR service delivery principles in real workplace

situations.

Each module in this study guide follows the structure of your prescribed KM-05 Learner Manual,

breaking the content into manageable sections. The summaries highlight the most important

ideas and concepts, using simple academic language to support your independent learning.

As a distance learner, you may not attend face-to-face classes, but this guide will help you

understand what is expected of you in each part of the course. Use it to review your learning,

test your understanding, and identify areas where you may need additional study or practice.

Remember: you are not alone on this learning journey. Stay organised, manage your study time

effectively, and reach out to your tutor whenever you need guidance or clarification.

Let’s get started and explore the practical and people-centred world of Human Resource

Management service delivery — at your own pace.

Resource Management terminology.

maintaining accurate records, supporting managers, using HR technology systems,

analysing workforce data, or communicating HR policies to employees. Linking theory to

practice will make the content more meaningful and easier to remember.

scenario-based tasks to test your understanding without referring to your notes.

manual, read the relevant study guide section first. The summaries give you a clear

overview of what to expect and highlight key concepts, processes, and responsibilities,

helping you revise more efficiently.

Create a realistic study timetable and set small weekly goals. Even one focused study

session a day can make a significant difference. Stay consistent, avoid last-minute

studying, and plan your learning around your daily responsibilities so studying becomes

part of your routine.

Introduction

Integrating and optimising the delivery of Human Resource Management services is essential

for supporting organisational performance and employee wellbeing. Modern HR services must

be efficient, consistent, and aligned with organisational goals. This module introduces how HR

services are delivered, structured, and improved within organisations.

Read page 9 of your learner manual for more information.

KT0101: Different Approaches and Models of HR Service Delivery

HR services can be delivered in different ways depending on organisational size, structure, and

needs. This unit explains what HR service delivery is, the main approaches used, and the

models that organisations apply to deliver effective HR support.

1.1 What Is HR Service Delivery?

HR service delivery refers to how the HR function provides support and services to employees

and management. It includes the processes, systems, and methods used to deliver HR services

efficiently and consistently.

9

1.2 Approaches to HR Service Delivery

Organisations use different approaches to deliver HR services, such as decentralised or

centralised approaches. Each approach affects how HR support is accessed, managed, and

experienced by employees.

1.3 Choosing the Right Approach

The most suitable HR service delivery approach depends on organisational size, resources, and

employee needs. Organisations must align their approach with operational requirements and

strategic goals.

1.4 Models of HR Service Delivery

HR service delivery models describe how HR services are structured and organised within an

organisation. These models help improve efficiency, consistency, and service quality.

1.5 The Four Primary Models of HR Service Delivery

The main HR service delivery models include the generalist model, the multi-tiered model, the

self-service model, and the outsourced model. Organisations may use one model or combine

models to meet their HR needs.

1.6 Choosing the Right Model

Selecting the right HR service delivery model requires careful consideration of organisational

complexity, technology, and employee expectations. The chosen model should support both

operational efficiency and positive employee experience.

KT0102: Professional Practice Standards for HR Service Delivery

Professional practice standards guide HR practitioners in delivering services ethically and

responsibly. This unit explains why these standards are important and how they are applied in

everyday HR service delivery.

2.1 The Significance of Professional Practice Standards

Professional practice standards ensure that HR services are delivered with integrity, fairness,

and competence. They help build trust between employees, HR professionals, and the

organisation.

2.2 Key Areas of Focus in Professional Practice Standards

Professional standards apply across all HR functions, including recruitment, performance

management, employee relations, and compliance. These standards promote consistency and

protect employee rights.

2.3 Applying Professional Practice Standards: Bringing It to Life

Professional standards are applied through ethical decision-making and consistent HR

practices. HR practitioners use these standards when handling employee information, resolving

issues, and delivering services.

2.4 Compliance and Enforcement

Compliance with professional practice standards is supported through organisational policies

and professional accountability. Following these standards reduces risk and supports a fair and

professional workplace.

KT0105: Definition of What HR Service Delivery Means in the Context of the Organisation

HR service delivery refers to how HR supports employees and managers throughout the

employment relationship. This unit explains HR service delivery within an organisational context

and its impact on performance and employee experience.

5.1 What HR Service Delivery Means in the Context of the Organisation

HR service delivery is the structured way HR provides support, guidance, and services to

employees and management. It ensures consistency, efficiency, and alignment with

organisational goals.

5.2 The Spectrum of HR Service Delivery

HR service delivery covers all stages of the employee lifecycle, from entry to exit. It includes

administrative support, employee relations, development, and engagement activities.

5.3 The Benefits of Effective HR Service Delivery

Effective HR service delivery improves employee satisfaction and productivity. It also supports

compliance, reduces risk, and strengthens organisational performance.

5.4 Technology and Innovation

Technology and innovation enhance HR service delivery by improving speed, accuracy, and

access to services. Digital tools support automation, data analysis, and better decision-making.

KT0106: Manage HR Technology SLAs and Contracts

HR technology requires clear agreements and effective management to deliver value. This unit

focuses on managing service level agreements and contracts throughout the HR technology

lifecycle.

6.1 Before Implementation

Before implementation, HR must assess organisational needs and select suitable technology

providers. Clear SLAs and contracts define expectations, performance standards, and

responsibilities.

6.2 During Implementation and Ongoing Management

During implementation, HR monitors progress and ensures agreed services are delivered.

Ongoing management focuses on performance monitoring, issue resolution, and contract

compliance.

6.3 A Holistic Approach to HR Technology Management

A holistic approach considers user adoption, data security, and system integration. Effective

management ensures long-term value and sustainable HR service delivery.

what people analytics is, how it works, and how HR ensures its ethical and professional use.

People analytics is the use of workforce data to understand and improve employee and

organisational performance. It supports evidence-based HR decisions.

KT0108: Ensure Implementation of and Compliance with HR Policies

and Procedures

HR policies and procedures guide fair and consistent people management. This unit explains

their purpose and the role of HR in ensuring they are applied correctly.

8.1 HR Policies

HR policies are formal guidelines that outline organisational expectations and standards. They

define employee rights, responsibilities, and acceptable behaviour.

8.2 HR Procedures

HR procedures explain how HR policies are applied in practice. They provide step-by-step

8.3 Why Are Human Resource Policies Important?

HR policies promote fairness, compliance, and clarity in the workplace. They reduce risk and

support effective employee management.
