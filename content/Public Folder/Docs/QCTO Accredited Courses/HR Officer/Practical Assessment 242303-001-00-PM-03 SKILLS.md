PM0301 ESTABLISH AND OVERSEE THE IMPLEMENTATION OF
INTEGRATED LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT PLANS
Activity 1
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to establish and oversee the
implementation of integrated Learning and Development Plans in your role as an HR Officer. You
will be provided with a simulated or real-world situation where oversight must be provided on the
implementation of Learning and Development plans.
The focus of the learning in this module is on providing learners the opportunity to practice and
master the skills required to learn and Development policies, procedures, and plans. Learners will
receive instruction in the methods, procedures, and techniques applicable to this specific discipline
and they will be required to select appropriate methods to resolve problems and/or facilitate
appropriate change. Learners will be required to demonstrate their application of the required skills
in an ethical way that is aligned to the professional practice standards.
Title: Integrated Learning and Development Plans Implementation Practical Task
Scenario: As an HR Officer, you are responsible for establishing and overseeing the
implementation of integrated Learning and Development (L&D) plans within your organization. You
will be provided with a simulated or real-world situation where oversight must be provided on the
implementation of L&D plans to address the organization's training needs effectively.
Resources Needed:
● Access to employee performance data, skills assessments, and training records
● Collaboration with department heads, managers, and training providers
● L&D needs analysis tools and templates
● Budgeting tools and financial data
● Evaluation models and metrics
● Time allocation for planning, coordination, and evaluation
11

Task Guidelines:
1. Conduct Learning and Development Needs Analysis (PS0101):
o Gather data on organizational goals, performance gaps, and employee skill sets
through performance evaluations, surveys, and interviews.
o Identify training needs and priorities based on the analysis of current competencies
and future requirements.
o Determine the target audience, learning objectives, and preferred delivery methods
for each training intervention.
o Document findings in a comprehensive L&D needs analysis report, outlining key
findings, recommendations, and proposed action plans.
2. Facilitate the Creation of Individual Development Plans (IDP) and/or Personal
Development Plans (PDP) (PS0102):
o Collaborate with managers and employees to develop tailored IDPs or PDPs based
on identified learning needs and career aspirations.
o Provide guidance and support to employees in setting SMART (Specific,
Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) development goals and objectives.
o Encourage ongoing feedback and dialogue between managers and employees to
ensure alignment between individual development goals and organizational
objectives.
o Document IDPs or PDPs in a standardized format, including timelines, milestones,
and resources required for implementation.
3. Facilitate the Consolidation of IDPs and PDPs into Section, Unit, Departmental, and
Divisional L&D Plans (PS0103):
o Compile individual IDPs or PDPs into cohesive section, unit, departmental, and
divisional L&D plans.
o Coordinate with department heads and managers to align L&D plans with
organizational priorities and strategic objectives.
o Ensure that L&D plans address the needs of all levels of the organization and
support a holistic approach to talent development.
o Communicate L&D plans to relevant stakeholders and obtain buy-in from senior
management for resource allocation and support.
12

4. Identify and Cost the Required and Appropriate L&D Interventions. Facilitate the
Creation and Monitoring of L&D Budgets (PS0104):
o Identify suitable training programs, courses, workshops, and other L&D
interventions to address identified learning needs.
o Obtain cost estimates from training providers and vendors, considering factors such
as course fees, materials, travel, and accommodation expenses.
o Develop L&D budgets for each section, unit, department, and division, ensuring
alignment with organizational financial constraints and priorities.
o Monitor L&D budgets throughout the implementation phase, tracking expenses and
adjusting allocations as needed to ensure cost-effectiveness.
5. Evaluate the L&D Intervention Utilizing Appropriate Evaluation Models and Metrics
(PS0105):
o Define evaluation criteria and performance indicators to measure the effectiveness
of L&D interventions.
o Select appropriate evaluation models, such as Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Training
Evaluation, to assess learning outcomes, behaviour change, and business impact.
o Collect feedback from participants, managers, and stakeholders through surveys,
focus groups, or interviews.
o Analyse evaluation data to identify strengths, weaknesses, and areas for
improvement in L&D programs.
o Use evaluation findings to inform future training decisions and continuously improve
the effectiveness of L&D initiatives.
6. Complete the Workplace Skills Plan (WSP) and Annual Training Report and Ensure
Compliance (PS0106):
o Compile data on training activities, budgets, and outcomes to prepare the
Workplace Skills Plan (WSP) and Annual Training Report.
o Ensure that the WSP and Annual Training Report comply with legislative
requirements and industry standards.
o Submit the WSP and Annual Training Report to relevant authorities within the
specified timelines to maintain compliance with regulatory requirements.
13

Examination Guidelines:
● Compliance with Processes and Steps (IAC0101): Evaluate the learner's adherence to
established processes and steps for conducting L&D needs analysis, facilitating IDP/PDP
creation, consolidating L&D plans, budgeting, evaluation, and compliance reporting.
● Execution within Time Limits (IAC0102): Assess the learner's ability to complete each
task within the allocated time frame. Ensure that they can effectively manage time and
prioritize tasks to meet deadlines.
● Competence in Application of Prescribed Techniques (IAC0103): Evaluate the
learner's proficiency in applying L&D planning, budgeting, evaluation, and compliance
techniques. Assess their ability to apply appropriate tools, methodologies, and best
practices effectively.
● Demonstration of Key Attributes (IAC0104): Observe the learner's communication skills,
teamwork, problem-solving abilities, and strategic thinking throughout the practical task.
Evaluate their professionalism, attention to detail, and ability to demonstrate leadership in
overseeing L&D processes.
Feedback and Assessment: Provide constructive feedback to the learner based on their
performance in each assessment criterion. Highlight strengths and areas for improvement, and
offer recommendations for further development. Use a scoring rubric to quantify the learner's
proficiency level in each skill area.
Note: The facilitator/assessor must be present and he or she must complete the attached
evaluation checklist.

PM0302 FACILITATE AND COORDINATE PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT.
Activity 2
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to facilitate and coordinate
performance management in your role as an HR Officer. You will be provided with a simulated or
real-world situation where Performance Management processes must be implemented.
The focus of the learning in this module is on providing learners the opportunity to practice and
master the skills required to Learning and Development policies, procedures, and plans. Learners

facilitate appropriate change. Learners will be required to demonstrate their application of the
required skills in an ethical way that is aligned to the professional practice standards.
Practical Task: Demonstrating Performance Management Facilitation
Scenario:
You are an HR Officer in a company with 50 employees. The company is implementing a new
performance management system and you are responsible for facilitating and coordinating the
process at the operational level (team and individual). This includes:
● Implementing and facilitating the performance management process.
● Guiding interventions for dealing with substandard performance.
● Coaching line managers in dealing with performance management.
Demonstration:
1. PS0201 Implementing and Facilitating the Performance Management Process at
Operational Level (Team and Individual):
● Explain:
o Describe the key steps involved in the performance management cycle, including:
▪ Setting clear performance goals and objectives aligned with individual and
team goals.
▪ Conducting regular performance reviews and providing feedback.
▪ Identifying areas for improvement and development.
▪ Recognizing and rewarding strong performance.
15

o Explain the importance of using standardized performance management tools and
templates to ensure consistency and fairness.
o Discuss the role of HR in supporting line managers throughout the performance
management process.
● Demonstration:
o Showcase a sample performance review form or template and explain how it should
be used.
o Role-play a scenario where you conduct a performance review with an employee,
providing constructive feedback and setting development goals.
o Discuss strategies for ensuring effective communication and documentation
throughout the performance management process.
2. PS0202 Facilitating and Guiding Interventions for Dealing with Substandard
Performance:
● Explain:
o Describe the different approaches to addressing substandard performance,
including:
▪ Providing additional coaching and support.
▪ Implementing performance improvement plans.
▪ Taking disciplinary action if necessary.
o Explain the importance of following fair and legal procedures when dealing with
substandard performance.
o Discuss the role of HR in supporting line managers in addressing performance
issues.
● Demonstration:
o Role-play a scenario where you guide a line manager through the process of
addressing an employee's performance concerns.
o Discuss the importance of clear communication, documentation, and following
company policies when dealing with performance issues.
16

3. PS0203 Coaching Line Managers in Dealing with Performance Management:
● Explain:
o Describe the importance of line managers playing a key role in the performance
management process.
o Explain how HR can provide coaching and support to line managers in areas such
as:
▪ Setting clear expectations and goals.
▪ Conducting effective performance reviews.
▪ Providing constructive feedback.
▪ Addressing performance issues.
o Discuss the importance of creating a culture of open communication and feedback
within the organization.
● Demonstration:
o Provide examples of training materials or resources that can be used to coach line
managers on performance management.
o Role-play a scenario where you coach a line manager on how to conduct a
performance review or address an employee's performance concerns.
Assessment Criteria:
● IAC0201 Observe and evaluate the extent to which learners comply with the
processes and steps required to apply the skill.
o The learner demonstrates a clear understanding of the key steps involved in the
performance management process.
o They explain the rationale behind different approaches and highlight the importance
of using standardized tools and procedures.
● IAC0202 Evaluate the extent to which learners execute the tasks within the required
time limits.
o The learner demonstrates effective communication and time management skills
when explaining the process and guiding interventions.
● IAC0203 Evaluate the level of competence in the application of the prescribed
techniques.
o The learner demonstrates a strong understanding of best practices in performance
management and relevant legal requirements.
o They provide clear and concise explanations of the different steps involved and
highlight the importance of using appropriate tools and techniques.
17

● IAC0204 Observe and evaluate the ability to demonstrate the key attributes as set
out in the applied knowledge section.
o The learner exhibits strong communication, interpersonal, and problem-solving
skills when explaining the process, coaching line managers, and addressing
performance issues.
o They display a professional and ethical approach to all aspects of performance
management.
Resources and Personnel:
● Performance management policy and procedures
● Performance review forms and templates
● Line managers
● Employees (for role-playing scenarios)
Examination:
● The learner will be assessed based on their ability to clearly and comprehensively explain
the key steps involved in the performance management process.
● They will be evaluated on their understanding of best practices, relevant policies, and legal
requirements.
● The learner's ability to demonstrate effective communication, problem-solving, and
coaching skills will also be considered.

Practical Task: Designing and Implementing Organizational Development Interventions
Scenario:
You are the HR Officer in a company with 200 employees. The company has recently experienced
a significant merger and is facing challenges with integrating the two cultures and improving
communication and collaboration between the two teams. The leadership team has identified the
need for organizational development (OD) interventions to address these issues and improve
overall organizational effectiveness.
Demonstration:
The learner will explain the steps involved in designing and implementing OD interventions,
focusing on the following key tasks:
PS0501 Define the goals and objectives:
● Explanation:
o The learner will explain the importance of clearly defining the goals and objectives
of the OD interventions. In this scenario, the goals might be to:
▪ Improve communication and collaboration between the merged teams.
▪ Foster a more integrated and cohesive company culture.
▪ Enhance employee engagement and morale.
o They will discuss the need to align these goals with the overall organizational
strategy and the specific challenges faced by the company.

● Demonstration:
o The learner will provide a framework for conducting a needs assessment to identify
the specific areas requiring intervention based on data, employee feedback, and
observations.
PS0502 Assess the current OD processes:
● Explanation:
o The learner will explain the importance of assessing the existing OD practices
within the organization. This could involve reviewing past initiatives, conducting
surveys, and holding focus groups with employees and managers.
o They will discuss the need to identify any gaps or areas for improvement in the
current approach to OD.
● Demonstration:
o The learner will showcase sample survey questions or interview prompts used to
gather feedback on existing OD practices and the current organizational climate.
PS0503 Determine the budget for the OD program:
● Explanation:
o The learner will explain the importance of determining a realistic budget for the OD
interventions based on the defined goals and objectives.
o They will discuss the need to consider different intervention options, such as team
building workshops, leadership development programs, and communication
training.
● Demonstration:
o The learner will provide a framework for estimating the costs associated with
different OD intervention options, considering internal resources and potential
external vendors.
PS0504 Design the OD program:
● Explanation:
o The learner will explain the importance of designing a program that is tailored to the
specific needs and preferences of the organization and its employees.
o They will discuss the need to consider a variety of intervention strategies, including:
▪ Human process interventions: Team building activities, communication
workshops, conflict resolution training.
▪ Techno-structural interventions: Restructuring teams, redesigning
workflows, implementing new technologies.
