PM0101 CREATE APPROPRIATE WORK PROFILES
Activity 1
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to create appropriate work
profiles in your role as an HR Officer. You will be provided with a simulated or real-world situation
where Fit for purpose work profiles must be created.
The focus of the learning in this module is on providing learners the opportunity to practice and
master the skills required to Implement fit for purpose HR Architecture in organisations. Learners will
receive instruction in the methods, procedures, and techniques applicable to this specific discipline
and they will be required to select appropriate methods to resolve problems and/or facilitate
appropriate change. Learners will be required to demonstrate their application of the required skills in
an ethical way that is aligned to the professional practice standards.
Practical Task: Creating Work Profiles
Scenario:
You are an HR Officer in a company undergoing restructuring. As part of this process, the company
needs to update and revise its existing work profiles to ensure they accurately reflect the current and
future needs of the organization. You are tasked with creating fit-for-purpose work profiles for a
specific department or team.
Tasks:
PS0101 Develop Job Descriptions:
1. Gather Information:
o Meet with the department head and key team members to discuss the roles and
responsibilities within the team.
o Review existing job descriptions and relevant organizational documents.
o Analyse current work practices and identify any changes or updates needed.
2. Define Key Elements:
o Determine the job title, reporting structure, and key accountabilities for each role.
o Identify essential duties and responsibilities, including tasks, skills, and knowledge
required.
o Specify minimum qualifications and experience required for each role.
3. Write Clear and Concise Descriptions:
o Use action verbs and clear language to describe the key elements of each job.
o Ensure the descriptions are specific and relevant to the actual work performed.
o Maintain a consistent format and style for all job descriptions.
11

PS0102 Develop Employee Specifications:
1. Define Desired Skills and Competencies:
o Based on the job descriptions, identify the specific skills, knowledge, and personal
qualities required for each role.
o Consider both technical skills and soft skills relevant to the job and the organization's
culture.
o Determine the level of experience and qualifications required for each skill and
competency.
2. Develop a Rating System:
o Establish a clear and consistent system for rating the importance of each skill and
competency.
o This could involve using a numerical scale or descriptive terms like "essential,"
"important," or "desirable."
3. Document the Specifications:
o Create a formal document outlining the employee specifications for each role.
o Ensure the document is clear, concise, and easy to understand.
PS0103 Assist with Job Evaluation:
1. Understand Job Evaluation Methods:
o Familiarize yourself with different job evaluation methods, such as point-factor analysis
or Hay method.
o Understand the purpose and principles of each method.
2. Collect Job Data:
o Work with the department head and relevant personnel to gather detailed information
about each role.
o This may include data on responsibilities, required skills, and the impact of the role on
the organization.
3. Apply the Chosen Method:
o Assist with applying the chosen job evaluation method to analyse and compare the
relative worth of different jobs within the department.
o Ensure the evaluation process is fair, transparent, and consistent.

PM0102 CREATE APPROPRIATE ORGANISATIONAL STRUCTURES
Activity 2
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to create appropriate
organisational structures in your role as an HR Officer. You will be provided with a simulated or
real-world situation where organisational structures must be created or reviewed.
The focus of the learning in this module is on providing learners the opportunity to practice and
master the skills required to Implement fit for purpose HR Architecture in organisations. Learners will
receive instruction in the methods, procedures, and techniques applicable to this specific discipline
and they will be required to select appropriate methods to resolve problems and/or facilitate
appropriate change. Learners will be required to demonstrate their application of the required skills in
an ethical way that is aligned to the professional practice standards.
Practical Task: Creating Organizational Structures
Scenario:
Your company is experiencing rapid growth and is planning to expand into a new market. This
requires the creation of a new department or team with specific responsibilities. You, as the HR
Officer, are tasked with designing the organizational structure for this new unit, ensuring it aligns with
the overall company strategy and workforce needs.

Strategic Plan:
● Overall Goal: Achieve sustainable growth and expand market reach by entering a new market.
● Target Market: [Name of the new market]

market. The learner should adapt this information to fit the specific scenario they are working with.
17

Tasks:
PS0101 Analyse work and current structures and determine appropriate structure models
aligned to organisational and workforce strategy:
1. Gather Information:
o Review the company's strategic plan and business objectives for the new market entry.
o Analyse existing organizational structures and identify any successful models or areas
for improvement.
o Consult with key stakeholders, including senior management and potential team
members, to understand the specific needs and requirements of the new unit.
2. Research Different Structure Models:
o Investigate various organizational structure models, such as functional, divisional,
matrix, or project-based structures.
o Analyse the strengths and weaknesses of each model in the context of the new unit's
goals and objectives.
3. Select the Most Appropriate Model:
o Based on the gathered information and analysis, choose the organizational structure
model that best aligns with the company's strategy, workforce needs, and the specific
requirements of the new unit.
PS0102 Determine the appropriate workforce complement:
1. Define Roles and Responsibilities:
o Identify the key roles and responsibilities required within the new unit to achieve its
objectives.
o Consider the skills, knowledge, and experience needed for each role.
o Analyse the workload and determine the appropriate number of employees for each
role.
2. Develop Job Descriptions:
o Create clear and concise job descriptions for each identified role, outlining the
essential duties, responsibilities, and required qualifications.
o Ensure the job descriptions align with the chosen organizational structure and the
overall company strategy.
PS0103 Develop organograms:
1. Visualize the Structure:
o Create a clear and visually appealing organogram that depicts the chosen
organizational structure of the new unit.
o Include all roles, reporting lines, and key responsibilities within the diagram.
o Ensure the organogram is easy to understand and reflects the flow of information and
decision-making within the unit.
18

2. Utilize Appropriate Tools:
o Utilize organizational charting software or templates to create the organogram.
o Ensure the format is consistent with existing company standards and professional best
practices.
PS0104 Obtain stakeholders support and agreement for the structures:
1. Present and Explain the Proposal:
o Clearly present the proposed organizational structure, including the chosen model,
workforce complement, and organogram, to key stakeholders.
o Explain the rationale behind the chosen structure and how it aligns with the company's
strategy and the new unit's goals.
2. Address Concerns and Feedback:
o Be prepared to address any questions, concerns, or suggestions from stakeholders
regarding the proposed structure.
o Demonstrate flexibility and willingness to adapt the structure based on valid feedback
while maintaining alignment with the overall objectives.
3. Seek Formal Agreement:
o Once the stakeholders have reviewed and understood the proposed structure, obtain
their formal agreement and approval.
o This may involve securing signatures on a document or conducting a formal vote
depending on the company's decision-making processes.
Assessment Criteria:
