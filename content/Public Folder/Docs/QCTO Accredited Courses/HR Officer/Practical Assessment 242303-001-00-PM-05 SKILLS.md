PM0501: FACILITATE THE DELIVERY OF SOLUTION BASED HRM
SERVICES.
Activity 1
In this practical task, you will be required to demonstrate your ability to facilitate the delivery of
solution based HRM Services in your role as an HR Officer. You will be provided with a simulated
or real-world situation where oversight must be provided on the implementation of Learning and
Development plans. Include details and facts regarding the internal and external environment of an
organisation as well as the organisation's vision, mission and strategic objectives and some data
relating to current performance and HRM dynamics.
The focus of the learning in this module is on providing learners the opportunity to practice and
master the skills required to Solution based and client centred HRM Services. Learners will receive
instruction in the methods, procedures, and techniques applicable to this specific discipline and
they will be required to select appropriate methods to resolve problems and/or facilitate
appropriate change. Learners will be required to demonstrate their application of the required skills
in an ethical way that is aligned to the professional practice standards.
Practical Task: Facilitating the Delivery of Solution-Based HRM Services as an HR Officer
Scenario/Case Study:
Organization: GlobalTech Solutions
Vision: To be a leading provider of innovative technology solutions that transform industries and
improve lives.
Mission: To deliver high-quality technology products and services that meet the evolving needs of
our clients, while fostering a culture of continuous improvement and professional development
among our employees.
Strategic Objectives:
1. Enhance innovation through advanced research and development.
2. Expand market presence by 25% in emerging markets over the next three years.
3. Improve employee performance and engagement through targeted learning and
development initiatives.
4. Achieve a 15% increase in client satisfaction by optimizing service delivery processes.
11

Internal Environment:
● Employee Base: 500 employees across various departments including R&D, Sales,
Marketing, and Customer Support.
● Current L&D Programs: Traditional classroom training and e-learning modules focusing on
technical skills and product knowledge.
● Recent Employee Survey: Indicated a need for more personalized career development
opportunities and leadership training.
External Environment:
● Industry Trends: Rapid technological advancements and increasing demand for digital
transformation solutions.
● Competitive Landscape: High competition from both established firms and startups,
requiring constant innovation and upskilling.
● Economic Factors: Economic uncertainty necessitating cost-effective yet impactful training
solutions.
Current Performance and HRM Dynamics:
● Performance Metrics: Average employee performance rating is 3.5 out of 5. High turnover
rate in the Sales department (18%).
● HRM Dynamics: Limited use of data analytics in assessing training needs. Existing
mentorship program underutilized.
Task Overview:
You, as an HR Officer, are tasked with overseeing the implementation of a new Learning and
Development (L&D) plan aimed at addressing the identified needs and aligning with the
organization's strategic objectives. Your role includes writing a detailed report, making
presentations to diverse decision-makers, identifying client-specific HR needs, designing
appropriate interventions, and using problem-solving techniques to address and prevent issues.

Detailed Guidelines:
1. Write and Present HR Information in Written Report Format (PS0101):
Step-by-Step Guidelines:
1. Gather Data: Collect data from employee performance reviews, survey results, and
existing L&D program outcomes.
2. Analyse Data: Identify key trends, skill gaps, and areas needing improvement.
3. Develop the Report:
o Introduction: Provide an overview of the organization's vision, mission, strategic
objectives, and the purpose of the L&D plan.
o Current State Analysis: Summarize current performance metrics and HRM
dynamics.
o Needs Assessment: Detail findings from data analysis, highlighting specific areas
for development.
o Proposed L&D Plan: Outline the new L&D initiatives, including objectives,
methods, and expected outcomes.
o Implementation Strategy: Describe the steps for rolling out the plan, timelines,
and responsible parties.
o Evaluation Metrics: Specify how the effectiveness of the L&D initiatives will be
measured.
o Conclusion: Summarize key points and recommend next steps.
2. Make Presentations Regarding HR Information to Diverse Decision-Makers (PS0102):
Step-by-Step Guidelines:
1. Prepare Presentation Slides:
o Create slides that summarize the key points from the written report.
o Use visuals (charts, graphs) to highlight data and trends.
o Include a slide on the alignment of the L&D plan with strategic objectives.
o Prepare a slide for each key proposal within the L&D plan, detailing benefits and
implementation steps.
2. Tailor the Presentation:
o Adjust the level of detail based on the audience (executive leadership, departmental
managers, etc.).
o Anticipate potential questions and prepare responses.
3. Deliver the Presentation:
o Use clear and concise language.
o Engage the audience with questions and interactive elements.
o Emphasize the strategic importance of the L&D initiatives and their expected
impact.
16

3. Identify Client-Specific HR Needs (PS0103):
Step-by-Step Guidelines:
1. Conduct Interviews and Surveys:
o Engage with employees across various departments to understand their career
aspirations and development needs.
o Use surveys to collect data on employee satisfaction with current L&D programs
and suggestions for improvement.
2. Analyse Feedback:
o Identify common themes and specific needs that align with organizational goals.
o Categorize needs into immediate, short-term, and long-term priorities.
4. Design Appropriate Interventions to Meet Identified Client Needs and Communicate
These Interventions (PS0104):
Step-by-Step Guidelines:
1. Develop Interventions:
o Immediate: Implement short courses and workshops on high-demand skills.
o Short-term: Develop personalized development plans based on performance
reviews.
o Long-term: Introduce leadership development programs and expand the
mentorship program.
2. Communicate Interventions:
o Draft an internal communication plan detailing the new interventions.
o Use emails, intranet posts, and team meetings to inform employees and managers.
o Highlight the benefits of the new interventions and how they align with career
development goals.
5. Use Problem-Solving Techniques to Find the Cause of Problems, Select Alternatives, and
Prevent Future Problems (PS0105):
Step-by-Step Guidelines:
1. Identify Problems:
o Use data analysis and feedback to pinpoint specific issues (e.g., high turnover in
Sales).
2. Root Cause Analysis:
o Apply techniques such as the 5 Whys or Fishbone Diagram to identify underlying
causes.
3. Develop Solutions:
o Brainstorm potential solutions and evaluate their feasibility.
o Select the most viable solutions based on impact and resources.
17

4. Implement Solutions:
o Develop an action plan for implementing the selected solutions.
o Assign responsibilities and set timelines.
5. Monitor and Adjust:
o Continuously monitor the impact of the interventions.
o Gather feedback and make necessary adjustments to prevent future issues.
Assessment Criteria:
1. IAC0101: Observe and evaluate the extent to which learners comply with the processes
and steps required to apply the skill.
2. IAC0102: Evaluate the extent to which learners execute the tasks within the required time
limits.
3. IAC0103: Evaluate the level of competence in the application of the prescribed techniques.
4. IAC0104: Observe and evaluate the ability to demonstrate the key attributes as set out in
the applied knowledge section.
Resources and Personnel Required:
● Access to HR-CMIS and relevant data
● Survey and feedback tools
● Presentation software (e.g., PowerPoint)
● Meeting space or virtual conference tools
● Mentorship program details
● Support from senior HR staff and departmental managers
Examination Details:
● Written Report: Learners will submit a comprehensive report following the provided
guidelines. Evaluation will focus on clarity, thoroughness, and alignment with organizational
objectives.
● Presentation: Learners will present their report to a panel of evaluators. They will be
assessed on their ability to communicate effectively and engage the audience.
● Client Needs Identification: Learners will conduct a simulated interview and survey
session to gather data. They will be assessed on their ability to identify and categorize
needs accurately.
● Intervention Design: Learners will develop and communicate detailed interventions. They
will be assessed on the relevance and practicality of their solutions.
● Problem Solving: Learners will perform a root cause analysis and propose solutions.
Assessment will focus on their problem-solving approach and the feasibility of their
recommendations.
