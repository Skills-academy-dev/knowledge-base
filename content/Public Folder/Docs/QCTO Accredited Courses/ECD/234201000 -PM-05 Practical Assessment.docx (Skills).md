PM-05-PS01: Identify and establish partnerships with parents and support
structure
Your facilitator shall provide you with task instructions, documentation, and templates.
Complete the following activities in the presence of your facilitator/instructor who must observe the
activity and complete the attached observation evaluation form.
Activity 1
Here's a detailed simulation for Practical Task Assessment on Identifying and Establishing Partnerships
with Parents and Support Structures:
Simulation Scenario:
You are a practitioner in an early childhood setting, working with children aged 0-6 years.
Task: Create a Contact List (PA0101)
Create a contact list of local support structures.
Deliverables:
1. Contact List (approx. 2-3 pages): Document local support structures.
2. Categorization System (approx. 1 page): Organize contacts by category.
3. Partnership Plan (approx. 200 words): Outline collaboration strategies.
Example Template: Contact List:
Government Organizations:
● Local Health Department: 555-1234
● Children's Services: 555-5678
Non-Government Organizations:
● Children's Charity: 555-9012
● Family Support Services: 555-1111
Community Structures:
● Parent-Teacher Association: 555-2222
● Local Library: 555-3333
11

Categorization System:
● Health and Wellbeing
● Education and Development
● Family Support
Partnership Plan:
● Regular meetings with local health department
● Collaborative events with parent-teacher association
● Referral processes with family support services
Assessment Criteria: PA0101:
1. Depth of understanding of local support structures
2. Effectiveness of contact list
3. Quality of categorization system
4. Clarity of partnership plan
5. Alignment with early childhood frameworks (e.g., EYFS, NAEYC)
Grading:
● Satisfactory (40-60%): Meets minimum requirements
● Good (61-75%): Demonstrates good understanding of local support structures
● Excellent (76-90%): Shows exceptional ability to establish partnerships
● Outstanding (91-100%): Exceeds expectations, demonstrating innovative and child-
centered approach
Please provide your submissions for the contact list, categorization system, and partnership plan.
Additional Tips:
● Research local organizations and services
● Consider cultural and individual differences
● Emphasize the importance of collaboration and communication
● Demonstrate knowledge of child development theories (e.g., Piaget, Vygotsky)

Activity 2
Simulation Scenario:
You are a practitioner in an early childhood setting, working with children aged 0-6 years.
Task: Work in Partnership with Parents/Caregivers (PA0102)
Organize activities to recognize and value parents' contributions.
Deliverables:
1. Parental Engagement Plan (approx. 2-3 pages): Outline activities.
2. Cultural Competence Strategy (approx. 1-2 pages): Document considerations.
3. Parent Feedback and Evaluation (approx. 100 words): Record feedback.
Example Template:
Parental Engagement Plan:
Activity 1: Parent-Child Workshop (e.g., storytelling, craft) Activity
2: Parent-Teacher Conferences (e.g., progress updates) Activity
3: Cultural Event (e.g., traditional food, music)

● Consider socio-cultural knowledge
● Respect child-rearing practices
● Incorporate diverse materials and resources

1. Depth of understanding of parental engagement
2. Effectiveness of cultural competence strategy
3. Quality of activities
4. Parent satisfaction
5. Alignment with early childhood frameworks (e.g., EYFS, NAEYC)

centered approach
Please provide your submissions for the parental engagement plan, cultural competence
strategy, and parent feedback.
Additional Tips:
