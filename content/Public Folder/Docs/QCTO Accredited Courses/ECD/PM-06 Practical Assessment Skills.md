INSTRUCTIONS TO THE FACILITATOR
This is a very responsible assignment because you have been tasked with the responsibility
of rounding off the learner’s practical exposure.
You must ensure that you are familiar with all aspects of the work covered in this Guide
because you must keep a daily account of the learner’s performance.
● Study the Guide and acquaint yourself with its content and format
● Remember this Guide is the learner’s full record of learning and workplace exposure.
● All activities which the learner participates in must be recorded, and all documents
produced in relation to this learnership must form part of the record of learning
● Get all the learners together and explain its purpose to them and also what is
required of them
● Remember the Facilitator is the creator of learning and exposure opportunities. You
should therefore not confine the learner’s exposure to this Guide alone
● Continuously guide them in doing the assignments and arranging the planned
exposure with the relevant departments
● File all duplicate records of learning on a file for each learner
● This Guide need not be followed chronologically, but please note that the learner is
required to work through the entire Guide by the end of the learning period

PM-06-PS01: Identify relevant administrative information,
documentation, processes and systems
Your facilitator shall provide you with given work instructions, relevant documentation
including templates, forms, report format and checklist the learner must be able to.
Complete the following activities in the presence of your facilitator/instructor who must
observe the activity and complete the attached observation evaluation form.
Activity 1
Simulation Scenario:
You are a manager/practitioner in an early childhood setting, working with children aged 0-6
years.
Task 1: Develop a Master File (PA0101)
Develop a master file for relevant documentation.
Deliverables:
1. Master File Index (approx. 1-2 pages): Outline contents.
2. Policy and Procedure Documents (approx. 5-7 pages): Include admission,
safeguarding, health and safety.
3. Administrative Forms (approx. 3-5 pages): Include registration, consent, and
contact information.
Example Template:
Master File Index:
● Policy documents
● Procedure documents
● Administrative forms
● Staff records
● Child records
11

Policy and Procedure Documents:
● Admission policy
● Safeguarding policy
● Health and safety policy
● Data protection policy
Administrative Forms:
● Registration form
● Consent form
● Contact information form
Task 2: Implement Resource Management Systems (PA0102)
Implement systems for resource management.
Deliverables:
1. Resource Management Plan (approx. 2-3 pages): Outline strategies.
2. Budget and Financial Records (approx. 2-3 pages): Document expenses.
3. Inventory Management System (approx. 1-2 pages): Track resources.
Example Template:
Resource Management Plan:
● Budgeting
● Procurement
● Maintenance
● Staff training
Budget and Financial Records:
● Income statement
● Expenditure report
● Balance sheet
12

Inventory Management System:
● Resource catalog
● Tracking log
● Maintenance schedule
Task 3: Maintain Child Records (PA0103)
Maintain accurate and up-to-date records of children.
Deliverables:
1. Child Record Template (approx. 1-2 pages): Document child information.
2. Attendance and Progress Records (approx. 1-2 pages): Track attendance and
progress.
3. Confidentiality and Data Protection Policy (approx. 1-2 pages): Ensure secure
storage.
Example Template:
Child Record Template:
● Personal details
● Contact information
● Medical history
● Developmental progress
Attendance and Progress Records:
● Attendance log
● Progress reports
● Observation records
Confidentiality and Data Protection Policy:
● Secure storage procedures
● Access protocols
● Data protection guidelines
13

Assessment Criteria:
PA0101-PA0103:
1. Depth of understanding of administrative tasks
2. Effectiveness of documentation systems
3. Quality of resource management
4. Accuracy of child records
5. Alignment with early childhood frameworks (e.g., EYFS, NAEYC)
Grading:
● Satisfactory (40-60%): Meets minimum requirements
● Good (61-75%): Demonstrates good understanding of administrative tasks
● Excellent (76-90%): Shows exceptional ability to manage resources and records
● Outstanding (91-100%): Exceeds expectations, demonstrating innovative and
child-centered approach
