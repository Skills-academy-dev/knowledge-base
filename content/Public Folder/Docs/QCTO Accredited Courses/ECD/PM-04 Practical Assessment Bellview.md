PM-04-PS01: Promote the safety and protection of children
Your facilitator shall provide you with relevant documents, accident report forms, menu
templates, scales and measures, and the Road to Health Booklet.
Complete the following activities in the presence of your facilitator/instructor who must
observe the activity and complete the attached observation evaluation form.
Activity 1
Simulation Scenario:
You are a practitioner in an early childhood setting, working with children aged 0-6 years.
Task 1: Create Safe Environments (PA0101)
Create an inclusive, age- and stage-appropriate, safe environment.
Deliverables:
1. Environment Checklist (approx. 2-3 pages): Document safety features.
2. Risk Assessment Report (approx. 200 words): Identify potential hazards.
3. Inclusion Strategy (approx. 200 words): Describe adaptations for diverse children.
Example Template:
Environment Checklist:
Outdoor Area:
● Soft surfacing
● Secure fencing
● Shade structures
Risk Assessment Report:
● Tripping hazards
● Equipment maintenance
● Staff-to-child ratios
Inclusion Strategy:
