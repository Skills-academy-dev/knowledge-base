PM-01-PS01: Plan and prepare activities and routines using an
approved programme based on the National Curriculum Framework
Complete the following activities in the presence of your facilitator/instructor who must observe
the activity and complete the attached observation evaluation form.
Activity 1
Task 1: Draw up a weekly plan (PA0101)
Create a weekly plan for a class of 20 children aged 3-5 years, using the National Curriculum

Task 2: Prepare daily activities (PA0102)
Objective: Prepare weekly and daily plans for a childcare setting, catering to different ages and
stages of children, incorporating adult-directed and child-initiated activities.
Simulation Scenario:
You are the lead educator at a childcare center with 20 children aged 2-5 years. Prepare plans for
the upcoming week, ensuring alignment with developmental guidelines.
Age Groups and Developmental Stages:
● 2-3 years (Toddler): Exploring independence, sensory development
● 3-4 years (Preschool): Social-emotional growth, language development
● 4-5 years (Pre-K): Cognitive development, fine motor skills
Weekly Plan:
Theme Learning Objectives Activities
Exploring Nature
Adult-Directed:
- Nature walk (cognitive)
- Planting seeds (fine motor)
Child-Initiated:
- Sensory bin exploration (toddler)
- Nature-themed puzzles (preschool)
Daily Plans:
Monday (2-3 years)
● Adult-Directed: Storytime (language development)
● Child-Initiated: Playdough exploration (fine motor)
Monday (3-4 years)
● Adult-Directed: Role-playing (social-emotional)
● Child-Initiated: Building blocks (cognitive)
12

Monday (4-5 years)
● Adult-Directed: Science experiment (cognitive)
● Child-Initiated: Art expression (creativity)
Tuesday-Friday Plans:
Repeat the process for each day, ensuring variety and progression.
Assessment Criteria:
● Alignment with developmental guidelines
● Balance of adult-directed and child-initiated activities
● Variety and diversity of activities
● Promotion of holistic development (cognitive, social-emotional, physical)
● Clear learning objectives and outcomes
Evidence Requirements:
● Weekly plan document
● Daily plan documents for each age group
● Observation records of children engaging with activities
● Feedback from colleagues or parents on plan effectiveness

Task 3: Select, make and adapt learning resources that are sensitive to
inclusivity and diversity (PA0103)
Objective: Create an inclusive learning environment conducive to learning activities for all
children, incorporating language and cultural diversity, and accommodating children with
barriers to learning.
Simulation Scenario:
You are the lead educator at a diverse childcare centre with 20 children aged 2-5 years,
including:
● 5 children with English as a second language
● 3 children with disabilities (visual, hearing, physical)
● 2 children from culturally diverse backgrounds (African, Asian)
Inclusive Learning Environment Setup:
Physical Space:
1. Accessible entrance and pathways
2. Adaptive furniture (e.g., wheelchair-accessible tables)
3. Sensory-friendly materials (e.g., soft lighting, textured surfaces)
Language and Cultural Diversity:
1. Multilingual signage and labels
2. Cultural-themed decorations and materials
3. Language-rich activities (e.g., storytelling, singing)
Accommodations for Children with Barriers to Learning:
1. Visual aids (e.g., pictures, charts) for children with visual impairments
2. Assistive listening devices for children with hearing impairments
3. Adaptive materials (e.g., large print, braille) for children with physical disabilities
14

Activity Stations:
1. Language Station: Multilingual books, language games
2. Cultural Station: Traditional clothing, cultural artefacts
3. Sensory Station: Textured materials, sensory bins
4. Art Station: Adaptive art tools, diverse materials
Assessment Criteria:
● Inclusivity and diversity considerations
● Accessibility and adaptability
● Promotion of language and cultural diversity
● Accommodations for children with barriers to learning
● Effective use of space and resources
Evidence Requirements:
● Photographs or videos of the learning environment
● Description of inclusive features and accommodations
● Observation records of children engaging with activities
● Feedback from parents, colleagues, or children on the inclusivity of the environment

Task 4: Work with others to plan and prepare activities (PA0104)
Objective: Select, make, and adapt resources to equip the learning environment in an inclusive
way, catering to diverse needs and abilities.
Simulation Scenario:
You are the lead educator at a childcare centre with 20 children aged 2-5 years, including:
● 5 children with English as a second language
● 3 children with disabilities (visual, hearing, physical)
● 2 children from culturally diverse backgrounds (African, Asian)
Resource Selection and Adaptation:
Inclusive Materials:
1. Multilingual books and flashcards
2. Tactile alphabet and number lines
3. Braille and large print materials
4. Culturally diverse puzzles and games
5. Adaptive art tools (e.g., mouth sticks, adaptive grips)
Sensory Resources:
1. Texture balls and sensory bins
2. Sound-emitting toys and instruments
3. Smell and taste exploration kits
4. Visual aids (e.g., pictures, charts)
Communication Resources:
1. Picture communication symbols
2. Sign language charts and videos
3. Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) devices
16

Adaptations:
1. Modify furniture for accessibility (e.g., wheelchair-accessible tables)
2. Create visual schedules and checklists
3. Adapt activities for children with physical disabilities (e.g., wheelchair-accessible obstacle
course)
Assessment Criteria:
● Inclusivity and diversity considerations
● Appropriateness of resources for diverse needs
● Adaptability and creativity in resource selection
● Effective use of resources to support learning
● Safety and durability of resources
Evidence Requirements:
● Photographs or videos of resources and adaptations
● Description of resource selection and adaptation process
● Observation records of children using resources
● Feedback from parents, colleagues, or children on resource effectiveness
17

Task 5: Compare and review weekly programme plans (PA0105)
Objective: Compare and review two weekly programme plans to evaluate their effectiveness in
promoting holistic development in children aged 2-5 years.
Simulation Scenario:
You are the lead educator at a childcare center with 20 children aged 2-5 years. You have been
provided with two weekly programme plans:
Plan A:
● Theme: Exploring Nature
● Activities: Outdoor play, sensory bin exploration, nature walk
● Learning Objectives: Develop cognitive skills, promote curiosity
Plan B:
● Theme: Cultural Diversity
● Activities: Cultural storytelling, traditional music, cultural art
● Learning Objectives: Promote cultural awareness, develop social skills
Comparison and Review:
Criteria for Comparison:
1. Alignment with learning objectives
2. Variety and diversity of activities
3. Promotion of holistic development (cognitive, social-emotional, physical)
4. Cultural sensitivity and awareness
5. Effective use of resources and materials
18

Comparison Table:
Criteria Plan A Plan B
Alignment with objectives
Variety and diversity
Holistic development
Cultural sensitivity
Resource use
Review and Recommendations:
Based on the comparison, provide recommendations for improving each plan:
Plan A:
● Incorporate more cultural activities to promote diversity
● Add music and movement activities to enhance physical development
Plan B:
● Include outdoor play activities to promote cognitive development
● Incorporate sensory bin exploration to enhance fine motor skills
Assessment Criteria:
● Thoroughness of comparison and review
● Effectiveness of recommendations
● Alignment with learning objectives
● Cultural sensitivity and awareness
Evidence Requirements:
● Comparison table
● Review and recommendations document
● Revised programme plans incorporating feedback

Task 7 Prepare Routines
Objective: Prepare various routines to ensure smooth transitions and promote healthy habits in
children aged 2-5 years.
Simulation Scenario:
You are the lead educator at a childcare centre with 20 children aged 2-5 years.
Routines to Prepare:
1. Transition Times
2. Arrival and Departure
3. Meal Times
4. Toilet and Hand-Washing
5. Rest Routines
Transition Times Routine:
● Objective: Ensure smooth transitions between activities
● Activities:
o Sing songs during transitions
o Use visual timers
o Encourage children to clean up
● Materials: Visual timers, transition charts
Arrival and Departure Routine:
● Objective: Establish a welcoming and secure environment
● Activities:
o Greet children upon arrival
o Use name tags
o Ensure children are accounted for during departure
● Materials: Name tags, attendance sheet
21

Meal Times Routine:
● Objective: Promote healthy eating habits
● Activities:
o Encourage social interaction
o Model proper table manners
o Serve nutritious meals
● Materials: Meal schedules, table settings
Toilet and Hand-Washing Routine:
● Objective: Promote hygiene and independence
● Activities:
o Encourage hand-washing after using toilet
o Use visual reminders
o Assist children with toilet training
● Materials: Hand-washing charts, toilet training equipment
Rest Routines:
● Objective: Ensure children's physical and emotional well-being
● Activities:
o Create a calming environment
o Encourage relaxation techniques
o Monitor sleep patterns
● Materials: Sleeping mats, soothing music
Assessment Criteria:
● Effectiveness of routines in promoting smooth transitions and healthy habits
● Alignment with learning objectives
● Consideration of diverse needs and abilities
● Use of visual aids and materials
.

Evidence Requirements:
● Routine schedules and charts
● Photographs or videos of routines in action
● Observation records of children during routines
● Feedback from parents, colleagues, or children on routine effectiveness

Task 8 Task: Prepare Child-Initiated Activities
Objective: Prepare a variety of child-initiated activities that promote inclusivity, diversity, and holistic
development for children aged 3-5 years.
Activity Stations:
Prepare the following activity stations, ensuring each station reflects inclusivity and diversity:
1. Outdoor/Indoor Play
● Sensory bin with diverse textures and materials
● Cultural dress-up corner with traditional attire
● Accessible obstacle course
2. Creative Art
● Multicultural-themed coloring pages
● Various art materials (crayons, markers, paint)
● Adaptive tools for children with disabilities
3. Free Play
● Diverse doll collection
● Inclusive bookshelf with multicultural stories
● Sensory balls and playdough
4. Imaginative and Fantasy Play
● Puppet theater with diverse characters
● Dress-up clothes and props
● Cultural-themed playsets (e.g., African, Asian, Latin American)
5. Book Nook
● Multicultural and inclusive book collection
● Audio books for children with visual impairments
● Comfortable seating for various abilities
6. Educational Games
● Inclusive board games (e.g., braille, large print)
● Cultural-themed puzzles
● Math games with diverse number systems
24

7. Construction/Block Play
● Multicultural-themed blocks (e.g., African, Asian, Latin American)
● Adaptive building tools for children with disabilities
● Diverse materials for construction (e.g., wood, fabric, recycled materials)
Inclusivity and Diversity Considerations:
Ensure activities cater to:
● Different abilities (physical, cognitive, sensory)
● Cultural and linguistic diversity
● Socio-economic backgrounds
● Family structures and compositions
● Age and individual interests
Assessment Criteria:
● Variety and diversity of activities
● Inclusivity and diversity considerations
● Accessibility and adaptability
● Promotion of holistic development (cognitive, social-emotional, physical)
● Cultural sensitivity and awareness
Evidence Requirements:
● Photographs or videos of activity stations
● Description of each activity and its inclusivity features
● Observation records of children engaging with activities
● Feedback from children, parents, or colleagues on activity effectiveness
