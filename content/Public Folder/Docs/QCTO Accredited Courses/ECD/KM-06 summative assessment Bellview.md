1. Assessment Submission Guideline:
● You must submit your completed assessment via email. Ensure that your entire
assessment is scanned as complete document and sent to
tutor@bellview.co.za
● The file must be a high-quality scanned copy in PDF format.
● Photographs of practical assessments will not be accepted.
2. Before submitting your assessment , please make a copy for yourself for the event that
the original gets lost.
3. Do not attach the question paper to your answer sheet. Only send the answer sheet.
