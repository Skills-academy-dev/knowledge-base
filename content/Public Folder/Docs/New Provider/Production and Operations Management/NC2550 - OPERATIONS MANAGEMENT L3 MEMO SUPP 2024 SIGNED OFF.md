3.5 ● People only do good work because they want to, as it gives them a sense
of achievement.
● Threats and coercion can get the job done, but it will not be with
enthusiasm.
● Keep instructions, documents and explanations to a minimum.
● Explain to team members why their tasks are important.
● Show team members that you notice good work and extra effort.
● Show employees that you trust and rely on them.
● Try not to make employees feel over-supervised.
● Employees will feel stressed if they do not know exactly what to do.
