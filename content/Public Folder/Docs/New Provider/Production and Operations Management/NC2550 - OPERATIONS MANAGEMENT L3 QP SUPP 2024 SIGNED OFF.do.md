NICKY’S FASHION GEAR TAKES ON THE WORLD

Nicky  is  a  passionate  young  man in his late twenties and the founder and operations
manager of Nicky’s Fashion Gear (NFG) based in Johannesburg. Nicky was born and
bred in the far north of Limpopo.   He obtained his National Diploma in Dressmaking &
Fashion Design from CJC TVET in 2018. He chose his career because of his passion
for fashion and having been inspired by the giants in this industry.

After  completing  his  studies,  little  did  he  know  that  he  will  be  counted  among  the
unemployed  young  graduates.  He then realised that the best option for him would be
to  start  his  own  business  in  the  big city, focusing on African designs. He knew that it
was  not  going  to  be  easy  as  he  needed  money  to  buy  equipment  and  material  and
also a place to operate from.

Through  the  support  from  SEDA  and  NYDA,  Nicky  was  able  to  receive  not  only
funding  for  his  business  but  also  mentoring and coaching to manage and sustain his
business successfully. Nicky’s Fashion Gear is now trending as he keeps on receiving
orders  for  his  designs,  mostly  for weddings across Limpopo and Gauteng. He is also
giving  back  to  the  community  by  offering  experiential  training  to  students  who
completed their N6 at different TVET Colleges in Limpopo and Gauteng.

"My  main  highlight  was  when  I  was  asked  to  design  a  shirt  for  one  of  the  cabinet
ministers for a World Economic Conference in Germany.   To see the minister wearing
that shirt on national TV was quite fulfilling," Nicky said during an interview with a radio
station.

Nicky  believes  in  changing  the  fashion  industry  and  has  an  aspiration  and  desire  to
see local trends coming to the forefront in African fashion as a whole.

 [Source:  MM Thuputlela, 2020]
