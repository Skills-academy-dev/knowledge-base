1. Purpose of QA Review
This report provides a formal quality evaluation of the Operations Management Advance
textbook. It is based on the weighted scoring framework from the official Skills Academy
Textbook Quality Evaluation Checklist, with an emphasis on instructional design, academic
rigour, learner support, and accessibility .

2. Evaluation Method
The review applied the checklist’s weighted criteria to all sections except References &
Resources and Feedback & Reviews, as requested. The total score is normalized to a new
base of 270 points.
