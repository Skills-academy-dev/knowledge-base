Efficiency Starts with Understanding

Behind every product or service is a system of people, processes, and resources working in sync to deliver value. Operations Management ensures those elements run efficiently and reliably so organisations can meet customer needs and strategic goals.

This beginner-friendly guide to Production & Operations Management covers the essentials with clarity and depth. It’s ideal for students, early professionals, and curious practitioners who want to see how smart process design, capacity planning, and quality discipline translate into real business results.

In this textbook, you will engage with the:

Foundations of Operations & Production: The role operations plays in value creation, the flow from inputs to outputs, and key performance dimensions (cost, quality, speed, flexibility, dependability). Process & Service Design: Mapping workflows (from SIPOC to value stream maps), choosing layouts (cellular, line, functional), and designing service delivery for consistency and customer experience. Capacity, Demand & Scheduling: Right-sizing resources through capacity planning, balancing demand with forecasting basics, and using scheduling methods (Gantt, line balancing) to reduce bottlenecks. Inventory Management & Control: When and how much to order (EOQ, reorder points), ABC analysis, safety stock, and inventory accuracy practices that protect cash while preventing stockouts. Operations & the Supply Chain: Linking internal operations with suppliers and logistics; coordinating lead times, lot sizes, and information flows for smoother end-to- end performance. Quality & Continuous Improvement: Building-in quality with SPC, basic problem- solving (PDCA, 5-Why), and simple Lean tools (5S, standard work) to eliminate waste and variation.

By mastering these fundamentals, you’ll be able to read an operation, spot constraints, and propose changes that improve throughput, quality, and cost. You’ll leave with a toolkit - maps, measures, and methods - that supports better decisions, continuous improvement, and confident day-to-day management.
