Strategy, Systems, and Scale

Operations is no longer just about running the line. At an advanced level, it is how organisations translate strategy into performance: building resilient networks, orchestrating technology, and embedding quality at every step.

This textbook deepens your toolkit for leading complex operations. You will connect high-level choices to day-to-day execution, align metrics to strategy, and use data, design, and disciplined improvement to drive lasting advantage.

In this textbook, you will learn to:

Align Operations Strategy With Business Goals: Cascade strategic priorities into capacity, footprint, and make-versus-buy decisions. Balance cost, speed, flexibility, quality, and sustainability using coherent performance frameworks. Coordinate Global Supply Chains: Design sourcing strategies, evaluate logistics trade-offs, and manage risk through dual sourcing, safety stocks, and network optimisation. Integrate S&OP for end-to-end alignment. Apply TQM and Six Sigma for Breakthroughs: Use prevention-first quality systems, DMAIC problem solving, and statistical tools to reduce variation, improve yield, and institutionalise standard work. Leverage Operations Analytics: Build data models for forecasting, inventory optimisation, and capacity planning. Translate dashboards and KPIs into action using scenario analysis and constraint-focused thinking. Lead Innovation in Products and Services: Accelerate time to market with concurrent engineering, design for manufacturability and serviceability, and agile methods that connect R&D to scalable operations. Embed Ethics and Sustainability: Measure environmental and social impact, implement circular practices, and ensure supplier compliance and traceability without sacrificing performance.

By the end, you will be able to design operations that scale, adapt, and endure. You will connect strategy to processes and metrics, lead cross-functional initiatives with confidence, and deliver measurable improvements in cost, quality, speed, and sustainability.
