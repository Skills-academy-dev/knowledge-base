MODULE 1: INTRODUCTION TO OPERATIONS MANAGEMENT

LEARNING OBJECTIVES

By the end of this module, students will be able to:

1.  Define operations management and explain its importance in various sectors.

2.  Distinguish between operations in manufacturing and service environments.

3.  Describe the historical evolution and global context of operations management.

4.  Identify the key functions and decision areas of operations management.

5.  Understand the input–transformation–output model of operations.

6.  Analyse the characteristics of different types of operational processes.

7.  Explain the hierarchy of operations processes within an organisation.

8.  Discuss the role of the operations function in achieving organisational goals.

9.  Explore how operations management interacts with other business functions.

10. Identify the different roles and responsibilities of operations managers.

11. Interpret the significance of performance objectives (quality, speed, cost, etc.).

12. Evaluate the role of operations in creating competitive advantage.

MODULE 2: SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT

LEARNING OBJECTIVES

 By the end of this module, students will be able to:

1.  Define supply chain management and describe its key components.

2.  Explain the difference between supply chains and value chains.

3.  Identify the major supply chain drivers and performance metrics.

4.  Evaluate strategies for designing efficient and responsive supply chains.

5.  Describe the roles of procurement, logistics, and distribution in the supply chain.

6.  Analyse the impact of inventory, information, and transportation on supply chain

performance.

7.  Discuss global supply chain challenges including customs, distance, and cultural issues.

8.  Explain the concept of sustainability and green practices in supply chain management.

9.  Identify sources of risk in supply chains and apply mitigation strategies.

10. Examine different models of supplier relationships and collaboration.

11. Apply the SCOR (Supply Chain Operations Reference) model to real scenarios.

12. Explore the use of technology and information systems in managing the supply chain.

11. Identify legal, ethical, and sustainability considerations in product and service design.

12. Apply practical design strategies to real-world operations problems.

MODULE 4: PROCESS DESIGN, STRATEGY AND MANAGEMENT

Learning Objectives

 By the end of this module, students will be able to:

1.  Define what constitutes a process and explain the key elements of process design.

2.  Differentiate among the major types of process structures (project, batch, job,

continuous, etc.).

3.  Evaluate process design choices based on volume and variety requirements.

4.  Describe how to align process strategy with overall business strategy.

5.  Explain how to analyse and document processes using flowcharts and process maps.

6.  Identify and measure key process performance indicators (KPIs).

7.  Assess the role of capacity planning in process management.

8.  Analyse the causes of bottlenecks and propose solutions to improve flow.

9.  Apply tools such as SIPOC, VSM, and root cause analysis to diagnose process issues.

10. Evaluate trade-offs among flexibility, efficiency, and responsiveness in process design.

11. Discuss continuous improvement principles (e.g., PDCA, Kaizen) in managing

processes.

12. Recommend strategies for process reengineering and transformation.
