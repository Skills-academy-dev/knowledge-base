INSTRUCTIONS

These instructions apply to both Assignment 1 and Assignment 2. Read carefully before you

begin.

General Guidelines

1.  Use your own words when writing reflections or explanations. Do not copy answers

from ChatGPT or your textbook without understanding them.

2.  Answer all sections of the assignment. Each section carries marks and will help test a

different skill.

3.  Where a task asks you to use ChatGPT, open the tool, type your prompt (instruction),

and read the AI’s response.

4.

Take a screenshot of the ChatGPT responses. These must be included with your

answers for all tasks that say “Use ChatGPT” or “Paste a screenshot”.

5.  Write full sentences for explanations, reflections, and case study questions. One-word

or bullet point answers are not accepted unless stated.

6.

For Create-A-Prompt Tasks, make sure you test your prompt in ChatGPT, improve it,

and explain your changes clearly.

7.

For Reflection Questions, answer honestly. These questions are about your own

thinking, so there are no wrong answers—but you must explain your ideas clearly.

ASSIGNMENT 2

Question 1: Scenario-Based Short Answer Question (10 Marks)

Purpose: To test your understanding of how ChatGPT can assist with common customer

and internal team communication.

Scenario:

Sipho works in a small customer support team. He often gets overwhelmed by repeated

queries such as:

“Where is my order?” and “Can I change my delivery address?”

Sipho wants to use ChatGPT to save time while staying professional.

Instructions:

Explain how ChatGPT can assist Sipho with:

•

•

•

•

Drafting polite response templates for common queries (4 marks)

Translating responses into another language, like isiZulu, for customer inclusivity (2

marks)

Avoiding tone issues and vague language when replying to team members (2 marks)

Identifying any potential risks when using AI-generated messages (2 marks)

Question 2: Practical Tasks and Exercises (15 Marks)

Purpose: To assess your ability to use ChatGPT to perform customer and team support

tasks effectively.

Complete all three tasks.

Task A: Create a Response Template (5 Marks)

Use ChatGPT to create a short, polite reply to this customer question:

“Can I get an update on my refund?”

Include placeholders for name, product, and date. Then write the exact prompt you would

give ChatGPT.

Task B: Translate and Adjust Tone (5 Marks)

Translate this sentence into polite isiXhosa or isiZulu using ChatGPT:

“Your package has been delayed. We apologise for the inconvenience.”

Also write the ChatGPT prompt you would use to generate a translation in a polite tone.

Task C: Write a Team Communication Message (5 Marks)

Your team needs to know that the next team meeting will now be held on Friday at 15:00.

Write a professional team message using ChatGPT, and include the prompt you would give.

Question 3: Reflection Questions (10 Marks)

Purpose: To encourage critical thinking about how ChatGPT supports communication in

the workplace.

Answer all five reflection questions briefly (2 marks each):

1.  What made ChatGPT useful for replying to customers?

2.  How did placeholder templates save time?

3.  Why is tone important in customer communication?

4.  What was one benefit of using AI for translations?

5.  How would you improve your own prompts next time?

Question 4: Case Study Analysis (10 Marks)

Purpose: To test your ability to analyse a customer complaint and use ChatGPT to produce

a polite, effective response.

Mini Case Study: Delayed Parcel Complaint

A customer named Johan sends this angry message:

"You said the parcel would arrive two days ago. It’s still not here. This is unacceptable."

As a support agent, you use ChatGPT to create a professional response.

Instructions:

1.  What are two mistakes to avoid when replying to an angry message? (2 marks)

2.  Write the prompt you would give ChatGPT to draft a professional reply (3 marks)

3.  Write the improved response to Johan’s complaint (3 marks)

4.  How can you use ChatGPT to make future replies faster? (2 marks)

Question 5: Create-A-Prompt Task (5 Marks)

Purpose: To evaluate your skill in designing effective prompts for customer service tasks.

Write a prompt that tells ChatGPT to:

•

•

Create a set of polite, friendly replies for five common customer complaints

Ask for the output in table format, with columns for “Complaint”, “Suggested Reply”,

and “Tone Notes”
