INTRODUCTION

This module will help you understand the fundamentals of ChatGPT, a powerful tool for

learning and academic work in a distance learning environment. You’ll learn how to craft

effective prompts, leverage context, and ask meaningful follow-up questions to make the

most out of your ChatGPT interactions.

Refer to Page 9–21 of your textbook for more information.

Unit 1: What is ChatGPT?

In this unit, we will explore the basics of ChatGPT, its capabilities, and its role in supporting

distance learning. By understanding the underlying technology, you can utilize ChatGPT

more effectively for academic tasks.

Key Points:







ChatGPT is an AI language model designed to assist with generating text-based

responses.

It is used in distance learning to support writing, research, and problem-solving.

ChatGPT operates on large datasets and uses patterns in language to generate

responses.

Refer to Page 10–21 of your textbook for more information

Unit 2: The Anatomy of a Good Prompt

This unit focuses on how to create clear, concise, and effective prompts to ensure you get

the best responses from ChatGPT. Crafting a good prompt is key to working smarter.

Key Points:







A good prompt provides clear instructions, context, and purpose.

Prompts should be specific, to avoid vague or overly broad responses.

Examples of effective prompts include "Explain the causes of World War I" or

"Summarize the key points of this article."

Refer to Page 22–28 of your textbook for further details on prompt structure and examples.

Unit 3: Using Context to Improve Responses

Understanding and providing context helps ChatGPT generate more relevant and accurate

responses. In this unit, we’ll look at how to frame questions with appropriate context.

Key Points:







Context provides background information, narrowing down the scope for a more

targeted response.

Including key details, such as the subject, timeframe, or specific requirements,

enhances the quality of the answer.

Example: "Give me an overview of the Renaissance period in art, focusing on Italian

artists."

Refer to Page 29–32 of your textbook for more on using context effectively.

Unit 4: Asking Follow-Up Questions

This unit covers how follow-up questions can help you refine and deepen your

understanding of a topic. Building on initial responses allows for more detailed exploration.

Key Points:







Follow-up questions allow for clarification, expansion, or exploration of new areas

related to the original topic.

These questions ensure that you can refine the answer or focus on a particular

aspect.

Example: After asking for a summary of a novel, follow up with "What themes are

explored in the novel?"

Refer to Page 32–36 of your textbook for more information

Complex prompts are often required for deeper or more specific responses. This unit will

Unit 6: Comparing Good vs. Weak Prompts

In this unit, we will compare effective prompts with ineffective ones. Recognizing the

difference will help you understand why certain prompts produce better results.

Key Points:



Good prompts are specific, focused, and well-structured, leading to clear and relevant

answers.

  Weak prompts are vague or overly general, often leading to unsatisfactory or

off-target responses.



Example: A weak prompt might be "Tell me about history," whereas a good prompt

would be "Summarize the key events of the French Revolution."

responses may need improvement.

Key Points:









thinking.

effort. Templates allow you to reuse well-crafted prompts for common tasks, ensuring con-

tasks.

They save time by removing the need to create a new prompt for every similar task.

Templates help maintain consistency in the responses you receive from ChatGPT.

You can adapt templates for different scenarios, improving your productivity.
