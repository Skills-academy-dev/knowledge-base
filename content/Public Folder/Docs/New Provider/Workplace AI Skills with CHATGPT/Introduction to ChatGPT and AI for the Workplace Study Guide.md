INTRODUCTION

This module introduces you to the world of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and ChatGPT. You will

learn how AI is used in everyday life, what makes ChatGPT special, and how it works

behind the scenes. This module gives you the foundation to understand and use these

tools at school, work, or home.

Unit 1.1: Understanding Artificial Intelligence

This unit explains what Artificial Intelligence (AI) is and why it is important today. You will

learn about two main types of AI—narrow and general—and how AI is already being used

in your daily life, from mobile apps to banking systems. This unit explores how AI helps

people and businesses work smarter.

Read pages 10—16 of your textbook for more information on the following:

•

1.1.1 Definition of AI – Page 11

This section defines AI in simple terms. It explains that AI is a machine or system

designed to copy human thinking. You will learn what makes a system "intelligent"

and what AI can do, such as solve problems, understand language, and recognise

images.

•

1.1.2 Types of AI (Narrow, General) – Page 12

This part looks at the two main types of AI: narrow AI, which is used today for specific

tasks, and general AI, which is still in development. This section explains how each

type works and gives examples to help you understand the difference.

•

1.1.3 Examples of AI in Daily Life – Page 14

Here you will see practical examples of how AI is already around you—like online

translators, voice assistants, and shopping recommendations. This section helps you

recognise AI in apps, websites, and tools you may already use.

Unit 1.2: What is ChatGPT?

This unit focuses on ChatGPT, one of the most well-known AI tools today. You will learn

what ChatGPT is, how it was developed, and why it is used by millions of people around

the world. This unit also explains what ChatGPT can and cannot do, so you know how to

use it responsibly and effectively.

Read pages 17— 23 of your textbook for more information on the following:

•

1.2.1 ChatGPT as a Type of AI – Page 17

This section introduces ChatGPT as a language-based AI tool. It explains the

meaning of "Generative Pre-trained Transformer" and how ChatGPT uses learned

information to create replies that sound natural.

•

1.2.2 Who Created ChatGPT and Why – Page 20

Here you will learn about OpenAI—the company that created ChatGPT—and its

goals. This section explains why ChatGPT was built and how it is meant to help

people work, study, and communicate better.

•

1.2.3 What ChatGPT Can and Cannot Do – Page 21

This part lists what ChatGPT is good at, such as writing emails and explaining topics,

and also what its limits are, like giving outdated or incorrect facts. Understanding

these limits helps you use ChatGPT wisely.

Unit 1.3: How ChatGPT Works

This unit explains how ChatGPT creates its responses. It introduces the idea of language

models and shows how ChatGPT uses patterns in text to generate useful replies. This unit

helps you understand the importance of writing clear prompts to get better results.

Read pages 24—34 of your textbook for more information on the following:

•

1.3.1 How It “Talks” Using Patterns – Page 24

This section explains how ChatGPT doesn’t think like a person. Instead, it looks for

patterns in your message and predicts the best way to reply. The explanation is

simple and includes examples to make it easy to understand.

•

1.3.2 Language Models in Simple Terms – Page 25

Here, you will learn what a language model is. The section compares ChatGPT to a

super reader and writer that has studied how people use language, helping it

generate natural responses.

•

1.3.3 The Role of Prompts and Responses – Page 27

This part explores how your questions or instructions—called prompts—affect

ChatGPT’s answers. It shows how a well-written prompt helps you get better results

and includes tips for writing effective prompts.

INTRODUCTION

This module explores how Artificial Intelligence (AI) is used in different workplaces to

improve daily tasks. From office admin to education and marketing, AI tools help workers

save time, reduce mistakes, and increase productivity. This module covers practical

examples that show how AI supports real-life jobs in South Africa and beyond

Unit 2.1: AI in Administration

This unit explains how AI supports admin work such as scheduling, email writing, and

managing files. It shows how common tools like Gmail, Microsoft Word, and Google Forms

manage newsletters, student data, and patient records.

students revise and learn independently

INTRODUCTION

This module explains how to begin using ChatGPT. It is designed for first-time users and

shows you how to sign up, navigate the platform, and create useful prompts. Whether you

are using ChatGPT for work, study, or everyday tasks, this module gives you step-by-step

guidance to feel confident using the tool

Unit 3.1: Creating Your ChatGPT Account

This unit shows you how to set up a ChatGPT account. You’ll learn the difference between

free and paid options, how to log in, and how to change your basic settings like name and

language. This unit covers everything you need to start using ChatGPT on your phone or

computer

Read pages 76—82 of your textbook for more information on the following:

•

3.1.1 Signing Up (Free and Paid Options) – Page 77

This section explains how to sign up for ChatGPT using your email, Google account,

or Apple ID. It also explains the benefits of the free version and when you might want

to use the paid plan.

•

3.1.2 Logging In and Basic Settings – Page 80

Here, you’ll learn how to log in to ChatGPT and adjust basic settings, such as

choosing a display name, theme (light or dark mode), and language preferences.

Unit 3.2: Exploring the Interface

This unit covers the main parts of the ChatGPT screen. You’ll learn where to type your

questions, where to read the answers, and how to find saved chats. This unit helps you feel

comfortable using the layout and finding your way around.

Read pages 83—88  of your textbook for more information on the following:

•

3.2.1 Where to Type and Where to Read – Page 83

This section explains how to use the message box to type prompts and how to view

ChatGPT's responses in the centre window.

•

3.2.2 Understanding the Left-hand Menu – Page 86

Learn how to start new chats, view saved chats, and access custom instructions, help,

and settings.

Unit 3.3: ChatGPT Basics and Prompting

This unit introduces the idea of a “prompt”—the message you send to ChatGPT. You’ll

learn how to write effective prompts, guide the tone and format of responses, and get better

results from your questions.

Read pages 89—98 of your textbook for more information on the following:

•

3.3.1 What is a Prompt? – Page 89

This section defines a prompt and gives examples of questions, instructions, and

requests that ChatGPT can respond to.

•

3.3.2 Asking Questions Effectively – Page 91

You’ll learn how to be specific, give enough detail, and use formats (like bullet points

or paragraphs) to improve ChatGPT’s answers.

•

3.3.3 Using Examples, Tone, and Clarity – Page 96

This part shows how to shape responses by using examples and setting a tone (e.g.

polite, fun, or formal), so ChatGPT sounds the way you want.

Unit 3.4: Personalising ChatGPT for Your Work

This unit explores how ChatGPT can be tailored to your needs. You’ll learn to use it as a

writing assistant, set reminders, summarise long texts, and use custom instructions to get

better support for work or school tasks

Read pages 99—104 of your textbook for more information on the following:

•

3.4.1 Making it Your Writing or Admin Assistant – Page 99

Discover how to use ChatGPT to help draft emails, edit writing, and handle basic

admin tasks like planning and organising information.

•

3.4.2 Setting Reminders and Summaries – Page 100

Learn how to ask ChatGPT to summarise notes or create reminders and checklists for

your tasks.
