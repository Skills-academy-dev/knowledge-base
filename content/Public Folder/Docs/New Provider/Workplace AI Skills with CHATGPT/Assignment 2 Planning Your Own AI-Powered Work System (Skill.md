ASSIGNMENT 2

Question 1: Short Answer Questions (4 x 5 = 20 Marks)

Answer the following questions in 3–5 sentences each.

1.  Explain the importance of prompt templates in creating a reusable AI system.

(5 marks)

2.  What are the benefits of comparing free and paid AI tools when designing your

system? (5 marks)

3.  Describe two common friction points users might face when testing an AI-powered

workflow. (5 marks)

4.  Explain how collecting feedback can improve the performance of an AI-powered

system. (5 marks)

Question 2: Case Study (12 Marks)

Case Scenario:

A student named Naledi has built an AI-powered daily planner using ChatGPT, Grammarly,

and Notion AI. She notices that some prompts don’t always give useful results, and her AI

tools are not integrating smoothly when she switches between tasks. Naledi wants to test

and improve her system before using it for her upcoming exam preparation.

Answer the following questions based on the scenario:

1.

Identify two problems Naledi is experiencing with her current AI system. (2 marks)

2.  Suggest one way she could revise her prompts to improve the output. (2 marks)

3.  What testing method from Module 5 could Naledi use to measure her system’s

effectiveness? (3 marks)

4.  Describe how Naledi could gather useful feedback about her AI routine. (2 marks)

5.  What should she include in a simple improvement log? Name at least three details.

(3 marks)

Question 4: Practical Task (Hands-On – 10 Marks)

Title: Create a Prompt Template Guide

Design a 1-page Prompt Template Guide that someone else (a classmate, colleague, or

teacher) could use to complete a common task more easily with AI.

Your guide must include:

1.

Task title and purpose – What the prompt helps with (e.g., planning, writing,

designing, studying).

2.

3 example prompts – Written clearly, realistic, and ready to use.

3.

Tips for better results – At least 2 tips (e.g., be specific, mention tone, give a goal).

4.

Tool suggestion – Name one AI tool that works well for this type of prompt and why.

This should be designed as if you’re helping someone who is new to AI tools and needs

quick, simple help.

(Total: 50 Marks)
