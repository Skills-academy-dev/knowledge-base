INSTRUCTIONS

These instructions apply to both Assignment 1 and Assignment 2. Read carefully before you

begin.

General Guidelines

1.  Use your own words when writing reflections or explanations. Do not copy answers

from ChatGPT or your textbook without understanding them.

2.  Answer all sections of the assignment. Each section carries marks and will help test a

different skill.

3.  Where a task asks you to use ChatGPT, open the tool, type your prompt (instruction),

and read the AI’s response.

4.

Take a screenshot of the ChatGPT responses. These must be included with your

answers for all tasks that say “Use ChatGPT” or “Paste a screenshot”.

5.  Write full sentences for explanations, reflections, and case study questions. One-word

or bullet point answers are not accepted unless stated.

6.

For Create-A-Prompt Tasks, make sure you test your prompt in ChatGPT, improve it,

and explain your changes clearly.

7.

For Reflection Questions, answer honestly. These questions are about your own

thinking, so there are no wrong answers—but you must explain your ideas clearly.

ASSIGNMENT 1

Question 1: Scenario-Based Short Answer Question (10 Marks)

Purpose: To test your understanding of how ChatGPT can support time and task

management in the workplace.

Scenario:

You are an intern in the marketing department of a college. You are juggling your studies, a

part-time job, and your internship duties. You often forget due dates and feel overwhelmed

with your tasks.

Instructions:

Explain how ChatGPT can help you manage your workload more effectively. Your answer

must include:

•

•

•

Two examples of prompts you would use (4 marks)

Two reasons why ChatGPT would improve your time management (4 marks)

One risk to avoid when relying on AI for planning (2 marks)

Question 2: Practical Tasks and Exercises (15 Marks)

Purpose: To assess your ability to use ChatGPT to complete real-life admin and

communication tasks.

Complete all three tasks below.

Task A: Daily Planner (5 Marks)

Write a list of six tasks you need to do (real or imagined).

Then write a prompt you would give to ChatGPT to organise the tasks into a prioritised daily

plan.

Task B: Polite Email Rewrite (5 Marks)

Rewrite this informal message to make it professional:

"hi. i need you to check the thing before i send it. Thanks"

Write the prompt you would use to improve the tone and grammar.

Task C: Weekly Update Draft (5 Marks)

You are asked to write a team update. The team finished data entry, started checking files,

and needs help with formatting.

Write a short work update using ChatGPT, and include the prompt used.

Question 3: Reflection Questions (10 Marks)

Purpose: To reflect on how your understanding of prompt writing and AI tools has

improved.

Answer all five questions briefly (2 marks each).

1.  What type of tasks did ChatGPT help you with the most in Module 1?

2.  How did prompt writing affect the quality of ChatGPT’s responses?

3.  Why is it important to proofread AI-generated text before sending it?

4.  How did using ChatGPT improve the tone of your communication?

5.  What was the most surprising thing you learned about using ChatGPT for

communication?

Question 4: Case Study Analysis (10 Marks)

Purpose: To evaluate your ability to use ChatGPT to solve communication issues in real

workplace scenarios.

Mini Case Study: Thandi’s Email Problem

Thandi is a junior admin assistant. She sent an email that said, “You forgot to do the report.

Do it now.” Her manager said it sounded too rude and asked her to rewrite it using a polite

tone.

Instructions:

Answer the following:

1.  What was wrong with Thandi’s original message? (2 marks)

2.  Write the prompt she should have given ChatGPT (3 marks)

3.  Rewrite the message in a polite and professional way (3 marks)

4.  How can Thandi avoid this mistake in future messages? (2 marks)

Question 5: Create-A-Prompt Task (5 Marks)

Purpose: To test your ability to write clear and purposeful prompts for ChatGPT.

Write a clear and specific prompt to help you:

•

•

Summarise a long report about customer feedback

Include: audience, tone, and length
