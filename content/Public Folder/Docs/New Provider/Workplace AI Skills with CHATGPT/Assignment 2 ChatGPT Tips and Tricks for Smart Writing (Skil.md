ASSIGNMENT 2

Question 1: Giving Clear Instructions (10 marks)

1. You want ChatGPT to help you with a school project.

a.  What important information should you include to make sure ChatGPT understands

what you want? (5 marks)

b.  Why is it important to be clear and specific when asking for help? (3 marks)

c.  What could happen if you leave out important details? (2 marks)

Question 2: Thinking Before You Ask (10 marks)

1. Before using ChatGPT, it helps to first understand your own task.

a.  Write down three things you should think about before asking ChatGPT for help.

(3 marks)

b.  Explain how planning your question or task carefully can improve the result you get.

(4 marks)

c.  Describe a time when planning ahead helped you do better in class or with homework.

(3 marks)

Question 3: Fixing Mistakes and Trying Again (10 marks)

1. Sometimes ChatGPT’s answer doesn’t help as much as expected — and that’s okay!

a.  What should you do if you get an answer that’s not helpful? (3 marks)

b.  Why is it useful to change the way you ask or give more information? (3 marks)

c.  What does this experience teach you about learning and problem-solving with tools

like ChatGPT? (4 marks)

Question 4: Reusing Your Best Work (10 marks)

1. Once you find a good way of working, it’s smart to use it again.

a.  Give one example of something useful you’ve done with ChatGPT that you could

reuse for another subject.  (3 marks)

b.  Why is it helpful to save or reuse work that gave you a good result? (4 marks)

c.  How could you organize or keep track of your best ChatGPT conversations or tips?

(marks 3)

Question 5: Communicating Style and Purpose (10 marks)

1. You are writing a paragraph for your English class, but then you also need help writing a

speech for a school event.

a.  What’s different about how you would ask ChatGPT to help with each of these?

(4 marks)

b.  How would you explain the tone you want (e.g., formal, fun, inspiring)? (3 marks)

c.  Why is it important to match the language style to the audience and purpose?

(3 marks)
