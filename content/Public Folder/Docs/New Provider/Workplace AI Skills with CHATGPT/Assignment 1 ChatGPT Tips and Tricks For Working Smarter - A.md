ASSIGNMENT 1

Section A: Understanding ChatGPT and Writing Great Prompts

(25 marks)

Question 1: Understanding ChatGPT and Prompts

1.

In your own words, explain what ChatGPT is and how it works. Then explain what a

“prompt” is in ChatGPT.

2.

Finally, write one example of a prompt that you could use to help you with your

schoolwork. (5 marks)

Question 2: Creating and Testing Useful Prompts

1.  Create three different prompts that you can use in ChatGPT for the following

situations:

a) You want to write a short story about a superhero.

b) You need help solving a basic algebra problem, such as 2x + 5 = 15.

c) You are studying for a test and need help revising the water cycle.

2.

Type each of your prompts into ChatGPT and paste the responses you receive.

After each response, explain in 1–2 sentences why you think the prompt worked well

or how you could improve it. (9 marks)

Question 3: Prompt Testing and Reflection

Choose the best or most helpful prompt that you created in Question 2.

1.  Paste the ChatGPT response to that prompt. Then, write a short paragraph

(4–5 sentences) explaining how the ChatGPT response helped you. Mention what you

learned and if you would use the same prompt again in future. (6 marks)

Section B: Fixing and Improving ChatGPT Responses (25 marks)

Question 1: Improving a Weak Response

1.  Copy the following sentences into ChatGPT and ask it to improve it by saying:

“Can you improve this paragraph?”

“The internet is good. It helps people with stuff. Also, it can be bad. Many use it

a) Paste the improved version of the paragraph from ChatGPT.

b) In your own words, list and explain three improvements ChatGPT made to the

Question 2: Making a Vague Prompt Better

1.

The following prompt is too general and not helpful:

“Help me with my school work.”

a) Rewrite this prompt to make it more specific, clear, and useful.

b) Type your improved prompt into ChatGPT and paste the response you receive.

c) Explain in 2–3 sentences what makes your new prompt better than the original one.

(5 marks)

Question 3: Editing Your Own Writing with ChatGPT

a) Write a short paragraph (4–5 sentences) about any school subject of your choice.

For example, write about the importance of recycling or about a topic in History or

Natural Science.

b) Paste your paragraph into ChatGPT and ask it to improve it by saying:

“Please help me improve this paragraph and make it more formal or better writ-

ten.”

c) Paste the improved version from ChatGPT into your answer sheet.

d) In 2–3 sentences, explain how ChatGPT improved your writing. (6 marks)

Question 4: Asking for Career Advice the Right Way

You are interested in becoming a doctor in the future.

a) Write a prompt that you would use to ask ChatGPT for advice on how to become a

doctor in South Africa.

b) Type the prompt into ChatGPT and paste the response you receive.

c) In 3–4 sentences, explain how you can check if the advice ChatGPT gave is useful

and reliable. (4 marks)
