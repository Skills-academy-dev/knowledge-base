INSTRUCTIONS

These instructions apply to both Assignment 1 and Assignment 2. Read carefully before you

begin.

General Guidelines

1.  Use your own words when writing reflections or explanations. Do not copy answers

from ChatGPT or your textbook without understanding them.

2.  Answer all sections of the assignment. Each section carries marks and will help test a

different skill.

3.  Where a task asks you to use ChatGPT, open the tool, type your prompt (instruction),

and read the AI’s response.

4.

Take a screenshot of the ChatGPT responses. These must be included with your

answers for all tasks that say “Use ChatGPT” or “Paste a screenshot”.

5.  Write full sentences for explanations, reflections, and case study questions. One-word

or bullet point answers are not accepted unless stated.

6.

For Create-A-Prompt Tasks, make sure you test your prompt in ChatGPT, improve it,

and explain your changes clearly.

7.

For Reflection Questions, answer honestly. These questions are about your own

thinking, so there are no wrong answers—but you must explain your ideas clearly.

Submitting Your Assignment

•

•

Type or write your answers clearly.

Include your name, student number, and date on the front page.

Paste your prompts and responses directly under each question.

Attach your screenshots after each task or at the end of the document.

•  Make sure your final file or document is neat, complete, and labelled.

•

Submit it on time using the method your tutor or course platform has provided.

Purpose: To check your problem-solving skills in a real-world task.

Task:

You are working in the HR department of a small business. A manager asks you to create a

job advert for a new marketing assistant using ChatGPT.

Question 4: Case Study Analysis (10 Marks)

Purpose: To check your understanding through detailed application.

Case Study:

A small construction company started using ChatGPT to handle client emails. At first, it

worked well and saved time. But one day, a customer received incorrect project dates,

which caused confusion and delayed a payment.

Questions:

1.  What are two benefits the company experienced by using ChatGPT? (2 marks)

2.  What problem occurred? (2 marks)

3.  How could the company reduce the risk of future mistakes? (3 marks)

4.  Would you recommend they continue using ChatGPT? Explain. (3 marks)

Question 5: Create-A-Prompt Task (5 Marks)

Purpose: To test your skill in writing effective prompts and improving results.

Task:

Create a prompt that asks ChatGPT to generate a monthly newsletter for a retail company.

Test the prompt, review the output, and revise it to make it better.

Include:

•

•

•

•

Your original prompt (1 mark)

ChatGPT’s response (1 mark)

Revised prompt and improved response (2 marks)

A short explanation of what you changed and why (1 mark)
