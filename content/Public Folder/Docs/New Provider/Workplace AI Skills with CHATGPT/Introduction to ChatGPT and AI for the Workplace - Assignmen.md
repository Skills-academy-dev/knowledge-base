INSTRUCTIONS

These instructions apply to both Assignment 1 and Assignment 2. Read carefully before you

begin.

General Guidelines

1.  Use your own words when writing reflections or explanations. Do not copy answers

from ChatGPT or your textbook without understanding them.

2.  Answer all sections of the assignment. Each section carries marks and will help test a

different skill.

3.  Where a task asks you to use ChatGPT, open the tool, type your prompt (instruction),

and read the AI’s response.

4.

Take a screenshot of the ChatGPT responses. These must be included with your

answers for all tasks that say “Use ChatGPT” or “Paste a screenshot”.

5.  Write full sentences for explanations, reflections, and case study questions. One-word

or bullet point answers are not accepted unless stated.

6.

For Create-A-Prompt Tasks, make sure you test your prompt in ChatGPT, improve it,

and explain your changes clearly.

7.

For Reflection Questions, answer honestly. These questions are about your own

thinking, so there are no wrong answers—but you must explain your ideas clearly.

Submitting Your Assignment

•

•

•

Type or write your answers clearly.

Include your name, student number, and date on the front page.

Paste your prompts and responses directly under each question.

Attach your screenshots after each task or at the end of the document.

•  Make sure your final file or document is neat, complete, and labelled.

•

Submit it on time using the method your tutor or course platform has provided.

Question 1: Scenario-Based Short Answer Question (10 Marks)

Purpose: To test your understanding of getting started with ChatGPT.

Task:

Your cousin wants to use ChatGPT but has never used an AI tool before. They need help

with the whole process—from creating an account to starting a new chat.

Instructions:

•

•

•

•

Use ChatGPT to explain the full process in plain English.

Paste the prompt you used.

Explain whether the answer was suitable for someone with no technical background.

Suggest how the explanation could be made even easier.

Question 4: Case Study Analysis (10 Marks)

Purpose: To check your understanding through detailed application.

Case Study:

A student used ChatGPT to help with writing a history project. The tool helped them

structure their paragraphs, suggest headings, and explain key terms. But they copied full

answers without editing or checking them, which caused their teacher to issue a plagiarism

warning.

Questions:

1.  What were the positive ways the student used ChatGPT? (2 marks)

2.  What mistake did they make? (2 marks)

3.  What could they do differently next time to avoid problems? (3 marks)

4.  Should students be allowed to use ChatGPT in class? Explain your opinion. (3 marks)

Question 5: Create-A-Prompt Task (5 Marks)

Purpose: To develop your skills in writing and refining AI prompts.

Task:

Write a prompt asking ChatGPT to generate tips for preparing for an online exam. Then test

the prompt, check the result, and improve it for better clarity.

Instructions:

•  Write your original prompt (1 mark)

•

•

•

•

Paste ChatGPT’s original output (1 mark)

Revise the prompt to improve the result (1 mark)

Paste the new response (1 mark)

Reflect briefly on what you changed and why (1 mark)
