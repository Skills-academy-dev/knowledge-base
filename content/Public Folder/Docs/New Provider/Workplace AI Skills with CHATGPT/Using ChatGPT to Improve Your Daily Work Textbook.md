Best Practices for Ensuring AI Accuracy

To use ChatGPT and other AI tools effectively, it's important to understand that while they

can generate useful content quickly and easily, they are not always accurate or reliable.

These tools are designed to assist you, not replace your judgment. AI-generated responses

can sometimes include outdated information, misunderstand your request, or present

incorrect facts with confidence.

Because of this, you should always approach AI-generated content with a critical eye. Don’t

assume that everything it produces is correct or suitable for your needs—especially when it

24

comes to academic work, professional communication, or decision-making in areas like

health, law, or finance.

By following a few key best practices, you can make sure that the information you receive is

not only helpful but also safe, accurate, and appropriate. Taking time to verify facts, check

tone, and apply your own judgment will ensure you get the most out of ChatGPT while

avoiding common pitfalls.

1\. Always Fact-check Critical Information

AI tools can sometimes produce responses that sound correct but are wrong or misleading.

This is especially risky in areas like medical, legal, or financial advice. Always double-check

these facts with trusted sources or professionals before using them.

2\. Cross-Reference with Trusted Sources

Think of AI as a helpful starting point not the final answer. Compare what it tells you with

information from reliable websites, textbooks, or experts in the field. This helps confirm

whether the AI’s output is correct and complete.

3\. Watch Out for AI "Hallucinations"

AI sometimes makes up information confidently, such as quoting fake statistics, inventing

sources, or creating false facts. These errors are called “hallucinations.” If something seems

unusual or too good to be true, ask for a source or rephrase your question.

4\. Don’t Rely on AI Alone

Use ChatGPT to support your work, not to do all the thinking for you. Your judgment,

experience, and common sense are still essential. AI is best used to generate ideas,

simplify information, or organize your thoughts—not replace your decisions.

5\. Clarify and Iterate Your Prompt

If the response from ChatGPT feels vague, confusing, or slightly off-topic, don’t just

accept it as it is. Remember, ChatGPT isn’t perfect—it works best when you guide it

clearly. If something doesn’t sound quite right, you have the power to improve the

result by asking follow-up questions or refining your prompt.

25

Here are three effective ways to get better results:

1\. Ask for Step-by-Step Explanations

If the answer feels rushed or incomplete, ask ChatGPT to break it down into smaller steps.

This is especially useful when you're trying to understand a process, solve a problem, or

learn something new.

● Example: “Can you explain this in steps?” or “Break this down for a beginner.”

2\. Request a Clearer or Simpler Version

If the language is too technical, wordy, or confusing, ask for a simpler rewrite. ChatGPT can

rephrase content in plain English, making it easier to understand—especially helpful when

you're working on complex topics.

● Example: “Can you explain this more clearly?” or “Make this easier to

understand.”

3\. Add More Detail to Your Original Prompt

Sometimes, ChatGPT gives a weak or generic answer simply because the prompt didn’t

include enough information. Try adding more context about what you’re doing, who the

audience is, or what format you need.

● Example: “This is for a Grade 10 project,” or “Write this as a formal email to my

teacher.”

Benefits:

1\. Time Savings

automate this process by rapidly analyzing and extracting key points from meeting

2\. Consistency

Human-generated summaries can vary in detail and focus, leading to inconsistencies across

each summary adheres to a uniform format and captures essential information consistently.

This uniformity enhances clarity and facilitates easier comprehension and comparison of

documents over time.

3\. Accessibility

Lengthy meeting notes can be overwhelming and challenging to navigate, especially for

extensive information into digestible formats, highlighting key decisions, action items, and

Limitations:

While ChatGPT can be a powerful tool for turning lengthy notes or discussions into concise

summaries, it’s important to understand its limitations. Knowing these drawbacks helps you

use the tool more responsibly and ensures the accuracy and safety of the information you're

working with.

1\. Accuracy Dependence

The effectiveness of a summary created by ChatGPT is directly tied to the quality of the

original input. If the notes you provide are incomplete, unclear, or contain errors, the

summary will reflect those flaws. In other words, poor input leads to poor output. ChatGPT

can’t fact-check your notes it simply builds on what it receives. That means if key information

is missing or incorrect in your source, the final summary may be misleading or inaccurate.

Always review and organize your notes before asking ChatGPT to summarize them. Clearer input leads to clearer results.

2\. Lack of Contextual Understanding

Unlike a human, ChatGPT doesn’t truly understand conversations or events. It processes

text based on patterns in language, not on real-world experience. Because of this, it may:

● Miss subtle meanings or emotional cues

● Overlook non-verbal context from meetings or discussions

● Misinterpret sarcasm, humor, or cultural nuances

This can lead to summaries that feel “off,” leave out important details, or fail to capture the

full tone or intention of what was discussed.

Remember: AI can help you summarize, but it’s still up to you to add or adjust key details that may have been missed.
