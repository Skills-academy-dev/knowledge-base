INTRODUCTION

Staying organised and managing your time well is important for any job. In this module, you

will learn how to use ChatGPT to help you plan your day, check your writing, and create

clear reports. This module explains how ChatGPT can support you in everyday admin

tasks, making your work more efficient and professional.

Unit 1.1: Planning Schedules and Daily Tasks

Planning your day helps you complete your work on time and avoid missing deadlines. This

unit covers how ChatGPT can create daily, weekly, or monthly plans, turn your to-do list

into an organised schedule, and help you prioritise tasks using AI.

Read pages 10—23 of your textbook for more information on the following:

•

1.1.1 Asking ChatGPT to Make a Daily, Weekly, or Monthly Schedule - Page 10

This section explains how to ask ChatGPT to create a schedule based on your tasks.

You will learn how to give clear prompts so ChatGPT can generate useful and realistic

plans.

•

1.1.2 Turning a List of Tasks Into a To-Do List or Planner - Page 15

This part covers how ChatGPT can take a simple list of tasks and turn it into a step-by

-step planner. You will learn how to sort tasks by time, importance, or

category.

•

1.1.3 Managing Priorities and Due Dates Using AI - Page 18

This sub-unit explores how ChatGPT can help you decide which tasks to do first. You

will learn how to use prompts to manage deadlines, plan around urgent tasks, and

stay focused.

Unit 1.2: Document Checking and Editing

Well-written documents show professionalism. This unit explains how ChatGPT can help

you proofread your writing, correct grammar and spelling mistakes, and improve the tone

and structure of letters and reports.

Read pages 24—31 of your textbook for more information on the following:

•

1.2.1 Using ChatGPT to Proofread and Correct Spelling and Grammar - Page 24

This part covers how ChatGPT can check your writing for spelling and grammar

errors. You will learn how to use prompts to get feedback and corrections quickly.

1.2.3 Making Sure Documents are Complete and Professional - Page 29

This sub-unit explores how ChatGPT can help you review your writing for

Unit 1.3: Writing and Summarising Reports

Writing reports can take time, but ChatGPT can make it easier. This unit explains how

ChatGPT can help you summarise long texts, write meeting notes, and turn short points

This module teaches you how to use ChatGPT to organise your tasks and improve your

admin work. You learn how to create schedules, make to-do lists, prioritise your tasks, and

manage deadlines. The module also shows you how to check your writing for errors,

improve the tone of your documents, and write clear summaries and reports.

INTRODUCTION

Clear communication helps teams work better together. This module explores how

ChatGPT can support your workplace communication. You will learn how to write emails,

give updates, and answer questions professionally and politely. These skills will help you

become a better communicator in any job.

Unit 2.1: Writing Emails and Work Messages

Emails and messages are used for many daily work tasks. This unit explains how ChatGPT

can help you write better emails, choose the right tone, and format your writing correctly.

You will learn how to avoid common mistakes and communicate more clearly.

2.1.3 Formatting Emails and Avoiding Mistakes—Page 50

can help you write short, clear messages about your progress or any changes. You will

learn how to make your updates useful for both team members and managers.

•

2.2.1 Writing Daily and Weekly Team Updates - Page 54

This sub-unit covers how to use ChatGPT to write updates about tasks, projects, or

goals. You will learn how to keep these messages simple and informative.

INTRODUCTION

In most workplaces, you will need to answer questions and provide support to both

customers and colleagues. This module explains how ChatGPT can help you reply faster

and more clearly. You will learn how to create reply templates, write FAQs, and improve the

clarity of messages for different audiences.

Unit 3.1: Creating Reply Templates

Reply templates save time when answering similar questions again and again. This unit

covers how to use ChatGPT to write templates that are clear, helpful, and professional. You

will learn how to make reusable messages that work for both customers and

colleagues.

Read pages 76—83 of your textbook for more information on the following:

•

3.1.1 What Are Reply Templates and Why Use Them? - Page 76

This section explains the purpose of reply templates and how they can help you work

workplace situations.

3.1.2 Making Reusable Templates for Emails and Messages - Page 79

This sub-unit covers how to use ChatGPT to write templates for different types of

•

3.1.3 Saving Time While Staying Professional - Page 81

This part explores how reply templates created with ChatGPT can help you save time

without losing professionalism. You will learn how to keep your tone friendly, clear,

and polite.

Unit 3.2: Writing FAQs and Help Messages

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) are a helpful way to support others without repeating

answers. This unit explains how ChatGPT can help you write clear FAQs and helpful

instructions. You will learn to organise your answers so they are easy to read and

understand.
