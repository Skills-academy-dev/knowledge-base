ASSIGNMENT 1

Question 1: Short Answer Question (10 marks)

a) In your own words, define what an AI-powered workflow is. (3 marks)

b) Explain the difference between automation and AI using one example from

your life or studies. (4 marks)

c) List three benefits of using AI tools in everyday work or study. (3 marks)

Question 2: Case Study (10 marks)

Scenario:

Lerato is a small business owner who struggles to keep up with administrative

tasks. She often forgets to reply to emails, has trouble summarizing meeting

notes, and finds content writing for her website time-consuming. A colleague

recommended she explore AI tools to lighten her workload.

Using your knowledge answer the following:

a) Identify three tasks from Lerato’s workflow that are suitable for AI support.

(3 marks)

b) Suggest a specific AI tool for each task and explain how it would help.

(6 marks)

c) What one piece of advice would you give Lerato before she starts using AI?

(1 mark)

Question 3: Reflection Question (5 marks)

Answer the following in paragraph form:

a) Identify one area in your current workflow where you often lose time or feel

stuck. Explain why this happens. (2 marks)

b) Reflect on how using an AI tool could help solve or reduce this issue.

Include the name of the tool if possible. (3 marks)
