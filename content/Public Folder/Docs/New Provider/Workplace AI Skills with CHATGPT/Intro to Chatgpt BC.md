Unlock the Power of AI & ChatGPT in Your Workplace

Step confidently into the age of AI with this clear, practical introduction to ChatGPT and Artificial Intelligence at work. This textbook helps learners of all backgrounds understand what AI is, how it works behind the scenes, and where it fits in day-to-day tasks and longer-term projects.

Moving from core ideas to hands-on use, you’ll build the skills to apply ChatGPT effectively, streamlining routine work, improving quality, and turning information into action.

What you’ll learn inside:

AI Fundamentals for Real Workplaces: Key terms (LLMs, training data, inference), strengths and limitations, and how AI is reshaping roles, collaboration, and productivity. How ChatGPT Works and Why it Matters: The basics of conversational models, what they’re good at, where they can err, and how to verify outputs for accuracy and reliability. AI in Action Across Functions: Practical examples in administration (drafts, summaries, minutes), education (lesson aids, rubrics), marketing (campaign ideas, variations), and more. Getting Started Step-by-Step: Create an account, navigate the interface, manage settings, and understand key features like chat history, attachments, and workspace organisation. Prompting that Delivers Better Results: Craft role-based prompts, add constraints and examples, iterate systematically, and use checklists to reduce errors and bias. Personalising ChatGPT for Your Role: Build lightweight workflows, reusable prompts, and simple guardrails for your context so outputs match your tone, brand, and compliance needs.

With concise checklists, mini projects, and real-world scenarios, this guide gives you practical confidence and not just theory. You’ll finish with a starter library of prompts, a simple verification routine, and a clear plan to use AI safely and effectively in your everyday work.
