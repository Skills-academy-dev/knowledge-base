1.1.1 Asking ChatGPT to Make a Daily, Weekly, or Monthly
Schedule
Creating a schedule helps you plan your time and stay organised. ChatGPT can help you
create daily, weekly, or monthly schedules by dividing your tasks into time blocks.
What is a Schedule?
A schedule is a time-based plan that helps you organise when specific tasks will be done. It is
often used to ensure that:
• Work is completed on time
• Time is used effectively
• Stress is reduced
How ChatGPT Helps with Scheduling
ChatGPT can quickly generate a schedule based on your working hours, task list, and
preferences. It can break your day into time blocks, add breaks, and even help you spread your
tasks over a few days.
Did You Know?
People who plan their days are more likely to meet their goals and feel less stressed.

1.2.2 Improving Tone and Clarity in Letters or Reports
Tone is how your message sounds, and clarity is how easy it is to understand. This section
explains how ChatGPT can help you rewrite your work in a more polite, professional, and
clearer way.
Why Tone and Clarity Matter
In the workplace, your message must be respectful, polite, and easy to understand. The tone is
how your writing “sounds,” and clarity means how clearly you express your ideas.
Poor tone may sound rude or too casual. Poor clarity makes your writing confusing or unclear.
Tone Examples Table
Original Message ChatGPT Improved Tone
“You didn’t send the file.” “It seems I haven’t received the file yet. Could you please check?”
“I want this done now.” “Please could you complete this as soon as possible?”
“Where is the report?” “May I kindly ask for an update on the report?”
Tip: Ask ChatGPT to “Make this message more polite and formal.”
Real-Life Example: Fixing a Rude Tone
Prompt:
Rewrite this in a polite and professional tone: ‘Why haven’t you replied to my message?’”
Improving Clarity
Sometimes your writing is too wordy or unclear. ChatGPT can help simplify the message.

progress, results, or actions. In this unit, you will learn how to use ChatGPT to summarise long
texts, write executive summaries, and expand short points into full paragraphs.
Learning Outcomes
At the end of this unit, you should be able to:
• Summarise long documents, meeting notes, or reports using ChatGPT
• Create clear executive summaries and structured report sections
• Expand bullet points into full, well-structured paragraphs
1.3.1 Asking ChatGPT to Summarise Long Texts or Reports
Summarising is useful when you need to explain long texts in a few words. This section shows
how to use ChatGPT to shorten reports, notes, or emails while keeping the most important
information.
What is a Summary?
A summary gives a brief version of a longer text. It highlights:
• The main points
• Important facts
• Key outcomes or results
It should remove small details that are not needed.
Real-Life Example:
Original Text:
The team meeting took place on Friday morning. The agenda included project deadlines,
budget concerns, and new client requests. The manager provided feedback on current
progress, and team members were asked to share weekly updates moving forward.

1.3.2 Creating Meeting Notes or Executive Summaries
Executive summaries and meeting notes give a quick overview of a longer report or discussion.
This section explains how ChatGPT can help you write summaries that are short, clear, and
professional.
What is an Executive Summary?
An executive summary is a brief overview placed at the start of a report. It helps readers
understand the purpose, key points, and findings of the report without reading everything.
Example Prompt and Output:
Prompt:
Write a short executive summary for this report: Sales increased by 12%, two new staff joined,
and the customer help desk received fewer complaints.

You may be given short bullet points and asked to write them as a full paragraph. In this

sentences.
Why Use Full Paragraphs?
Paragraphs help you:
• Organise ideas clearly
• Present information formally
• Improve flow and readability
