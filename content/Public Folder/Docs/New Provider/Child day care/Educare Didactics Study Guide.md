Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, learners should be able to:

  Understand the significance of a well-structured nursery layout in both home-based

and day care settings.



Identify the general requirements essential for setting up an effective and safe baby

room.

  Recognise the impact of color choices on infants and the importance of appropriate

acoustics for a baby-friendly environment.



Examine the role of textures, ventilation, temperature control, and lighting in creating a

conducive atmosphere for infants.



Evaluate the importance of choosing suitable doors, wall coverings, curtains, flooring,

display spaces, and storage solutions tailored for a nursery.

  Develop insights into the optimal layout of a baby playroom, focusing on essential

areas such as sleeping, eating, and bathing zones.

  Understand the essentials of baby transportation within a facility and the significance

of carriers and travel gear.



Assess the unique needs of babies in centre-based nursery settings and learn the

crucial points to consider when designing a baby room in these environments.

  Comprehend the importance of designated activity areas, with a focus on indoor play

spaces, routine areas, and the requirements for setting up outdoor playgrounds.

  Recognise the needs and roles of staff and caregivers in a nursery setting,

emphasising the importance of a conducive work environment and the responsibilities

associated with ensuring the nursery's safety and functionality.

INTRODUCTION

This module focuses on the general requirements for a baby room and the layout of the

playroom. It also looks at the physical layout of a centre-based baby section, the needs of

the baby and the activity areas for the baby.

This unit assist you in understanding the physical layout of a centre-baby section (nursery)

Chapter Summary

Module 1 explored the setup of nursery sections in both home-based and daycare

environments, focusing on the design aspects such as color, acoustics, ventilation, and

lighting. It discussed the arrangement of furniture, storage solutions, and the layout of play

and routine areas, including sleeping, eating, and bathing zones. The module also

addressed the layout of center-based nurseries, emphasising the importance of activity

spaces and outdoor playgrounds, and concluded by highlighting the roles and needs of staff

and caregivers in maintaining a safe and functional nursery environment.

concluding with an introduction to the components of a Lesson Plan Form for younger

toddlers, providing an example for clarity.

Chapter Summary

an active tool to understand each child's unique strengths and developmental needs. It

learning environment.
