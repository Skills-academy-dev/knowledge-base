Participants’ Workbook Customer Service Excellence
GOAL
Participants will understand the importance of Customer Service Excellence
(CSE) and will be equipped to develop and implement a CSE plan in their
respective organizations.
OBJECTIVES
By the end of the workshop, participants will be able to:
(cid:1) Describe the impact of CSE
(cid:1) Identify the extent to which they need to develop their Customer-focus
abilities
(cid:1) Promote a culture based on CSE
(cid:1) Explain why most companies lose their customers
(cid:1) Demonstrate a Positive Mental Attitude while implementing CSE
(cid:1) Develop long-term Service Excellence strategies
(cid:1) Focus on Customer Service Values
(cid:1) Generate a 360-degree view of the customer
NORMS
We need to:
(cid:1) Keep the spirit of participatory training alive
(cid:1) Derive something positive from all video presentations and exercises
(cid:1) Actively participate in all the exercises
(cid:1) Share our experiences and learn from each other
(cid:1) Encourage everyone to participate
(cid:1) Keep discussions on track
(cid:1) Work as a team
DIMENSIONS OF THE WORKSHOP
1. There is no Customer Service Excellence (CSE) without Employee Service
Excellence (ESE)
2. Successful implementation of CSE requires the commitment and support
of the Top Management, as CSE is the responsibility of everyone in the
organization
3. Customer Service Excellence (CSE) can be compared to how a dedicated
medical professional takes care of his patients

WHAT IS CUSTOMER SERVICE EXCELLENCE?
It is:
(cid:1) Our Number one priority
(cid:1) Making customers feel important and special
(cid:1) Being proud of our job
(cid:1) More customers for our company
(cid:1) Extending courtesy to customers
(cid:1) Maintaining a professional attitude
(cid:1) Having ample product / service knowledge
(cid:1) Identifying, meeting and exceeding customer expectations
(cid:1) Being totally committed to the customer and company
IMPACT OF CUSTOMER SERVICE EXCELLENCE
1. Great Service
2. Delighted Customers
3. Positive word-of-mouth
4. Excellent reputation
5. Better customer retention
6. Loyal customers
7. More repeat and new business
8. Higher profits
9. Higher employee income
10. Increased employee morale and enthusiasm

WHAT IS CUSTOMER SERVICE EXCELLENCE?
It is:
• Our Number ONE priority
• Making customers feel important
and special
• Being proud of our job
• More customers for our company
• Extending courtesy to customers
• Maintaining a professional attitude
• Having ample product / service
knowledge
• Identifying, meeting and exceeding
customer expectations
• Being totally committed to the
customer and company

• Are not dependent on us. We are
