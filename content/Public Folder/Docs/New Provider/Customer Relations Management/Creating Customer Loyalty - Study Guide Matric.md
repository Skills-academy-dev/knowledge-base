Unit 3.1: Understanding Your Customers

A problem in customer service is any issue that negatively impacts the customer's experi-

ence. Problems can range from minor inconveniences to significant service failures. They

can arise due to various reasons, including misunderstandings, service errors, unmet ex-

pectations, or external factors.

pects and what is delivered.



Service Errors: Mistakes made during service delivery, such as incorrect orders or bill-

ing errors.



Unmet Expectations: When the service provided does not meet the customer's expec-

tations.

3.1.2 Common Causes of Customer Service Problems



Lack of Communication: Poor communication can lead to misunderstandings and er-

rors.



Inadequate Training: Staff who are not adequately trained may not perform to the ex-

pected standards.



System Failures: Technical issues or system failures can disrupt service delivery.
