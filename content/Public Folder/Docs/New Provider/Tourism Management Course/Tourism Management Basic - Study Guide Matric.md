MODULE 1

INTRODUCTION TO TOURISM

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

  Define and explain the concept of tourism.

  Recognise the importance of tourism in various contexts.

  Discuss the key features and characteristics of tourism businesses.

Identify and address common issues with tourism data.

  Analyse and debate different definitions of what constitutes tourism.

Identify major international tourism flow patterns.

  Understand and illustrate the tourism system model.

  Explain how tourism integrates multiple disciplines.

  Describe the structure of the South African tourism industry.

INTRODUCTION

This module covers the basics of tourism, including who tourists are, the importance of the

tourism industry, and how we track and measure tourism activities. You'll also learn about

the challenges in managing tourist data and the broader impacts of tourism.

Each section of this module is designed to give you a clear understanding of the

fundamental aspects of tourism. You'll start by defining key terms and concepts, and then

explore how tourism affects economies and cultures globally.

By the end of this module, you'll have a strong foundation in tourism principles, ready to

dive deeper into more complex topics in future modules. Prepare to explore the exciting

and dynamic field of tourism management!

Unit 1.1: Defining Tourists

Explore what constitutes a tourist, focusing on various types of travellers and the criteria

that distinguish tourists from other types of travellers. This unit delves into the fundamental

definitions that form the basis of tourism studies.

1.1.1 Categories of Tourists

1.1.2 Understanding Tourism

Understand the major economic, social, and environmental impacts of tourism globally.

This unit highlights why tourism is a vital sector, contributing significantly to economies

worldwide.

Read pages 15—16 of your textbook for  more information.

This unit discusses the reasons and benefits of studying tourism management, focusing on

Module Summary

This module offers a comprehensive overview of tourism, detailing types like leisure,

cultural, and business tourism and their economic and cultural significance. It addresses

the challenges in gathering accurate tourism data, crucial for effective management and

planning. The module also explores the tourism system model, explaining the

interconnected roles of tourist-generating regions, transit routes, and destination regions.

By the end, students will understand tourism's fundamental concepts, its impact on society

and the economy, and the importance of strategic management for sustainable growth.

INTRODUCTION

This module explores the link between tourism and geography, focusing on how natural

and man-made features influence where people travel and what they do there. We start by

looking at core tourism resources like natural wonders, cultural heritage sites, and

entertainment options that attract visitors to a location.

We then examine South Africa's diverse attractions, analyzing key tourist spots to

understand their geographic distribution and impact on tourism. We'll see how South

Africa's unique geography makes it a popular destination, with examples of specific

attractions and their roles in the tourism industry.

Throughout this module, you'll learn how geographic factors affect tourism both globally

and in South Africa. By the end, you should be able to explain why certain places are

popular tourist destinations and how geography and tourism management are

interconnected. This introduction prepares you for a deeper study of how tourism and

geography interact.

INTRODUCTION

This module focuses on the importance of visitor attractions in South Africa's tourism

sector. We will discuss how these attractions not only draw both local and international

visitors but also significantly impact the tourism industry and local communities. Throughout

the module, we'll refer to them as 'visitor attractions' to highlight their importance to all

types of visitors, including local residents. These attractions are often the main reason

people visit a destination and are vital for the industry's sustainability. By the end of this

module, you'll understand the role of visitor attractions, how to manage them through their

lifecycle, and the challenges they face now and in the future. This knowledge will help you

manage these attractions effectively, ensuring their ongoing success and positive impact

on South Africa's tourism. Read more on pages 64 - 68 of your  textnook.

strategies for managing them effectively. We discussed how these attractions,

encompassing natural, built, cultural, and social components, are fundamental reasons for

travel and significantly contribute to the local economy and the broader tourism landscape.

stages and management strategies, addressing economic impacts such as job creation and

revenue generation, and exploring management challenges like maintaining quality,

managing visitor flows, adapting to technological changes, and ensuring sustainability. By

issues in managing attractions to ensure they continue to benefit the tourism sector

INTRODUCTION

In this module, we'll explore the crucial role of transportation in tourism. Transport is not

just about moving tourists from one place to another; it significantly enhances their travel

experience and is integral to the success of the tourism industry. We'll examine how

various forms of transport—air, land, and water—each contribute uniquely to the tourism

experience. We'll delve into how air transport connects distant destinations and enables

global tourism, how land transport like buses and trains offers flexibility and scenic views,

and how water transport provides relaxing and picturesque journeys. Additionally, we'll

discuss the infrastructure that supports these modes of transport, including airports, roads,

ports, and railways, to understand how they collectively foster a thriving tourism

environment. By the end of this module, you'll appreciate how integral transport is to

shaping tourist experiences and supporting the tourism industry.
