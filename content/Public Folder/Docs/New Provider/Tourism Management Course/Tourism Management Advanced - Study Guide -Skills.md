INTRODUCTION
This module explores the key skills needed to manage tourism businesses effectively,
crucial for the growth of South Africa's economy. You'll learn about the main tasks of
management—planning, organising, leading, and controlling. These are essential for
running a tourism business smoothly and adapting to changes in the market.
It covers how to develop strategies for planning, create supportive organisational
structures, and apply leadership techniques to motivate your team. By the end of this
module, you'll understand the basics of managing a tourism business and be ready to apply
these skills in practical settings.
Unit 10.1: What is Management?
This unit explores the concept of management in the tourism industry, focusing on how
effective management involves coordinating resources and leading people to achieve
organisational goals. It covers the basics of planning, organising, leading, and controlling
within a tourism business.

sustainability of tourism businesses. Throughout this module, you will learn about financial
planning, budgeting, and control, all of which are vital to making informed business
decisions and maintaining financial health.

tourism, commission structures, and cost classification. Each unit is designed to deepen
your understanding of how financial operations are managed within tourism businesses and
the impact of financial decisions on operational effectiveness.
By the end of this module, you'll have a comprehensive understanding of financial
management processes in tourism, including how to calculate the break-even point and use
financial information for strategic decision-making.
