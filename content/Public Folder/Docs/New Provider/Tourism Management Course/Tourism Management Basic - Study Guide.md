industries, from planning and logistics to design and execution. You'll learn how to manage

various types of events, address potential risks, and integrate sustainable practices. The

course also includes strategies for marketing and securing sponsorships to attract the right

audience and achieve organisational goals. By the end, you'll be equipped to effectively

manage events that connect and engage communities in meaningful ways.
