Our evaluation method thoroughly checks the textbook to pinpoint improvement areas, making
sure it meets and surpasses our quality standards.The process includes:

Conclusion
The Tourism Management 102 textbook is highly effective, achieving a 99.47% quality score,
indicating its superior quality and alignment with educational standards. The content is
well-structured, engaging, and suitable for the intended audience of 13-15 year-olds, effectively
blending theoretical and practical learning. Minor recommendations include improving the cover

design and enhancing content diversity. Overall, the textbook is an exemplary educational
resource, and with the suggested improvements, it will continue to be a valuable tool for
teaching tourism management.
