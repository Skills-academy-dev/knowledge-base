Tourism Management 102 Assignment 1 Quality Checklist - Skills Academy

1\. Cover Page

● Updated Information: The cover page appears current and accurate with the title "Tourism Management 102" and "Assignment 1" for the year 2024. It includes all necessary elements.

2\. Edition

● Updated Edition: The edition is correctly stated as "First Edition: 2024."

3\. Table of Contents

● Correspondence with Page Numbers: The table of contents accurately corresponds with the page numbers listed, aligning with the sections provided in the document.

4\. Information Page

● Relevance: The information page (Skills Academy Contact Information) is relevant and up-to-date, providing necessary contact details and instructions for students.

5\. Instructions

● Clarity: Instructions are clearly written and easy to follow. They provide straightforward guidance on how to complete and submit the assignment.
