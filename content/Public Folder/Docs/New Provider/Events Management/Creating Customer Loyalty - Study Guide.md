Unit 3.1: Understanding Your Customers

A  problem  in  customer  service  is  any  issue  that  negatively  impacts  the  customer's

experience. Problems can range from minor inconveniences to significant service failures.

They can arise due to various reasons, including misunderstandings, service errors, unmet

expectations, or external factors.

Read pages 42—43 of your textbook for more information on the following:

Characteristics of Customer Service Problems

Common Causes of Customer Service Problems

3.1.1 Characteristics of Customer Service Problems

  Misunderstandings: These occur when there is a gap between what the customer

expects and what is delivered.



Service Errors: Mistakes made during service delivery, such as incorrect orders or

billing errors.



Unmet Expectations: When the service provided does not meet the customer's

expectations.

3.1.2 Common Causes of Customer Service Problems



Lack of Communication: Poor communication can lead to misunderstandings and

errors.



Inadequate Training: Staff who are not adequately trained may not perform to the

expected standards.



System Failures: Technical issues or system failures can disrupt service delivery.
