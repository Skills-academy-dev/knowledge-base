INTRODUCTION

Events management is an essential discipline that involves planning, coordinating, and

executing events of various types and scales. It plays a crucial role in business, culture,

and tourism by creating opportunities for networking, entertainment, and economic growth.

This chapter provides an overview of event management, focusing on its historical

development, strategic planning, and the role of key stakeholders in ensuring successful

event execution. Students will also explore challenges in event management and ethical

considerations that influence decision-making.

Unit 1: Introduction to Event Management

Event management is a structured approach to planning and executing various types of

events, from small meetings to large international conferences. It involves coordinating

multiple aspects, including logistics, budgeting, risk management, and stakeholder

engagement, to ensure a seamless and impactful experience. This unit introduces students

to the fundamental concepts of event management, including its significance, key

processes, and industry trends.

Key Points

Definition and scope of event management.

Importance of event management in various industries (business, tourism, social, and

cultural sectors).

Fundamental components of event management: planning, organizing, executing, and

evaluating events.

The evolving role of technology in modern event management.

The significance of professional ethics and standards in event management.
