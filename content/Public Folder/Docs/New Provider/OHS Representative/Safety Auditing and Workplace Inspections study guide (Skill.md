Introduction to the Safety Auditing and Workplace

Inspections Study Guide

Welcome to your Safety Auditing and Workplace Inspections Study Guide!

We are pleased to have you on this important learning journey into the world of occupational

health and safety (OHS)

.  Workplace safety is not only about following rules — it is

about protecting people, preventing injuries, and creating environments where everyone can

work confidently and securely. In this course, you will learn how to identify hazards, conduct

inspections, and support safe working practices across different types of workplaces.

Think of this study guide as your learning companion throughout the course. It works

alongside your textbook to help you understand key concepts, simplify technical information,

and keep your learning structured and organised. The guide highlights important ideas,

summarises key topics, and helps you prepare for your activities and assignments with

confidence.

To succeed in this course, it is important to work consistently through both the textbook and

this study guide. You will be required to:

●  Study Modules 1–4 and complete Assignment 1, and

●  Study Modules 5–8 and complete Assignment 2.

There are no formal examinations for this course. However, both assignments are

compulsory and must be completed and submitted for assessment. Once submitted, your

assignments will be returned with tutor feedback. This feedback is valuable, as it helps you

identify areas for improvement, correct mistakes, and strengthen your understanding of

workplace safety practices.

As you progress through the modules, you will explore topics such as hazard identification,

risk assessment, types of workplace inspections, safety controls, reporting procedures, and

continuous safety improvement. The activities and real-world examples included in this

course will help you apply your knowledge in practical workplace situations, preparing you to

contribute effectively to a safer working environment.

Before you begin studying, please make sure you have received:

●  Your Safety Auditing and Workplace Inspections Textbook

●  This Study Guide

●  Assignment 1 and Assignment 2

How to Use This Study Guide

This study guide has been designed to support your learning and help you succeed in the

Safety Auditing and Workplace Inspections course. It works together with your textbook to

make your learning clearer, more structured, and easier to manage.

To get the most benefit from this study guide, follow these simple steps:

●  Start with the textbook

Begin each module by reading the relevant sections in your textbook. This will give

you a full understanding of the topic and introduce you to key concepts.

●  Use the study guide for support

After reading the textbook, use this study guide to review and reinforce what you

have learned. The guide summarises important information and highlights key points

that you need to remember.

●  Focus on key terms and concepts

Pay close attention to important terms such as hazards, risks, inspections, and

control measures. Understanding these terms is essential for success in this course.

●  Work through each module step by step

Do not rush through the content. Take your time to understand each unit before

moving on to the next one. Each module builds on the previous one.

●  Complete all activities and exercises

The activities in your textbook and study guide are designed to help you apply what

you have learned. Completing them will improve your understanding and prepare you

for your assignments.

●  Prepare for your assignments

Use the study guide to revise important topics before starting your assignments.

Make sure you understand the questions and follow all instructions carefully.

●  Learn from feedback

When your assignments are returned, read your tutor’s feedback carefully. This will

help you improve your knowledge and avoid making the same mistakes in the future.

●  Stay organised and manage your time

Create a study schedule and set goals for each week. This will help you stay on track

and complete your course successfully.

Introduction

Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) focuses on protecting workers from harm in the

workplace. It involves identifying hazards, managing risks, and promoting safe working

practices. A safe work environment helps reduce injuries and improves productivity. Both

employers and employees play an important role in maintaining safety. Understanding OHS

helps organisations meet legal requirements and build a strong safety culture. Learning

these principles prepares you to recognise and respond to workplace risks effectively.

Refer to page 10 in your textbook for more information.

Occupational Health and Safety provides a framework for managing safety in the workplace.

It includes systems, policies, and procedures that protect employees from hazards.

Understanding OHS helps improve working conditions and reduces accidents. It also

supports organisations in meeting legal and ethical responsibilities.
