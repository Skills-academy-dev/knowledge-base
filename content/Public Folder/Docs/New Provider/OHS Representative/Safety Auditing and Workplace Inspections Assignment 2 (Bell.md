Assignment 2

Task 1: Hazard Analysis and Risk Prioritisation (Marks: 15)

Scenario

Mohammed manages a small grocery shop. During busy hours, customers move quickly

through the aisles while staff restock shelves. You notice that:

●  Some boxes are temporarily placed in walkways

●  A leaking fridge causes water to collect on the floor

●  Workers lift heavy items without proper technique

●  Electrical plugs are overloaded near the counter

●  Damaged electric cables on the floor

Mohammed says the shop has never had a serious accident, so he is not too concerned.

Instructions

1. Hazard Identification (5 marks)

Identify five hazards from the scenario or based on what could realistically happen in this

environment.

2. Risk Assessment (5 marks)

For each hazard:

●  Assign a risk level (low, medium, or high)

●  Briefly explain your decision

3. Risk Prioritisation (5 marks)

●  Select the two most critical hazards

●  Explain why they must be addressed first in terms of safety impact

Task 2: Inspection Planning and Checklist Design (Marks: 15)

Scenario

Zanele is opening a small salon and wants to ensure it is safe before customers arrive.

She has asked you to help her prepare for her first workplace inspection, but no

inspection system is currently in place.

Instructions

1. Inspection Planning (5 marks)

Explain how you would prepare for this inspection. Your answer must include:

●  The purpose of the inspection

●  The areas that need to be inspected

●  The tools or documents you would use

2. Inspection Checklist (10 marks)

Create a clear and structured inspection checklist with at least 8 items that can be used

in the salon.

✔ Your checklist must:

●  Be practical

●  Cover different safety areas

●  Be written as clear inspection questions or statements

Task 3: Inspection Findings and Root Cause Analysis (Marks: 15)

Scenario

During a workplace inspection at Rajesh Logistics warehouse, the following was observed:

●  Some workers are not wearing safety gloves while handling goods

●  A fire extinguisher is not easily accessible

●  One of the storage aisles is blocked with boxes

●  Employees say they were not trained on updated safety procedures

7

Instructions

1. Identify Issue Types (5 marks)

For each issue:

●

Indicate whether it is an unsafe condition or an unsafe behaviour

2. Root Cause Analysis (5 marks)

Explain the possible reasons why these issues are happening in the workplace.

3. Corrective Actions (5 marks)

Suggest three appropriate actions to correct these problems and improve safety.

Task 4: Corrective Action Planning and Continuous Improvement (Marks: 15)

Scenario

After reviewing inspection findings, management at the warehouse wants a structured plan

to fix safety problems and prevent them from happening again. They also want to ensure

that improvements are maintained over time.

Instructions

1. Corrective Action Plan (8 marks)

Create a corrective action plan table with at least 4 entries, including:

●  Hazard or issue

●  Corrective action

●  Responsible person

●  Deadline

2. Monitoring and Follow-Up (4 marks)

Explain how you will:

●  Check that corrective actions have been completed

●  Ensure that safety improvements are effective

3. Continuous Safety Improvement (3 marks)

Suggest three practical ways the organisation can improve safety continuously.

TOTAL: 60 MARKS
