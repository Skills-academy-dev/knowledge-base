Assignment 1
Task 1: Workplace Hazard Identification (Marks: 15)
Scenario
You are required to conduct a basic safety observation in a familiar environment such as a
small business, or a community space. The purpose is to identify unsafe conditions that may
cause harm.
Instructions
1. Hazard Identification (5 marks)
Identify five hazards in the environment you have chosen.
2. Hazard Classification (5 marks)
Classify each hazard according to its type.
3. Risk Explanation (5 marks)
Explain the possible consequences of each hazard if no action is taken.
Task 2: Legal Compliance and Responsibilities (Marks: 15)
Scenario
A small packaging company has been experiencing minor workplace incidents. During a
visit, you observe that some safety procedures are not consistently followed, and certain
working conditions appear unsafe. Management believes that safety is mainly the
responsibility of employees.
Instructions
1. Identify Compliance Concerns (5 marks)
Identify three possible legal or safety compliance concerns based on the scenario.
2. Employer Responsibilities (5 marks)
Explain the key responsibilities of the employer in ensuring a safe workplace.
3. Recommendations for Compliance (5 marks)
Suggest three practical actions that the organisation should take to improve compliance
and safety.
6

Task 3: Safety Audit Evaluation (Marks: 15)
Scenario
An internal safety audit was recently conducted at a workplace. The audit process was
completed, and findings were documented. However, after the audit, there was little change
in workplace conditions, and similar issues continue to occur.
Instructions
1. Audit Process Evaluation (5 marks)
Identify three possible weaknesses in how the audit process was handled.
2. Importance of Follow-Up (5 marks)
Explain why follow-up actions are critical after a safety audit.
3. Improvement Strategies (5 marks)
Recommend three ways to improve the effectiveness of future audits.
Task 4: Workplace Inspection Planning (Marks: 15)
Scenario
You have been asked to assist in preparing for a workplace inspection in a small
organisation. The inspection has not yet been planned, and there is no structured approach
in place.
Instructions
1. Purpose of Inspection (3 marks)
Explain the main purpose of conducting a workplace inspection.
2. Inspection Focus Areas (4 marks)
Identify four key areas that should be included in the inspection.
3. Inspection Checklist Development (4 marks)
Develop a simple inspection checklist with at least four items.
4. Roles and Responsibilities (2 marks)
Explain who should be involved in the inspection process.
5. Post-Inspection Actions (2 marks)
Describe what should happen after the inspection is completed.
TOTAL: 60 MARKS
