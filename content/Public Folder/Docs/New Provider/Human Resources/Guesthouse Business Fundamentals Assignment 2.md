1. The main purpose of advertising is to:

A. Increase awareness and attract potential guests

B. Replace word-of-mouth referrals

C. Reduce marketing expenses

A. Newspaper and radio advertisements

B. Instagram reels

C. Email newsletters

1. Digital marketing helps guesthouses by:

A. Reaching a wider audience at a lower cost

B. Limiting brand visibility

C. Avoiding direct communication with guests

A. Booking platforms such as Expedia or Airbnb

B. Local newspapers

C. Tourism expos only

A. Positive reviews influence new bookings

B. It reduces the need for staff training

C. It only affects long-term guests

1. When responding to a negative review, the manager should:

A. Stay calm, apologise, and offer to resolve the issue

B. Ignore the feedback

C. Delete the comment

1. Social media marketing is most effective when:

A. Posts are consistent, engaging, and relevant to the brand

B. Updates are rare and unplanned

C. Only promotions are shared

1. The key goal of public relations is to:

A. Maintain a positive public image and build community trust

B. Sell as many rooms as possible

C. Replace marketing strategies

1. Promotional offers such as “stay three nights, pay for two” are examples of:

A. Sales promotions

B. Digital branding

C. Guest relations

1. Loyalty programmes are designed to:

A. Reward returning guests and encourage repeat visits

B. Increase first-time bookings only

C. Reduce online marketing

1. Guest feedback is valuable because it:

A. Highlights strengths and areas for improvement

B. Is only useful for complaints

C. Has no effect on future bookings

1. The guest experience begins:

A. Before arrival, when the first contact is made

B. Only at check-in

C. After the stay ends

1. Personalised service involves:

A. Remembering guest preferences and exceeding expectations

B. Offering the same treatment to all guests

C. Avoiding special requests

1. Effective communication with guests includes:

A. Listening actively and responding clearly

B. Talking more than the guest

C. Ignoring cultural differences

1. The best way to handle guest complaints is to:

A. Acknowledge the issue and resolve it promptly

B. Argue with the guest

C. Avoid admitting mistakes

1. Empathy in guest service means:

A. Understanding and caring about guest feelings

B. Ignoring emotional needs

C. Following policies only

1. Guest satisfaction leads to:

A. Repeat business and positive referrals

B. Increased maintenance costs

C. Fewer bookings

1. Cross-selling in hospitality means:

A. Suggesting additional products or services to guests

B. Outsourcing to competitors

C. Refusing special requests

1. A guest comment card is used to:

A. Gather feedback on services and facilities

B. Record financial transactions

C. Track inventory levels

1. Which action shows good guest relationship management?

A. Following up after check-out to thank the guest

B. Ignoring reviews

C. Sending generic mass emails

1. The main goal of human resource management is to:

A. Recruit, train, and retain competent staff

B. Increase marketing reach

C. Replace management roles

1. Staff orientation is important because it:

A. Helps new employees understand policies and culture

B. Delays work schedules

C. Replaces formal training

1. A job description outlines:

A. Duties, responsibilities, and expectations for a position

B. Only salary information

C. The company’s mission statement

1. Effective recruitment ensures that:

A. The right person is hired for the right job

B. Positions are filled randomly

C. Employment equity is ignored

1. Performance appraisals are used to:

A. Evaluate employee performance and provide feedback

B. Punish poor behaviour

C. Replace training sessions

1. Motivated staff are more likely to:

A. Deliver better service and improve guest satisfaction

B. Work slower

C. Ignore teamwork

1. Training in a guesthouse improves:

A. Staff competence, confidence, and service quality

B. Costs and staff turnover

C. Marketing expenses

1. Teamwork in guesthouse operations encourages:

A. Cooperation and shared responsibility

B. Competition among staff

C. Isolation of departments

1. A good leader in hospitality should:

A. Inspire, guide, and support employees

B. Command obedience

C. Focus only on profits

1. The best way to improve staff morale is to:

A. Recognise and reward good performance

B. Ignore achievements

C. Avoid communication

1. Delegation in management means:

A. Assigning tasks and authority to capable staff

B. Doing everything alone

C. Hiring outside contractors

1. Workplace diversity is important because:

A. It encourages creativity and cultural understanding

B. It causes conflict

C. It limits innovation

1. Conflict resolution should focus on:

A. Finding fair solutions that maintain teamwork

B. Avoiding discussion

C. Punishing employees

1. Time management in hospitality is essential to:

A. Ensure efficient operations and guest satisfaction

B. Allow delays

C. Avoid scheduling

1. Ethical behaviour in the workplace includes:

A. Honesty, fairness, and respect for others

B. Ignoring policies

C. Taking shortcuts to save time

1. Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) laws protect:

A. Employees and guests from harm or injury

B. Only managers

C. Property damage only

1. Why is staff retention important for guesthouses?

A. Experienced employees maintain service consistency

B. It increases turnover rates

C. It reduces staff knowledge

1. Job satisfaction is closely linked to:

A. Motivation and working conditions

B. Irregular schedules

C. Strict discipline

1. Which of the following promotes teamwork?

A. Staff meetings, open communication, and shared goals

B. Ignoring feedback

C. Competing for tips

1. Effective communication in the workplace results in:

A. Fewer misunderstandings and better collaboration

B. Increased errors

C. Lower productivity

A. Smooth daily activities and consistent service standards

C. Allow staff to improvise

A. Meeting or exceeding guest expectations

C. Cutting corners

A. Reduce equipment breakdowns and costly repairs

B. Increase downtime

C. Avoid inspections

A. Reducing waste and using resources responsibly

C. Buying imported goods only

A. Review and enhance processes for long-term success
