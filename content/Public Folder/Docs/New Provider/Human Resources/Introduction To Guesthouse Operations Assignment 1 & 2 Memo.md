through check-in and check-out interactions. (Page 80)

records of guest details, preventing double bookings. (Page 81)

3.  A. Asking for guest identification – Checking ID is part of the standard check-in

procedure to confirm the guest’s identity. (Page 82)

4.  A. Requesting feedback and thanking guests for their stay – A polite farewell and

feedback request encourage loyalty and positive reviews. (Page 82)

5.  B. Ensuring cleanliness and comfort in rooms – The main role of housekeeping is

maintaining cleanliness and comfort to satisfy guests. (Page 83)

6.  C. It needs repairs or maintenance – Rooms marked “Out of Order” cannot be used

until maintenance issues are fixed. (Page 83)

7.  C. A report of cold shower water – A guest complaint is any expression of

dissatisfaction, such as issues with amenities. (Page 86)

8.  B. Listen carefully without interrupting – Listening first allows guests to feel heard

and respected before resolving the issue. (Page 86)

9.  A. To track recurring problems and prevent them – Complaint records help identify

trends and prevent repeat issues. (Page 87)

10. A. Clean linen and working appliances – Guest comfort is achieved through

cleanliness, working equipment, and attention to detail. (Page 85)

11. B. Breakfast – Breakfast is the main meal provided in most guesthouses and sets the

safe for consumption. (Page 95)

16. B. Food preparation certificate – Guesthouses must have proof of compliance with

food hygiene standards. (Page 95)

management ensures reliable ingredients and cost efficiency. (Page 98)

20. A. Recognition and good communication – Staff motivation improves with

appreciation and teamwork. (Page 99)

21. A. To improve safety, hygiene, and teamwork – Training ensures staff follow hygiene

rules and work efficiently. (Page 98)

to the variety of backgrounds guests come from. (Page 107)

23. A. It helps staff communicate respectfully and avoid misunderstandings – Cultural

awareness prevents offence and improves relations. (Page 106)

24. B. Greeting guests using their cultural norms – Adapting greetings shows cultural

sensitivity and respect. (Page 110)

25. B. Speaking slowly, using simple words, and visual aids – These techniques help

overcome language barriers. (Page 108)

26. B. Gestures, facial expressions, and body language – Non-verbal communication

includes physical cues that convey meaning. (Page 108)

27. A. Using comment cards or online reviews – Feedback methods help guesthouses

assess service quality. (Page 87)

28. A. Treating all guests with equal respect and meeting diverse needs – Inclusivity

30. A. Organising local cooking lessons or storytelling evenings – Cultural exchange
