14. A. Zoning laws control how land and buildings may be used, determining whether a property

(Pages 31–32)

(Page 37)

(Page 41)

23. B. A liquor licence is required from the provincial Liquor Board to sell or serve alcohol legally

to guests.

(Page 40)

royalties.

(Page 40)

regulations builds trust and stability.

(Page 42)

(Pages 36–38)

Guesthouse Start-Up – Assignment 2

1. B. Financial management ensures the guesthouse earns enough to cover costs, make a

profit, and remain sustainable.

(Pages 59–60)

2. A. Start-up costs are once-off expenses such as renovations, furniture, and initial marketing

before opening.

(Page 61)

3. B. Operating costs are recurring expenses like salaries, utilities, and maintenance required to

run daily operations.

(Page 62)

4. B. Fixed costs, such as rent and insurance, remain constant regardless of occupancy levels.

(Page 63)

5. A. Variable costs change according to guest numbers and include food, cleaning supplies,

and utilities.

(Page 63)

6. B. Hidden costs like linen replacement and booking commissions are often overlooked but

reduce profit margins.

(Page 64)

7. A. Cost forecasting estimates future expenses to plan budgets and ensure financial stability.

(Page 64)

8. A. Seasonal demand affects guesthouses with high and low booking periods throughout the

year.

(Pages 65–66)

9. A. Dynamic pricing adjusts room rates depending on demand, events, and season to

maximise profit.

(Page 67)

10. A. Value-based pricing considers what guests are willing to pay for perceived quality and

experience.

(Page 67)

11. A. The break-even point is reached when income equals expenses, showing when profit

begins.

(Page 68)

12. A. A budget helps monitor income and spending, ensuring financial control and informed

decisions.

(Page 69)

13. A. The Income Statement records income and expenses to calculate profit or loss over time.

(Page 70)

14. A. Cash flow management ensures funds are available when needed to pay bills and staff.

(Page 71)

15. A. Even profitable guesthouses can fail if they cannot meet short-term cash obligations.

(Page 71)

16. A. Offering discounts or off-season promotions helps maintain occupancy and cash flow

during quiet periods.

(Page 72)

17. A. Profitability occurs when total income exceeds total costs, allowing reinvestment and

growth.

(Page 73)

18. A. Financial forecasting predicts future trends and income, helping guide pricing and

investment decisions.

(Page 73)

19. A. Accurate recordkeeping ensures compliance with SARS and helps track income,

expenses, and taxes.

(Page 74)

20. B. Financial planning helps owners make informed decisions, manage risk, and ensure

long-term stability.

(Page 75)

satisfaction.

(Page 78)

22. B. Recruitment focuses on hiring people whose values and attitudes align with the

guesthouse culture.

(Page 79)

23. A. Job descriptions clarify roles, duties, and expectations, reducing conflict and confusion.

(Page 80)

24. A. In hospitality, enthusiasm, attitude, and service-mindedness often matter more than

experience.

(Page 81)

25. A. Training develops staff skills, ensuring consistent quality and professionalism in service

delivery.

(Page 85)
