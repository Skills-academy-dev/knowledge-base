1. A guesthouse is best described as:

A. Provide entertainment only
B. Offer personalised accommodation and cultural experiences
C. Replace lodges and hotels

A. Owner-managed and homely atmosphere
B. Fully automated and corporate-style service

A. Are larger and may offer meals beyond breakfast
