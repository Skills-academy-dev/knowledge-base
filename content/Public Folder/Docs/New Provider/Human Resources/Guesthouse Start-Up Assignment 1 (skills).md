ASSIGNMENT 1

1\. Market research helps guesthouse owners to:

A. Avoid paying business taxes

B. Identify customer needs and reduce business risk

C. Copy competitors’ business ideas

A. The entire travelling population

B. The specific group most likely to book your guesthouse

C. All hotels in your area

3\. Understanding your target market helps you to:

A. Offer random services

B. Design services and prices that match guest expectations

C. Avoid advertising

A. A government inspector

B. A family on holiday at the coast

C. A supplier delivering goods

5\. A feasibility study is conducted to:

A. Test if a guesthouse idea is practical and sustainable

B. Choose the best furniture for rooms

C. Design the guesthouse logo

6\. Which factor most influences market demand for accommodation?

A. Tourism activity and accessibility

C. Avoid setting prices

A. Income equals total expenses

B. The guesthouse earns double its costs

C. Prices are reduced during holidays

A. The long-term dream or direction of the business

B. The feature that sets your guesthouse apart from competitors

C. A type of room package
