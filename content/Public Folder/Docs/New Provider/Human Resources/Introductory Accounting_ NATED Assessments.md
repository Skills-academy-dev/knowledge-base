## Question 1: Multiple-Choice Questions and Ledger Accounts (20 Marks)

Part A: Multiple-Choice Questions (10 Marks)

Choose the correct option. Write only the question number and the correct letter (e.g., 1.1 A).

1.1) Which of the following is an example of a service business?
A. Supermarket
B. Law Firm
C. Clothing Store
D. Electronics Manufacturer

1.2) The accounting equation is:
A. Assets = Liabilities + Owner’s Equity
B. Assets = Liabilities - Owner’s Equity
C. Liabilities = Assets + Owner’s Equity
D. Owner’s Equity = Assets - Liabilities

1.3) What is the purpose of a trial balance?
A. To create a financial statement
B. To check that total debits equal total credits
C. To list all cash transactions
D. To prepare inventory records

1.4) In double-entry accounting, every transaction affects:
A. Only one account
B. Only cash accounts
C. Two or more accounts
D. Owner’s equity only

1.5) Which document records a purchase on credit?
A. Receipt
B. Invoice
C. Bank Deposit Slip
D. Sales Order
