## **ASSIGNMENT 2**

1. The main goal of financial management in a guesthouse is to:

A. Decorate rooms attractively

B. Ensure the business remains profitable and sustainable

C. Eliminate all expenses

1. Start-up costs refer to:

A. Once-off expenses needed before opening

B. Monthly utility bills

C. Staff bonuses

1. Operating costs are:

A. Once-off payments

B. Ongoing expenses to keep the business running

C. Expenses only for renovations

1. Which of the following is an example of a fixed cost?

A. Laundry detergent

B. Staff salaries and insurance

C. Food and linen per guest

1. Variable costs change depending on:

A. The number of guests or occupancy levels

B. The owner’s preferences

C. Municipal by-laws

1. Hidden costs may include:

A. Loan repayments

B. Linen replacement and booking commissions

C. Staff training materials

1. Cost forecasting helps guesthouse owners to:

A. Predict future expenses and prepare reserves

B. Avoid making budgets

C. Guarantee 100% occupancy

1. Seasonal fluctuations affect guesthouses by:

A. Creating predictable peaks and low-demand months

B. Keeping income the same all year

C. Increasing fixed costs only

1. Dynamic pricing means:

A. Adjusting rates based on demand, season, or events

B. Charging the same rate year-round

C. Using competitor discounts only

1. Value-based pricing focuses on:

A. The perceived value and experience offered to guests

B. The lowest possible prices

C. Competitors’ room rates only

1. The break-even point shows:

A. When total income equals total expenses

B. When profit doubles

C. When occupancy reaches 100%

1. A budget is useful because it:

A. Controls spending and plans financial performance

B. Eliminates the need for recordkeeping

C. Replaces legal compliance

1. Which document shows income and expenses over a specific time?

A. Income Statement (Profit & Loss)

B. Balance Sheet

C. UIF Form

1. Cash flow management ensures:

A. There is enough cash to cover expenses at the right time

B. Staff are paid more than profits allow

C. Only suppliers are prioritised

1. Poor cash flow can cause:

A. Business failure despite profitability

B. Lower utility bills

C. Increased guest satisfaction

1. To manage low-season cash flow, guesthouses should:

A. Offer promotions or discounts

B. Close for the season

C. Avoid advertising

1. Profitability means that:

A. Income is greater than expenses

B. Guests are always satisfied

C. Costs and income are the same

1. Which tool helps predict future financial performance?

A. Forecasting

B. SWOT Analysis

C. Consent Use

1. Why is recordkeeping essential?

A. It ensures financial accuracy and SARS compliance

B. It replaces budgeting

C. It is only for large hotels

1. The main purpose of financial planning is to:

A. Impress investors only

B. Support decision-making and sustainability

C. Reduce prices permanently

1. The staff of a guesthouse are known as:

A. The face of the business

B. Temporary labour

C. External stakeholders only

1. Recruitment in guesthouses should focus on:

A. Hiring friends and family

B. Finding people who fit the business culture and values

C. Speeding up the process regardless of fit

1. A well-written job description:

A. Clarifies duties and prevents confusion

B. Is not needed for small guesthouses

C. Should be verbal only

1. In the hospitality industry, which is often more valuable than experience?

A. Positive attitude and willingness to learn

B. Formal education only

C. Family connections

1. Training ensures that:

A. Staff deliver consistent, high-quality service

B. Guesthouses save on salaries

C. Employees never leave

1. Orientation training introduces staff to:

A. The mission, values, and culture of the guesthouse

B. Fire drill procedures only

C. Payroll and bonuses

1. The Basic Conditions of Employment Act regulates:

A. Working hours, overtime, and leave entitlements

B. UIF contributions only

C. Minimum wage increases

1. The National Minimum Wage Act ensures:

A. Staff receive fair pay per hour worked

B. Employers can decide any rate

C. Only management earns salaries

1. UIF protects workers by:

A. Providing income support during unemployment or maternity leave

B. Reducing employer costs

C. Paying for staff training

1. UIF contributions total:

A. 2%, shared equally between employer and employee

B. 5%, paid by the employee only

C. 1%, paid by government

1. The Occupational Health and Safety Act ensures:

A. Safe workplaces for staff and guests

B. Regular salary increases

C. Free accommodation for employees

1. Which factor most motivates staff?

A. Recognition, fair pay, and opportunities to grow

B. Strict rules without feedback

C. Unpaid overtime

1. A key strategy for retaining staff is to:

A. Offer fair treatment and recognition

B. Replace them often

C. Ignore their suggestions

1. Incentive programmes help by:

A. Rewarding good performance and building loyalty

B. Reducing wages

C. Avoiding teamwork

1. Verbal agreements are:

A. Unreliable and not legally binding

B. Sufficient for employment

C. Preferred by labour inspectors

1. Risk management in guesthouses aims to:

A. Identify, assess, and reduce potential business threats

B. Replace insurance

C. Eliminate guest feedback

1. A financial risk example is:

A. Seasonal drop in occupancy

B. Poor staff communication

C. Positive guest reviews

1. Operational risks arise when:

A. Equipment or systems fail, disrupting service

B. Staff are highly trained

C. Profits increase

1. Legal risks may result from:

A. Ignoring zoning or labour laws

B. Buying insurance early

C. Conducting feasibility studies

1. Reputational risk can be caused by:

A. Negative online reviews or poor guest service

B. Staff training

C. Having a website

1. Environmental risks include:

A. Floods, storms, and droughts

B. High commission rates

C. Seasonal promotions

1. Property insurance covers:

A. Buildings, furniture, and equipment from fire or theft

B. Staff injuries only

C. Tour cancellations

1. Liability insurance protects against:

A. Guest injury or damage claims

B. Staff bonuses

C. Load-shedding disruptions

1. Employee cover ensures:

A. Workers are protected if injured on duty

B. Guests receive refunds

C. Staff bonuses increase

1. Business interruption insurance provides:

A. Compensation when operations stop after a disaster

B. Discounts for guests

C. Funds for marketing campaigns

1. Networking helps guesthouses to:

A. Build relationships and increase visibility

B. Operate independently

C. Avoid competition

1. Local partnerships benefit guesthouses by:

A. Providing added value and authentic experiences for guests

B. Reducing guest choices

C. Avoiding community involvement

1. Partnering with tour operators allows guesthouses to:

A. Offer complete travel packages and experiences

B. Replace restaurant partnerships

C. Operate without licences

1. The TGCSA grading system helps by:

A. Building credibility and trust through star ratings

B. Setting food prices

C. Managing staff training

1. Cross-promotions between businesses are effective because they:

A. Reduce marketing costs and expand visibility

B. Eliminate social media

C. Replace word-of-mouth
