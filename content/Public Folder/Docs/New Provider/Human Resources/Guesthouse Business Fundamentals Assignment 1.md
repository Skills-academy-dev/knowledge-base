## **ASSIGNMENT 1**

1. A guesthouse is best described as:

A. A large, international hotel

B. A privately owned accommodation offering personal service

C. A backpacker hostel

1. The main role of guesthouses in South Africa’s tourism industry is to:

A. Provide entertainment only

B. Offer personalised accommodation and cultural experiences

C. Replace lodges and hotels

1. Which of the following is a distinguishing feature of a guesthouse?

A. Owner-managed and homely atmosphere

B. Fully automated and corporate-style service

C. Open 24 hours with 100+ rooms

1. Guesthouses differ from B&Bs mainly because they:

A. Are larger and may offer meals beyond breakfast

B. Only serve breakfast

C. Operate without registration or grading

1. Which organisation grades guesthouses in South Africa?

A. SARS

B. Tourism Grading Council of South Africa (TGCSA)

C. Department of Labour

1. The economic and social value of guesthouses includes:

A. Reducing community employment

B. Supporting local suppliers and job creation

C. Limiting tourism in small towns

1. Key stakeholders in a guesthouse include:

A. Owners, staff, guests, regulators, and community

B. Only the guests

C. Banks and investors only

1. A major success factor for guesthouses is:

A. Location, customer service, and compliance

B. Offering the lowest possible prices

C. Avoiding social media

1. Which of the following best explains “business model”?

A. A plan showing how a guesthouse creates and delivers value

B. A list of furniture and décor

C. A government policy on tourism

1. A family-run guesthouse typically:

A. Employs over 100 people

B. Is owner-managed and offers a homely experience

C. Operates as a luxury franchise

1. A boutique guesthouse is known for:

A. Stylish décor and personalised service

B. Self-catering only

C. Low-cost shared rooms

1. An eco-lodge business model focuses on:

A. High-end luxury only

B. Sustainable and environmentally friendly practices

C. Servicing business travellers only

1. Aligning a business model with a target market helps to:

A. Increase guest satisfaction and profitability

B. Limit marketing opportunities

C. Avoid customer reviews

1. Which of the following is an example of a revenue stream?

A. Room rentals, meals, and events

B. Staff training

C. Paying taxes

1. Legal compliance protects a guesthouse by:

A. Reducing staff salaries

B. Preventing fines and reputational damage

C. Avoiding taxes

1. A guesthouse must register its business with:

A. The Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC)

B. The Department of Tourism

C. The local bank

1. The Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA) regulates:

A. Staff working hours, leave, and contracts

B. Property ownership

C. Customer complaints

1. A food handler’s certificate is required to:

A. Serve meals safely to guests

B. Register a company with SARS

C. Apply for a liquor licence

1. Which document protects both employer and employee?

A. Verbal agreement

B. Written employment contract

C. Business licence

1. Which is a risk of non-compliance?

A. Increased profit

B. Fines, closure, or loss of reputation

C. Higher staff morale

1. Fixed costs are expenses that:

A. Change with the number of guests

B. Stay the same every month

C. Only apply during high season

1. Which of the following is a hidden cost?

A. Booking commissions and broken furniture

B. Salaries

C. Rent

1. A start-up cost for a new guesthouse includes:

A. Electricity bills

B. Furniture, renovations, and marketing

C. Monthly groceries

1. Variable costs increase when:

A. Occupancy rises

B. The guesthouse is closed

C. There are fewer guests

1. The main purpose of a budget is to:

A. Guess future income

B. Plan and control financial performance

C. Avoid paying suppliers

1. Which pricing method adds a markup to costs?

A. Cost-based pricing

B. Value-based pricing

C. Discount pricing

1. Seasonal pricing allows guesthouses to:

A. Keep prices fixed all year

B. Adjust rates based on demand

C. Avoid online platforms

1. The break-even point shows:

A. When income equals expenses

B. The highest possible profit

C. Guest satisfaction levels

1. Which of the following improves cash flow?

A. Requesting deposits before arrival

B. Ignoring bills until month-end

C. Increasing expenses

1. Profitability measures whether:

A. Guests are satisfied

B. The business earns more than it spends

C. Cash is available daily

1. Which financial document shows income and expenses over time?

A. Income statement

B. Balance sheet

C. Cashbook

1. A balance sheet shows:

A. Profit for the year

B. Assets, liabilities, and owner’s equity

C. Staff wages and leave days

1. What is the main benefit of accurate recordkeeping?

A. Helps comply with SARS and track finances

B. Makes staff happy

C. Reduces marketing costs

1. Cash flow problems often occur because:

A. Guests pay late or bookings are seasonal

B. Owners save too much

C. Staff take leave

1. Diversifying revenue means:

A. Offering additional services like meals, tours, and events

B. Sticking to one product

C. Avoiding online sales

1. A target market refers to:

A. Everyone in South Africa

B. The specific group a business aims to attract

C. Only international tourists

1. Market segmentation divides customers by:

A. Random chance

B. Similar needs and preferences

C. Location only

1. Which marketing channel best reaches international guests?

A. Local radio

B. Booking.com and Airbnb

C. Community posters

1. Why is competitor analysis important?

A. It helps identify gaps and opportunities in the market

B. It reduces the need for staff

C. It prevents online marketing

1. Guest needs differ depending on:

A. Cultural background, purpose of travel, and budget

B. Weather

C. Staff uniforms

1. Branding is:

A. A logo only

B. The total image and personality of the guesthouse

C. A pricing method

1. A strong slogan should:

A. Be long and complex

C. Include unrelated quotes

1. A logo should be:

A. Simple, memorable, and adaptable

C. Complicated to impress guests

1. A Unique Selling Proposition (USP) is:

A. The feature that sets a guesthouse apart from competitors

C. A tourism law

1. Positioning helps a guesthouse:

A. Define how it is perceived compared to competitors

B. Decide which rooms to clean first

C. Hire seasonal workers

1. A family-run guesthouse brand should emphasise:

A. Affordability and personal warmth

B. Corporate image

C. Minimal staff interaction

A. Using the same logo, colours, and tone across all platforms

C. Rebranding frequently

C. Font style
