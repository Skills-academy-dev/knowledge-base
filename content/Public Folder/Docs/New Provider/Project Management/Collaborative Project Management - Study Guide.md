INTRODUCTION

In Module 1, we explore the fundamental concepts of projects and the teams that bring

them to life. Understanding these basics will set the foundation for successful project

management. You’ll discover what makes a project unique and learn about the different

types of project teams, their dynamics, and the common pitfalls they might encounter. This

section covers essential knowledge that will prepare you to contribute effectively to any

project team.

Unit 1.1 Project Fundamentals

This unit explains the core aspects of project fundamentals. It covers the key stages of a

project, from initiation to review, and outlines the characteristics of a successful project.

You will learn what differentiates a project from routine operations and understand the

critical factors that contribute to project success, such as objectives, time, cost, and

satisfaction. This unit explores the structure and management of projects to ensure they

meet their goals.

Module Summary

In this module, we explored the essential concepts of projects and project teams. Projects

are unique efforts with specific goals, differing from regular business operations by having

clear start and end dates. The module covered the stages of a project, including initiation,

planning, execution, monitoring, and review. We also examined what makes a project

successful, such as meeting objectives, staying within budget, and satisfying stakeholders.

Additionally, we discussed project teams, their dynamics, common pitfalls, and strategies to

keep teams focused and effective. Understanding these fundamentals prepares students to

lead and contribute to project success.

INTRODUCTION

In Module 5, we focus on the essential process of decision-making within a project team.

Effective decision-making is crucial for guiding a project to success, as it ensures that the

best possible choices are made at every stage. This module covers various methods of

decision-making, from individual choices to group decisions, and explores how to navigate

conflicts and achieve consensus within your team. By mastering these skills, you will be

better equipped to lead your team through complex decisions that drive your project

forward.

Unit 5.1 Methods of Decision-making

This unit explores the different methods of decision-making that can be used in a project

team. You will learn about various approaches, including authoritative, consultative, and

consensus-based decision-making, and understand when each method is most

appropriate. The unit also covers the benefits and challenges of each method, helping you

choose the right approach for different situations. Understanding these methods will enable

you to make informed decisions that reflect the needs and goals of your team

Read pages 84—85 of your textbook for more information.

building consensus, ensuring that all team members are on the same page and committed

to the decisions made. By mastering conflict resolution and consensus-building techniques,

you can create a more cohesive and productive team environment.
