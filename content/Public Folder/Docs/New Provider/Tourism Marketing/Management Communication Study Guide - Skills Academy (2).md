Effective communication is essential in the workplace and involves sharing information and
ideas between people who see things differently. It includes both speaking and listening,
and can be direct, like face-to-face conversations, or indirect, like emails. Non-verbal cues,

different methods and improving listening skills are key to better communication.

Chapter Summary
This chapter explains how self-image influences our interactions with others. Self-image
includes various dimensions that shape our behavior and attitudes towards ourselves and
others. Imbalances in self-image can create challenges in relationships, and practical
solutions are suggested for real-life applications. The chapter also explores Maslow’s
hierarchy of needs, which helps understand self-development progression.
Society is made up of different groups that influence our values and behaviors, which can
have positive or negative effects. The concept of reference groups is introduced,
categorising how we relate to different social circles.
Psychological models show that interacting with others involves dealing with diverse
viewpoints and potential communication barriers. Johari’s Window is used to frame these
interactions and suggest ways to overcome communication challenges. The chapter
emphasises communication in social interactions as opportunities to address and resolve
issues effectively.
