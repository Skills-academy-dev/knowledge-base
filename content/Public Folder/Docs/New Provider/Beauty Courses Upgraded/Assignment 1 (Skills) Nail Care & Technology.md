Section B: Practical Task 1 – Professional Image and Workstation Setup (10

marks)

Activity:

Demonstrate how a professional nail technician should present themselves and prepare a

clean, organised workstation before a manicure or pedicure service.

Requirements:

●  Present a neat and professional appearance (clean uniform, tidy hair, short nails).

●  Prepare a workstation with sanitised tools, clean towels, and properly arranged

materials.

●  Display correct hygiene and safety practices (use of PPE and disinfectants).

Evidence to Submit:

●  One clear photo of your professional appearance.

●  One clear photo of your workstation setup.

●  A short paragraph (5–6 sentences) explaining why professional presentation and

hygiene are important in building client trust.

Section C: Practical Task 2 – Infection Control and Nail Anatomy Identification

(15 marks)

Activity:

Demonstrate your understanding of infection control and the anatomy of the nail by completing

the following:

1.  Prepare a short written procedure describing how you clean and disinfect tools and

surfaces in the salon.

2.  Draw and label a clear diagram of the structure of the nail.

Evidence to Submit:

●  One written paragraph (6–8 lines) outlining your sanitation and disinfection routine.

●  One labelled diagram of the nail showing at least five parts: Matrix, Nail Plate, Nail Bed,

Cuticle, Free Edge.

●  One photo of your labelled diagram (hand-drawn).

8

Section D: Practical Task 3 – Product Safety and Aftercare Poster (15 marks)

Activity:

Design a one-page poster that could be displayed in a salon to teach clients how to safely care

for their nails after a manicure or pedicure.

Your poster must include:

●  Five aftercare tips (e.g., moisturise daily, avoid using nails as tools).

●  Two safety reminders about product handling or storage.

●  One image, sketch, or graphic to make your poster appealing.

●  Use neat, readable writing and professional presentation.

Evidence to Submit:

●  A clear photo or scanned copy of your completed poster.
