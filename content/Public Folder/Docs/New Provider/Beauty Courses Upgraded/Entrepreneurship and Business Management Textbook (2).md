1\. Introduction: What is Entrepreneurship?

Entrepreneurship is the innovative establishment of a business or businesses to generate profit. An entrepreneur is a person who sets up a business with the aim of making a profit. Entrepreneurs see possibilities and solutions, whereas the average person only sees annoyances and problems. Bill Gates and Steve Jobs are the world's most celebrated entrepreneurs. An understanding of what en- trepreneurship is can help more people recognise the value they contribute to the world.

By starting a business or businesses, entrepreneurs employ themselves and create jobs for others. Entrepreneurs can employ several other people in their business. For example, they can employ someone to work as an intrapreneur. An intrapreneur is an employee who is given the authority and support to create a new product without having to be concerned about whether or not the product will become a source of revenue for the company.

He or she can be a manager within a company whose role is to promote innovative product develop- ment and marketing of the company's products or services. Unlike an entrepreneur, who faces per- sonal risk when a product fails to produce revenue, an intrapreneur will continue to receive a salary even if the product fails to make it to production.

Entrepreneur: An entrepreneur is a person who takes on financial risks with the goal of starting and managing a new business venture.

Intrapreneur: An Intrapreneur is an employee within a company who takes on an entrepreneurial role, typically by developing new ideas or projects within the organisation.

1.1 The Role of Entrepreneurs and Intrapreneurs

The role of the entrepreneur is different from that of an intrapreneur. This section covers these roles.

1.1.1 The Role of an Entrepreneur

An entrepreneur performs quite a few roles. They are:

• Employment creation

• Creation of an industrial environment

• Transformers or agents of change

• Organiser of the society's productive resources

• Help in capital formation

• Searching for new markets and development of existing markets

• Development of new production techniques and raw materials

• Contribution to the execution of government policies

Page | 8

Employment creation

Entrepreneurs create jobs. They create maximum employment opportunities in society by way of establishing new industries, developing and expanding existing industries, and undertaking innova- tive activities.

As a result, unemployed persons in society get opportunities for employment. Entrepreneurs take on the risk of employing themselves first, and their ambition to continue their business's growth eventually leads to the creation of new jobs for others. As their business contin- ues to grow, even more jobs are created. Thus, lowering unemployment rates and reducing poverty in the country.

The creation of jobs is a social cause achieved through entrepreneurship. Increasing employment opportunities by establishing new industries, as well as increasing per capita in- come and rate of capital investment. All these activities prove highly helpful in bringing the people of the country out of the vicious circle of poverty. Thus, the entrepreneur contributes to the removal of poverty. When new avenues of employment are opening, various new commodities and services will be produced and distributed, and the consumers will get these at the lowest possible cost, and the economic and social problems will gradually decrease.

Creation of an Industrial Environment

They search for opportunities to create an industrial environment which inspires other people. An entrepreneur makes optimum utilisation of natural and human resources available in the country, and this enhances national productivity by efficient utilisation of unused resources with their mana- gerial skills. They shift the economic resources from low-productivity areas to high-productivity are- as, which helps in establishing an industrial environment.

Through this role, entrepreneurs establish industrial ventures by arranging the necessary resources, developing innovations and bearing related uncertainties. They help in awakening several industrial possibilities. In this role, entrepreneurs become captains of modern industries because they not only bear the risks and uncertainties, but they manage, predict, invent new production methods and build an economic structure to maximise profits.

Transformers or Agents of Change

Entrepreneurs are agents of change. They might create a new product that solves a burning prob- lem or take on the challenge of exploring something never explored before. Entrepreneurs add to national income by generating new wealth in an economy.

New ideas and improved products or services from entrepreneurs allow for the growth of new mar- kets and new wealth to be created in an economy. In their transformation role, entrepreneurs en- courage investigation and research on business opportunities and other prospects for social and economic development.

They carry out innovations and encourage new techniques of production, taking risks in using new machinery and equipment and developing new markets. The entrepreneur tries to work out the pos- sibilities of production and distribution of new products and services through innovations, inventions, and experiments and thereafter presents such products and services in the markets. Thus, they are always attentive to the possibilities of new commodities and services.

The Organiser of the Society's Productive Resources

An entrepreneur organises society's productive resources. In performing this role, entrepreneurs assemble the unused natural, physical and human resources of the society, establishing effective

coordination between them. This makes economic activities dynamic through maximum production at minimum cost, which enables the development of new production areas and dimensions.

In doing this, entrepreneurs also discover various new uses of raw materials and semi-finished products and also give material shape to these new inventions. This enables the optimum utilisation of resources.

Help in Capital Formation

Entrepreneurs help in the formation of capital. The increase in the rate of capital formation is quite essential for the economic development of any country. Where there is no capital formation, a coun- try remains backwards from an industrial development point of view. Thus, through capital for- mation, entrepreneurs provide stimulation to economic forces in capital formation. Successful entre- preneurs do not get satisfied only with the existing establishment of a business enterprise but go on making efforts by the establishment of several new business enterprises to increase their chances of making profits.

Searching for New markets and Development of Existing Markets

Entrepreneurs do not feel satisfied with the existing market products but continuously go on search- ing for new markets for sale promotion and also develop the markets. They are also regularly occu- pied in developing and expanding the existing markets to increase their profits.

This is achieved through the use of various techniques and methods, like market research, motiva- tion research, buying habits survey, consumer satisfaction, advertisements and sales promotions, product diversifications, incentives to intermediaries and sellers, etc. They also facilitate the estab- lishment of industries in undeveloped and backward areas of the country.

Development of New Production Techniques and Raw Materials

Entrepreneurs do not feel contented with the existing techniques of production; hence they conduct various experiments to save time, labour and capital in the production process. In the same way, they search for ways to improve the variety and quality of their product and service. As a result, they facilitate improvements in products and services such that consumers regularly get good quality products. Entrepreneurs also search for new sources of raw materials and semi-finished products and development of their alternative substances. They are responsible for executing innovations in each area and activity of production, which gives them an important role in transforming production processes. Entrepreneurs bring a revolution in productivity.

Contribution to the Execution of Government Policies

Entrepreneurs provide an important contribution to implementing government policies and achieving national goals. They cooperate with the government in the implementation of the development plans of the country. For example, entrepreneurs search industrial opportunities and establish various new industries to utilise those opportunities, which contributes to the rapid economic development of the country. In this respect, entrepreneurs widen the path of development by joining hands with the gov- ernment. In this regard, they help the government to minimise socio-economic problems.

Entrepreneurs help increase household incomes, savings and capital formation by establishing busi- ness enterprises and encouraging industrial ventures. This reduces various social problems, like poverty, illiteracy, low living standards, dowry system, social offences, atrocities on women, and ex- ploitation of child labour, among others. By supporting government policies, entrepreneurs also play a significant role in building up of self-sufficient society.

1.1.2 The Role of an Intrapreneur

After establishing the enterprise, the entrepreneur hands over the running of the firm into the hands of an efficient manager or an intrapreneur who will lead the company to high productivity. An intra- preneur performs the following roles:

• Strategy formulation and planning

• Innovation and idea generation

• Leadership and motivation

• Strategy idea implementers

• Problem-solving

• Expansion of the enterprise

Strategy Formulation and Planning

It is the mandate of the intrapreneur to ensure that appropriate strategies and plans for the firm are established so that the organisation can be successful. Effective intrapreneurial strategies always help the organisation to win over its competitors and grab every opportunity it can. The ability of an enterprise to gain a competitive advantage is based on excellent intrapreneurship. Through strategic planning, intrapreneurs increase the productivity of the business. They are focused on meeting work goals to reach milestones.

Innovation and Idea Generation

Intrapreneurs are innovative and create effective ideas to effect change in the company. An intrapre- neur will recognise what moves the company needs to make to reach a new level of growth. They are always imagining solutions that would make the operations of a company better than it was be- fore. Intrapreneurs identify the idea to evaluate if it is user-friendly, market adaptable and compatible with the set goals and strategies of the enterprise. The intrapreneur presents the innovative idea to other members of an organisation and takes their feedback and suggestions for improvement.

Leadership and Motivation

The utmost task of an intrapreneur is to be the leader and drive other employees within his team and then the organisation for sustainability. Recognising leadership capabilities and supporting intrapre- neurs is the biggest challenge for companies today. In their leadership capacity, intrapreneurs will motivate teams within an organisation to become driven and goal-orientated. They will keep track of workflow and consistently come up with ways to improve the overall production of a company.

Strategy idea Implementers

To implement the discussed proposals, the intrapreneur forms a team which puts the idea into ac- tion and makes the idea an enterprise. They are responsible for implementing start-up practices within a large organisation, producing valued innovation and suggesting change with caution. During the implementation of programmes, projects, policies and plans, intrapreneurs come up with solu- tions to problems that cause glitches in the workflow to ensure effective productivity from the team.

Problem-Solving

Intrapreneurs are described as skilled problem solvers as they identify and address all internal oper- ational challenges to improve the processes and systems of the company. They are tasked with glossing over every interference that slows down the business’s output. They do this by investigat- ing the issues and suggesting creative ways to fix the issue.

Page | 11

In this role, intrapreneurs may see the gaps in the business and try to fill them. They will identify methods that need to be improved and evaluate how this can be achieved. Intrapreneurs will rise to the occasion and suggest brave business changes that will bring success.

Expansion of the Enterprise

The ultimate goal of intrapreneurship is to create the kind of entrepreneurial mindset and infrastruc- ture which are needed to support growth in an organisation. Intrapreneurs help organisations gener- ate new business growth.

Generally, intrapreneurs are business-minded people and focus on how they can best improve the processes of the business to achieve success. Intrapreneurs are responsible for introducing ideas, inspiring creativity, and leading and motivating those around them. They take proactive steps to im- prove business operations, and they ensure the business meets targets to produce great results. Intrapreneurs are almost like-minded entrepreneurs in that they are driven to increase the growth of the company.

Intrapreneurs work extremely well with entrepreneurs in that they help them increase production within a company. They take your business to another level which makes them valuable employees that cause the business to soar and become prosperous. Business owners need to have a trained eye to recognise these intrapreneurs and allow them to lead. When an entrepreneur has identified these individuals, they should be promoted and tasked with managing the operations of your com- pany. They should be groomed and given guidance while they ensure the smooth running of your business.

Intrapreneurs are passionate about achieving success. As a result, they take risks and are not afraid to fail. They are brave risk-takers who are not afraid to revolutionise the companies that they work for. They are willing to challenge every excuse to find the perfect resolution. Intrapreneurs will take risks that may not be favourable in the eyes of other employees, but their overall goal is to see the company thrive. They are eager to explore options that may take the business to new horizons. In- trapreneurs are not afraid to fail. Intrapreneurs know that failure is sometimes inevitable, and they are not easily discouraged. They will learn quickly from their mistakes, do better and keep on going forward.

As leaders, intrapreneurs become gems to the company that fuels the purpose of the business and provide direction to other employees. The competencies shown by an intrapreneur are creativity, leadership, perseverance, collaboration, planning and being a change agent. The main difference between an intrapreneur and an entrepreneur is that an intrapreneur fo- cuses on the internal operations of the company, whereas an entrepreneur focuses on the vision and overall goals of the business.

1.2 Personal Qualities of the Entrepreneur

There is a matrix of personal qualities and characteristics that ensure that entrepreneurs succeed in their endeavours. These can be natural traits or qualities that can be developed over time. The main personal qualities and characteristics that can make someone a successful entrepreneur include but are not limited to self-belief, having effective planning skills, the ability to analyse the business envi- ronment one is operating in, exceptional ability to manage finances and the ability to establish and develop business networks. These characteristics and qualities are not mutually exclusive. Rather, they are mutually reinforcing and should be possessed by someone as a combination of successful entrepreneurship. The following are some of the main qualities expected from an entrepreneur:

• Confidence in their ability to succeed

• Preference for moderate risk

• Desire for responsibility

• Energetic

• Identity opportunities

• Skilled at organising

• Urge to achieve

• Desire for immediate feedback

Confidence in their Ability to Succeed

Entrepreneurs are confident and optimistic, and they are open to new ideas which cross their path. An entrepreneur does not ask questions about whether they can succeed or whether they are wor- thy of success. They are confident in the knowledge that they will make their businesses succeed. They exude that confidence in everything they do. These individuals are determined to make their businesses work and eliminate any hindrances or distractions to their goals. They have overarching strategies and outline the tactics to accomplish them. Successful entrepreneurs are disciplined enough to take steps every day toward the achievement of their objectives.

They are possibilities thinkers who believe there are possibilities and are not limited by commonly held boundaries. They look beyond to ask the "what ifs" questions. They look for answers and solu- tions and aren't daunted by challenges. They relish challenges.

Preference for Moderate Risk

Entrepreneurs engage in calculated risk-taking. Contrary to common wisdom, entrepreneurs are not risk addicts.

Instead, they take reasonable or moderate risks. While taking risks is part of the entrepreneurship process, those who succeed weigh the options, see what else is out there, and be careful about the types of risks they are willing to take for themselves and those that might rely on them. As part of risk management, entrepreneurs reach out for help when needed. Successful entrepreneurs realise that they don't know everything. To prepare for possible risks, entrepreneurs will pull in resources and seek the advice of experts to help them achieve their goals. They know that the key to getting things done is to find the right people who can do the job. They understand their strengths and weaknesses – and find people who can complement them in areas that they need the most.

Desire for Responsibility

Entrepreneurs have a stronger desire for responsibility, and they always want to be in control and capable of handling more responsibilities.

Page | 13

They welcome the responsibility that goes into owning a business. They carve their path, relying on their talents, instincts, and skills and do not take orders from anyone. They are responsible enough to make decisions on their own without waiting for someone to push them. While being in charge can be daunting at times, entrepreneurs are motivated by the rewards of seeing the results of all the hard work are tremendous.

Successful entrepreneurs strongly believe in themselves. Many of them are supremely confident and responsible individuals. However, their strong belief in themselves doesn't necessarily mean that they don't experience self-doubt. They experience fear, but instead of running away from their fears, they embrace what they fear the most and just plod on. Successful entrepreneurs are not afraid of making mistakes because they see it as an opportunity to learn and improve. They don't focus on blame and retribution and instead work on finding solutions to rectify the mistake and pre- vent it from happening again.

Energetic

Entrepreneurs possess a high level of energy and sustainability over long hours to make their busi- nesses successful. They are tenacious, always finding a way to display persistence or able to dig deep within themselves to find it. When things don't go their way, they can handle thousands of re- jections along the journey. They genuinely love their work, and they direct all their energy towards the success of their businesses.

Due to their high energy levels, they are willing to put in those extra hours to make the business suc- ceed. Successful entrepreneurs want to see what the view is like at the top of the business moun- tain. Once they see it, they want to go further. They use their energy to ensure that their products or services succeed not only in terms of profits but to bring about positive change in the communities they live in by solving existing problems or filling existing gaps.

Entrepreneurs have a strong faith in their ideas, their capabilities and themselves. After all, to suc- ceed, they constantly need to ask themselves hard questions and then come up with ways to an- swer them. Their energy is tied to their passion for doing their work. Thus, successful entrepreneurs are passionate about what they do, and their work feels more like fun, play and inspiration. They are energised by the activities and challenges of their business. Even if they fail often, their passion keeps them going until they get it right.

Identify Opportunities

Entrepreneurs are open-minded as they realise that every event and situation is a business oppor- tunity. They see opportunities where others do not. Those who succeed in business have finely honed business skills based on their environment, education, experience and innate intuitive abili- ties. What sets them apart is their inherent sense of what is "right" for a business. They can spot op- portunities for growth with loads of courage to pursue these opportunities. They love the challenge of pitting their resources and skills against the environment.

Thus, an entrepreneur must be sensitive to the business environment to identify opportunities, ex- ploit them and grow their business empires. Opportunity identification is possible through environ- mental analysis. Thus, successful entrepreneurs should not adopt a rigid mindset because the envi- ronment is not static but dynamic.

A prolific entrepreneur is one who is capable of scanning the environment and identifying possible threats that require responsive action so that he remains relevant in a demanding and competitive environment.

Page | 14

The ability to adapt and be flexible about clients and customer or market needs is one quality that a successful entrepreneur must possess. The ability to be sensitive to the environment also helps the entrepreneur to have adequate knowledge about markets and products or services required by cli- ents. This will enable the entrepreneur to analyse possible threats that can emanate from competi- tors and design ways to survive in a competitive industry.

Skilled at Organising

Effective organising skills are also essential for entrepreneurs to achieve their endeavours. This will enable the entrepreneur to establish accomplishable goals that will be worked on over a defined pe- riod. For an entrepreneur to be successful, there is first a need to develop or design a clear set of goals to guide all possible courses of action. In organising, the entrepreneur must be capable of developing a realistic plan or roadmap that sets out what to be done, when it will be done and who will be responsible for the activities to be done.

The absence of clarity of purpose is a sign of poor organising skills, and this can lead the entrepre- neur into regrettable realities such as financial losses and a tarnished corporate or individual image or reputation. They are self-starters; that is, entrepreneurs know that if something needs to be done, they should start it themselves. They set the parameters and make sure that projects follow that path. They are proactive, not waiting for someone to permit them.

Entrepreneurs have a clear vision for the future – what they want to achieve, what opportunities they need, and how they can accomplish it. Their vision gives them purpose and helps maintain their fo- cus on what they want the business to achieve. It also helps them overcome the many humps and challenges they meet along the way. The skill of organising requires the entrepreneur to have strong people management skills.

The entrepreneur must have strong communication skills to motivate employees. Most successful entrepreneurs know how to motivate their employees so the business grows overall. They are very good at highlighting the benefits of any situation and coaching others to their success. Thus, an en- trepreneur's skill at the organisation is aided by good interpersonal skills. They recognise that much of their success will depend on how well they deal with people.

Urge to Achieve

The most important personal quality for successful entrepreneurs is a strong belief in their capability to achieve big things in life. The desire to achieve makes entrepreneurs dream big; that is, they can envision a new reality and have the will to bring it to fruition. They have crazy big dreams, even though, to some, these dreams may not be realistic or practical. But it is the big dreamers who suc- ceed. Successful entrepreneurs must not have a sceptical mentality but should possess confidence in their ability to succeed. The entrepreneur needs to develop self-confidence and self-motivation in all their endeavours, especially when venturing into new projects or making moves to exploit new markets.

An exceptional entrepreneur should possess stubborn faith towards the accomplishment of set goals and orient themself to a strong work ethic and commitment to working hard. Without astound- ing faith and self-belief in one's vision, it is very difficult and next to impossible for the entrepreneur to have a successful career. Together with having self-belief, the entrepreneur should also be disci- plined and dedicated to being successful.\\

Exceptional entrepreneurs are risk-takers who are always ready to dive deep into a future of uncer- tainty, albeit with caution. Thus, a successful entrepreneur should not be afraid to take risks but must do so carefully. Driven by the urge to achieve, entrepreneurs become competitive as they have a strong conviction that they can do a job better than another. succeed, such that they will try and try again until it does.
