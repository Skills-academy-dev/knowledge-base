ASSIGNMENT 1
Modules Covered: 1 – 3
Assessment Type: Practical (Photographic Evidence) with Student Reflection
Total Marks: 60
General Instructions
● This assignment is a practical assessment.
● You must submit clear photographic evidence for each task as instructed.
● All images must be clearly labelled (Image 1, Image 2, etc.).
● Images must reflect your own practical work.
● Client identity must be protected in all images.
● Written sections are student reflections, not theory answers.
● Poor-quality or missing images will result in marks being deducted or not awarded.
TASK 1: Hygiene, Safety, And Client Preparation
This task assesses your ability to prepare a professional lash and brow workspace according
to hygiene, safety, and client consultation requirements. Correct preparation is essential to
prevent infection, manage risk, and ensure professional service delivery.
Photographic practical evidence (15 Marks)
Submit the following labelled images:
● Image 1: Workstation Preparation
Clean treatment bed, trolley, lighting, and surrounding area (2 marks)
● Image 2: Tool Sanitation Setup
Disinfected tools stored correctly (e.g. tweezers, scissors) (3 marks)
● Image 3: Disposable Materials
Single-use items prepared for service (e.g. micro-brushes, mascara wands, pads) (2
marks)
● Image 4: Technician Hygiene
Clean hands, gloves, hair secured, professional appearance(3 marks)
● Image 5: Client Consultation Record
Completed consultation form (client details may be covered) (5 marks)
Subtotal: 15 Marks
5

Marking Rubric (Practical– 15 Marks)
Level Description
Excellent Images are clear, well-lit, and clearly demonstrate correct professional
preparation
Satisfactory Images demonstrate preparation but lack clarity or minor elements are
missing
Limited Images are unclear, poorly labelled, or show incomplete preparation
Not Achieved Images missing or do not demonstrate required preparation
Student Reflection (5 Marks)
Write a short reflection (approximately 3-5 sentences) in which you reflect on:
● What you learned about professional hygiene and client preparation
● One aspect you found challenging or required extra attention
● How proper preparation supports client safety and professional practice
Subtotal: 5 Marks

TASK 2: Temporary Lash Application
This task evaluates your ability to apply temporary lash enhancements safely and correctly.
Temporary lashes are designed for short-term wear and require appropriate product
selection, careful application, and correct aftercare guidance.
Photographic practical evidence (15 Marks)
Submit the following labelled images:
● Image 1: Selected Temporary Lash Type
Strip, cluster, or magnetic lashes (2 marks)
● Image 2: Lash Measurement and Preparation
Measuring, trimming, or preparing lashes before application (3 marks)
● Image 3: Adhesive Application
Correct adhesive type and amount used (3 marks)
● Image 4: Lash Placement
Lashes positioned correctly along the lash line (4 marks)
● Image 5: Final Finished Look
Lashes secured, blended, and sitting correctly (3 marks)
Subtotal: 15 Marks
Marking Rubric (Practical– 15 Marks)
Level Description
Excellent Application process is clearly demonstrated with correct
technique
Satisfactory Most steps are shown but clarity or technique is inconsistent
Limited Images show incomplete or incorrect application steps
Not Achieved Images missing or application not demonstrated
7

Student Reflection (5 Marks)
Write a short reflection (approximately 3-5 sentences) in which you reflect on:
● What you learned about temporary lash application
● One step you found difficult or required practice
● How correct application and aftercare affect the final result and client comfort
Subtotal: 5 Marks
TASK 2 TOTAL: 20 MARKS
8

TASK 3: Semi-Permanent Lash Design And Mapping
This task focuses on the planning and design stage of semi-permanent lash extensions.
Proper lash mapping, material selection, and weight control are essential for achieving a safe,
balanced, and professional outcome.
Photographic practical evidence (15 Marks)
Submit the following labelled images:
● Image 1: Lash Material and Curl Selection Trays
showing selected material and curl (3 marks)
● Image 2: Client Eye Assessment
Natural lashes and eye shape (client identity protected) (3 marks)
● Image 3: Lash Mapping Diagram
Drawn lash map on pad or paper (4 marks)
● Image 4: Length and Weight Planning
Selected lengths and diameters showing safe planning (3 marks)
● Image 5: Style Reference
Chosen style (Natural, Doll, or Cat Eye) illustrated or labelled (2 marks)
Subtotal: 15 Marks
Marking Rubric (Practical– 15 Marks)
Level Description
Excellent Lash design and mapping are clear, accurate, and professionally
planned
Satisfactory Design is mostly correct but lacks precision or clarity
Limited Design planning is incomplete or incorrect
Not Achieved Images missing or design principles not demonstrated
9

Student Reflection (5 Marks)
Write a short reflection (approximately 3-5 sentences) in which you reflect on:
● What you learned about lash mapping and design planning
● One area where you felt uncertain or needed careful consideration
● How planning before application helps prevent damage and poor retention
Subtotal: 5 Marks
