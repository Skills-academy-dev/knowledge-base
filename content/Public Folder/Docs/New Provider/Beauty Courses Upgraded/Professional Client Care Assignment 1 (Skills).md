ASSIGNMENT 1
SECTION A: THEORY (15 Marks)
QUESTION 1: Multiple Choice (10 Marks)
Choose the correct answer for each question.
1. Which of the following BEST describes “professionalism” in client care?
A. Wearing expensive clothing
B. Acting with skill, respect, and care
C. Using complex terminology
D. Giving clients discounts
2. Which communication method creates the strongest first impression?
A. Silence
B. Body language
C. Long emails
D. Formal reports
3. When a client expresses dissatisfaction, the FIRST step is to:
A. Ignore the complaint
B. Blame another department
C. Actively listen and acknowledge
D. Ask the client to put it in writing
4. Good non-verbal communication includes:
A. Rolling your eyes
B. Crossed arms
C. Facing the client and nodding
D. Checking your phone
5. Which of the following shows service orientation?
A. Telling a client to wait without explanation
B. Providing clear, proactive assistance
C. Using slang with the client
D. Giving short, one-word answers
6. A key principle of client confidentiality is:
A. Sharing client info with colleagues freely
B. Keeping all client details private unless required by law
C. Printing client records for personal reference
D. Asking other clients for advice
7. Active listening requires:
A. Talking more than the client
B. Interrupting politely
C. Preparing your next answer
D. Showing interest through verbal and non-verbal responses
5

8. Emotional intelligence helps client-care workers to:
A. Avoid all emotional responses
B. Understand and manage emotions
C. Become defensive
D. Work without feedback
9. A client needs assessment helps you understand:
A. The client’s favourite activities
B. Unrelated personal details
C. The client’s expectations, priorities, and concerns
D. Your own goals
10. A professional greeting must include:
A. Your name and purpose of contact
B. A joke
C. A long personal story
D. Whispering your introduction
QUESTION 2: True or False (5 Marks)
State whether the following statements are TRUE or FALSE.
1. Non-verbal communication has little impact on client interactions.
2. Good client service includes listening, empathy, and respect.
3. Clients should be addressed by slang or nicknames to build rapport.
4. Apologising professionally is an important part of service recovery.
5. Body language can influence how safe and comfortable a client feels.

SECTION B: PRACTICAL (35 Marks)
QUESTION 1: Write a Professional Client Email (15 Marks)
Scenario: A client emailed your organisation complaining that their query was not responded
to within the promised 48 hours. They feel ignored and are considering switching to another
service provider.
Write a professional email responding to the client. Your email must include:
1. A respectful greeting
2. Acknowledgement of the complaint
3. A professional apology
4. A clear explanation or assurance
5. The next steps/solutions
6. A courteous closing
Length: ± 10–14 lines
Format: Professional email structure
QUESTION 1 MARKING RUBRIC (15 Marks)
Criteria Marks Description
Tone & Professionalism 3 Respectful, calm, client-centred
Acknowledgement & Apology 3 Shows empathy and accountability
Explanation / Clarification 4 Clear, helpful, appropriate
Solution / Next Steps 3 Action-focused and reassuring
Structure & Grammar 2 Neat, readable, no major errors
Total: 15
