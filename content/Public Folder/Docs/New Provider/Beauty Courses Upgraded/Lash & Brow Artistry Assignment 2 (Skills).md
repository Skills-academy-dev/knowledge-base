ASSIGNMENT 2
Modules Covered: 4 – 6
Assessment Type: Practical (Photographic Evidence) with Student Reflection
Total Marks: 60
General Instructions
● This is a practical, evidence-based assignment
● You must use materials, tools, notes, and equipment you already have.
● Students are NOT required to purchase new products or tools for this assignment
● Where a live model or product is not available, simulated, practice-based, or
planning evidence is acceptable.
● You must submit clear photographic evidence as instructed.
● All images must be clearly labelled (Image 1, Image 2, etc.).
● Images must show your own work.
● Client identity must be protected at all times.
● Written sections are student reflections, not theory answers.
● Unclear, incorrect, or missing images will result in marks being deducted.
TASK 4: Lash lift & tint services
This task assesses your understanding and practical application of lash lift and tint services.
You may demonstrate this task using a live model, practice head, training lashes, or
simulated setup using the tools and materials you already own.
Photographic practical evidence (15 Marks)
Submit the following labelled images using available or simulated materials:
● Image 1: Lash Lift Preparation
Prepared eye area, eye pads, tools, or a simulated preparation layout (3 marks)
● Image 2: Shield/Rod Selection
Correct shield or rod choice demonstrated using available tools or training aids (3
marks)
● Image 3: Lash Lift Application Technique
Demonstration of lifting, alignment, or mapped placement (live, practice, or simulated)
(4 marks)
● Image 4: Lash Tinting Process
Tint application shown using real product, mock product, or labelled simulation
(3 marks)
5

● Image 5: Lash Lift Aftercare Preparation
Aftercare card, notes, or visual instructions prepared for the client (2 marks)
Subtotal: 15 Marks
Marking Rubric (Practical– 15 Marks)
Level Description
Excellent Steps are clearly demonstrated using available or simulated
resources
Satisfactory Most steps shown, minor gaps or unclear images
Limited Incomplete demonstration or weak simulation
Not Achieved Images missing or unrelated
Student Reflection (5 Marks)
Write a short reflection (3-5 sentences) on:
● What you learned about lash lift and tint services
● One step you found challenging, even in simulation
● How correct preparation and aftercare protect natural lashes

TASK 5: Design, Shaping, Colour, And Lamination
This task assesses your ability to plan and demonstrate brow services using existing tools,
practice sheets, brow mapping templates, or a live/practice model. You are not required
to perform a full service if products are unavailable.
Photographic practical evidence (15 Marks)
Submit the following labelled images using what you already have:
● Image 1: Natural Brow Assessment
Live model, practice head, printed face chart, or brow diagram (3 marks)
● Image 2: Brow Mapping
Mapping shown using pencil, string, ruler, or drawn template (4 marks)
● Image 3: Brow Shaping Technique
Demonstration of shaping method (tools laid out, hand positioning, or diagram) (3
marks)
● Image 4: Brow Colour or Lamination Planning
Tint/lamination products OR written colour/lamination plan shown visually (3 marks)
● Image 5: Final Brow Design
Completed brow shape (real, practice, or illustrated) (2 marks)
Subtotal: 15 Marks
Marking Rubric (Practical– 15 Marks)
Level Description
Excellent Brow planning and design clearly demonstrated using available
resources
Satisfactory Design mostly correct with minor inconsistencies
Limited Incomplete planning or unclear demonstration
Not Achieved Images missing or unrelated

Student Reflection (5 Marks)
Write a short reflection (3-5 sentences) on:
● What you learned about brow design and services
● One aspect you found difficult, such as symmetry or colour choice
● How brow planning improves client satisfaction

TASK 6: Building your own Lash & Brow Business
This task assesses your ability to apply business concepts using planning documents,
mock setups, digital tools, or handwritten materials. You are not required to register a
business or incur any costs.
TIP: Using Free Digital Tools
You are encouraged to use free digital design tools such as Canva to create any required
designs, layouts, price lists, aftercare cards, marketing material, or business visuals for this
assignment.
Using Canva or similar free platforms allows you to demonstrate planning and creativity
without needing to print materials or spend money. Screenshots or exported designs are
acceptable as photographic evidence.
Photographic practical evidence (15 Marks)
Submit the following labelled images using existing or mock materials:
● Image 1: Business Setup Concept
Home workspace, mock salon layout, or drawn setup plan (3 marks)
● Image 2: Service Price List
Handwritten, printed, or digital price list (3 marks)
● Image 3: Booking or Scheduling System
Appointment book, phone calendar, or mock booking page (3 marks)
● Image 4: Client Retention Tool
Loyalty card design, aftercare reminder, or rebooking plan (3 marks)
● Image 5: Marketing or Branding Example
Social media draft, flyer design, logo sketch, or caption plan (3 marks)
Subtotal: 15 Marks
9

Marking Rubric (Practical– 15 Marks)
Level Description
Excellent Business planning is realistic and professionally presented
Satisfactory Most elements shown but lack refinement
Limited Incomplete or unclear planning
Not Achieved Images missing or unrelated
Student Reflection (5 Marks)
Write a short reflection (3-5 sentences) on:
● What you learned about running a lash and brow business
● One area you found challenging or unfamiliar
● How good planning supports long-term success
