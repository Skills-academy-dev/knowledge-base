Section B: Practical Task 1 – Nail Enhancement Application (10 marks)

Activity:

Demonstrate how to apply one nail enhancement system safely and correctly.

You may choose between:

●  Acrylic, Gel, or Dipping System.

Requirements:

●  Prepare the natural nail correctly (cleaning, filing, and applying primer).

●  Apply and shape the enhancement evenly.

●  Ensure neat application and professional finishing.

Evidence to Submit:

●  2 clear photos: one during the process, one after completion.

●  A short written paragraph (5–6 sentences) describing the method you used and the

safety precautions you followed.

Section C: Practical Task 2 – Add-On Treatment or Nail Art Design (15 marks)

Activity:

Perform one of the following:

●  A hand or foot add-on treatment (e.g., paraffin wax, exfoliation, or mask), OR

●  A simple nail art design (e.g., ombré, dotting, or striping).

Requirements:

●  Prepare and sanitise the area.

●  Apply or perform the treatment/design safely.

●  Present neat, creative, and professional results.

Evidence to Submit:

●  2–3 clear photos showing before, during, and after.

●  Short written reflection (6–8 sentences) explaining the purpose and benefits of the

chosen service for the client.

8

Section D: Practical Task 3 – Professional Salon Practice (15 marks)

Activity:

Demonstrate professional salon conduct and safe post-service procedures.

This task includes:

●  Cleaning and disinfecting the workstation after a service.

●  Properly disposing of waste materials.

●  Completing a sample client record card with aftercare advice.

Evidence to Submit:

●  1 photo of the clean, sanitised workstation.

●  Completed client record card (real or simulated).

●  Short written paragraph explaining why documentation and client aftercare are

important.

Grand Total: 50
