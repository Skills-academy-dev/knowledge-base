ASSIGNMENT 1
TASK 1
Professional Practice, Hygiene & Treatment Area Setup
This task helps you demonstrate your understanding of professionalism, hygiene, safety
standards, and how to set up a treatment area correctly. Even at home, you must show that
you can prepare yourself and your environment like a real facial therapist.
Instructions
● You must prepare yourself and a simple treatment space at home.
● Submit photos and a short written reflection.
Rules
● You can use items you already own (towels, bowls, soap-free cleanser, etc.).
● The area must be clean and safe.
● You must present yourself professionally (hair tied back, neat, hygienic).
● No showing faces of household members unless they consent.
● No candles or unsafe setups.
Steps
A. Photo Evidence Submission
Photo 1: You in professional presentation
Photo 2: Full treatment area
Photo 3: Tools/products organised
Photo 4: Towels, PPE (if available), and waste bin
Photo 5: A hygiene or safety action (e.g., sanitising a surface)
B. Written Reflection (5–7 sentences)
● Why hygiene matters
● How your setup supports client comfort
● Any challenges you faced

TASK 2
Client Consultation & Skin Analysis
This task helps you practise real client communication, consultation techniques, skin
assessment, and treatment planning. You will perform a basic consultation using household
lighting and simple tools.
Instructions
Consult with a volunteer and complete a full skin analysis using whatever you have at home
(mirror, light, magnifying mirror etc.).
Rules
● Use warm, respectful communication.
● Maintain eye contact and good posture.
● Use only clean hands and tools.
● Keep the consultation private and confidential.
● Use safe, gentle pressure during tactile analysis.
Steps
A. Photo Evidence Submission
Photo 1: You consulting with your model
Photo 2: Analysing the forehead
Photo 3: Analysing cheeks/nose
Photo 4: Checking elasticity/texture
B. Complete a simple consultation form (handwritten allowed):
● Client details
● Skin concerns
● Lifestyle factors
● Health information
● Skincare routine
C. Write a Reflection assessment explaining:
● Skin type
● Skin conditions
● Barrier condition
● Recommended treatment goal

TASK 3
Full Facial Procedure Demonstration (20 Marks)
This task allows you to practise a full facial procedure from start to finish. You will
demonstrate cleansing, exfoliating, steaming, massage, masking, and finishing steps using
whatever safe items you have at home.
Instructions
Perform a basic facial on your volunteer and take photo evidence of each step.
Rules
● Use only safe products.
● Do NOT perform extractions if the skin is inflamed or if you are unsure.
● Ensure your model is comfortable at all times.
● Keep your hands clean throughout.
● Work gently and never tug at the skin.
Steps
A. Photo Evidence Submission
Photo 1: Preparation & draping
Photo 2: First cleanse
Photo 3: Second cleanse
Photo 4: Exfoliation
Photo 5: Steaming (towel method or warm bowl)
Photo 6: Extraction technique (optional & safe)
Photo 7: Massage movement
Photo 8: Mask application
Photo 9: Finishing product (moisturiser/SPF if available)
B. Write a short reflection on:
● What went well
● What was difficult
● What you would improve
TASK 3 RUBRIC (20 Marks)
MAIN ASSESSMENT AREAS MARKS
Preparation & client safety 5
Correct execution of main facial steps 10
