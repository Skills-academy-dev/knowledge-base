SECTION B: PRACTICAL (35 Marks)
QUESTION 1: Create a Service Recovery Response Plan (15 Marks)
A client reports that their service request was closed without being resolved. They are
frustrated and say your organisation “never listens.” They want immediate action.
TASK:
Create a Service Recovery Response Plan that includes:
1. Immediate Action Steps (What you will do within the next hour)
2. Communication Strategy (How you will update the client)
3. Service Recovery Message (Short paragraph you would SAY to the client)
4. Root Cause Check (How you will prevent this from happening again)
Length: ± 1 page
QUESTION 1 RUBRIC (15 Marks)
Criteria Marks Description
Immediate action steps 4 Clear, relevant, urgent steps
Communication strategy 4 Professional, client-centred
Service recovery message 4 Empathy, accountability, clarity
Root cause prevention 3 Shows awareness of system improvement
