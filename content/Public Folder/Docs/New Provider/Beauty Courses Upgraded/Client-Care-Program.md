Objectives
We want to be the partner that can be relied upon to achieve our clients’ business goals and our Client
Care program is a fundamental part of realising this objective.
The Client Care program drives our ability to understand the business context that our clients are
operating in, their needs and priorities as well as ways we can tailor service solutions to better meet
their needs.
Client Care visits are a rich source of feedback at which we focus on service needs and less about
technical projects.
It provides a considered and strategic response from our clients and is consistent with our belief in
continuous improvement. The implementation of this program will help us better understand our
clients’ business model, their priorities and provide a path forward to excel at delivery.
Our objective is to complete at least one client care visit annually for each of our Key Clients across our
regional and operating centre portfolios.
For clients, contributing feedback:
• Encourages direct and candid communication between clients and
GHD representatives on performance, issues and actionable improvements.
• Improves GHD’s client service and project delivery to them.
For GHD, gathering feedback:
• Provides us with a mechanism to have deeper conversations and develop relationships with
executives, senior leaders & decisions makers at the client.
• Tells us specifically what we must do to truly exceed our clients expectations.
• Encourages deeper engagement between OCMs, Market Leaders, Service Group Managers,
Relationship Managers (RM), Project Directors and Job Managers on positive and negative
results.
• Provides a consistent reporting mechanism on client satisfaction across GHD.
• Develops an information store to help drive business development.
• Creates measurable outcomes to provide input to KPIs for teams and individuals.

STEP 1 - Understand the purpose
Our clients see the Client Care program as an opportunity to shape the way service is delivered to them
– to get what they want, the way they want it.
It is an opportunity for us, through active listening, to meaningfully strengthen our relationships.
The Client Care Visit provides the forum to better understand our clients and the business and context
they operate in. It provides us the ability to truly learn of their needs, priorities and how they prefer to
communicate with us. Importantly it will help us connect the potential – to look further head and find
better ways.
STEP 2 - Securing the client
RMs should initiate the contact with the client in the first instance, to formally brief them on the
objectives of the visit and ensure the right people will be attending.
When contacting the client, the following messaging can be relayed,
• objective to better understand their needs and the business and context they operate in.
• as they are important to us, the visit will be undertaken by Senior representatives of GHD, provide
names and titles.
• meeting will take no longer than 45min, where we will ask them to respond to questions on our
service and communication style rather then technical issues.
• We will attend at their offices (unless the client determines an alternative location].
• inform them that a formal calendar invitation will be sent to confirm meeting details.
Cold call email
The RM may need to set up a meeting with someone from the client organisation who they
haven’t met before. Visit www.ghd.com/coldcallemail for the fail safe formula to conquer the
cold call email, utilising the messaging above.
STEP 3 - Pre-briefing meeting and using the Interview Guide
The RM meets with interviewers to brief them on how the relationship is working.
• Provide the interviewers with a copy of the Client Care interview guide (page 5/6 of this document).
The objective of these questions is to listen and learn about the client and to look at ways in
which GHD can align our business with theirs.
• Brief interviewers on the nature of the clients business and their best knowledge of
the strategic drivers, ‘burning platform’ and market issues impacting their client.
• Discuss any areas they wish to learn more about.
• Arm interviewers with areas for further investigation that may prove providing difficulties
within the relationship.

Client Care Visit Interview Guide
INTRODUCTION – suggested
Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today. GHD highly values our relationship with
your company and we are committed to making sure we provide our service in a way that makes
us easy to work with and makes your life a little easier.
Today we would like to talk mostly about you and your requirements. We don’t anticipate talking about
technical issues or specific jobs. More about the ‘how we work with you’, rather than the ‘what we do’.
We are keen to talk about service, our people, communication and the other elements that ultimately
make relationships stronger.
RECOMMENDED QUESTIONS
• What are the major issues/opportunities facing your organisation at the moment?
• Of those areas where we work with you, how strategically important are they to your overall
business plan?
• Have you seen our relationship becoming stronger over time? What things should GHD focus on
to ensure this continues to happen?
• Are there things we are doing that you really value? How could we do more of those things?
• Are there things other service providers do (not just in our field) that we could learn from?
• Are there challenges or changes on your horizon that may affect the way we work with you?
How would you like us to respond to those?
• When thinking of a professional relationship with any service provider, what is the most important
thing for you in that relationship?
• If you were assessing all of the firms available to you in our profession, what would prompt you
to choose GHD over any others?
• Are there any particular things you would you like us to stop doing, or do more of?
SPECIFIC ISSUE QUESTIONS – suggest using a mix of these depending on direction of discussion
We UNDERSTAND
• How did you find our people to work with?
• What do your people say about GHD people when working with them?
• How do you rate the way we communicate with you – the channels we use, the frequency?
• Does your team prefer oral or written communications – what level of formality suits you
best?
