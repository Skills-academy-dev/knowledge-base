TASK 1

Advanced Exfoliation & Treatment Documentation Practical

This task allows students to practise advanced exfoliation theory in a safe, home-friendly
way while learning to document treatment outcomes professionally. Because students cannot
perform real chemical peels or microneedling at home, this task simulates the professional
process using safe alternatives, focusing on technique understanding, protocols, and
documentation.

Instructions

You will perform a safe, at-home mechanical exfoliation and complete a full post-treatment
record, just like in a medi-facial clinic.

Rules

●  NO chemical peels allowed.
●  NO dermaplaning with blades.
●  NO microneedling tools of any kind.
●  Only safe household exfoliation can be used (soft washcloth, mild scrub, paste, etc.).

Steps

A. Safe Mechanical Exfoliation Demonstration

Perform an exfoliation using a safe method such as:

●  Warm washcloth
●  Gentle  paste (Oatmeal + Honey Paste/ Aloe vera gel + honey paste/ Mashed

avocado paste / Cucumber + yoghurt paste)

●  Store-bought mild scrub

NOTE : Students must avoid harsh ingredients like baking soda,coarse salt and lemon

Provide 3 photos:

●  Skin before exfoliation
●  Exfoliation technique in progress
●  Skin immediately after

B. Contraindication Screening Sheet

Write a short screening checklist BEFORE exfoliating, including:

●  Skin sensitivity
●  Sunburn
●  Eczema/psoriasis
●  Breakouts
●  Medication use
●  Recent treatments

Tick YES/NO and indicate whether you proceeded or modified the plan.

6

C. Post-Treatment Documentation

Complete a short record including:

●  Skin response (pinkness, smoothness, sensitivity)
●  Comfort level
●  Any adverse reactions
●  Recommended aftercare
●  Suggested next treatment

TASK 2

Holistic Facial Experience Design & Demonstration

This task focuses on the holistic theories, relaxation, energy flow, natural products, and
therapeutic touch. Students will design and perform a Holistic Micro Facial using natural
ingredients and holistic techniques.

Instructions

You will create a 10-minute holistic facial routine for a volunteer, using:

●  Natural household ingredients
●  Safe essential oils (optional)
●  Holistic massage movements
●  Relaxation techniques

Rules

●  Essential oils must be diluted if used (1 drop in 1 tbsp carrier oil).
●  No oils on pregnant clients unless safe options are used.
●  No allergens (nuts, cinnamon, citrus on sensitive skin).
●  The model must be comfortable and safe.

Steps

A. Design Your 10-Minute Holistic Facial Routine (Written Plan)

Create a 5–7 step sequence that fits into 10 minutes total, including approximate times.
Your steps must look like the following steps:

●  Breathwork / grounding (30–60 seconds)
●  Light acupressure or pressure point work (1–2 minutes)
●  Holistic facial massage movements (2–3 minutes)
●  Natural mask application (3–4 minutes)
●  Relaxation or energy-balancing technique (30–60 seconds)

Submit your written 10-minute routine with steps + times.

B. Perform the Routine

Submit  4  photos:

●  Beginning breathwork/centering
●  One pressure point or lymph drainage technique
●  Natural mask application
●  Finishing grounding touch / shoulder hold

8

C. Teach Your Client One Self-Massage Technique

Explain verbally and write down:

●  What movement they should do
●  How often
●  What benefit it gives (e.g., calming, drainage, tension relief)

TASK 3

Professional Identity, Salon Startup Planning & Client Care Simulation

Module 6 covers professionalism, business setup, marketing, client care, and legal
awareness. This task helps students practise real-world salon management skills,
including branding, client experience, and business planning.

Instructions

You will create a mini business starter pack for your future facial therapy service.

Rules

●  All information must be original.
●  Business must reflect professional ethics.
●  No real personal information.
●  Keep the design simple and clean.

Steps

A. Create a Professional Identity Statement

Write 4–6 lines describing:

●  Your values
●  Your service style
●  Your mission as a facial therapist

B. Create a Mini Service Menu (4 Items)

Include:

●  2 basic facial treatments
●  1 advanced treatment
●  1 holistic treatment

Each with:

●  Name
●  Short description
●  Estimated duration

C. Create a Simple Social Media Post Mock-Up

Use any free tool (Canva, phone notes, screenshot, or hand-drawn sketch).
The post must include:

●  Your salon name
●  A clean layout
●  A short marketing message
●  A call to action (e.g., “DM to book!”)

Submit  a  screenshot  or  photo.

10

D. Client Care Scenario Response

Write a polite, professional reply to this scenario:

“A client messages you saying: “Hi, my skin is burning a bit after yesterday’s facial. Is
this normal?”

Students must respond with empathy, professionalism, and safety awareness.
