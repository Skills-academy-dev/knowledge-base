ASSIGNMENT 2
TASK 1 — MODULE 4: TIP APPLICATION, BLENDING & TROUBLESHOOTING
PRACTICE (20 Marks)
TASK GOAL: Demonstrate your ability to correctly select, apply, and blend tips while
managing a real problem nail shape (e.g., ski-jump, bitten, fan-shape, hooked, uneven
sidewalls).
IMAGE SUBMISSIONS REQUIRED (4 IMAGES)
Learners must upload:
Image 1: Natural nails before tip application (show the problematic nail shape)
Image 2: Correctly selected tips placed next to the nail (to demonstrate sizing)
Image 3: Tips applied (showing correct angle, no air bubbles, proper contact area)
Image 4: Tips fully blended into the nail plate
MARKING GUIDE — 20 MARKS
Criteria Marks
Correct tip sizing (matching width & curvature) 5
Correct tip application technique (angle, adhesive use, no 5
bubbles)
Clean blending without damaging natural nail 5
Image clarity, professionalism & sequence 5
REFLECTION (Compulsory, Not Marked):
After completing this task, reflect on what you have learned so far and describe any
difficulties you faced during the process.
TASK 2 — MODULE 5: MAINTENANCE, INFILL & REBALANCE PRACTICAL (20 Marks)
TASK GOAL: Perform a maintenance service on an existing enhancement, addressing
lifting, apex regrowth, and structural imbalance.
IMAGE SUBMISSIONS REQUIRED (4 IMAGES)
Learners must upload:
Image 1: Grown-out enhancement before work begins
Image 2: Area after filing and removing lifting
Image 3: Product reapplied and apex rebuilt
Image 4: Finished maintenance nail after filing and refining
(Images should reflect real growth progression, visible lifting or imbalance, as described in
Module 5 content.)
5

MARKING GUIDE — 20 MARKS
Criteria Marks
Safe lifting removal without damaging natural nail 5
Correct rebuilding of apex + structural balance 5
Smooth, clean finishing and refinement 5
Image quality, sequence and professionalism 5
REFLECTION (Compulsory, Not Marked):
After completing this task, reflect on what you have learned so far and describe any
difficulties you faced during the process.
TASK 3 — MODULE 6: ADVANCED DESIGN, BRANDING & PORTFOLIO
PRESENTATION PRACTICAL (20 Marks)
TASK GOAL: Produce a portfolio-ready nail image that showcases one advanced design
using correct lighting, angles, and branding presentation.
IMAGE SUBMISSIONS REQUIRED (3 IMAGES)
Learners must upload:
Image 1: Workstation + photography setup (lighting, background, props if used)
Image 2: Final nail design (high-end, clean, editorial-style execution)
Image 3: Final edited/portfolio-ready image with branding (e.g., watermark, logo, name)
(Design may be: encapsulation, ombré, chrome blend, marbling, line detailing, foils, 3D
elements—anything covered in Module 6.)
✔ MARKING GUIDE — 20 MARKS
Criteria Marks
Professional photography setup (lighting, angles, background) 5
Quality of nail design (neatness, creativity, alignment with Module 6 standards) 5
Final image presentation (branding, cropping, clarity, colour accuracy) 5
Overall professionalism of submission 5
REFLECTION (Compulsory, Not Marked):
After completing this task, reflect on what you have learned so far and describe any
difficulties you faced during the process.
