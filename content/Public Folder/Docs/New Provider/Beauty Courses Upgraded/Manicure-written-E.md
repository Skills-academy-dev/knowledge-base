The National Nail Technology Theory Examination is the licensure examination for Nail Technicians, which is
developed by the National-Interstate Council of State Boards of Cosmetology (NIC).  This bulletin contains
IMPORTANT INFORMATION regarding the examination, including content outline covered by the theory
examination, sample questions and answers, and references.  The time allowed for the Nail Technology Theory
Examination is 90 minutes.
PLEASE REVIEW ALL INFORMATION CAREFULLY.

IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS
  Do not leave the examination area without permission.  Permission must be obtained to leave the examination
area for any reason, including restroom usage or at the completion of the examination. Picture ID is required for
re-entry into examination.
  With the exception of verbal instructions, the proctors and examination administration personnel are not allowed
to communicate with candidates.
  If you have an emergency situation please notify the proctor.
