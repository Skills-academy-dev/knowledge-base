Marks)

Goal : Demonstrate real-world readiness, hygiene, client interaction, and professional setup.

IMAGE SUBMISSIONS REQUIRED (3 IMAGES)

Image 1: Fully prepared workstation (sanitised + organised + PPE visible)

TASK 2 — MODULE 2: NATURAL NAIL PREPARATION PRACTICAL (20 Marks)

Goal : Demonstrate safe, structured, correct nail preparation technique.

IMAGE SUBMISSIONS REQUIRED (4 IMAGES)

Image 1: Natural nails BEFORE prep
Image 2: After cuticle softening & pushing
Image 3: After removal of non-living tissue and shaping
Image 4: Final prepared nail plate ready for product

All 4 images must be clear, close-up, and consistent lighting.

MARK ALLOCATION (20 Marks)

Criteria

TASK 3 — MODULE 3: ENHANCEMENT APPLICATION + BASIC ART (20 Marks)

Goal: Demonstrate foundation-level enhancement application + simple art detail.

Learner chooses ONE system:

●  Acrylic sculpted nail
●  Hard gel sculpted nail
●  Tip + blend + overlay
●  Builder gel overlay

IMAGE SUBMISSIONS REQUIRED (4 IMAGES)

Image 1: Form or tip applied correctly
Image 2: Product built application before filing
Image 3: After finish filing (structure clearly visible)
Image 4: Final nail with one simple art detail
(Art can be: dot work, line work, chrome accent, glitter fade, or striping tape.)

MARK ALLOCATION (20 Marks)

Criteria

Marks

Structure & product control

Finish filing smoothness & refinement

Final appearance + neat art detail
