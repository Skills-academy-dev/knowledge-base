QUESTION 1: Service Recovery Response Plan (15 Marks)

Below is a model answer. Accept any equivalent response that meets criteria.

MODEL ANSWER SAMPLE

(Students do NOT need to match wording exactly — concepts must be correct.)

1\. Immediate Action Steps (Example)

Reopen the client’s service request immediately.

Contact the client within 30 minutes to acknowledge the issue.

Notify the responsible department about the incomplete resolution.

Request urgent rework and set a clear deadline for completion.

2\. Communication Strategy

Call the client first to apologise personally.

Follow up with a written email confirming next steps.

Provide updates every 24 hours until resolved.

Keep communication calm, respectful, and transparent.

3\. Service Recovery Message (Example)

“Thank you for informing us about the incomplete resolution of your request. I sincerely apologise for the inconvenience caused. I understand how frustrating this must have been for you. I have personally reopened your case and escalated it to the relevant team. You can expect an update within the next 24 hours. We appreciate your patience and are committed to resolving this matter fully.”

4\. Root Cause Prevention (Example)

Investigate why the case was closed prematurely.

Identify system or human errors.

Provide staff training on closure protocols.

Update internal checklists to ensure accurate resolution before closing cases.

MARKING RUBRIC (15 Marks)

| Communication Strategy | 4 | Client-centred, timely, professional |

| Root Cause Prevention | 3 | System-based, not blaming the client |
