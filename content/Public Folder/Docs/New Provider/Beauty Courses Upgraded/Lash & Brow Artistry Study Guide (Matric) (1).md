Welcome to your Lash and Brow Artistry Study Guide!

We’re genuinely excited you’re here and yes, this is your official entry into the world where

precision meets beauty and skill meets responsibility. Lash and brow artistry isn’t just

about great results (though we love a clean set and a sharp brow). It’s about understanding

hair science, anatomy, hygiene, ethics, and professional practice and this programme is

designed to help you master all of it, step by step.

Think of this study guide as your steady anchor throughout the course. It’s here to keep you

focused, organised, and confident as you work through the content at your own pace. You’re

building real, industry-ready knowledge, the kind that protects clients, builds trust, and sets

you apart as a professional technician. And remember, if you ever feel stuck or unsure,

support is always within reach. You’re not doing this solo, we're firmly in your corner.

To succeed in this course, it’s essential that you work through the textbook alongside this

study guide and complete all required learning activities. You will need to:

●  Study Modules 1–3 and complete Assignment 1, and

●  Study Modules 4–6 and complete Assignment 2.

There are no formal exams for this qualification, but both assignments are compulsory and

must be submitted for assessment. Once marked, you’ll receive tutor feedback  and trust us,

that feedback is invaluable. It’s your roadmap for refining technique, improving decision-

making, and growing professional confidence.

As you move through each module, you’ll encounter summaries, guided activities, and

reflection points designed to help you apply theory to real lash and brow environments. These

aren’t just academic exercises — they mirror real client situations, salon standards, and

industry expectations. The goal isn’t only to learn what to do, but to understand why it matters.

Before you begin, make sure you have received:

●  Your Lash and Brow Artistry Textbook

●  This Study Guide

●  Assignment 1 and Assignment 2

We wish you every success as you begin this journey. Approach each module with curiosity,

discipline, and respect for the craft. Lash and brow artistry is both technical and artistic and

mastering that balance is what turns a beginner into a true professional. Go forward with

confidence. Build your skills properly. Protect your clients. Honour the industry. You’ve got this

and we can’t wait to see the professional you become.

7 STUDY TIPS

1.  Break Your Study into Short, Focused Sessions

Your brain absorbs information better when you study in focused bursts of 20–40

minutes rather than long, exhausting sessions. Short study blocks help you stay

sharp, reduce overwhelm, and improve long-term retention.

2.  Practise Techniques as You Learn Them

Lash and brow artistry is a practical skill. Practising techniques such as isolation,

mapping, tint application, or styling while you study helps develop muscle memory

and builds confidence faster.

3.  Take Smart Notes (Not Long Notes)

Focus on key steps, safety rules, definitions, and procedures rather than rewriting

entire sections. Clear, concise notes make revision quicker and more effective before

assessments.

4.  Use Your Phone Camera as a Study Tool

Take photos or short videos of your practice work to track progress and identify areas

for improvement. Visual review often highlights details that are easy to miss at the

moment.

5.  Teach Someone Else What You Learned

Explaining concepts like hygiene protocols, service selection, or aftercare to someone

else helps reinforce your understanding and exposes any gaps in your knowledge.

6.  Build Mini-Practice Sessions into Your Week

Short, regular practice sessions—such as quick mapping drills or isolation

practice—are more effective than occasional long sessions. Consistency leads to skill

improvement.

7.  Stay Curious and Willing to Experiment

Approach learning with curiosity and confidence. Exploring different techniques, tools,

and styles helps you grow creatively while learning from mistakes in a safe, controlled

way.

Introduction

This module introduces learners to the foundational knowledge required for safe and

professional lash practice. It explains what natural lashes are, how they grow, and why lash

health must always be prioritised before performing any service. The module also highlights

the importance of sanitation, hygiene, and ethical responsibility, ensuring learners develop

professional habits from the very beginning of their lash career.

Read pages 12–15 of your textbook for more information on the topics below.

explains how lashes vary from person to person and addresses common misconceptions that

beginners often have about natural lashes.

Explains the biological purpose of eyelashes, highlighting their protective role for the eyes as

well as their contribution to facial expression.

Describes the structural and functional differences between upper and lower lashes and

explains why treatments focus mainly on the upper lash line.

Outlines how lash length, thickness, and curl vary naturally between individuals and why

these factors affect service suitability.

Explains how natural lash direction differs across the lash line and between clients,

unsafe or unrealistic beliefs held by beginners.
