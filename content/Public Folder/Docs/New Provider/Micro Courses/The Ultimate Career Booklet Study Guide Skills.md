BEFORE YOU START

Now it is time to begin your learning. Before you start studying, first read through the

contents page to familiarise yourself with the topics that will be covered. These topics are

reflected in your Ultimate Career Booklet and Study Guide.

You will find subject outcomes or learning outcomes in your study guide to assist you in

understanding each chapter. These learning outcomes are repeated at the beginning of

every module to remind you of what is expected of you.

When you are ready to begin, read through the first module in your Study Guide thoroughly.

Do not progress to the next module until:

●  You are confident that you understand everything that has been covered in the

module

●  You have completed all the activities in the module

●  You are able to answer all the self-assessment questions confidently

●  You are competent in each of the learning outcomes listed at the beginning of the

module

We wish you success with your course. Remember that the more thoroughly you study, the

better prepared you will be for your career. The time you spend on additional reading,

research, and learning from others will further strengthen your understanding and skills.

Please read everything in this booklet carefully, follow all instructions, and complete all the

exercises and activities provided.

Please note: This booklet does not replace the textbook. The textbook and this booklet must

be used together to develop a full understanding of the concepts in this subject. This

includes completing and studying all activities, exercises, examples, and case studies

provided in the textbook.
