## BEFORE YOU START

Now it is time to begin your learning. Before you start studying, first read through the **contents page** to familiarise yourself with the topics that will be covered. These topics are reflected in your Ultimate Career Booklet and Study Guide.

You will find **subject outcomes or learning outcomes** in your study guide to assist you in understanding each chapter. These learning outcomes are repeated at the beginning of every module to remind you of what is expected of you.

When you are ready to begin, read through the first module in your Study Guide thoroughly.

**Do not progress to the next module until:**

* You are confident that you understand everything that has been covered in the module
* You have completed all the activities in the module
* You are able to answer all the self-assessment questions confidently
* You are competent in each of the learning outcomes listed at the beginning of the module

We wish you success with your course. Remember that the more thoroughly you study, the better prepared you will be for your career. The time you spend on additional reading, research, and learning from others will further strengthen your understanding and skills.

Please read everything in this booklet carefully, follow all instructions, and complete all the exercises and activities provided.

**Please note:** This booklet does not replace the textbook. The textbook and this booklet must be used together to develop a full understanding of the concepts in this subject. This includes completing and studying all activities, exercises, examples, and case studies provided in the textbook.

# **Module 1**

# **Understanding Yourself and Your Career Goals**

**Learning Objectives**

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

1. **Explain** the importance of self-awareness in effective career planning and decision-making.
2. **Identify** their personal strengths and weaknesses and describe how these influence career choices.
3. **Analyse** how personal values affect job satisfaction and long-term career success.
4. **Recognise** personal interests and passions and link them to potential career paths.
5. **Differentiate** between short-term, medium-term, and long-term career goals.
6. **Apply** the SMART goal-setting framework to develop realistic and achievable career goals.
7. **Match** personal skills and abilities to suitable career opportunities in the job market.
8. **Evaluate** how self-doubt can impact career decisions and professional growth.

10. **Develop** a basic personal career plan aligned with self-knowledge, values, goals, and confidence-building strategies.

**Where to find in textbook page 129-131**

### UNIT 5 Following Up After the Interview

This unit focuses on the importance of follow-up after an interview. Following up also helps candidates reflect on their performance and prepare for future opportunities.

**Where to find in textbook page 132-134**

## Summary
