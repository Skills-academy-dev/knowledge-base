Assignment 1
1. Supply Chain Management is best described as the management of:
A. Internal business operations only
B. The movement of goods within a warehouse
C. Upstream and downstream relationships to deliver customer value at lower cost
D. Transportation activities between countries
2. Logistics management primarily focuses on the:
A. Creation of customer demand
B. Strategic management of procurement, movement, and storage of goods
C. Financial control of supply chain activities
D. Legal compliance in international trade
3. Competitive advantage in supply chain strategy is mainly achieved through:
A. High advertising expenditure
B. Cost leadership and value differentiation
C. Increased product variety only
D. Vertical integration of all suppliers
4. A supply chain structure that includes multiple suppliers and customers
interacting simultaneously is best described as a:
A. Linear chain
B. Vertical hierarchy
C. Network structure
D. Closed system
5. Performance measurement in supply chains is important because it helps
organisations to:
A. Increase employee wages
B. Identify inefficiencies and improve decision-making
C. Eliminate the need for inventory
D. Replace logistics managers

6. The global supply chain environment is strongly influenced by:
A. Local warehouse layouts
B. International trade regulations and market integration
C. Domestic transport costs only
D. Internal production schedules
7. Incoterms are mainly used to define:
A. Product quality standards
B. Ownership of goods in domestic trade
C. Responsibilities, risks, and costs between buyers and sellers
D. Customs duty rates
8. Which of the following documents is essential for clearing goods through
customs?
A. Certificate of Origin
B. Packing list only
C. Customs declaration documentation
D. Sales contract
9. Statutory compliance in logistics primarily ensures that organisations:
A. Reduce transportation costs
B. Increase speed of delivery
C. Follow legal and regulatory requirements
D. Outsource logistics activities
10. The main purpose of transportation systems in supply chains is to:
A. Store goods for long periods
B. Convert raw materials into finished products
C. Enable the efficient movement of goods between supply chain nodes
D. Eliminate the need for inventory

11. Which component of the supply chain is responsible for converting raw materials
into finished products?
A. Customers
B. Transport providers
C. Manufacturers
D. Warehouses

A. Move goods between locations
B. Enable coordination through orders, forecasts, and schedules
C. Transfer ownership of goods
D. Reduce transportation costs
13. Which logistics activity focuses on loading, unloading, and internal movement of
goods?
A. Warehousing
B. Inventory control
C. Material handling
D. Order processing
14. A supply chain strategy that focuses on offering faster delivery and flexible
service is known as a:
A. Cost-based strategy
B. Risk-avoidance strategy
C. Service-based strategy
D. Centralised strategy
15. Which supply chain structure includes many suppliers, intermediaries, and
customers?
A. Simple supply chain
B. Complex supply chain
C. Vertical supply chain
D. Horizontal supply chain

16. Collaboration among supply chain partners mainly helps to:
A. Increase individual company control
B. Reduce customer involvement
C. Improve information sharing and efficiency
D. Eliminate operational risks

are fulfilled?
A. Lead time
B. Inventory value
C. Order accuracy
D. Transport cost

to customers?
A. Lead time
B. Customer satisfaction score
C. Damage rate
D. Inventory turnover

A. Issuing transport contracts
B. Valuing goods for duty purposes
C. Planning distribution routes
D. Managing warehouse operations

A. Product branding
B. Employee productivity
C. Cost, delivery speed, and service reliability
D. Supplier ownership

avoid shortages and excess inventory?
A. Inventory control
B. Transportation
C. Order processing
D. Material handling

A. Increase production capacity
B. Deliver the correct products at the right time and place
C. Reduce supplier competition
D. Control selling prices
23. Supply chain strategy contributes to competitive advantage mainly because it:
A. Focuses only on operational efficiency
B. Integrates supply chain decisions with overall business objectives
C. Eliminates the need for coordination
D. Replaces marketing and finance strategies
24. A horizontal supply chain structure is characterised by:
A. A single organisation controlling all stages
B. Organisations operating at the same level of the supply chain
C. Suppliers selling directly to final customers
D. A linear flow of goods
25. Which supply chain flow includes invoices, payments, and credit terms?
A. Information flow
B. Product flow
C. Physical flow
D. Financial flow

between:
A. Cost, speed, and reliability
