Assignment 1
1. Which of the following best describes consumer surplus?
A. The total cost of producing a product
B. The difference between what customers are willing to pay and what they actually pay
C. The profit earned by a logistics company
D. The value added by transportation

A. What demanders get for what they give
B. Quality received for the price paid
C. Low price
D. Quantitative product benefits
3. Customer service is best described as:
A. A marketing activity only
B. A series of activities that enhance sales and support product use
C. An after-sales function only
D. A financial control process
4. Which of the following is a pre-transaction customer service element?
A. Order fill rate
B. Call-out time
C. Written customer service policy
D. Product tracing
5. Which measure of availability considers whether all items ordered are delivered?
A. Fill rate
B. Stock-out frequency
C. Order cycle time
D. Orders shipped complete

D. Opportunity cost
