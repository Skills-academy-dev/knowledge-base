How to Use This Study Guide

This study guide is designed to support your learning as you work through the Supply Chain and

Logistics Management Advanced textbook. It helps you focus on key concepts, understand how

the modules are structured, and prepare effectively for your assignments.

To make the best use of this study guide:

●  Begin each module by reading the Learning Objectives to understand what knowledge

and skills you are expected to develop.

●  Use the study guide alongside the prescribed textbook, as the textbook contains the

full explanations, examples, case studies, and exercises.

●  Follow the module and unit structure in the study guide to guide your reading in the

textbook using the provided page references.

●  Pay close attention to key terms, definitions, tables, and summaries, as these

highlight important concepts.

●  Review each module summary to reinforce your understanding before attempting

assignments.

●  Complete the end-of-module exercises by referring back to the relevant textbook

sections.

●  Work through the case studies carefully to develop practical understanding and

application of supply chain concepts.

●  Study consistently and revise regularly to build confidence and ensure steady progress

through the course.

By using this study guide together with the textbook, you will develop a clear understanding of

supply chain and logistics principles and be well prepared to complete your assignments

successfully

7 Study Tips

1.  Create a study schedule

Set aside regular study time each week and stick to it. Short, consistent sessions are

more effective than last-minute studying.

2.  Use the study guide with the textbook

Read the study guide first to understand the key points, then refer to the textbook pages

for detailed explanations and examples.

3.  Focus on learning objectives

Use the learning objectives at the start of each module to guide your studying and check

your understanding as you progress.

4.  Take clear notes

Write down key concepts, definitions, and examples in your own words. This helps you

remember and understand the content better.

5.  Revise regularly

Review module summaries and key points often, especially before starting assignments,

to reinforce what you have learned.

6.  Apply concepts to real situations

Think about how supply chain and logistics principles apply to real businesses and

everyday examples to deepen your understanding.

7.  Ask for support when needed

If you are unsure about any topic, contact the college or your tutor early for guidance

rather than waiting until assignments are due.

LOGISTICS FOUNDATIONS

Learning Objectives

1.  Define supply chain management and explain its importance in business operations.

2.  Describe the role of logistics within the supply chain.

This module introduces the basic principles of supply chain and logistics management. It

explains how goods, services, information, and money move from suppliers to customers

through a connected system. The module also explores how organisations design supply chain

strategies to gain a competitive advantage, particularly in the South African business

environment.

Introduction

This module introduces transportation systems, freight, and cargo operations in supply chain

management. It explains how transportation supports the movement of goods, the role of

different transport modes, freight forwarding, cargo handling, transport documentation, and

freight costing in ensuring efficient and cost-effective supply chain operations.
