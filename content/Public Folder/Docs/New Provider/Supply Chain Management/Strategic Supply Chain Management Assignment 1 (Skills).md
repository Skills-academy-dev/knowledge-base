Assignment 1
1. Strategic supply chain management mainly focuses on:
A. Daily ordering and deliveries
B. Long-term planning and alignment with business goals
C. Warehouse layout design
D. Employee supervision
2. A supply chain is best described as:
A. A single organisation producing goods
B. A network of organisations involved in producing and delivering products
C. A transport system only
D. A marketing process
3. Which of the following is a key objective of strategic supply chain management?
A. Increasing paperwork
B. Reducing customer choices
C. Improving customer satisfaction
D. Eliminating suppliers
4. Supply chains are considered strategic business systems because they:
A. Operate independently from business strategy
B. Support long-term competitiveness and value creation
C. Focus only on cost reduction
D. Replace management decision-making
5. Strategic thinking in supply chain decision-making mainly involves:
A. Short-term problem solving
B. Long-term decision-making with future impact
C. Daily scheduling of deliveries
D. Handling customer complaints

6. Business strategy and supply chain alignment means that:
A. Supply chains operate separately from business goals
B. Supply chain decisions support the organisation’s strategy
C. Only suppliers influence strategy
D. Alignment is not necessary for success
7. Strategic network design decisions focus on:
A. Employee salaries
B. Location of facilities and flow of products
C. Advertising channels
D. Office administration systems
8. Strategic sourcing refers to:
A. Buying goods at the lowest possible price
B. Long-term planning of supplier selection and relationships
C. Transporting goods to customers
D. Managing warehouse staff
9. Outsourcing as a strategic decision means:
A. Doing all activities internally
B. Transferring selected activities to external suppliers
C. Eliminating supplier contracts
D. Avoiding long-term planning
10. Inventory plays a strategic role in supply chains because it:
A. Eliminates transportation needs
B. Helps balance supply and customer demand
C. Reduces the need for suppliers
D. Increases production delays
11. Supply chain design is mainly concerned with:
A. Deciding how products should be advertised
B. Determining the structure of the supply chain
C. Managing daily deliveries
D. Supervising employees

A. Staff training programmes
B. Customer complaints
C. The placement of facilities and flow of products
D. Office administration
14. Globalisation affects supply chains by:
A. Reducing the number of suppliers
B. Increasing supply chain complexity
C. Eliminating transportation
D. Removing competition
15. Strategic supplier relationships are important because they:
A. Increase supplier competition
B. Reduce communication
C. Focus only on short-term pricing
D. Encourage long-term cooperation
16. Outsourcing means that an organisation:
A. Performs all activities internally
B. Transfers some activities to external suppliers
C. Avoids using suppliers
D. Eliminates contracts

A. Responsible and fair sourcing practices
B. Choosing suppliers randomly
C. Ignoring labour conditions
D. Reducing product quality

C. Increase shortages
D. Reduce customer satisfaction

A. Ignoring disruptions

C. Identifying and managing risks
D. Eliminating suppliers
