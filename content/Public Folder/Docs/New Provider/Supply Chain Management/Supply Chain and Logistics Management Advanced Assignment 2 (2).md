Assignment 2
1. The main purpose of a warehouse in the supply chain is to:
A. Manufacture finished products
B. Store goods and support efficient distribution
C. Replace transportation activities
D. Eliminate inventory costs
2. A warehouse layout designed to minimise travel time and handling costs focuses
mainly on:
A. Efficient flow of goods
B. Marketing display requirements
C. Supplier negotiation strategies
D. Product pricing policies
3. Which material handling equipment is most suitable for moving palletised loads
within a warehouse?
A. Conveyor belts
B. Hand trolleys
C. Forklifts
D. Shelving units
4. Inventory management helps organisations mainly by:
A. Eliminating the need for warehousing
B. Increasing inventory investment
C. Avoiding all stockholding
D. Balancing customer service levels and inventory costs
5. Which inventory control technique calculates the most economical order size?
A. FIFO
B. JIT
C. Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)
D. ABC analysis

B. Warehousing costs
