Module 1: Introduction to Customer Service Management

Learning Objectives:

●  Define customer service management (CSM) and its role in organisations.

●  Distinguish between customer service delivery and management.

●  Understand customer lifecycle management and its impact on service strategies.

Introduction:

This module introduces the fundamentals of customer service management, positioning it as a
critical business function beyond frontline service delivery. Students will explore the scope of
CSM, its strategic role, and how it supports overall customer experience.

Unit 1: What is Customer Service Management?

Unit 2: Strategic Role of CSM in Organisations

Unit 3: Customer Lifecycle Management

Summary:

Students will understand the foundational concepts of managing customer service as an
operation and strategy, not just a support function.

Exercise:

Identify and compare the differences between service delivery and service management in
various industries.

Module 2: Designing and Managing Customer Service
Operations

Learning Objectives:

●  Design customer service processes and workflows.

●  Explain various service delivery models.

●  Understand the use of SLAs and escalation processes.

Introduction:

This module focuses on process and operations design for customer service functions, ensuring
efficient, scalable, and consistent service delivery.

Unit 1: Service Delivery Models (In-house, Outsourced,
Self-Service)

Unit 2: Process Design and Service Mapping

Unit 3: Workflow Management and Escalation Protocols

Unit 4: Service Level Agreements (SLAs) Management

Summary:

Students will be able to map and manage customer service processes, ensuring consistency,
efficiency, and reliability.

Exercise:

Map the service process for an online retailer, including touchpoints, SLAs, and escalation
steps.

Module 3: Customer Service Technologies and Systems

Learning Objectives:

●

Identify and use key service management technologies.

●  Understand CRM, ticketing, and contact centre systems.

●  Explore automation and AI tools for customer service.

Introduction:

This module introduces key technologies that support customer service management, focusing
on tools that improve efficiency, reporting, and customer engagement.

Unit 1: Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Systems

Unit 2: Contact Centre Technology (IVR, Chatbots, Ticketing
Systems)

Unit 3: Automation and AI in Service Operations

Unit 4: Omni-Channel Service Management and Integration

Summary:

Students will understand how technology supports and streamlines customer service operations
and data management.

Exercise:

Prepare a proposal for integrating a chatbot into an existing service process, including benefits
and risks.

Module 4: Performance, Quality, and Risk Management in
Customer Service

Learning Objectives:

●  Measure and manage service performance using KPIs.

●  Conduct quality assurance and process audits.

●

Identify risks in service delivery and apply mitigation strategies.

Introduction:

This module focuses on monitoring, measuring, and improving customer service operations,
ensuring alignment with organisational goals and compliance standards.

Unit 1: Measuring Service Performance (KPIs, CSAT, NPS, FCR)

Unit 2: Quality Assurance and Process Audits

Unit 3: Risk Identification and Mitigation in Service Delivery

Unit 4: Compliance Standards (ITIL, ISO, POPIA, GDPR)

Summary:

Students will develop the skills to evaluate service effectiveness and manage risks proactively.

Exercise:

Design a service quality checklist for a contact centre and explain how it would be monitored.

Module 5: Leadership, Planning, and Strategy in Service
Management

Learning Objectives:

●  Apply leadership and team management skills in service environments.

●  Plan workforce and resource requirements.

●  Explore emerging trends and strategies in customer service management.

Introduction:

This module develops the leadership and strategic skills needed to manage service teams and
resources effectively, ensuring long-term service excellence.

Unit 1: Workforce Planning, Rostering, and Resource
Management

Unit 2: Budgeting and Cost Control in Service Operations

Unit 3: Leadership in Service Teams

Unit 4: Strategic Trends in Customer Service Management

Summary:

Students will gain managerial and leadership skills to lead service teams and implement
strategic improvements.

Exercise:

Develop a service operations improvement plan, including staffing, budget, and technology
upgrades.
