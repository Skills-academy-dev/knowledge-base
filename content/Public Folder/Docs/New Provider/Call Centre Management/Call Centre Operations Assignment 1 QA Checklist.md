# Call Centre Operations Assignment 1 QA Checklist

1\. Cover Page

✔️ Updated Information:

The cover page clearly states the subject title, assignment number, and institution.

Copyright and publication details are current and accurate.

2\. Edition

✔️ Updated Edition:

The cover notes it is the First Edition under "Publishing History."

3\. Table of Contents

✔️ Correspondence with Page Numbers:

TOC includes:

Contact Information (pg 4)

Instructions (pg 5)

Assignment 1 (pg 6)

Answer Sheet (pg 20)

These headings match the actual page numbers accurately.

4\. Information Page

✔️ Relevance:

The "Skills Academy Contact Information" page (pg 4) is clear, with contact details for student support and finance.

Banking details and submission instructions are included.

5\. Instructions

✔️ Clarity:

Instructions are clearly presented (pg 5), using simple language and a numbered list.

They are appropriate for distance learning students.

6\. Questions and Options

✔️ Clarity and Numbering:

All 50 questions are clearly phrased and follow a consistent format (A–D options).

Numbering is sequential with no missing or duplicate numbers.

7\. Total Marks

✔️ Clarity:

Total marks are stated at the end of the assignment:
 “(50 x 2) (100 marks)” and “GRAND TOTAL: 100 MARKS” (pg 19).

8\. Answer Sheet Cover Page

✔️ Completeness:

The answer sheet includes:

Fields for name, student number, ID number

Declaration of originality

Signature and date lines

A checklist to prepare for submission

Space for subject expert comments and percentage result

9\. Answer Sheet Design

✔️ Clarity and Numbering:

The answer sheet (pgs 20–22) is clearly laid out in a table format.

Each question has one line, labelled by number, with checkboxes for A–D.

Easy to follow and well-spaced.

Conclusion

The Call Centre Operations – Assignment 1 successfully meets all the checklist criteria. It is well-structured, student-friendly, and aligns with professional standards for distance learning assessments.
