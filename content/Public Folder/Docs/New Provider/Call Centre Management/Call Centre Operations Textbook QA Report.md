# Purpose of the Evaluation

The purpose is to identify specific issues in the textbook that need to be addressed to enhance its quality. Also, to determine if the quality is up to our standards so that we can release the book to Supply Chain.

The purpose is also to improve on the [QA Policy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1V6VzwFleBo96wRgNz2g0LpC-Il7MrBv9/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=114443468049277155895&rtpof=true&sd=true), and improve on the [Textbook Development and Pre-Production Guidelines](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RzR-i5rd11ycrnntSqyTm9dawANsuWXcK0mRmtZbLGo/edit?usp=sharing), by means of lessons learnt from each quality review we do.

# Methodology

Our evaluation method thoroughly checks the textbook to pinpoint improvement areas, making sure it meets and surpasses our quality standards.The process includes:

Quick overall review - Human read through

Quickly review the book for errors, focusing on the author, editor, and QC names, ISBN, page numbers, table of contents, heading consistency, and image wording. Check for exam-type questions at chapter ends to help reinforce learning and prepare for exams. Look for other common issues.

Initial Assessment with AI Tools

Utilise AI to conduct a review of the textbook against relevant academic standards, including syllabi and past exam papers.

Content and Organization

Assess the structure, clarity, and logical flow of the textbook, including the preface, table of contents, chapter organisation, and the index.

Pedagogical Approach

Evaluate the teaching strategies employed, language appropriateness, inclusion of diverse perspectives, and the effectiveness of examples and exercises.

Scoring and Weighting

Apply a scoring system to each evaluation criterion, considering its importance.

Syllabus and Past Exam Papers

Analyse textbook with subject syllabus and past exam papers.

Recommendations and Action Plan

Based on the findings, draft list of recommendations.

## Recommendations and Action Plan

Enhance Module Introductions

Action: Begin each module with a real-life example, scenario, or question to help learners connect with the topic immediately.

Integrate Case Studies Within Modules

Action: Move or reference one relevant case study into each module instead of placing all case studies at the end of the textbook.

Evaluate the Need for an Index

Action: Consider including an index in future editions if the book is expanded or feedback suggests it’s needed.

## Conclusion

The Call Centre Operations textbook is an excellent learning resource, scoring 185 out of 190 (97.4%). It is clear, well-organised, inclusive, and well-suited for South African distance learners aged 15 and up.

The content balances theory and practice, with strong pedagogy, relevant examples, and accessible language. Only minor improvements—such as enhancing introductions, integrating case studies within modules, and possibly adding an index in future editions—are recommended.

Overall, the textbook is ready for academic use and supports learner success in the call centre industry.
