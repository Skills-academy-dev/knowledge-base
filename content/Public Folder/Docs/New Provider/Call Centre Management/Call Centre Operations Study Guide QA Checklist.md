# Call Centre Operations Study Guide QA Checklist

1\. Structure and Organisation

Learning Objectives

✅ Present – Each module starts with clear learning outcomes. For example, Module 1 begins with statements like: “By the end of this module, you should be able to…” which define specific learning targets for the student.

Content Organisation

✅ Well-structured – The guide follows a logical sequence of 6 modules, each divided into units with clear topics. A study planner is included to support navigation and progress tracking.

Summaries

✅ Included – Each module concludes with a summary section highlighting key takeaways, followed by an exercise section that refers to the textbook for practice.

2\. Content Quality

Relevance

✅ Aligned with Objectives – Each unit builds directly towards the learning outcomes and supports assignment completion. For example, Modules 1–3 relate to Assignment 1, and Modules 4–6 relate to Assignment 2.

Accuracy

✅ Accurate – Content reflects standard practices in call centres, aligned with both South African and global industry understanding. The integration with a reliable textbook reinforces content validity.

Clarity

✅ Clear and Understandable – Language is simple but formal, and explanations are concise. Examples and real-world terms (e.g., ACD, CRM) are used meaningfully throughout.

3\. Visual and Aesthetic Elements

Typography and Layout

✅ Readable – Font and formatting are consistent, with well-labelled units, bullet points, tables of contents, and clearly separated summaries and exercises.

Navigation Tools

✅ Available – Includes a table of contents, a study planner, and consistent labelling of modules and units. While an index and glossary are not present in this study guide, the textbook is referenced for definitions and further reading.

4\. Accessibility and Usability

Language Simplicity

✅ Appropriate – The language is age-appropriate (15 years and up), with explanations made simple enough for distance learners. Complex concepts like customer retention or CRM are broken down into easy-to-understand parts.

5\. Referencing the Textbook

Assignment Coverage

✅ Clearly Indicated –

Assignment 1: Covers Modules 1–3

Assignment 2: Covers Modules 4–6

This structure is explicitly outlined in the Study Planner and introduction.

No Questions in the Guide

✅ Compliant – The study guide does not include questions. Exercises refer students to pages in the textbook for practice questions and answers.

Consistency

✅ Maintained – Standard formatting is used consistently across all modules, units, and exercise instructions.

Conclusion

The Call Centre Operations Study Guide meets all key requirements in the checklist. It is structured, relevant, accessible, and designed for effective learning support without duplication of textbook content or assessment tasks. Minor optional improvements could include:

A glossary of key terms.

A visual map or timeline of learning progress.

Otherwise, it is a well-developed guide for South African distance learners aged 15 and up.
