TEXTBOOKOffice PracticeBasicAuthor: Thiana Pretorius • Editor: Itai NgoshiQuality Checked by Aludwe Mthini and Getrude Tsemwa Developed byCape Town, South Africa
Unlock Your Potential in the Modern WorkplacePrepare for your first role in the office with confidence. This practical guideintroduces you to the real-world duties and expectations of a modern secretary,management assistant, or office administrator in an organised and easy-to-followway.The Office Practice Basic textbook breaks down everyday office tasks into clearsteps, supported by examples and activities that show how your work contributes tothe smooth running of an organisation. You will discover what happens behind thescenes in a professional office - and how you can play an important part.In this textbook, you will:Explore the Secretarial Career Path and Role: Understand what a secretary ormanagement assistant does, how the role is changing with technology, andwhich factors influence promotion and remuneration.Develop Professional Telephone Skills: Learn about business telephone systemsand apply correct techniques for answering, transferring, and managing calls,including in emergency situations.Build Reception Skills and Basic Business Etiquette: Practise welcomingvisitors, managing the reception area, interpreting organisational diagrams, andusing correct protocol when introducing people or handling invitations.Assist with Meetings and Conferences: Gain confidence in supportingmeetings, seminars, and conferences, from preparing materials and venues tounderstanding your duties before, during, and after events.Manage Office Supplies Effectively: Discover how to purchase, receive, store,issue, and control office supplies so that the workplace remains organised andwell-equipped.Handle Information and Documents Responsibly: Develop skills in gatheringand processing information, copying and distributing documents, and setting upfiling systems that keep records organised, secure, and easy to retrieve.With its strong focus on practical skills and everyday office tasks, the Office PracticeBasic textbook gives you a solid starting point for further study and employment. Bymastering the content in these pages, you will be ready to step into the officeenvironment and contribute professionally from your very first day.Office Practice BasicDeveloped byCape Town, South Africa
