A. A sole proprietorship

B. A separate legal entity

D. A cooperative

D. Informal business structure

A. Registering with CIPC

B. Signing a lease agreement

D. Hiring employees

A. Business Proposal

B. Memorandum of Incorporation

C. Employment Contract

D. Tax Clearance Certificate

C. Handle employees’ personal finances

D. Set tax rates

A. A warning letter

B. Disqualification and legal penalties
