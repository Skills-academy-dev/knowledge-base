Question 1: Inspection of Furniture (5 marks)

Identify the key inspection areas, and explain how you would inspect this piece of furniture

before reupholstering.

Marking Guide:

●  2 marks = Identification of key areas (frame, springs, padding)

●  2 marks = Explanation of inspection process

●  1 mark = Clarity

Total: 5 marks

Question 2: Reupholstery Decision (5 marks)

Evaluate whether this sofa is worth reupholstering and justify your answer.

6

Marking Guide:

●  2 marks = Correct evaluation (worth/not worth)

●  2 marks = Justification (condition, cost, quality)

●  1 mark = Clarity

Total: 5 marks

Question 3: Upholstery Process (5 marks)

Describe the basic steps involved in a correct order in the upholstery process.

Marking Guide:

●  3 marks = Logical steps (e.g. remove old fabric)

●  1 mark = Sequence/order

●  1 mark = Clarity

Total: 5 marks

Question 4: Repairs & Improvements (5 marks)

Identify possible repairs needed before reupholstering and explain how they improve this

furniture.

Marking Guide:

●  2 marks = Identification of repairs (frame, springs, padding)

●  2 marks = Explanation of improvements

●  1 mark = Practicality

Total: 5 marks
