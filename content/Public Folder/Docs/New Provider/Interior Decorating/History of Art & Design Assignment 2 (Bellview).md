3.  You may use your textbook, notes, and online research to help you complete the

assignment.

4.  Only ONE image is required for the entire assignment.

Assignment 2

Task 1: Modern Artwork or Design Analysis (15 Marks)

●  Romanticism and Emotional Expression

●  Realism and Social Representation

●

Impressionism and Visual Perception

●  Post-Impressionism and Decorative Expression

●  Arts and Crafts Movement

●  Early Modernism and Functional Design

●  Bauhaus and Design Integration

●  Art Deco and Decorative Modernism

●  Mid-Century Modern Design

●  Minimalism and Spatial Reduction

●  Architectural Styles and Spatial Design

●  African and South African Design Contexts

Find ONE artwork, building, furniture design, or poster linked to one of the topics listed

above.

Paste or attach ONE image and answer the following:

1.  Name the artwork/design/building. (2)

2.  Name the artist/designer/architect. (2)

3.  Identify the art movement or style. (3)

4.  Give TWO characteristics of this style from the textbook. (4)

5.  Explain how the work shows these characteristics. (4)

Task 2: Understanding Modern Art and Design Movements (15 Marks)

Choose ONE of the following topics:

●  Romanticism

●

Impressionism

●  Arts and Crafts Movement

●  Bauhaus

●  Art Deco

6

●  Mid-Century Modern Design

●  Minimalism

Answer the following:

1.  Which movement did you choose? (2)

2.  Describe TWO important characteristics of the movement. (4)

3.  Explain how the movement influenced art, design, or architecture. (4)

4.  What colours, materials, or shapes are commonly linked to this movement? (3)

5.  Name ONE famous artist, architect, or designer linked to the movement. (2)

Task 3: Comparing Modern Design Styles (15 Marks)

Choose TWO styles or movements from the topics listed on task 2.

Answer the following:

1.  Name the TWO styles/movements. (2)

2.  Explain ONE similarity between them. (4)

3.  Explain ONE difference between them. (4)

4.  Which style would work best in modern interior design? Explain your answer. (5)

Task 4: Creative Interior Design Planning (15 Marks)

Imagine you are designing a modern café, bedroom, or office inspired by ONE design

movement studied in this section of the textbook.

Answer the following:

1.  Which movement would inspire your design? (3)

2.  What colours, furniture, or decorations would you use? (4)

3.  How would the design reflect the chosen movement? (4)

4.  Explain why people would enjoy this design style today. (4)
