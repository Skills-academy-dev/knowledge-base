Introduction to the Textiles for Interior Applications Study

Guide

Welcome to your Textiles for Interior Applications Study Guide!

We’re excited to have you here and you are about to develop valuable knowledge and

practical skills in interior textiles. Textiles in interior design are not only about decoration, but

about making informed decisions that improve comfort, functionality, and the overall

experience of a space. It involves understanding materials, performance, and how different

textiles work together to create interiors that are both visually appealing and practical.

Think of this study guide as your support tool throughout the course. It works together with

your textbook to help you understand concepts in a simple and structured way. The guide

highlights key ideas, summarises important content, and helps you focus on what matters

most as you move through each module. It is designed to make your learning more

manageable and effective.

To succeed in this course, it is important to work consistently through both the textbook and

this study guide. You will need to:

●  Study Modules 1–3 and complete Assignment 1, and

●  Study Modules 4–6 and complete Assignment 2.

There are no formal examinations for this course. However, both assignments are

compulsory and must be completed and submitted for assessment. Once submitted, your

assignments will be returned with tutor feedback. This feedback will help you identify areas

for improvement, strengthen your understanding, and build confidence in applying textile

knowledge in real-life interior spaces.

As you progress through the modules, you will explore important topics such as textile

materials, fabric construction, performance and durability, upholstery, window and floor

textiles, and soft furnishings. You will also learn how to plan textile use in different spaces,

apply design principles, and solve practical interior design challenges. The activities and

case studies included in the course will help you apply your knowledge in real-world

situations.

Before you begin studying, make sure you have received:

●  Your Textiles for Interior Applications Textbook

●  This Study Guide

●  Assignment 1 and Assignment 2

6

How to Use This Study Guide

●  Work through the modules in order

Follow the modules from 1 to 6, as each module builds on the knowledge from the

previous one.

●  Use the study guide together with your textbook

Read the summaries in the guide first, then refer to the textbook for detailed

explanations using the given page references.

●  Focus on key concepts and terms

Pay attention to important ideas, definitions, and examples highlighted in each unit.

●  Complete all activities and case studies

Apply your knowledge by completing the case studies and exercises in your

textbook.

●  Check your understanding regularly

Use the summaries and exercises to test what you have learned before moving to

the next module.

●  Take notes while studying

Write down important points, examples, and questions to improve your

understanding and memory.

●  Use feedback to improve

Review tutor feedback on your assignments carefully and use it to strengthen your

skills and knowledge.

Introduction

Interior textiles are an essential part of designing comfortable and functional spaces. They

are used in homes, offices, and public environments to improve both appearance and

performance. Textiles influence how a space looks, feels, and functions by affecting light,

temperature, and sound. Understanding how textiles work helps you avoid poor design

choices and select suitable materials. This module introduces the basic concepts needed to

understand textile use in interiors.

Refer to page 10 in your textbook for more information.

Unit 1: Introduction to Interior Textiles

Interior textiles form the foundation of interior decoration and design. They are used in

different ways to improve both function and appearance in a space. Understanding the types

and roles of textiles helps you make informed decisions. This unit builds your knowledge of

what interior textiles are and why they are important.

Refer to page 11-13 in your textbook for more information.
