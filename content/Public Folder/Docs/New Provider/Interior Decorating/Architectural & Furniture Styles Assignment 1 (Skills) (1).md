Task 3: Create a Mood Board

(Module 3) 20 Marks

Instructions

Choose one room:

●  Bedroom

●  Lounge

●  Dining room

●  Office

●  Study area

Choose one design style.

Create a simple mood board showing:

●  Furniture

●  Colours

●  Lighting

●  Decorations

●  Flooring or wall ideas

You may use:

●  Magazine cut-outs

●

Internet pictures

●  Drawings

●  Canva, Inshot, PowerPoint or any other apps to create mood board.

Interior styling

●  Visual presentation
