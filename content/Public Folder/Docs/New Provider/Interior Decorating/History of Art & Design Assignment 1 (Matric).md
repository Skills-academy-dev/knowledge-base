Instructions

1.  Read the relevant sections in your textbook before answering the assignment.

2.  Answer all questions in your own words.

3.  You may use your textbook, notes, and online research to help you complete the

assignment.

4.  Only ONE image is required for the entire assignment.

5.  Make sure the image you choose is linked to one of the art styles or historical periods

studied in the textbook.

6.  Keep your answers clear, simple, and well organised.

7.  Where required, explain your answers using information learnt from the textbook.

8.  You may type your assignment or write it neatly by hand.

9.  Learners with limited resources may answer the creative thinking questions using

written descriptions only.

10. Check your work carefully before submitting the assignment.

Assignment 1

Task 1: Artwork Identification and Analysis (15 Marks)

Find ONE artwork related to:

●  Prehistoric Art

●  Egyptian Art

●  Greek Art

●  Roman Art

●

Islamic Art

●  Renaissance Art

●  Gothic Art

●  Byzantine Art

Paste or attach ONE image of the artwork and answer the following:

1.  Name the artwork. (2)

2.  Name the artist or civilisation linked to the artwork. (2)

3.  Identify the art style or historical period. (3)

4.  Give TWO characteristics of this art style from the textbook. (4)

5.  Explain how the artwork shows these characteristics. (4)

Task 2: Understanding Historical Art Styles (15 Marks)

Choose ONE of the following topics:

●  Ancient Egyptian Art and Design Systems

●  Classical Greek Art and Aesthetic Ideals

●  Roman Art, Engineering, and Domestic Design

●

Islamic Art and Decorative Systems

●  Romanesque and Gothic Art and Architecture

●  The Italian Renaissance

6

Answer the following questions:

1.  Which topic did you choose? (2)

2.  Describe TWO important characteristics of this style or period. (4)

3.  What materials, colours, or techniques were commonly used? (4)

4.  Explain the purpose or meaning of the art/design during this period. (3)

5.  Name ONE famous artwork, building, or artist linked to this topic. (2)

Task 3: Comparing Historical Art Styles (15 Marks)

Choose TWO styles from the topics below:

●  Egyptian Art

●  Greek Art

●  Roman Art

●  Gothic Art

●

Islamic Art

●  Renaissance Art

Answer the following:

1.  Name the TWO styles. (2)

2.  Explain ONE similarity between them. (4)

3.  Explain ONE difference between them. (4)

4.  Which style do you find more interesting? Give a reason. (5)

Task 4: Creative Design Thinking (15 Marks)

Imagine you are decorating a room inspired by ONE historical art style studied in this section

of the textbook.

Answer the following:

1.  Which art style would inspire your room? (3)

2.  What colours or patterns would you use? (4)

3.  What type of furniture, decorations, or materials would suit the style? (4)

4.  Explain why this style would work well in a modern room. (4)

Total: 60 Marks
