Assignment 1

Task 1: Interior Architecture and Human Experience

Module 1: Foundations of Interior Architecture

Marks: 20

Interior Architecture focuses on creating spaces that support human activities, comfort,

wellbeing, and functionality.

Instructions

Choose a room in your home, workplace, educational institution, or community building.

Examples include:

• Bedroom

• Living room

• Kitchen

• Classroom

• Office

• Library

Part A: Room Observation (5 Marks)

Provide one of the following:

• A clear photograph of the room, OR

• A simple hand-drawn sketch of the room. (5)

Part B: User and Function Analysis (10 Marks)

Answer the following questions:

1.  What is the main purpose of the space? (2)

2.  Who are the primary users of the space? (2)

3.  Describe three ways the design of the space supports the users' activities, comfort,

or wellbeing. (6)

6

Part C: Interior Architecture Principles (5 Marks)

Explain how the room demonstrates any three of the following principles:

• Functionality

• Safety

• Accessibility

• Comfort

• Spatial organisation

• User-centred design (5)

Task Total: 20 Marks

Task 2: Design Elements and Principles Analysis

Module 2: Design Theory and Visual Communication

Marks: 20

Interior Architects use design elements and principles to create visually pleasing and

functional environments.

Instructions

Choose an interior space that you can observe directly or from a photograph.

Examples include:

• Restaurant

• Coffee shop

• Office

• Bedroom

• Retail store

• Hotel lobby

7

Part A: Design Elements (8 Marks)

Identify and explain four design elements visible in the space.

Choose from:

• Line

• Shape

• Form

• Colour

• Texture

• Light

Provide one explanation for each element.

(2 × 4 = 8)

Part B: Design Principles (8 Marks)

Identify and explain four design principles visible in the space.

Choose from:

• Balance

• Rhythm

• Emphasis

• Proportion

• Harmony

• Contrast

Provide one explanation for each principle.

(2 × 4 = 8)

Part C: Design Improvements (4 Marks)

Recommend two improvements that could enhance the design of the space.

For each recommendation:

• Describe the improvement.

• Explain why it would improve the space.

(2 × 2 = 4)

Task Total: 20 Marks

8

Task 3: Space Planning and User Needs

Module 3: Space Planning and Human Behaviour

Marks: 20

Good Interior Architecture requires careful planning of space to support user needs and

efficient movement.

Scenario

A distance-learning student requires a small study area at home where they can comfortably

study, complete assignments, and attend online classes.

Part A: Floor Plan Development (8 Marks)

Create a simple floor plan showing the location of:

• Desk

• Chair

• Storage area

• Lighting source

• Circulation space

All items must be clearly labelled. (8)

Part B: Design Explanation (12 Marks)

Write a short explanation discussing:

1.  How the design supports productivity. (4)

2.  How the design allows comfortable movement within the space. (4)

3.  How the design meets the needs of the student. (4)

Task Total: 20 Marks
