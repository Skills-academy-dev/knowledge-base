Task 1: Furniture Materials Activity

(Module 4) 20 Marks

Instructions

Choose 4 furniture materials.

Examples:

●  Wood

●  Metal

●  Glass

●  Fabric

●  Leather

●  Plastic

For each material:

1.  Add a picture or drawing.

2.  Write:

○  One advantage

○  One disadvantage

○  One place where the material is used

Present your work in a table or short report.

Practical Skill Practised

●

Identifying furniture materials

●  Understanding material uses

●  Evaluating advantages and disadvantages

Task 2: Room Layout Plan

(Module 5) 20 Marks

Instructions

Choose one room in your home or create an imaginary room.

Draw a simple floor plan showing:

●  Furniture placement

●  Doors

●  Windows

●  Walking space

Label all furniture items.

Write a short paragraph explaining:

●  Why you arranged the furniture this way

●  How the layout makes the room comfortable and practical

You may draw by hand or digitally.

Practical Skill Practised

●  Space planning

●  Furniture arrangement

●  Functional room planning

Task 3: Client and Style Analysis

(Module 6) 20 Marks

Instructions

Choose ONE room:

●  Living room

●  Bedroom

●  Home office

●  Dining room

●  Café seating area

Create a simple client and style analysis.

Include:

●  A short client profile

●  ONE interior style suitable for the client

●  A short explanation of why the style suits the client

●  Main features of the chosen style

●  Suitable furniture, colours, lighting, and decorative items for the space

●  A short explanation of how the design meets the client’s needs
