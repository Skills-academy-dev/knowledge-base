PART 1: VISUAL ANALYSIS (20 MARKS)

Study the provided image of a living room (sofa, cushions, décor).

Image: Modern cozy living room with plants

Source : AI-Generated

Question 1: Identify Upholstery Elements (5 marks)

Name 3 visible upholstery elements and explain their function.

Marking Guide:

●  1 mark × 3 = Correct identification of elements (3 marks)

●  1 mark × 2 = Clear explanation of functions (2 marks)

Total: 5 marks

6

Question 2: Design Elements Analysis (5 marks)

Analyse:

●  Colour

●  Texture

●  Pattern

●  Space

●  Explain how elements work together

Marking Guide:

●  1 mark × 4 = Correct identification of each element (4 marks)

●  1 mark = Overall explanation of how elements work together

Total: 5 marks

Question 3: Evaluation of Upholstery Choice (5 marks)

Evaluate whether the upholstery is suitable for everyday use.

Marking Guide:

●  2 marks = Correct judgement (suitable/not suitable with reason)

●  2 marks = Relevant factors (e.g. durability, fabric type, maintenance)

●  1 mark = Clear explanation

Total: 5 marks

Question 4: Improvement Task (5 marks)

Suggest 2 improvements and explain why.

Marking Guide:

●  2 marks = Two relevant improvements (1 mark each)

●  2 marks = Justification (why each improves the design)

●  1 mark = Practicality of suggestions

Total: 5 marks

✔ What the assessor is looking for:

●  Ability to observe and analyse

●  Understanding of upholstery function

●  Basic design thinking

PART 2: DESIGN CREATION TASK (20 MARKS)

Design a sofa for the given client:

Client Profile:

●  Family with children

●  Has pets

●  Modern home

●  Needs durability and comfort

Question 1: Fabric Selection (6 marks)

Choose a suitable upholstery fabric and explain why it is appropriate in terms of durability,

maintenance, comfort, and how it meets the needs of a client with children, pets, and

everyday use.

Marking Guide:

●  2 marks = Appropriate fabric choice

●  2 marks = Justification (durability, maintenance, comfort)

●  2 marks = Link to client needs

Total: 6 marks

Question 2: Material Justification (4 marks)

Recommend a suitable padding material and outer fabric for the sofa, and briefly explain

why each is appropriate based on comfort, durability, and the client’s needs.

Marking Guide:

●  1 mark = Suitable padding material

●  1 mark = Suitable outer fabric

●  2 marks = Clear explanation of suitability

Total: 4 marks

Question 3: Design Description (5 marks)

Describe your sofa design by stating the colour, texture, and pattern you would use, and

explain how these choices suit the client’s lifestyle and modern style.

8

Marking Guide:

●  1 mark = Colour choice

●  1 mark = Texture

●  1 mark = Pattern

●  2 marks = Explanation linked to client lifestyle and style

Total: 5 marks

Question 4: Problem Solving (5 marks)

Identify possible problems that may affect your sofa over time (such as wear, fading, or

stains) and explain practical solutions to prevent or fix these issues.

Marking Guide:

●  2 marks = Identification of realistic problems (e.g. wear, fading, stains)

●  2 marks = Practical solutions

●  1 mark = Clarity and relevance

Total: 5 marks

✔ What the assessor is looking for:

●  Practical thinking

●  Correct material knowledge

●  Ability to design for real clients

PART 3: SCENARIO-BASED WINDOW DESIGN (20 MARKS)

Scenario

Large window:

●  Busy street

●  Strong sunlight

●  Low privacy

●  Modern style room

Question 1: Window Treatment Selection (5 marks)

Recommend a suitable window treatment for the space and explain why it is appropriate in

terms of both function (e.g. privacy and light control) and style.

9

Marking Guide:

●  2 marks = Suitable treatment choice

●  2 marks = Justification (function + style)

●  1 mark = Clarity

Total: 5 marks

Question 2: Functional Analysis (5 marks)

Explain how your chosen window treatment provides privacy, controls light, and improves

comfort (such as reducing glare or heat) in the room.

Marking Guide:

●  2 marks = Privacy explanation

●  2 marks = Light control explanation

●  1 mark = Comfort (glare/temperature)

Total: 5 marks

Question 3: Design Enhancement (5 marks)

Suggest suitable colours and materials for the window treatment and explain how they

improve the room’s design while matching the modern style.

Marking Guide:

●  2 marks = Suitable colours/materials

●  2 marks = Design improvement explanation

●  1 mark = Relevance to modern style

Total: 5 marks

Question 4: Correction & Improvement (5 marks)

Identify what is wrong with the current window treatment (sheer curtains only) and explain

how you would improve it using practical solutions, with clear professional reasoning.

Marking Guide:

●  2 marks = Identification of problem (sheer curtains insufficient)

●  2 marks = Practical improvements (e.g. layering, blinds, blackout lining)

●  1 mark = Professional reasoning

Total: 5 marks

10

✔ What the assessor is looking for:

●  Ability to solve real design problems

●  Understanding of privacy vs light balance

●  Professional decision-making
