Instructions

●

In this assignment, you are an interior designer working with a client.

●  You will use a real-life scenario to make design decisions.

●  You do not need to buy anything — all answers are based on planning,

observation, and creativity.

●  You may use your own home or imagine a realistic space.

●  Where required, include a photo, drawing, or AI-generated image.

●  All explanations must be in your own words.

●  Apply what you have learned in:

○  Furniture textiles (upholstery)

○  Window, wall, and floor textiles

○  Soft furnishings and interior design planning

SCENARIO

A client, Mr. Naidoo, wants to improve his living room.

The room currently has:

●  A sofa and two chairs

●  Curtains

●  A carpet

●  A few cushions

Client Concerns:

●  Furniture looks old and uncomfortable

●  The room feels dark and dull

●  Textiles do not match well

●  The space must be easy to clean and suitable for daily use

You must redesign the space using textiles only.

PART A: COMFORT AND STYLE – FURNITURE TEXTILES IN ACTION [20 MARKS]

Question 1: Evaluating the Furniture (10 marks)

1.  Identify TWO problems with the current furniture textiles.

(4 marks)

2.  Explain how each problem affects:

○  Comfort

○  Appearance

(4 marks)

3.  Suggest ONE suitable fabric type for the sofa.

(2 marks)

Question 2: Improving the Furniture Design (10 marks)

1.  Suggest TWO improvements to the furniture textiles.

(4 marks)

2.  Explain how each improvement will:

○

Improve durability

○

Improve comfort

(4 marks)

3.  Explain how your changes will make the room more attractive or modern.

(2 marks)

PART B: BUILDING A FUNCTIONAL AND BALANCED SPACE [20 MARKS]

Question 3: Analysing Curtains and Flooring (10 marks)

1.  Identify ONE problem with the curtains and ONE problem with the carpet.

(4 marks)

2.  Explain how these problems affect:

○  Light in the room

○  Comfort in the room

(4 marks)

3.  Suggest ONE improvement for either the curtain OR carpet.

(2 marks)

Question 4: Designing for Comfort and Function (10 marks)

1.  Suggest ONE curtain solution to improve light control.

(2 marks)

2.  Explain how your curtain choice improves the space.

(4 marks)

3.  Suggest ONE improvement to the floor textile.

(2 marks)

4.  Explain how this improves comfort, temperature, or sound.

(2 marks)

PART C: ADDING STYLE AND THINKING LIKE A DESIGNER [20 MARKS]

Question 5: Your Design Concept (12 marks)

You must create your own design idea for the living room.

You may:

●  Draw your design

●  Take a photo of a real setup

●  OR generate an image using AI (based on your idea)

1.  Insert your image/drawing/design.

(2 marks)

2.  Show at least TWO soft furnishing items (e.g. cushions, throws).

(2 marks)

3.  Describe your design in terms of:

○  Colour

○  Texture OR pattern

(2 marks)

4.  Explain how your design improves:

○  Appearance

○  Comfort

(2 marks)

5.  Explain how your design meets the client’s need for a brighter and more attractive

space.

(2 marks)

Question 6: Professional Interior Design Thinking (8 marks)

1.  Identify TWO client needs from the scenario.

(4 marks)

(4 marks)
