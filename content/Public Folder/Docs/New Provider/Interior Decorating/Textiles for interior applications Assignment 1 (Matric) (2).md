Instructions

●  This is a practical assignment. You will use a real room or indoor space (for

example: your bedroom, living room, kitchen, office, or classroom).

●  You do not need to buy anything.

●  You must observe and analyse textiles that already exist in your space.

●  Where required, you must take your own photo as proof.

●

If you cannot take a photo, you may draw the item clearly and label it.

●  Use full sentences when explaining your answers.

●  Make sure your answers are based on what you learned in:

○  Module 1: Interior textiles and design

○  Module 2: Materials and construction

○  Module 3: Performance and functionality

Assignment 1

PART A: UNDERSTANDING TEXTILES IN YOUR SPACE [20 MARKS]

Question 1: Identify Textiles in a Room (10 marks)

Choose ONE room or indoor space (for example: bedroom, lounge, or office).

1.  List FOUR different textiles in the room.

(4 × 1 = 4 marks)

2.  For EACH textile, state its main function in the room.

(4 × 1 = 4 marks)

3.  Choose ONE textile that is mainly decorative.

○  Name it

○  Explain why it is decorative and not mainly functional

(2 marks)

Question 2: Design and Mood in the Room (10 marks)

Use the same room from Question 1.

1.  Identify TWO design elements used in the textiles.

(2 × 2 = 4 marks)

2.  Explain how these elements affect the mood or feeling of the room.

(4 marks)

3.  Suggest ONE simple improvement to the textiles in the room.

○  Explain what you would change

○  Explain why this will improve the space

(2 marks)

6

PART B: MATERIALS AND FABRIC CONSTRUCTION [20 MARKS]

Question 3: Fibre Types in Your Room (10 marks)

Select TWO textiles from your chosen room.

For EACH textile:

1.  Identify the type of fibre:

(2 × 2 = 4 marks)

2.  Give a reason for your answer based on:

○  How it looks

○  How it feels

○  Where it is used

(2 × 2 = 4 marks)

3.  Explain why this material is suitable for its use in that space.

(2 marks)

Question 4: Fabric Construction and Use (10 marks)

Choose ONE textile item

1.  Identify the fabric construction type.

(2 marks)

2.  Explain how this construction affects the textile’s:

○  Strength OR

○  Flexibility OR

○  Comfort

(4 marks)

3.  State:

○  ONE advantage of this textile

○  ONE disadvantage of this textile

(2 × 2 = 4 marks)

7

PART C: TEXTILE PERFORMANCE AND REAL-LIFE APPLICATION

[20 MARKS]

Question 5: Image-Based Textile Evidence (10 marks)

You  must include your own image or drawing.

Choose ONE textile item in your space

1.  Insert a photo or drawing of the textile.

(2 marks)

2.  Identify the type of textile.

(2 marks)

3.  Describe the function of this textile in the room.

(2 marks)

4.  Identify ONE performance requirement, such as:

○  Durability

○  Comfort

○  Moisture resistance

○  Light control

(2 marks)

5.  Explain why this performance requirement is important for this textile.

(2 marks)

Question 6: Improving Textile Performance (10 marks)

Use the same room.

1.  Identify TWO textiles that need to be durable.

(2 marks)

2.  Explain why durability is important for each.

(2 × 2 = 4 marks)

8

3.  Identify ONE environmental factor affecting textiles in the room.

(2 marks)

4.  Suggest ONE practical improvement to make a textile last longer.

(2 marks)
