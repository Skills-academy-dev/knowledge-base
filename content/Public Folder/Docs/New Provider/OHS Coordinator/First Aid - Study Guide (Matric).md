MODULE 1

EMERGENCY SCENE MANAGEMENT

The following concepts are covered in this module:

Safety

Medico-legal implications

Good Samaritan clause

Abandonment

Learning Outcomes

After completing this module, as a learner, you must:

Be able to explain the maintenance of personal safety in terms of preventing

injuries to self and infectious diseases.

In a position to explain the methods of safeguarding the emergency scene in

accordance with relevant practices and legislation.

Explain the methods of safeguarding the injured person in accordance with

relevant practices and legislation.

Explain the medico-legal implications of rendering First Aid in terms of the

relevant legislation.

10

INTRODUCTION

This module covers First Aid as the first point of assistance when someone suddenly gets

sick or injured. It is important to note that not every incident requiring first aid will be a

life-and-death situation, and most commonly, first aid knowledge is used to manage minor

injuries at home or work. The module, therefore, unpacks the importance of prompt care

and attention needed prior to the arrival of an ambulance.

Unit 1.1: Safety

This unit covers the essential safety protocols and precautions necessary at any

emergency scene, emphasising risk assessment and protection for all involved.

Refer to pages 10 - 11 of your textbook for more on the following:

1.1.1 Personal Safety

1.1.2 Safety of the Scene

1.1.3 Safety to the Injured Person

Unit 1.2:  Medico-legal implications

This section examines the legal responsibilities and potential legal consequences of

actions taken while providing emergency aid.

Refer to pages 11 - 12 of your textbook for more on the following:

1.2.1 Consent and its Types

Unit 1.3:  Good Samaritan clause

This unit explores the Good Samaritan laws that protect individuals who assist others in

emergency situations from legal liability. Refer to page 13 of your textbook for more

information.

Unit 1.4: Abandonment

This section delves into the ethical and legal issues of ending assistance in an emergency,

outlining safe and legal practices for disengagement. Refer to page 14 of your textbook for

more information.

11

Module Summary

Module 1 focused on the fundamental aspects of safely and legally managing an

emergency scene. It emphasised the critical importance of assessing the scene's safety to

ensure the protection of oneself and others. The module also delved into the medico-legal

aspects of administering first aid and highlighted the protections provided by the Good

Samaritan law. Furthermore, it addressed the issue of abandonment, stressing the

obligation to remain with the casualty until it is suitable to transfer care.

bodies operate both in health and disease.

the recovery position was also included. Additionally, the module emphasised the

importance of a well-equipped first aid kit, detailing its essential contents and demonstrating

how to use them effectively.
