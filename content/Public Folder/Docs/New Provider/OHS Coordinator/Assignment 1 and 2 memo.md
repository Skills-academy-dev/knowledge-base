Task 1: Workplace Hazard Identification (15 marks)

1. Hazard Identification (5 marks)

Allocate: 1 mark per valid hazard (any reasonable answer)

Accept any relevant hazards such as:

●  Wet or slippery floors

●  Loose electrical wires

●  Poor lighting

●  Cluttered walkways

●  Broken equipment

●  Sharp objects

●  Poor ventilation

●  Overloaded plugs

●  Unsafe storage

●  Lack of signage

✔ Accept any realistic hazard observed in the environment

2. Hazard Classification (5 marks)

Allocate: 1 mark per correct classification

Possible classifications:

●  Physical

●  Chemical

●  Biological

●  Ergonomic

●  Mechanical

●  Psychological

✔ Accept correct matching between hazard and type

✔ Allow reasonable justification if classification is arguable

3. Risk Explanation (5 marks)

Allocate: 1 mark per valid explanation

Expected answers:

●  Slipping and falling injuries

●  Electric shock or fire

●  Cuts or physical injury

●  Breathing problems

●  Muscle strain

●  Long-term health effects

●  Accidents due to poor visibility

✔ Must clearly link hazard → possible harm

Task 1: Hazard Analysis and Risk Prioritisation (15 marks)

1. Hazard Identification (5 marks)

Allocate: 1 mark per valid hazard (max 5)

Possible answers:

●  Boxes in walkways (trip hazard)

●  Wet/slippery floor from leaking fridge

●

Improper manual handling (lifting heavy items)

●  Overloaded electrical plugs

●  Congested working area

●  Poor housekeeping

●  Lack of warning signage

✔ Accept any realistic hazard inferred from the scenario

2. Risk Assessment (5 marks)

Allocate: 1 mark per correct risk + explanation (max 5)

Examples:

●  Wet floor → High (risk of slipping injury)

●  Overloaded plugs → High (fire risk)

●  Boxes in walkway → Medium/High (trip hazard)

●  Poor lifting → Medium (muscle injury)

✔ Accept:

●  Logical risk level

●  Reasonable explanation

3. Risk Prioritisation (5 marks)

Allocate:

●  2 marks for correct identification of high-risk hazards

●  3 marks for explanation

Expected high-priority hazards:

●  Electrical overload

●  Wet/slippery floor

Reasons:

●

Immediate danger

●  Risk of serious injury or fire

●  High likelihood of occurrence

✔ Accept any well-justified prioritisation
