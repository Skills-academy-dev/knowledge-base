Introduction To Medical Studies Basic Study Guide

Welcome to your Medical Studies Basic Study Guide!

We’re genuinely excited to have you here — no dramatics, this is a solid step into the world of

healthcare knowledge, and that’s a big deal

You’ve

officially begun a journey where

science, care, and professionalism come together. This programme is designed to help you

understand how the human body works, how healthcare systems function, and how safe,

ethical care is delivered every day. Whether your goal is further medical studies or working in

a healthcare-related environment, this course lays the groundwork you’ll build on for years.

Think of this study guide as your steady, reliable sidekick. It’s here to keep your learning

focused, organised, and manageable — no overwhelm, no chaos. It works hand-in-hand with

your textbook, helping you break content down, revise effectively, and stay on track.

To succeed in this course, make sure you work through the textbook and complete all

required learning activities. You will need to:

●  Study Modules 1–3 and complete Assignment 1, and

●  Study Modules 4–6 and complete Assignment 2.

must be completed and submitted for assessment. Once submitted, your work will be

returned with tutor feedback — and trust us, that feedback matters. It helps you strengthen

your understanding, correct gaps, and grow your confidence in applying medical knowledge

accurately.

As you move through each module, you’ll encounter activities, reflection points, and practical

thinking tasks designed to help you connect theory to real healthcare situations. These tasks

are designed to help you think like someone working in or alongside healthcare professionals,

where accuracy, care, and responsibility are essential.

Before you begin, make sure you have received:

●  Your Medical Studies Basic Textbook

●  This Study Guide

●  Assignment 1 and Assignment 2

Go in with confidence. You belong here and you’re more capable than you think.

How to Use the Medical Studies Basic Study Guide

●  Study the modules in order

The content is structured to build understanding step by step, especially for medical

terms and basic concepts.

●  Focus on understanding key concepts

Take time to understand ideas rather than memorising, as many topics explain how

the body and healthcare systems work.

●  Learn medical terminology as you Refer

New terms appear throughout the textbook and should be learned alongside the

related content.

●  Use examples to understand theory

Examples help explain how medical concepts apply in real healthcare situations.

●  Refer carefully before answering activities

Activities are based directly on the content and require careful Refering of the module.

●  Revise each module before moving on

Reviewing content regularly helps reinforce knowledge and prevents overload later.

●  Use assignments as a learning tool

Assignments help you identify which topics need more attention and revision.

Using this guide consistently will help you build a strong foundation in medical studies and

prepare confidently for assessments.

Introduction

Module 1 introduces the essential foundations of medical studies that underpin all healthcare

learning and practice. It provides an overview of how healthcare systems are structured and

how different healthcare professionals contribute to patient care. The module emphasises

professionalism, ethical behaviour, and effective communication as key elements of

healthcare practice. It also introduces patient-centred care, basic concepts of health and

disease, and medication awareness. These topics help learners understand how healthcare

services function in real-world settings. The knowledge gained in this module forms the basis

for all subsequent modules in the course.

Unit 1: Introduction to Medical Studies

This unit introduces medical studies as a field that supports healthcare delivery and patient

care across a variety of settings. It explains what medical studies involve and why they are

important within healthcare environments. The unit highlights the relevance of medical

studies to both clinical and non-clinical roles. It also introduces pathways for education and

training within the healthcare sector.

Introduction

Module 2 focuses on medical terminology and communication as essential components of

safe and effective healthcare practice. Healthcare environments rely on a shared medical

language to ensure accuracy, clarity, and consistency in patient care. This module introduces

how medical terms are structured and how terminology is applied across different body

systems. It also explores communication methods used in healthcare, including verbal,

written, and digital communication. Emphasis is placed on professionalism, accuracy, and

safety when using medical language. The knowledge gained in this module supports clear

communication and reduces the risk of errors in healthcare settings.

Unit 1: Structure of Medical Terms

This unit introduces the basic structure of medical terms and explains how words are formed

using specific components. It focuses on understanding how medical terms are built to

convey precise meaning. The unit helps learners break down complex terms into

understandable parts. This foundational knowledge supports accurate interpretation and

correct use of medical terminology.

Refer to pages 45-47 of your textbook for more information on the topics below:

1.1 Root Words

Explains root words as the core part of medical terms that provide the main meaning. It

highlights how understanding root words helps identify the body part or concept being

described.

1.2 Prefixes

Defines prefixes as word parts added to the beginning of medical terms. It explains how

prefixes modify meaning by indicating location, number, size, or condition.

1.3 Suffixes

Defines suffixes as word parts added to the end of medical terms. It explains how suffixes

often describe procedures, conditions, or diseases.

1.4 Combining Forms

Explains combining forms as root words joined with a vowel to make pronunciation easier. It

highlights how combining forms are commonly used when building complex medical terms.

medical studies. Anatomy focuses on the structure of the human body, while physiology

explains how body parts function and interact. This module explores how the body is

organised from the cellular level to complete body systems. It also introduces key systems

responsible for movement, circulation, breathing, digestion, elimination, control, and

regulation. Understanding these systems helps learners recognise normal body function and

identify changes that may indicate illness. The knowledge gained in this module supports

safe healthcare practice and effective communication.
