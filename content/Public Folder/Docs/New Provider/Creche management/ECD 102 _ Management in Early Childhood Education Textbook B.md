A Practical Guide to Leading and Running an ECD Centre

High-quality ECD management turns values into daily practice. It aligns teams, resources, and spaces so children experience safe, engaging, and coherent learning. Strong leadership, transparent documentation, and reliable routines create calm classrooms where growth is visible and sustained.

This textbook equips you to build that standard. You’ll learn to map outcomes into workable plans, organise staffing and budgets, create inclusive learning environments, implement curriculum confidently, observe and assess progress, meet compliance requirements, and collaborate with families for lasting impact.

Inside this text, you'll explore:

Understanding the ECD landscape: Explore the historical context, philosophies, and current trends that shapes the ECD field. Establishing a strong foundation: Establish and manage an ECD centre, from developing policies and procedures to ensuring compliance with regulations. Creating a nurturing environment: Cultivate a safe, inclusive, and developmentally appropriate learning environment for children of all abilities. Effective leadership and teamwork: Hone leadership skills and foster a collaborative team culture among ECD centre staff. Curriculum development and implementation: Design and implement age-appropriate, engaging curricula that promote holistic child development. Child observation and assessment: Observe and assess children’s progress to tailor learning experiences that meet individual needs. Parent involvement and partnerships: Build strong partnerships with parents and families to enhance children’s learning and wellbeing.

You will leave ready to run daily operations with confidence, document progress clearly, and lead a centre where children learn well and families feel supported.
