Instructions

●  Use any available smartphone or digital camera to complete all tasks.

●  Take all photos yourself — do not use images from the internet.

●  Use your home, school, or surrounding environment (no spending required).

●  Ensure all photos are clear, visible, and relevant to the task.

●  Submit the correct number of photos for each task as instructed.

●  Clearly label each photo according to Part and Task number.

●  Focus on demonstrating the concept and effort, not perfection.

Assignment 2

PART 1 (20 MARKS)

Task 1: Types of Lighting (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 3 photos showing different lighting conditions:

●  Natural light (sunlight)

●  Artificial light (lamp or indoor light)

●  Low light (dim or night setting)

Assessment Criteria:

●  Correct representation of lighting types (4)

●

Image clarity and visibility (3)

Task 2: Direction of Light (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 3 photos of the same subject using:

●  Front lighting

●  Side lighting

●  Backlighting

Assessment Criteria:

●  Correct use of lighting direction (4)

●  Visible effect of light (shadows/highlights) (3)

Task 3: Shadows and Mood (6 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 2 photos showing:

●  Strong shadows

●  Soft or minimal shadows

6

Assessment Criteria:

●  Clear shadow differences (3)

●  Mood and visual impact (3)

PART 2 (20 MARKS)

Task 1: Colour Temperature (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 3 photos showing:

●  Warm tones (yellow/orange)

●  Cool tones (blue)

●  Neutral tones

Assessment Criteria:

●  Correct colour temperature shown (4)

●  Visual clarity and colour accuracy (3)

Task 2: White Balance Settings (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 3 photos of the same subject using:

●  Daylight setting

●  Tungsten/Indoor setting

●  Auto setting

Assessment Criteria:

●  Clear difference between white balance settings (4)

●

Image quality and consistency (3)

Task 3: Colour in Composition (6 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 2 photos showing:

●  Strong, vibrant colours

●  Limited or muted colours

7

Assessment Criteria:

●  Effective use of colour (3)

●  Visual impact and composition (3)

PART 3 (20 MARKS)

Task 1: Motion and Long Exposure (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 2 photos showing:

●  Motion blur

●  Light movement or trails

Assessment Criteria:

●  Clear motion effect (4)

●  Creativity and effort (3)

Task 2: Creative Techniques (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 3 photos using:

●  Reflection (mirror, water, glass)

●  Unusual angle (high or low)

●  Framing (objects around subject)

Assessment Criteria:

●  Correct use of techniques (4)

●  Creativity and composition (3)

Task 3: Storytelling Through Images (6 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 2 photos that tell a story:

●  One showing an action

●  One showing the result

8

Assessment Criteria:

●  Clear storytelling sequence (3)

●  Visual clarity and meaning (3)

●  Reflection questions do not have marks.

Reflection Question

How did your control of lighting, colour balance, and creative techniques influence the mood

and visual impact of your images, and how would you refine your technical and artistic

choices to produce more professional and intentional results in future photography work?

TOTAL: 60 MARKS
