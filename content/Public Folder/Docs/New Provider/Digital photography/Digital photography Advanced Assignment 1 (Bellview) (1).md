Instructions

●  Use any available smartphone or digital camera to complete all tasks.

●  Take all photos yourself — do not use images from the internet.

●  Use your home, school, or surrounding environment (no spending required).

●  Ensure all photos are clear, visible, and relevant to the task.

●  Submit the correct number of photos for each task as instructed.

●  Clearly label each photo according to Part and Task number.

●  Focus on demonstrating the concept and effort, not perfection.

Assignment 1

PART 1 (20 MARKS)

Task 1: Photography Purpose Series (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 3 different photos, each showing a different purpose of photography:

●  Memory (e.g., family item, personal object)

●  Communication (e.g., sign, activity, message)

●  Creativity (e.g., artistic or abstract image)

Submission: 3 photos

Marks:

●  Correct representation of each purpose (3)

●  Creativity and effort (4)

Task 2: Camera Feature Practice (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 3 photos of the same subject, using different camera features:

●  Flash ON

●  Flash OFF

●  Any special mode (portrait, HDR, night mode, etc.)

Submission: 3 photos

Marks:

●  Clear difference between images (4)

●  Correct use of camera features (3)

Task 3: Composition Awareness (6 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 2 photos of the same scene:

●  One poorly framed (subject not clear or off-centre)

●  One well-framed (subject clear and balanced)

6

Submission: 2 photos

Marks:

●  Visible improvement in composition (4)

●  Subject clarity and framing (2)

PART 2 (20 MARKS)

Task 1: Lighting Conditions (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 3 photos of the same subject:

●  Bright light (outdoor daylight)

●  Low light (evening or dim room)

●

Indoor light

Submission: 3 photos

Marks:

●  Clear difference in lighting (4)

●  Visibility of subject (3)

Task 2: Motion Capture (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 2 photos of a moving subject:

●  One where motion is frozen (sharp subject)

●  One where motion is blurred

(Examples: walking person, running water, moving car)

Submission: 2 photos

Marks:

●  Clear motion difference (4)

●  Technical attempt (3)

7

Task 3: Brightness and ISO Effect (6 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 2 photos in low light:

●  One darker image

●  One brighter image (use night mode or exposure adjustment)

Submission: 2 photos

Marks:

●  Visible brightness difference (3)

●

Image clarity (3)

PART 3 (20 MARKS)

Task 1: Focus Points (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 3 photos of the same scene, focusing on:

●  Foreground

●  Middle ground

●  Background

Submission: 3 photos

Marks:

●  Clear focus differences (4)

●  Sharpness of focused area (3)

Task 2: Depth of Field (7 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 2 photos of a subject:

●  One with blurred background

●  One with sharp background

Submission: 2 photos

8

Marks:

●  Clear depth difference (4)

●  Subject emphasis (3)

Task 3: Perspective and Distance (6 Marks)

Instructions:

Take 2 photos of the same object:

●  Close-up shot

●  Far/distant shot

Submission: 2 photos

Marks:

●  Clear change in perspective (3)

●  Composition and framing (3)

●    Reflection questions have no marks

Reflection Question

How did your understanding of exposure, focus, and composition influence the decisions you

made while capturing your images, and in what ways would you refine your approach to

achieve more technically controlled and visually impactful photographs in future shoots?
