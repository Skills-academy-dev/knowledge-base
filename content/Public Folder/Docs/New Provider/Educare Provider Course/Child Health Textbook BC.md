Developed byCape Town, South AfricaLearn the Foundations for Safe, Nurturing CareEvery calm feed, settled nap, and curious giggle begins with the care children cancount on. When routines are predictable and messages are clear, little bodies andgrowing brains feel safe enough to explore, connect, and thrive. Small decisions - howwe hold, soothe, clean, notice, and respond - become the moments that shape healthybeginnings.Child Health is your practical companion for turning those moments into confident care.It brings together people-first skills, clear frameworks, and simple systems you can usefrom day one, linking what you know about development with what you do in dailyroutines.In this guide, you’ll learn to:Understand Child Health Fundamentals (Birth – 6 Years): Define health, recognisethe signs of a healthy child, trace factors from conception, and connect health tototal development within a coordinated team approach.Connect Health and Environment: Grasp community and environmental health,how diseases spread, and practical prevention strategies - hygiene routines,immunisation support, and safe, child-friendly spaces.Build Anatomy & Physiology Know-How: Get an accessible overview of theskeletal, muscular, nervous, respiratory, circulatory, digestive, urinary, sensory, andreproductive systems to spot concerns early and communicate clearly.Support Maternal and Newborn Care: Understand prenatal care and the birthprocess, and how maternal health shapes feeding, bonding, and earlydevelopment.Provide Nutrition From Birth to 6 Years: Compare breast- and formula feeding,manage weaning and the introduction of solids, and recognise common nutritionalproblems with practical, family-aware guidance.Deliver Confident Daily Care for Babies (0–2 Years): Practise safe handling andsoothing, bath and nappy hygiene, layette setup, and calm routines that supportsleep, comfort, and secure attachment.Approachable and action-ready, this book turns knowledge into care you can see andfeel. With realistic scenarios, checklists, visuals, and conversation prompts for familiesand the health team, you’ll build a calm, dependable rhythm where everyone knowswhat to do and why it matters.Child Health
