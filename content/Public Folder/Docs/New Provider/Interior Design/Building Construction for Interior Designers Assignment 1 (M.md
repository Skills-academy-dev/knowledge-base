Assignment 1
Question 1: Construction Team and Project Planning (15 Marks)
You have been appointed as the interior designer for a new coffee shop in Durban.
Task:
Create a project planning document that includes:
1. Identify five construction professionals who may be involved in the project and
explain their roles. (5)
2. List the five main phases of the construction project in the correct order. (5)
3. Explain how the interior designer will contribute during the design and construction
phases. (5)
[15 Marks]
Question 2: Structural Assessment Report (20 Marks)
You are renovating an existing office building.
The client wants:
● Two walls removed
● A suspended ceiling installed
● New flooring throughout the office
Task:
Prepare a short structural assessment report that includes:
1. Explain the difference between a load-bearing wall and a partition wall. (4)
2. Describe two risks of removing a load-bearing wall without professional approval. (4)
3. Identify two floor systems that may be suitable for this office project and explain why. (4)
4. Recommend a ceiling system and justify your choice. (4)
5. Explain why an engineer should be consulted before alterations begin. (4)
[20 Marks]
6

Question 3: Site and Environmental Analysis (15 Marks)
A client wants to build a small design studio in a coastal area of the Western Cape.
Task:
Prepare a site analysis that includes:
1. Identify four environmental factors that must be considered before construction begins. (4)
2. Explain how each factor may affect the building. (4)
3. Recommend two suitable materials or finishes for a coastal environment and explain your
choices. (4)
4. Suggest one sustainable building strategy suitable for this project. (3)
[15 Marks]
Question 4: Building Regulations and Safety Audit (15 Marks)
You are asked to inspect a small retail store before it opens to the public.
During your inspection, you notice:
● No wheelchair access ramp
● Poor emergency signage
● Slippery floor tiles near the entrance
● Narrow circulation routes
Task:
Write a safety and compliance report that:
1. Identifies the four problems. (4)
2. Explains why each issue creates a safety or accessibility concern. (4)
3. Recommends an appropriate solution for each problem. (4)
4. Explains the importance of complying with SANS standards and building regulations. (3)
[15 Marks]
7

Question 5: Building Materials and Interior Finishes Selection (20 Marks)
A client is redesigning a modern open-plan office.
The client wants:
● Durable materials
● Easy maintenance
● Good acoustics
● Sustainable design
Task:
Prepare a material selection proposal that includes:
5.1 Structural and Building Materials
Select and justify:
1. One masonry, concrete, or stone material. (3)
2. One timber product. (3)
3. One metal product. (3)
4. One glass product. (3)
5.2 Interior Finishes
Select and justify:
1. One floor finish. (2)
2. One wall finish. (2)
3. One ceiling finish. (2)
5.3 Sustainability
Recommend one innovative or sustainable material and explain its benefits. (2)
[20 Marks]
8

Question 6: Interior Component Application Project (15 Marks)
A client wants a reception area designed for a professional consulting business.
Task:
Create a concept proposal that includes:
1. A simple labelled floor plan sketch showing:
● Reception desk
● Waiting area
● Circulation space
● Storage area (5)
2. Explain how ergonomics influenced your layout decisions. (3)
3. Explain how accessibility requirements were considered. (3)
4. Recommend suitable furniture, joinery, and interior components for the space and
justify your choices. (4)
[15 Marks]
