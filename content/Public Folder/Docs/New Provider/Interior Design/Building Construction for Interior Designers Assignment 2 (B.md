Question 1: Building Services Assessment Report (20 Marks)
You have been appointed as the interior designer for a new medical consulting practice.
The client requires:
● Reliable plumbing systems
● Comfortable indoor temperatures
● Efficient lighting
● Strong security measures
Task
Prepare a building services report that includes:
1.1 Plumbing and Water Supply Systems
Identify and explain:
● Two plumbing fixtures required for the practice. (4)
● Two water-saving strategies that can be implemented. (4)
1.2 HVAC Systems
Recommend a suitable ventilation and air-conditioning system and explain:
● Why it is suitable for the building. (4)
1.3 Electrical and Lighting Systems
Recommend:
● Two lighting types for the reception area. (4)
1.4 Security Systems
Recommend:
● Two security systems for the practice and explain their purpose. (4)
[20 Marks]

Question 2: Fire Safety and Emergency Planning (15 Marks)
You are designing a two-storey office building.
Task
Develop a fire and life-safety proposal that includes:
1. Identify five fire safety systems or features that should be included in the building. (5)
2. Explain how each feature improves occupant safety. (5)
3. Create a simple emergency evacuation procedure consisting of five steps. (5)
[15 Marks]
Question 3: Space Planning and Ergonomics Project (20 Marks)
A client wants to convert a 60 m² open-plan area into a modern office space for eight
employees. The space must include:
● Reception area
● Workstations
● Meeting area
● Storage area
Task
3.1 Space Planning Sketch
Create a labelled floor plan showing:
● Reception area
● Eight workstations
● Meeting area
● Storage area
● Circulation routes (10)
7

3.2 Ergonomics
Explain:
● Three ergonomic principles used in your design. (6)
3.3 Human Comfort
Recommend:
● Two ways to improve employee comfort and productivity. (4)
[20 Marks]
Question 4: Accessibility and Universal Design Assessment (15 Marks)
You are reviewing a community library that will be used by people of different ages and abilities.
Task
Prepare an accessibility assessment report.
Include:
1. Four accessibility features that should be provided. (4)
2. Explain how each feature improves accessibility. (4)
3. Identify two barriers that may prevent access for people with disabilities. (2)
4. Recommend solutions for each barrier. (2)
5. Explain the importance of universal design in public buildings. (3)
[15 Marks]

Question 5: Construction Documentation Exercise (15 Marks)
You are preparing documentation for a small restaurant renovation project.
Task
5.1 Construction Drawings
Explain the purpose of:
● Floor plans
● Elevations
● Sections
● Construction details (8)
5.2 Documentation Review
List:
● Four items that should be checked before issuing drawings for construction. (4)
5.3 Site Coordination
Explain why site coordination is important during construction. (3)
[15 Marks]

A client wants to renovate a small café to create a modern and environmentally friendly interior.
The client requires:
● Sustainable design
● Efficient space planning
● Good lighting
● Comfortable seating

6.1 Sustainable Design
Recommend:
● Three sustainable design features and explain their benefits. (6)
6.2 Interior Layout
Explain:
● How the layout will improve customer movement and comfort. (4)
6.3 Lighting Design
Recommend:
● Two lighting solutions suitable for the café. (2)
6.4 Project Management
Identify:
● Three project management activities that should be carried out during the
renovation project. (3)
