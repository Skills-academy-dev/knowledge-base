Assignment 2
Task 1: Materials and Finishes Selection (20 Marks)
Scenario:
You are designing a small open-plan kitchen and dining area.
Questions:
1. Identify three types of interior materials you would use (e.g. wood, tile, fabric). (3)
2. For each material, explain its use in the space. (6)
3. Suggest two suitable surface finishes (e.g. matte, gloss). (2)
4. Explain why these finishes are suitable for a kitchen/dining area. (4)
5. Suggest one cost-effective material alternative and explain why it is suitable. (5)
Task 2: Lighting Plan (20 Marks)
1. Identify three types of lighting:
● Ambient
● Task
● Decorative (3)
2. Explain the purpose of each type. (6)
3. Suggest one example of each lighting type for your space. (3)
4. Explain how lighting affects:
● Mood (4)
● Functionality (4)
Task 3: Interior Decorating and Styling (20 Marks)
1. Choose a theme/style for the space. (2)
2. Explain your choice. (4)
3. List four decorative elements you would include. (4)
6

4. Explain how these elements improve the space. (6)
5. Explain two styling techniques you would use (e.g. grouping, focal point). (4)
Task 4: Creating a Styled Space Plan (20 Marks)
1. Draw a basic layout of the kitchen/dining area. (8)
2. Show:
● Furniture placement
● Decorative elements (6)
3. Identify two ways to avoid overcrowding the space. (4)
4. Explain how your layout ensures:
● Comfort
● Practical use (2)
Task 5: Real-Life Interior Application (20 Marks)
Scenario:
A client wants to redesign a small café or retail space to attract more customers.
Questions:
1. Identify two key design considerations for a commercial space. (2)
2. Explain how the design can:
● Improve customer experience (4)
● Increase functionality (4)
3. Suggest three design features suitable for this space. (6)
4. Explain two differences between residential and commercial design. (4)
