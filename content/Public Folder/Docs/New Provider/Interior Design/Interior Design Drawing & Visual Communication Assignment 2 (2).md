Assignment 2
SECTION A: SPACE PLANNING & DESIGN PRINCIPLES (20 MARKS)
Task 1: Living Room Layout Scenario (20 marks)
Scenario:
A family living room feels cramped and unbalanced. The furniture is placed randomly, there is
no clear focal point, and movement through the room is difficult.
Instructions:
1. Draw an improved layout of the living room. (10)
2. Show clear circulation space (movement paths). (4)
3. Apply at least TWO design principles (e.g., balance, harmony, emphasis). (4)
4. Label key elements (e.g., sofa, table, TV, walkway). (2)
SECTION B: PERSPECTIVE DRAWING IN PRACTICE (30 MARKS)
Task 2: One-Point Perspective Scenario (15 marks)
Scenario:
You are presenting a simple bedroom design to a client who wants to understand depth and
layout.
Instructions:
Draw a one-point perspective of a bedroom that includes:
● A bed
● A window
● A desk
Your drawing must show:
● Horizon line
● One vanishing point
● Depth
6

Task 3: Two-Point Perspective Scenario (15 marks)
Scenario:
A client wants to see how a cupboard will look from an angle.
Instructions:
Draw a two-point perspective of a cupboard or room corner showing:
● Two vanishing points
● 3D structure
● Clear depth
SECTION C: VISUAL PRESENTATION & COMMUNICATION (30 MARKS)
Task 4: Mood Board Scenario (15 marks)
Scenario:
A client wants a modern home office that feels calm and productive.
Instructions:
Create a mood board that shows:
● Colour scheme
● Materials (e.g., wood, metal, fabric)
● Furniture style
● Overall theme
(Can be hand-drawn or cut-and-paste style)
Task 5: Presentation Layout Scenario (15 marks)
Scenario:
You need to present your design ideas to a client in a clear and professional way.
7

Instructions:
Create a presentation page that includes:
● A title
● At least one drawing (from this assignment)
● Labels or short notes explaining your design
● A neat layout
SECTION D: INTEGRATED DESIGN PROJECT (20 MARKS)
Task 6: Small Apartment Design Scenario
Scenario:
A young professional lives in a small apartment. The space must be:
● Functional
● Comfortable
● Visually appealing
The current layout is cluttered and poorly organised.
Instructions:
1. Draw a floor plan showing an improved layout. (8)
2. Draw a simple perspective view of the space. (6)
3. Show at least two design improvements in your drawings. (2)
4. Ensure your design shows good use of space and layout. (4)
TOTAL: 100 MARKS
