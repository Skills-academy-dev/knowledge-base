Assignment 1
Task 1: Understanding the Client Brief (20 Marks)
You are given the following scenario:
A client wants to redesign their small living room. They need a comfortable space for
relaxing and entertaining guests. The room has limited space and a moderate budget.
Questions:
1. Identify three client needs. (3)
2. Identify two functional requirements. (2)
3. Identify two aesthetic requirements. (2)
4. Create a basic client brief using the format from the textbook. (8)
5. Suggest a suitable budget-saving strategy. (5)
Task 2: Design Concept and Mood Board (20 Marks)
1. Choose a design style/theme (e.g. modern, traditional). (2)
2. Explain your choice. (4)
3. Create a mood board including:
● Colours
● Materials
● Furniture ideas (8)
4. Explain how your mood board reflects the client’s needs. (6)
Task 3: Colour and Design Principles (20 Marks)
1. Select a colour scheme for the room. (4)
2. Explain how the colours will affect:
● Mood (3)
● Space (3)
6

3. Identify and explain three design principles used:
● Balance
● Harmony
● Contrast (6)
4. Give two examples of how these principles are applied. (4)
Task 4: Space Planning and Layout (20 Marks)
1. Draw a simple floor plan of the room. (8)
2. Show:
● Furniture placement
● Movement space (circulation) (6)
3. Explain two layout decisions you made. (4)
4. Identify one space-saving solution. (2)
Task 5: Furniture and Decoration Plan (20 Marks)
1. List four furniture items you will include. (4)
2. Explain why each item is suitable. (8)
3. Suggest three decorative elements (e.g. cushions, plants). (3)
4. Explain how your design improves:
● Comfort (3)
● Functionality (2)
TOTAL: 100 MARKS
