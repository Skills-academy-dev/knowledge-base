South Africa.

court can hear, and recognise that the Constitutional Court is the highest court in

INTRODUCTION

This module introduces you to the concept of law, its purpose in society, and how it applies

in South Africa. You will learn about different types of laws, the sources of South African

law, and how the judicial system is structured. By the end of this module, you will have a

solid understanding of the role that law plays in maintaining order and resolving disputes in

society.

Unit 1.1: Introduction to the Law in South Africa

This unit explains what law is and why it is important for society. You will learn how the

South African legal system is based on Roman-Dutch and English law and how the

Constitution is the highest law in the country. This unit covers how laws are enforced and

how they are different from other rules like moral and social rules.

Read pages 11—13 of your textbook for more information.

Unit 1.2: The Purpose and Function of a Legal System

This unit explores the reasons why laws exist and how they help maintain order and justice

in society. You will learn about the different functions of a legal system, such as protecting

people's rights, settling disputes, and punishing lawbreakers. The unit also explains how

laws promote fairness and equality for all.

between real rights (such as owning property) and personal rights (such as the right to be

paid for work). This unit helps you understand how rights and duties create relationships

INTRODUCTION

This module introduces you to the National Credit Act (NCA), which protects consumers

when they borrow money or buy on credit. You will learn about different types of credit

agreements, how credit providers must share information, and how customers are

protected from unfair practices. The module also covers how to handle over-indebtedness,

settle disputes, and understand the role of credit bureaus.

Unit 4.1: The National Credit Act

This unit explains what the National Credit Act (NCA) is and why it was created. You will

learn how the NCA protects consumers, promotes fair lending practices, and prevents

reckless lending.
