See Around Corners: Master Strategic Risk ManagementStrategic Risk Management is the discipline of seeing around corners. It helps leadersanticipate uncertainty, weigh upside against downside, and make decisions thatprotect value while unlocking growth. At its core, it blends governance, analytics, andculture so organisations can act early, respond decisively, and emerge stronger.This textbook is a practical roadmap for students, analysts, and managers who want tomaster the full risk lifecycle - from identification and assessment to treatment, monitoring,and strategic integration. It translates standards and theory into tools you can use inboardrooms, project offices, audits, and crisis rooms.Within these pages, you'll gain expertise in:Grasping the strategic role of risk: Learn how risk informs objectives, resourceallocation, and value creation so decisions are balanced, defensible, and timely.Framing risks in context: Use structured scans (like PESTLE and SWOT) andstakeholder input to surface what truly matters across functions and time horizons.Assessing with rigour: Apply qualitative and quantitative techniques to comparelikelihood and impact, set priorities, and align with appetite, tolerance, andcapacity.Translating analysis into action: Select treatment strategies, design proportionatecontrols, and build clear action plans with owners, timelines, and milestones.Building organisational readiness: Develop crisis management and businesscontinuity capabilities that shorten recovery times and protect reputation.Monitoring what moves the needle: Establish KRIs, reporting rhythms, andassurance activities that keep leadership informed and accountable.Shaping culture and capability: Embed behaviors, training, and change practicesthat make risk awareness part of everyday decision-making.Scanning the horizon and anticipating emerging risks - from cyber and ESG tosupply chain and M&A - and factor scenarios into strategy. When you can frame uncertainty clearly, you reduce surprises, focus resources, andbuild trust. This book equips you to set appetite and limits, read early signals, andtranslate analysis into action. Master these skills to stay proactive under pressure, reduceuncertainty, and earn confidence from your team, your clients, and your stakeholders.Strategic Risk ManagementDeveloped byCape Town, South Africa
