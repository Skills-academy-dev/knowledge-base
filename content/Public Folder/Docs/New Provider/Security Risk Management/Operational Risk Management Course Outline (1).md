Module 1: Foundations and

Definitions

Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students should be able to:

1.  Define operational risk and distinguish it from other risk types.

2.  Summarize key definitions from Basel II/III and COSO frameworks.

3.  Explain the Four-Cause Model: processes, people, systems, and external factors.

4.  Describe internal versus external risk drivers and their impact.

5.  Illustrate the operational risk taxonomy and classification categories.

6.  Analyze historical operational loss events for lessons learned.

7.  Introduce major course case studies (e.g., Chernobyl, Barings) and their significance.

Module 2: Governance and

Frameworks

Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students should be able to:

1.  Describe the roles of the Board, Chief Risk Officer, and three lines of defense.

2.  Develop a risk appetite statement aligned with organizational strategy.

3.  Outline policy development processes for operational risk management.

4.  Map organizational structure and accountability in ORM.

5.  Assess the function of internal audit and independent reviews.

6.  Identify major regulatory requirements (e.g., Basel, RBI, FMA).

7.  Apply maturity models for ORM framework implementation.

Module 4: Risk Assessment and

Measurement

Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students should be able to:

1.  Compare qualitative and quantitative assessment approaches.

2.  Perform loss distribution modeling to estimate expected and unexpected loss.

3.  Design Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) for operational metrics.

4.  Evaluate capital charge methodologies (BIA, TSA, AMA).

5.  Execute risk scoring and heat mapping.

6.  Ensure data quality and governance.

7.  Apply backtesting and validation techniques.
