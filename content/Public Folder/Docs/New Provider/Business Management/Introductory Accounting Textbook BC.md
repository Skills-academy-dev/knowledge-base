Your Gateway to the Language of AccountingIntroductory Accounting is your step-by-step guide to understanding how moneymoves through a business. Written in clear, accessible language, this textbookturns accounting from a confusing subject into a practical tool you can use tomake better financial decisions in both your personal and professional life.From starting a trading business to preparing final accounts, you’ll build a solidfoundation in the principles, documents, and procedures that every organisationrelies on.In this textbook, you will:Discover how businesses create and manage wealth through differentforms of ownership, including sole traders, partnerships, and companies.Work through the real-world steps of opening a trading concern, fromplanning and registration to acquiring capital and setting up operations.Learn to handle essential accounting documents, including sourcedocuments, filing systems, bank account opening procedures, andauthorisation of transactions.Build a strong foundation in accounting principles and concepts,including the accounting cycle, accounting equation, and IFRS-basedguidelines.Apply double-entry bookkeeping to record transactions, post to generalledger accounts, and prepare a trial balance with confidence.Use key subsidiary journals in trading concerns, such as the Cash ReceiptsJournal, Cash Payments Journal, Analysis Cash Book, and Petty Cash Journal,and post them to the ledger.Master practical tasks like recording wages and salaries, reconciling bankstatements, and preparing final accounts - including the Trading Account,Profit and Loss Account, and Statement of Financial Position.With its structured modules, worked examples, and practice exercises,Introductory Accounting equips you with the knowledge and skills to understandfinancial information and tackle real-world accounting challenges.Introductory AccountingDeveloped byCape Town, South Africa
