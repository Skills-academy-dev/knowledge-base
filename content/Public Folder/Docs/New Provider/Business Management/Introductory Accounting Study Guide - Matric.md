explains the various forms of business ownership, such as sole traders, partnerships, and

Module Summary

In this module, you learned about the purpose of businesses and their primary goal of

generating profit. You explored how businesses can increase or decrease wealth over time,

along with the factors that influence profitability. The module also examined how service

and trading businesses manage their capital growth or reduction.

You were introduced to various forms of business ownership, including sole traders,

partnerships, and companies (both for-profit and non-profit). Each type of ownership was

explained in terms of structure, liability, and wealth generation. The module concluded by

exploring the key activities of businesses—such as service, trading, and manufacturing—

and how these activities contribute to wealth creation.

Module Summary

In this module, you learned how to manage cash transactions for a trading business using

subsidiary journals, which help document cash inflows and outflows accurately.

•

Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ): Records all cash received, such as sales and debtor

payments.

•

•

Cash Payments Journal (CPJ): Tracks all payments, like expenses and purchases.

Cash Book: Combines the CRJ and CPJ to give an overview of cash movements and

assist with bank reconciliation.

•

Petty Cash Journal: Manages small daily expenses and maintains the petty cash fund.

These tools help you organise financial records, ensure accuracy, and manage cash

efficiently—key skills for any trading business.
