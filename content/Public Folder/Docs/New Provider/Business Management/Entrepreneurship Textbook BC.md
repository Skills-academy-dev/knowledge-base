Developed byCape Town, South AfricaStart Smart and Grow Stead with EntrepreneurshipGreat ventures are crafted, not chanced. You frame a problem clearly, explorepossibilities, choose a path, and execute with discipline. With focus andfeedback, the distance from idea to impact gets shorter - and your confidencegrows.The Entrepreneurship textbook equips you with the tools to build that discipline. Itconnects creative exploration with market evidence, planning with execution,and vision with viable numbers. Use it to design a business you can explain in aparagraph, test in a week, and scale with intention.In this guide, you’ll learn to:Understand the Entrepreneur’s Role: What entrepreneurship is, howentrepreneurs and intrapreneurs create value, qualities that matter,economic impact, and common traps to avoid.Spark & Shape Innovation: Generate ideas, apply creativity techniques,protect your concepts, and convert them into testable opportunities.Validate Demand: Define your product, choose a market position, collectand analyse market information, run a SWOT, and articulate your advantage.Make the Money Make Sense: Estimate start-up and total costs, forecastsales, and use scenarios to judge feasibility and guide decisions.Plan for Customers: Build a concise business plan and a marketing plancovering product, pricing for profit, promotion, and distribution.Organise & Finance Operations: Decide on ownership and legal steps, maproles, operations, suppliers, and equipment, and create a financial plan withsources of finance, projections, and loan requirements.Concise, practical, and action-oriented, this book helps you move from ideas toimpact so you can build value for customers and a business that works in the realworld.Entrepreneurship
