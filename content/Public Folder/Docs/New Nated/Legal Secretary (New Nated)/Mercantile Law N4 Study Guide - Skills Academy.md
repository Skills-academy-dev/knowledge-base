MODULE 1

INTRODUCTION TO THE LAW

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

1\. Explain what the term "law" means and how it is different from other rules in South

Africa.

2\. Describe the purpose and function of a legal system in society.

3\. Identify the difference between a real right and a personal right, using examples.

4\. Explain how a legal entity is different from a natural person, as well as their similarities.

5\. List and describe the different sources of South African law.

6\. Understand how court decisions (precedent system) work and explain the difference

between ratio decidendi and obiter dicta.

7\. Define what is meant by national law.

8\. Describe the difference between public law and private law, with examples.

9\. Explain what mercantile law is and how it applies to business and the public sector in

South Africa.

10\. Identify and define the different courts in South Africa.

11\. Understand how the jurisdiction of courts works, meaning which types of cases each

court can hear, and recognise that the Constitutional Court is the highest court in South Africa.

INTRODUCTION

This module introduces you to the concept of law, its purpose in society, and how it applies in South Africa. You will learn about different types of laws, the sources of South African law, and how the judicial system is structured. By the end of this module, you will have a solid understanding of the role that law plays in maintaining order and resolving disputes in society.

Unit 1.1: Introduction to the Law in South Africa

This unit explains what law is and why it is important for society. You will learn how the South African legal system is based on Roman-Dutch and English law and how the Constitution is the highest law in the country. This unit covers how laws are enforced and how they are different from other rules like moral and social rules.

Read pages 11—13 of your textbook for more information.

Unit 1.2: The Purpose and Function of a Legal System

This unit explores the reasons why laws exist and how they help maintain order and justice in society. You will learn about the different functions of a legal system, such as protecting people's rights, settling disputes, and punishing lawbreakers. The unit also explains how laws promote fairness and equality for all.

Unit 1.3: Elements of Law

This unit covers the basic parts that make up the law. You will learn about the difference between real rights (such as owning property) and personal rights (such as the right to be paid for work). This unit helps you understand how rights and duties create relationships between people and businesses. .
