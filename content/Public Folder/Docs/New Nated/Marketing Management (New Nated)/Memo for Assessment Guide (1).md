introduction stage, heavy advertising is needed. The growth stage focuses on expanding

companies may rebrand or discontinue products.

Section D: Short Answer Questions (30 Marks)

26. Correct Answer: Price skimming helps businesses maximize early profits by setting a
high initial price and gradually lowering it. This is common in tech products and luxury
markets.
Page Reference: Page 99, Marketing Management Advance Textbook

27. Correct Answer: Psychological pricing affects how consumers perceive value. It
includes techniques like charm pricing (R99.99 instead of R100), price anchoring
(placing high-priced items next to lower-priced ones), and scarcity pricing (limited-time
discounts).
Page Reference: Page 91, Marketing Management Advance Textbook

28. Correct Answer: The four types of promotional strategies are:
●  Advertising: Paid media like TV, social media ads.
●  Sales Promotion: Discounts, coupons, and offers.
●  Public Relations (PR): Managing brand image through media.

●  Personal Selling: One-on-one customer interactions.

Page Reference: Page 113, Marketing Management Advance Textbook

across platforms.

●  AI-driven marketing enhances personalization, data-driven decision-making, and

customer engagement.

●  Automation reduces costs and improves efficiency in digital advertising.
●  Potential risks include data privacy issues, ethical concerns about manipulation, and

reduced human control.

●  Example: Facebook’s AI-driven ad targeting and its ethical implications.
