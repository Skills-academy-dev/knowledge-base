INTRODUCTION

This module introduces you to the core concepts of marketing, focusing on how businesses

identify and meet customer needs while creating value. You will learn about the origins and

evolution of marketing, the marketing mix, and how businesses align strategies with their

goals. By the end of this module, you’ll understand the fundamentals of marketing and be

ready to apply these principles to real-world scenarios.

Unit 1: The Nature of Marketing

Unit 1.1: What Marketing Is

This unit defines marketing as the process of identifying, anticipating, and satisfying

customer needs profitably. It highlights the importance of marketing in creating value for

both businesses and customers, and explains how marketing facilitates relationships

through activities such as product development, promotion, and distribution.

Read page 13 of your textbook for more information.

Unit 1.2: The Origin of Marketing

Explore the historical roots of marketing, from ancient trade systems to modern-day

practices. This unit discusses how marketing evolved alongside technological

advancements and changing consumer behaviours to become a key business function.

Read pages 13—14 of your textbook for more information.

Unit 1.3: Micro and Macro Perspective

This unit introduces the micro-marketing perspective, focusing on individual business

strategies, and the macro-marketing perspective, which examines the broader societal

impact of marketing. Together, these perspectives demonstrate how marketing operates on

different levels.
