QUESTION 2: CASE STUDY ANALYSIS (50 MARKS)

Scenario

A local company is launching an eco-friendly clothing line targeting environmentally conscious

consumers. The clothing is produced using sustainable materials and fair-trade practices. The

company wants to position itself as the top choice for consumers who prioritise sustainability

and ethical practices.

Questions:

1.  Segmentation Approach:

Explain the segmentation approach the company should adopt, using examples of

demographic, geographic, and behavioural segmentation criteria. (15 marks)

2.  Positioning Strategy:

Develop a positioning strategy for this clothing line, including a clear positioning

statement. (15 marks)

3.  Differentiation Strategy:

Propose three ways the company can differentiate its products from competitors. (10

marks)

4.  Promotion Methods:

Suggest two promotional strategies the company could use to create awareness and

encourage customer loyalty. (10 marks)
