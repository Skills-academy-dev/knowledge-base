BEFORE YOU START
Now it’s time to start working, but before you start studying. First read the contents page to
see what topics we will be discussing. These are mirrored in your Study Guide. You will find
subject outcomes or learning outcomes in your study guide to help you understand each
chapter. The learning outcomes are repeated at the start of every module, to remind you
what is expected of you.
When you are ready to start your studies, read through the first module in your Study Guide
thoroughly.
Do not progress to the next module until:
 you are sure that you understand everything that has been covered in the
module,
 you have completed all the activities in the module,
 you are able to answer all the self-assessment questions confidently, and
 you are competent in each of the learning outcomes listed at the beginning of the
module.
Good luck with your course. Remember that the more thoroughly you study, the better you
will do in your new career. Also remember that the more time you spend doing extra reading
and researching, and learning from others, the more beneficial this learning
process is going to be.
Please read everything that is in the guide, follow all instructions and do all the
exercises and activities prepared for you.
Please note that this study guide does not substitute the textbook. The textbook and the
study guide must be used together to develop full understanding of the concepts in this
subject. This means that the activities and exercises as well as examples and case
studies in the textbook are equally important and should be completed and studied.

MODULE 1
THE CONTEMPORARY CHALLENGES OF
ENTREPRENEURSHIP
The following concepts are covered in this module:
 The concept of entrepreneurship
 The Role of Entrepreneurs and Inttrapreneurs
 Personal Qualities of the Entrepreneur
 Personal resources of the successful entrepreneur
 Economic importance of the entrepreneur
 The merits of entrepreneurship
 Why entrepreneurs fail
 How to avoid pitfalls
Learning Outcomes
By the end of this module, you should be able to:
 Define and understand the main principles of entrepreneurship and
intrapreneurship.
 Distinguish between the roles of entrepreneurs from those of intrapreneurs.
 Name and describe the main qualities of an entrepreneur, applying practical
examples.
 Describe and explain the personal resources required for successful
entrepreneurship.
 Explain the contribution of entrepreneurs to the South African economy.
 State and explain the advantages and opportunities of entrepreneurship.
 Explain the reasons why entrepreneurs fail and state measures to overcome
these failures.
 Name and explain the reasons why businesses succeed.

INTRODUCTION
This module explores the different types of entrepreneurs, what makes them succeed, and
the essential skills for thriving in today's business world. Additionally, it also examines
common reasons for business failures and strategies to avoid them. Navigate the dynamic
landscape of entrepreneurship and discover how to turn ideas into successful ventures.
Unit 1: What is Entrepreneurship?
This section explores the fundamental concept of entrepreneurship, delving into the
process of identifying opportunities and creating value through innovative ventures
Unit 1.1: The Role of Entrepreneurs and Intrapreneurs
This unit delves into the distinct roles of entrepreneurs and intrapreneurs, examining how
they drive innovation and contribute to organisational and economic growth.
