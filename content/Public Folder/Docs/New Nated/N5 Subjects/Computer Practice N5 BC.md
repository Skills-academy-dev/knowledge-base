Practical Computing That Transforms Information Into Insight

Computer practice is the skillset behind modern problem-solving. It’s how raw information becomes insight, routine work gets automated, and results are communicated in ways people can trust.

The Computer Practice N5 textbook takes you from core concepts to advanced features across office applications, networks, data workflows, and the web. You’ll gain the techniques employers expect — and the reasoning that makes your work dependable.

Inside the book, you’ll explore:

Core Computing & Document Mastery: operating-system File Management, intermediate word-processing features, Mail Merge in Word Processing, and Using Electronic Notebooks to capture and organise ideas. Analysis With Spreadsheets: Managing and Editing Spreadsheets, compelling Creating and Editing Charts in Spreadsheets, and Advanced Spreadsheet Functions and Formulas for sound decisions. Networking Foundations: Different Types of Networks and Architectures and The Role of Networks in the Digital Age — from local sharing to cloud collaboration. Data Literacy & Computational Thinking: apply Computational Thinking to break down problems, Find, Access, and Process Data, design Online Forms, and manage shared online workspaces responsibly. Web Fluency: make smart use of Internet Applications, Plug-ins, and Web- Based Services, understand Internet Service Technologies, and conduct credible Searching and Using Online Information. Digital Citizenship & Careers: track Modern Developments and Trends, link ICT to the economy, practise safe, Ethical ICT Usage, and map routes into ICT Careers.

Clear explanations, practice files, and self-checks turn features into fluent habits. By the end, you’ll plan tasks, choose the right tools, protect data, and present results — the foundations of confident, professional work in the digital economy.
