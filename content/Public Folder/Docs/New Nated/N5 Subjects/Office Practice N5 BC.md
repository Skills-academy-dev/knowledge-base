Level Up Your Admin. Lead With Confidence.

Modern offices run on clear communication, reliable systems, and people who can organise work under pressure. Office Practice N5 is for rising administrators and coordinators who want to sharpen judgment, master tools, and support teams at a professional standard in South African workplaces.

You’ll connect the evolving role of office professionals to core principles that drive performance — from communication and time management to technology, HR support, and finance. Each topic moves from context to practice with checklists, templates, and locally relevant notes (including POPIA and basic compliance) you can apply immediately.

Inside the book, you’ll explore:

Professional Communication: write, speak, and present with impact — from polished email and reports to confident meetings and digital collaboration. Time Management & Productivity: prioritise tasks, plan calendars, and manage deadlines using proven methods (e.g., task batching, blockers, and review cycles). Office Technology & Data Practices: use office software, cloud tools, and shared drives effectively; apply basic data management and POPIA-aligned handling. Human Resources Support: assist with recruitment logistics, onboarding documentation, training coordination, and performance admin — discreetly and accurately. Financial Administration & Controls: prepare budgets and petty cash reconciliations, process invoices, and maintain audit-ready records with simple checks and approvals. Customer Service & Professional Standards: manage reception and stakeholder queries, resolve issues diplomatically, and uphold etiquette that reflects the organisation’s values.

This textbook turns sound principles into daily habits you can trust. With practical activities, real scenarios, and quality-focused guidance, you’ll communicate clearly, protect information, and support inclusive, ethical practice.

Walk into your next role ready to coordinate people and processes — and to grow into a trusted, high-impact office professional.
