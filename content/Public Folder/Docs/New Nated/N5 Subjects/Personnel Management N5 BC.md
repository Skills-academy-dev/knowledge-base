Lead People. Grow Performance.

Strong organisations start with intentional people practices. This N5 textbook gives you a clear, practical pathway to build high-performing teams and a positive workplace - from day-one HR operations to strategic, data-driven decisions.

You’ll connect modern HR context with core principles and hands-on application: how personnel management evolved, where it fits in the business, and how to apply fair, compliant, and inclusive practices in real workplaces.

Inside the book, you’ll explore:

The Core Practices: Gain a deep understanding of recruitment, employee selection, training programs, and career development strategies. Learn how to conduct workplace research, analyze trends, and use tools like SWOT analysis and surveys to support data-driven decision-making. Building Relationships: Master techniques for fostering strong interpersonal connections and encouraging team collaboration. Explore recruitment and selection strategies that ensure diverse and inclusive workplaces, while learning how to handle workplace conflicts with professionalism. Motivation and Culture: Discover how to inspire employees, drive engagement, and build a thriving workplace culture that attracts and retains top talent. Dive deeper into performance appraisals and compensation strategies to effectively reward and motivate your team. Enhancing Job Satisfaction: Learn strategies to create a positive work-life balance, reduce employee turnover, and ensure workplace health and safety while promoting long-term career development. Real-World Scenarios: Apply HR principles through case studies, illustrating how the concepts of personnel research, HR provisioning, compensation management, and quality of work life can transform organizational success.

Through practical tools and realistic case studies, you’ll build the habits of a trusted HR practitioner: clear communication, consistent processes, and measurable improvement. Use these skills to support people, strengthen performance, and contribute meaningfully to your organisation’s success.
