Unlock the Key to Strategic Marketing Success

Marketing Management plays a crucial role in shaping business success by influencing consumer perceptions, optimizing product placement, and driving profitability. With the rise of digital transformation, businesses must adopt forward-thinking strategies to remain competitive.

The Marketing Management N5 textbook provides an in-depth analysis of advanced marketing principles, focusing on the strategic components that determine business growth and sustainability.

Topics covered inside the textbook include:

Product and Service Policy: From concept to commercialization, delve into product strategy, including new product development, product portfolio management, and service differentiation in a competitive landscape. Distribution Policy: Analyze distribution channels, logistics, and supply chain management to create efficient and effective systems that deliver products to customers at the right place and time. Price Policy: Explore advanced pricing models, including dynamic pricing, price discrimination, and psychological pricing tactics, to maximize revenue and capture market share. Promotion Policy: Integrate various promotional tools, including digital marketing, social media marketing, content marketing, and traditional advertising, to build brand awareness and drive sales.

With a strong focus on analytical thinking and strategic decision- making, this book is designed for marketing students, business leaders, and professionals eager to refine their marketing acumen.
