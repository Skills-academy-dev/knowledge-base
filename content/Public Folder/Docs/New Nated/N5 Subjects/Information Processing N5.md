TEXTBOOK

Produce Professional Documents. Every Time.

From emails to board packs, the modern office runs on well-prepared information. The Information Processing N5 textbook is your practical guide to fast, accurate typing, meticulous formatting, and reliable transcription across common business scenarios.

You’ll connect disciplined typing technique with real workplace tasks: formatting correspondence and reports, preparing agendas and minutes, laying out financials and tables, and transcribing audio with clarity.

Inside the book, you’ll explore:

Accuracy & Speed Fundamentals: Structured practice plans, 10-minute Accuracy Tests, and techniques to maintain speed under pressure. Editing Mastery & Formatting Rules: Proofreading Signs, layout decisions (Margins, Line Spacing, Alignment), and efficiency with Keyboard Shortcuts. Professional Business Outputs: Letter structures and tone, Circular and Sales Letters, Invitations with correct content and placement, and branded Letterhead conventions. Meetings From Notice To Minutes: Notices that inform, Agendas that guide action, and Minutes using both Standard and Action formats with tables for accountability. Data-Rich Documents: Reports (Director’s and Financial), Programmes and Itineraries with clear sequencing, Financial Statements keyed accurately, and Tabular Statements that make numbers readable. Language & Transcription Capability: Formatting and typing in African Languages with correct diacritics, plus Audio Transcription techniques (speed control, backtrack, accuracy checks) for speeches, minutes, and dictated correspondence.

Designed for real work, this book blends examples with hands-on tasks so you leave with portfolio-ready outputs. Gain the confidence to deliver polished documents on deadline and the discipline to keep improving as your responsibilities grow.
