MODULE 1
ACCURACY AND SPEED DEVELOPMENT
Learning Outcomes
By the end of this module, you should be able to:
→ Maintain a typing speed of at least 45 w.p.m. with a high level of accuracy through
daily practice and structured drills focused on improving both speed and precision.
→ Achieve a minimum speed of 45 w.p.m. in timed speed tests lasting 10 minutes,
ensuring that errors do not exceed 23 keying-in mistakes per test.

INTRODUCTION
In this module, you will learn how to improve both your typing speed and your accuracy.
These skills are important in the workplace, especially in office environments where fast
and correct typing helps you complete tasks quickly and professionally. You will also
practise timed exercises to track your improvement.
Unit 1.1: Importance of Speed and Accuracy
This unit explains why both speed and accuracy are essential in information processing. It
discusses how these two skills affect productivity, the quality of your work, and your ability
to meet deadlines. You will learn how small mistakes can lead to big problems and why
careful typing is just as important as fast typing.
Read page 12 of your textbook for more information.
Unit 1.2: Daily Keyboarding Drills
This unit explores how daily keyboarding practice can help you improve your typing
technique. You will learn how regular drills help build muscle memory, reduce errors, and
increase your confidence. The unit also shows how to set up a proper practice routine for
long-term success.
Read page 13 of your textbook for more information.
Unit 1.3: Timed Accuracy Tests (10 minutes)
This unit covers how to take and benefit from 10-minute timed accuracy tests. These tests
help you track your progress and show how well you can type under pressure. You will also
learn how to identify areas where you need to improve, so you can focus your practice
more effectively.
Read page 14 of your textbook for more information.
