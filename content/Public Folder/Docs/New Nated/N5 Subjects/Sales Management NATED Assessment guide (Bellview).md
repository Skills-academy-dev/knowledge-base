ASSIGNMENT 2
Section A: Case Study Questions (30 Marks)
Question 1: Sales Forecasting and Budgeting
TechAdvance Ltd. is planning its sales strategy for the next financial year. Based on previous perfor-
mance and market analysis, the management aims to achieve a 20% increase in sales revenue.
The sales manager must allocate budgets and set realistic quotas for the team while considering
resource constraints and competitive pressures.
1.1 Identify and explain two methods of sales forecasting that TechAdvance Ltd. could use. (10
marks)
1.2 Discuss the importance of aligning the sales budget with organizational goals and resource
availability. (10 marks)
1.3 Propose two steps the sales manager should take to ensure achievable quota setting. (10
marks)
Section B: Essay-Type Questions (30 Marks)
Question 2: Sales Team Motivation
Motivating a sales team is critical for achieving organizational objectives.
2.1 Explain two intrinsic and two extrinsic motivators that influence sales performance. Provide ex-
amples to illustrate your points. (12 marks)
2.2 Discuss how leadership styles impact sales team motivation, referencing two leadership theo-
ries. (18 marks)
