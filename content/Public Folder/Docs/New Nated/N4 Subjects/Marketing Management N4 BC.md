Think Strategically. Market Confidently.

Marketing drives how organisations create value, reach the right people, and grow sustainably. This textbook is for N4 learners who want a solid, practical foundation — from principles and environments to consumer behaviour, segmentation, and research.

You’ll connect classic marketing theory to South African realities: how markets evolve, how external forces shape choices, and how brands position themselves to win. Each chapter moves from core ideas to tools you can apply in class, at work, or in your own venture.

Inside the book, you’ll explore:

The Fundamental Principles Of Marketing: Clarify what Marketing is, how it evolved, and how the Marketing Concept, Orientations, and the 4Ps fit into a practical Marketing Process and Cycle. The Marketing Environment: Analyse the Micro Environment (Suppliers, Competitors, Customers) and the Macro Environment (Social, Economic, Political–Legal, Technological, Natural, International) to spot Opportunities and Threats early. The South African Consumer Market: Understand Demographics, Consumer Behaviour models, Decision-Making Processes, and the factors — Psychological, Socio-Cultural, and Economic — that drive choice. Segmentation, Targeting & Positioning (STP): Apply the Market Segmentation Process, choose Target Markets, differentiate your offer, and craft Positioning that customers remember. Marketing Information & Research: Build a Marketing Information System, plan Research, gather and interpret data, and turn insights into better decisions. Consumerism, CPA & Social Responsibility: Know Consumer Rights, the Consumer Protection Act, and Sustainability principles so your strategies are ethical and effective.

With examples, exercises, and simple frameworks, this book turns concepts into confident practice. You’ll communicate clearly, make evidence-based decisions, and design strategies that respect consumers and deliver results — a strong start to your marketing career.
