People First. Performance Always.

Great organisations start with the right people in the right roles at the right time. This textbook is for N4 learners who want a practical, business-focused introduction to personnel management — where good decisions about people translate into stronger operations and results.

You’ll move from core management tasks and enterprise functions to how organisations really work: culture, structure, effectiveness, and the HR systems that plan, design, and support jobs. Each chapter links concepts to tools you can apply immediately.

Inside the book, you’ll explore:

Management Tasks & Enterprise Functions: Understand Basic and Additional Management Tasks and how Marketing, Operations, Finance, and HR connect to deliver value. Organisation, Culture & Effectiveness: Read the Organisation as a system, interpret Organisational Culture, and assess Effectiveness from multiple perspectives. Human Resources Planning & Projections: Align staffing with strategy through Workforce Forecasts, Skills Planning, and simple Headcount and Cost Projections. Job Analysis, Description & Specification: Break roles into tasks and requirements, then write clear Job Descriptions and Job Specifications that support fair selection. Productivity & Work Study Fundamentals: Use methods study and simple time measures to streamline workflows and raise output without sacrificing quality. Manager’s Role In People Success: Coach performance, maintain constructive relationships, and apply ethical, compliant practices that build trust.

With scenarios, checklists, and templates, this book turns principles into daily practice. You’ll plan talent needs, design better jobs, and support inclusive, ethical people management - helping your organisation work like a well-oiled machine while people do their best work.
