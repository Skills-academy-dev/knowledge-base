TEXTBOOK

Start Something Real. Build Something That Lasts.

Great businesses don’t begin with luck - they begin with clarity, grit, and a plan. This textbook is for aspiring founders, side-hustlers, and future managers who want to turn ideas into viable ventures while avoiding the classic start-up mistakes.

You’ll connect contemporary entrepreneurship challenges with practical tools: how to think like an entrepreneur (and intrapreneur), generate and protect ideas, test market viability, prove financial feasibility, and assemble a plan that investors and customers can trust.

Inside the book, you’ll explore:

Entrepreneurship Mindset & Pitfalls: The role of Entrepreneurs and Intrapreneurs, the Personal Qualities that matter, and how to Avoid the Pitfalls that sink new ventures. Creativity & Idea Generation: Practical methods to Generate Ideas, turn Opportunities into concepts, and Protect Your Ideas before you go to market. Market Feasibility: Define your Product Description, position with precision, and use Market Information, SWOT Analysis, and Competitive Advantage to validate demand. Financial Feasibility: Calculate Start-Up Costs and Total Costs, then use Sales Forecasting and Scenario Planning to show how the numbers work. Business & Marketing Plans: Craft a compelling Business Plan, choose the right Business Name and sources of Assistance, and build a Marketing Mix that drives reach and revenue. Management & Financial Plans: Select the best Form of Ownership, handle Legal Formalities and Operations, and create Projected Financial Statements and Funding Strategies.

This textbook turns ambition into execution. With checklists, worked examples, and realistic exercises, you’ll apply ethical, customer-centred practices, collaborate with mentors and partners, and make confident decisions from first draft to first sale.

Finish with a credible plan — and the skills to build a business that grows on purpose.
