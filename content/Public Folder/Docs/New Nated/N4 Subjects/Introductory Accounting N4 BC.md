Start Your Journey to Accounting Greatness

Embark on your accounting journey with Introductory Accounting, a practical guide designed to build your confidence and understanding of this essential field.

With a focus on core principles and practical techniques, this book covers everything from business ownership basics to preparing financial statements. Each chapter is packed with examples and activities to make complex concepts easy to grasp and apply.

Inside this book, you’ll discover:

Forms of Ownership and Wealth Generation: Explore various business structures, from sole traders to companies, and learn how businesses grow and manage wealth. Accounting Documents and Authorisation: Dive into the world of source documents, filing systems, and the authorisation processes for financial transactions. Accounting Principles and Concepts: Gain a solid foundation in IFRS-based principles and learn how to apply them to financial activities. Accounting Entries and Financial Records: Master the double-entry system, maintain accurate ledgers, and prepare a trial balance. Subsidiary Journals and Ledger Posting: Develop practical skills with journals like the Cash Receipts Journal and learn how to update ledgers effectively. Wages and Salaries Journal: Understand payroll systems and how to record wages and salaries correctly. Bank Reconciliation: Ensure accuracy in financial records by reconciling bank statements with accounting records. End of Accounting Period Procedures: Prepare final accounts, including Trading Accounts, Profit and Loss Accounts, and Statements of Financial Position.

With its engaging and accessible approach, this textbook will provide you with the tools and knowledge you need to confidently tackle real-world accounting challenges.
