BEFORE YOU START

Now it’s time to start working, but before you start studying. First read the contents page to

see what topics we will be discussing. These are mirrored in your Study Guide. You will

find subject outcomes or learning outcomes in your study guide to help you understand

each module. The learning outcomes are repeated at the start of every module, to remind

you what is expected of you.

When you are ready to start your studies, read through the first module in your Study

Guide thoroughly.

Do not progress to the next module until:

• you are sure that you understand everything that has been covered in the

module,

• you have completed all the activities in the module,

• you are able to answer all the self-assessment questions confidently, and

• you are competent in each of the learning outcomes listed at the beginning of

the module.

Good luck with your course. Remember that the more thoroughly you study, the better you

will do in your new career. Also remember that the more time you spend doing extra

reading and researching, and learning from others, the more beneficial this learning

process is going to be.

Please read everything that is in the guide, follow all instructions and do all the

exercises and activities prepared for you.

Please note that this study guide does not substitute the textbook. The textbook and the

study guide must be used together to develop full understanding of the concepts in this

subject. This means that the activities and exercises as well as examples and case

studies in the textbook are equally important and should be completed and studied.

CHAPTER 1

LAYOUT OF THE BABY SECTION (NURSERY) IN BOTH A HOME-BASED AND DAY CARE CENTRE

The following concepts are covered in this module:

→ General requirements for a baby room.

→ The layout of the baby room.

→ The physical layout of a centre-based baby section.

→ The needs of the baby.

→ Activity areas.

→ The needs of the staff and educarer.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, learners should be able to:

→ Understand the significance of a well-structured nursery layout in both home-based

and day care settings.

→ Identify the general requirements essential for setting up an effective and safe baby

room.

→ Recognise the impact of colour choices on infants and the importance of appropriate

acoustics for a baby-friendly environment.

→ Examine the role of textures, ventilation, temperature control, and lighting in creating a

conducive atmosphere for infants.

→ Evaluate the importance of choosing suitable doors, wall coverings, curtains, flooring,

display spaces, and storage solutions tailored for a nursery.

→ Develop insights into the optimal layout of a baby playroom, focusing on essential

areas such as sleeping, eating, and bathing zones.

→ Understand the essentials of baby transportation within a facility and the significance

of carriers and travel gear.

→ Assess the unique needs of babies in centre-based nursery settings and learn the

crucial points to consider when designing a baby room in these environments.

→ Comprehend the importance of designated activity areas, with a focus on indoor play

spaces, routine areas, and the requirements for setting up outdoor playgrounds.

→ Recognise the needs and roles of staff and caregivers in a nursery setting,

emphasising the importance of a conducive work environment and the responsibilities

associated with ensur-ing the nursery's safety and functionality.
