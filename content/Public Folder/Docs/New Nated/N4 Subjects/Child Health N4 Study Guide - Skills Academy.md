BEFORE YOU START
Now it’s time to start working, but before you start studying. First read the contents page
to see what topics we will be discussing. These are mirrored in your Study Guide. You will
find subject outcomes or learning outcomes in your study guide to help you understand
each chapter. The learning outcomes are repeated at the start of every module, to remind
you what is expected of you.
When you are ready to start your studies, read through the first module in your Study
Guide thoroughly.
Do not progress to the next module until:
 you are sure that you understand everything that has been covered in the
module,
 you have completed all the activities in the module,
 you are able to answer all the self-assessment questions confidently, and
 you are competent in each of the learning outcomes listed at the beginning of
the module.
Good luck with your course. Remember that the more thoroughly you study, the better
you will do in your new career. Also remember that the more time you spend doing extra
reading and researching, and learning from others, the more beneficial this learning
process is going to be.
Please read everything that is in the guide, follow all instructions and do all the
exercises and activities prepared for you.
Please note that this study guide does not substitute the textbook. The textbook and the
study guide must be used together to develop full understanding of the concepts in this
subject. This means that the activities and exercises as well as examples and case
studies in the textbook are equally important and should be completed and studied.

MODULE 1
HEALTH OF THE YOUNG CHILD (BIRTH TO 6 YEARS)
The following concepts are covered in this module:
Theme 1
 Concept of health.
 Characteristics of a Healthy Child (Birth to 6 years).
 Factors which may affect the health of the child (from the time of conception).
 The influence of health on the total development of the child.
 A team approach to the health of the young child (Health team).
Theme 2
 Community and Environmental Health.
 Spreading and prevention of diseases.
Learning Outcomes
By the end of this module, learners should be able to:
THEME 1 : GENERAL IMPORTANCE OF HEALTH
 Define health and explain why it's essential for young children.
 List what makes a child healthy.
 Recognise how a child should grow and behave when they're healthy.
 Spot the indicators of good health in children aged 0-6 years.
 Understand how a child's health can be affected before and after they're born.
 Consider how different factors like the environment, genes, and daily habits impact a
child's growth.
 Discuss the role health plays in how a child thinks, moves, and interacts with others.
 Identify ways to support children's health for their best development.
 Learn the value of teamwork among healthcare workers in caring for children.
 Work with others to look after the health of children effectively.
THEME 2: HEALTH AND THE ENVIRONMENT
 Connect how community services can help keep children healthy.
 Explore the effect of the place where a child lives on their health.
 Find out what help is available in the community for children and their families.
