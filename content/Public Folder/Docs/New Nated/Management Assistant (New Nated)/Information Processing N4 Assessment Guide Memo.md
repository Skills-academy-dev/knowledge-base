QUESTION 2: SHORT ANSWER QUESTIONS (20 MARKS)

2.1 What are three advantages of using tables instead of plain text for displaying data? (4 marks)

Improves readability and organisation of data. (2 marks)

Allows better alignment and structure. (1 mark)

Enables sorting and filtering of data. (1 mark)

2.2 Why is using headers and footers important in professional documents? (4 marks)

Provides consistency in document layout. (2 marks)

Helps identify important details like page numbers and titles. (2 marks)

2.3 What is the main difference between a letter of application and a business letter? (4 marks)

A letter of application is used to apply for a job and highlights the applicant’s skills and experience. (2 marks)

A business letter is a formal communication between businesses or professionals for various purposes. (2 marks)

2.4 Explain how to insert and format page numbers in a document. (4 marks)

Go to the Insert tab in Microsoft Word. (1 mark)

Click on Page Number and select a position (top or bottom of the page). (1 mark)

Choose a numbering style and format it accordingly. (1 mark)

Click OK to apply the changes. (1 mark)

2.5 What is the purpose of using "Find and Replace" in document editing? (4 marks)

Helps locate specific words or phrases quickly. (2 marks)

Allows users to replace multiple occurrences of a word efficiently. (2 marks)
