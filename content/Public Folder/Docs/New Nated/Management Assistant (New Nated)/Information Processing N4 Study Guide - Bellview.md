INTRODUCTION

Typing is an essential skill in today's digital world. Whether in school, the workplace, or personal projects, the ability to type quickly and accurately ensures efficiency and professionalism. This module introduces key techniques for improving typing speed and accuracy while maintaining proper posture and ergonomic practices.

Unit 1.1: Accuracy and Speed Drills

This unit explores the importance of accuracy and speed in typing. Accuracy ensures that documents are error-free, while speed enhances productivity. Through structured drills and exercises, you will develop muscle memory and improve your ability to type efficiently without looking at the keyboard.

Read pages 14—17 of your textbook for more information on the following:

• 1.1.1 Why is Accuracy and Speed Important?

• 1.1.2 The Importance of Touch Typing

• 1.1.3 The QWERTY Keyboard Design

Unit 1.2: Proper Ergonomic Keying in Posture

This unit explains the correct typing posture to prevent discomfort and strain. Proper ergonomics reduce the risk of repetitive strain injuries (RSI) and improve typing endurance. You will learn how to position your hands, wrists, and body correctly while typing.

Read pages 18—21 of your textbook for more information on the following:

• 1.2.1 Proper Posture

• 1.2.2 Good Sitting Posture

• 1.2.3 Finger Positioning

• 1.2.4 Basic Tips for Touch Typing

Unit 1.3: Teaching the Keyboard

This unit introduces the standard QWERTY keyboard layout and the principles of touch typing. You will learn how to place your fingers on the home row and navigate the keyboard without looking, which improves both speed and accuracy over time

Please read pages 21 – 22 of your textbook for more information.

Unit 1.4: Speed Drill Exercises

This unit provides targeted exercises to help increase typing speed while maintaining accuracy. Short and long drills, as well as timed typing tests, will help you measure and improve your performance progressively.

Please read pages 22 – 24 of your textbook for more information.

Unit 1.5: 10-Minute Timed Accuracy Tests

This unit focuses on assessing your progress through 10-minute timed tests. You will aim to reach a typing speed of at least 35 words per minute with minimal errors. Regular practice with these tests will help you track your improvement and build confidence.

Please read pages 24 – 27 of your textbook for more information.

Summary

In this module, you learned essential skills to improve your typing accuracy and speed. By practising touch typing, maintaining proper posture, and using the QWERTY keyboard effectively, you worked towards achieving a speed of at least 35 words per minute with minimal errors. Key techniques included accuracy and speed drills, ergonomic posture, and timed accuracy tests to help you type more efficiently.

Consistent practice will enhance your confidence and professionalism in academic and work-related tasks. Keep refining your skills through regular exercises, as strong typing abilities are valuable in many areas of life.

INTRODUCTION

Well-structured paragraphs improve the clarity and professionalism of documents. This module explains different paragraph formatting techniques, including unnumbered and numbered paragraphs, and the use of manuscript signs. You will also learn how to apply the Dewey numbering system, a widely used method for structuring information in an organised manner

This unit explores how to format paragraphs that do not require numbering but include headings. These are commonly used in reports, essays, and official documents. You will learn how to properly align headings, apply line spacing, and maintain consistent margins for a professional layout

Read page 73 of your textbook for more information.

Unit 4.2: Unnumbered Main Paragraphs with Manuscript Signs

This unit explains how to apply manuscript signs, which are symbols used in proofreading to indicate necessary formatting changes. You will learn how to correctly use symbols such as CAPS, italics, paragraph breaks, and spacing indicators to ensure a well-structured and error-free document

Read pages 73 – 74 of your textbook for more information.

Unit 4.3: Main and Subparagraphs with Headings (Without Numbers)

This unit focuses on how to structure main and subparagraphs using headings rather than numbering. You will learn how to organise content logically, apply consistent indentation, and use formatting techniques that improve document readability

Read page 74 of your textbook for more information.

By applying these techniques, you can now create well-organised paragraphs for essays, reports, and business documents. Regular practice will help you refine these skills, making your writing clearer and more professional.
