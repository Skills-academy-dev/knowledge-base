## Module 1: The Management Function of Marketing

Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

Explain what marketing management is and why it is important.

Describe the role of marketing in an organisation.

Explain how the marketing department is organised.

Identify and explain the main tasks of a marketing manager.

Describe the strategic marketing process.

Analyse the internal and external marketing environment.

Explain how an organisation sets its mission, goals and target market.

Identify and explain different marketing strategies.

Explain how marketing plans are implemented and controlled.

Apply the strategic marketing process to a practical business situation.
