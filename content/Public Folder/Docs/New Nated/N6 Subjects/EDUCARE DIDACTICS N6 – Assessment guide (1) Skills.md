Assessment Rules

●  Write your name, address and student number on the front of the cover page.

●  The Assessment Guide may be typed or written.

●  Write neatly and clearly. If your handwriting is illegible, please type your

Assessments. Your grade might suffer due to illegible handwriting.

●

If you choose to write the assessment guide, it must be written in blue or black ink.

●  Plan your answer before writing. This will help you to present a clear interpretation

and a balanced response.

●

If you choose to type your assessment guide, your font size may not be smaller than 12pt.

●  Arrange and number your assessment answers according to the questions.

●  Use recent references.

●  Do not write on the back of the page, or print double-sided.

●  Any student who is found copying the work of others will be taken through a

disciplinary process by Skills Academy.

●  External sources should complement the information in your textbook but should not

replace it.
