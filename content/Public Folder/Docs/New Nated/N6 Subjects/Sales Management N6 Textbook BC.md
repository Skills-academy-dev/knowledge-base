Lead People, Not Just Pipelines

Great sales managers create the conditions for others to win. That means clear roles, smart territory and account design, rigorous hiring, meaningful training, motivating rewards, and an evaluation rhythm that turns feedback into growth.

The Sales Management N6 textbook gives you the tools to do exactly that. It links people decisions to commercial outcomes, showing how structure, selection, development, compensation, and measurement reinforce one another across the sales cycle.

Inside, you’ll explore topics such as:

Structuring the Sales Force: Define what a sales organisation is for, apply core organising principles, weigh specialisation strengths and weaknesses, and set the control systems managers need to steer performance. Recruiting and Selecting the Sales Force: Choose ethical recruitment channels, craft clear specifications, run structured interviews, apply selection tests wisely, and document decisions that stand up to scrutiny. Equipping the Sales Team for Success: Scope skills gaps, build training curricula, pick delivery formats that stick, leverage managers as coaches, and evaluate whether training changes real behaviours. Rewarding and Retaining the Sales Force: Design compensation packages that align effort and outcomes, combine monetary and non- monetary levers, and reduce turnover with credible, transparent policies. Monitoring and Measuring Sales Success: Use volume, mix, margin, and cost views; evaluate individual representatives; and feed insights into team development, budgets, and forward plans.

You’ll leave with clear playbooks: how to staff, teach, motivate, and measure. More importantly, you’ll know how to lead — with consistent expectations, fair systems, and coaching that turns potential into sustained performance.
