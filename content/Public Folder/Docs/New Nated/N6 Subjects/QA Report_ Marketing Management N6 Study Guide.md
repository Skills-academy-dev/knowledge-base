# **QA Report: Marketing Management N6 Study Guide**

This QA report evaluates the **Marketing Management N6 Study Guide** against the **Study Guide Quality Control Checklist**, with a focus on structure, syllabus alignment, content accuracy, visual layout, accessibility, and exam preparation for South African distance learning students. The review is based on the official study guide and aligned DHET examination requirements.

## **1. Structure and Organisation**

The Marketing Management N6 Study Guide is well structured and logically organised in line with the DHET syllabus for the subject.

**Table of Contents**

* Clearly presented and accurately reflects all modules, units, and supporting sections.
* Page numbering is consistent and supports easy navigation.
* Modules are sequenced logically from foundational marketing management concepts to applied international marketing topics.

**Introduction**

* Provides a clear overview of the purpose of the study guide.
* Explains how the guide supports distance learning students alongside the prescribed textbook.
* Clarifies assessment coverage (Assignments 1 and 2, and Internal Exam).

**Module and Unit Design** Across all four modules, the guide consistently includes:

* Clear module titles and learning objectives.
* Short introductions outlining the focus of each module.
* Unit-by-unit summaries aligned to examinable outcomes.
* Application sections and case studies to reinforce understanding.
* End-of-module summaries and exercises for revision.

**Verdict**The structure is coherent, learner-friendly, and well suited to independent distance learning.

**Score:** ⭐ **5 / 5**

## **2. Content Quality**

**Curriculum Alignment**

* Fully aligned with the Marketing Management N6 syllabus.
* All major examinable areas are covered, including:
  + Marketing as a management function
  + Strategic marketing planning
  + Industrial (business) marketing
  + Services marketing
  + International marketing

**Accuracy**

* Marketing concepts are explained correctly and reflect accepted marketing management theory.
* Content aligns with DHET examination expectations and past paper themes.

**Terminology**

* Key marketing terms such as *marketing mix, buyer behaviour, market segmentation, derived demand, service characteristics,* and *foreign market entry strategies* are used accurately and consistently.

**Practical Application**

* The guide regularly links theory to practical business and workplace scenarios.
* Case studies and application units support higher-order exam questions.

**Verdict**The content is accurate, comprehensive, and examination-focused.

**Score:** ⭐ **5 / 5**

## **3. Visual and Aesthetic Elements**

**Layout**

* Clean and consistent formatting throughout the study guide.
* Headings, subheadings, and unit titles are clearly differentiated.

**Typography**

* Font size and style are readable and suitable for long study sessions.
* Adequate spacing improves readability and reduces cognitive overload.

**Use of Visual Elements**

* Diagrams (e.g. marketing management cycle) support conceptual understanding.
* Structured lists and tables summarise key information effectively.

**Design Consistency**

* Uniform formatting across all modules enhances familiarity and ease of use.

**Verdict**The visual presentation supports clarity, consistency, and effective learning.

**Score:** ⭐ **5 / 5**

## **4. Accessibility and Usability**

**Language**

* Language is clear, formal, and appropriate for N6-level students.
* Explanations are concise and avoid unnecessary jargon.

**Tone**

* Supportive and instructional, suitable for distance learning and self-study.

**Readability**

* Effective use of:
  + Bullet points
  + Short explanatory paragraphs
  + Logical sequencing of ideas

**Navigation**

* Topics are presented in a logical order.
* Related concepts are grouped together, reducing confusion.

**Support Information**

* Clear guidance on how to use the study guide with the textbook and assessments.
* Contact and administrative support information is clearly provided.

**Verdict**The study guide is highly accessible and well designed for independent distance learners.

**Score:** ⭐ **5 / 5**

## **5. Referencing and Exam Integration**

**Textbook and Syllabus Integration**

* Content closely follows the prescribed textbook structure.
* Learning objectives and summaries align with DHET learning outcomes.

**Exam Preparation**

* Strong emphasis on:
  + Definitions and explanations
  + Lists and comparisons
  + Application-based questions
  + Case study interpretation

**Revision Support**

* End-of-module summaries and exercises support effective exam revision.
* Overview of exam paper structure prepares students for assessment expectations.

**Verdict**The guide is well integrated with curriculum and examination requirements.

**Score:** ⭐ **5 / 5**

## **6. Final QA – Overall Review**

The **Marketing Management N6 Study Guide** is a high-quality, exam-ready academic resource that meets institutional and DHET standards for distance learning.

**Key Strengths**

* Strong syllabus alignment
* Clear and structured explanations
* Practical and applied focus
* Exam-orientated content presentation
* Consistent and professional layout

**Areas for Improvement**

* No major quality issues identified.
* Minor enhancement opportunity: inclusion of additional summary tables in selected units could further support visual learners.
