From Behaviour to Belonging: Psychology That Improves Learning

Educational psychology helps you match teaching to how children actually learn. It links readiness, behaviour, and method selection so your planning is proactive, your responses are calm and consistent, and your lessons meet real developmental needs.

The Education Psychology N6 textbook brings these pieces together for South African practice. It blends clear explanations with observation guides, activity ideas, and community-aware approaches that respect families and local contexts.

Inside the book, you’ll explore topics such as:

School Readiness: Defining readiness across domains; using fair indicators; and implementing a responsive School Readiness Programme that strengthens foundations for literacy, numeracy, and social learning. Behaviour Problems: Understanding Parenting and Moral Development pathways; mapping Causes of Inappropriate Behaviour; applying restorative Techniques for Dealing with Misbehaviour; and supporting Immature and Habit-Forming Behaviour without stigma. Teaching Methods: Planning with the MATAL Method (assess → plan → teach → review); applying Montessori Method elements (prepared environment, independence); drawing on ELRU community practices; and using Grassroots methods to create meaningful experiences with accessible materials.

With practical tools and reflective tasks, this book helps you plan lessons that fit learners, coach behaviour with empathy, and build partnerships with families. The result is a classroom culture that is calm, caring, and ready for deep learning — one child at a time.
