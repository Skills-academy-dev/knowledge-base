Question 1 : True or False (10 x 2 = 20 Marks)

Indicate whether the following statements are True or False. If the statement is False, rewrite it

correctly.

1.  A grievance is a formal complaint by an employee about unfair treatment in the

workplace.

2.  Arbitration is a voluntary process in which a neutral third party makes a binding decision

to resolve a dispute.

3.  Conciliation aims to help parties reach an agreement through a neutral facilitator before

arbitration is considered.

4.  A layoff is a temporary suspension of employment, usually due to lack of work or

financial constraints.

5.  Shop stewards act as representatives of trade unions in dealings with management at

the workplace.

6.  An unprotected strike may expose employees to disciplinary action or dismissal.

7.  Mediation is identical to arbitration because both involve binding decisions.

8.  Industrial conflict can arise from unfair labour practices, wages, or poor communication

between employees and management.

9.  Collective bargaining allows employers and unions to negotiate wages, working

conditions, and other terms of employment.

10. The CCMA (Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration) assists in resolving

disputes between employers, employees, and unions.
