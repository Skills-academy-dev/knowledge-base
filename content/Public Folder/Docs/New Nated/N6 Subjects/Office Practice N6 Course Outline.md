## Module 1: Management and the Office Professional

Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

Explain the different levels of management and how organisations are structured.

Distinguish between formal and informal organisational structures and explain their functions and influence.

Describe the core functions of management and the skills required at different management levels.

Explain the role and responsibilities of managers within a business organisation.

Analyse the role of the secretary or management assistant at senior management level.

Compare the roles of managers and secretaries and explain how effective teamwork supports organisational success.

## Module 3: Advanced Secretarial and Professional Practice

Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

Identify and manage difficult workplace situations using professional and ethical practices.

Apply effective communication, listening, problem-solving, and decision-making skills in office environments.

Demonstrate how to support and assist managers through effective gatekeeping and communication.

Apply principles of business etiquette, protocol, and professional conduct.

Plan and manage office hospitality and corporate functions in line with organisational standards.

Monitor and use media information appropriately for business purposes.

Liaise professionally with clients and stakeholders.

Compile professional correspondence and office documents in accordance with corporate identity standards.
