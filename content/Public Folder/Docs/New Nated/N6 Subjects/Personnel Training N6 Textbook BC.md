From Needs Analysis to Results on the Job

Learning and development connects real work problems to practical solutions. It asks the right questions, designs for performance, equips facilitators to lead adults, and follows through with evaluation and management.

The Personnel Training N6 textbook brings this full pathway together for day-to- day use. It combines clear explanations with tools you can adapt — scoping templates, session plans, evaluation forms, and simple dashboards for system oversight.

Inside this guide, you’ll explore topics such as:

Competency-based Training: Describing CBT in plain terms and contrasting competency-based modular training with traditional norm-based approaches. Facilitating Skills: The facilitator’s role and stance, and techniques that activate participation, feedback, and reflection in adult groups. Designing and Developing Training Programmes: Diagnosing needs, writing aims, creating and sequencing a programme, and piloting to refine delivery. Presenting Training Programmes: Facilities and materials readiness, operating schedules and activities, and personal strategies for stress management. Evaluating Training Programmes: Purposes of evaluation, practical strategies, the main levels of evaluation, and how to develop reliable instruments. Managing a Training System: Building a realistic budget, using statistics to monitor uptake and impact, commissioning research, and leveraging computerised training management systems alongside general management principles.

Whether you are preparing to train, already facilitating, or managing a learning function, this book helps you deliver training that changes performance. Plan with clarity, teach with confidence, and show results that matter.
