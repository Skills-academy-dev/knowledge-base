Learn the Skills to Run a Centre That Families Can Trust

Effective daycare management sits at the intersection of children’s needs, staff capability, and community expectations. It requires clear standards for health and safety, transparent finances, purposeful staffing, and daily routines that make quality care repeatable.

The Daycare Management N6 textbook is your handbook for building those quality systems. It threads together operational detail, legal requirements, and human relationships to help you deliver safe, nurturing care at scale.

Inside, you’ll explore topics such as:

Nutrition and Food Management: age-appropriate nutrition, balanced menus, kitchen hygiene, and food service administration that supports growth and well- being. Financial Management in Daycare Centres: internal controls that prevent errors, budgeting and auditing cycles, and protective measures for funds and records. Staff Management and Professional Development: recruiting and scheduling, employment policies, wellness practices, and training plans that build professional skill. Administrative Management and Governance: committee composition and duties, procedure design, and record-keeping that makes compliance visible. Health, Safety, and Emergency Care: risk assessments, first-aid readiness, transport protocols, and communication during and after emergencies. Technology and Aids to Management: digital registers, communication platforms, document storage, and data-informed planning. Legislation, Registration, and Compliance: navigating requirements, completing registrations, maintaining standards, and managing subsidy applications. Parent Involvement and Community Relations: creating welcoming channels for input, running workshops, and collaborating with local services.

Use the scenarios, forms, and reflection prompts to convert plans into practice. You’ll learn to lead calmly, report accurately, improve continuously, and strengthen community ties — so children experience consistent care and families can see the difference.
