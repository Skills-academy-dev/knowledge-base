## 1\. Scope and Context of This QA Review

This QA review evaluates the academic content, pedagogical integrity, structure, and internal formatting of the Office Practice N6 textbook excluding front and back cover pages, as per standard publishing workflow.

Items such as:

ISBN number allocation, and

Final editorial sign-off declarations

are acknowledged as publication-stage elements and are therefore out of scope for this content QA review.

## 2\. Methodology

Full review against the official Office Practice N6 syllabus and course outline.

Evaluation of internal structure, module flow, assessments, learner support elements, and distance-learning suitability.

Technical formatting reviewed only for internal pages, excluding cover-related requirements.

## 3\. Academic Alignment and Syllabus Coverage

Learning objectives align accurately with unit content and assessments.

✔ Fully compliant
