ASSIGNMENT RULES
 Write your name, address and student number on the front of the cover page.
 Write neatly and clearly. If your handwriting is illegible, please type your assignment.
Your grade might suffer due to illegible handwriting.
 The assignment may be typed or written.
 If you choose to write the assignment, it must be written in blue or black ink.
 Plan your answer before writing. This will help you to present a clear interpretation
and balanced response.
 If you choose to type your assignment, your font size may not be smaller than 12pt.
 Arrange and number your assignment answers according to the assignment
questions..
 Use recent references.
 Do not write on the back of the page, or print double-sided.
 Any student who is found copying the work of others will be taken through a
disciplinary process by Skills Academy

replace it.
