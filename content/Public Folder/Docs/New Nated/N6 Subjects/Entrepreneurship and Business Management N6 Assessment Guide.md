Section A: Multiple-Choice Questions (10 Marks)

1.  What is the main purpose of strategic planning in a small business?

a) To record employee attendance

b) To align goals with business direction

c) To design advertising posters

d) To increase prices automatically

2.  The mission statement of a business mainly expresses:

a) Future dreams of the business

b) Operational steps and tactics

c) The business’s purpose and customers it serves

d) The products only

3.  Which tool analyses external environmental factors such as political and economic

trends?

a) SWOT

b) PESTEL

c) KPI chart

d) Balanced Scorecard

4.  Which of the following is a SMART objective?

a) “Improve everything soon.”

b) “Increase sales by 15% within 6 months.”

c) “Sell more.”

d) “Grow quickly.”

5.  In a SWOT analysis, “Threats” refer to:

a) Internal weaknesses

b) External risks or pressures

c) Financial resources

d) Employee skills

6.  A leading KPI is used to:

a) Measure results after a project ends

b) Predict future performance and trends

c) Record company history

d) Show annual profits only

7.  The Balanced Scorecard helps managers:

a) Focus only on financial performance

b) Combine financial, customer, process, and learning goals

c) Eliminate customer measures

d) Measure stock value

8.  Which change-management model includes the stages Unfreeze → Change →

Refreeze?

a) Kotter’s 8 Steps

b) Lewin’s Model

c) ACE Model

d) PDCA Cycle

9.  According to Porter’s Five Forces, high rivalry among competitors usually means:

a) Higher profits

b) Fewer customers

c) Lower profitability in the industry

d) No new entrants

a) Decision Matrix

b) Cost–Benefit Analysis

c) PESTEL

d) Ansoff Matrix
