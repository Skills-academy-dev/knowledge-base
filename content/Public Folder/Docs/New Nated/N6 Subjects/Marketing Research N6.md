Marketing Research N6

Understanding Customers Through Scientific Inquiry

Every marketing question – Who is our customer? Why are sales falling? Which advert works best? – is a research question. The ability to design and interpret research gives marketers a powerful advantage. It helps them reduce risk, find opportunities and back up decisions with evidence instead of opinion.

The Marketing Research N6 textbook takes you through the logic and tools of modern marketing research. It explains the role and limitations of research, guides you through research designs and methods, and shows you how to collect reliable data, avoid common errors, analyse results and communicate insights.

When you study this textbook, you will learn how to:

Nature of Marketing Research: Describe the role, applications, advantages and limitations of marketing research in different business contexts Research Planning: Classify broad types of research designs and apply the main steps of the research process to real marketing problems Method Choices: Compare qualitative and quantitative methods and select suitable techniques for different research objectives Primary and Secondary Data: Gather and evaluate secondary data and design primary research using surveys, observation, panels, interviews, focus groups and experiments Survey Quality and Errors: Identify types of survey error – definition, estimation and explanation – and apply strategies to minimise them Questionnaires and Fieldwork: Design effective questionnaires, select question formats and manage fieldwork to collect accurate, usable data Forecasting and Reporting: Use research results for demand analysis and sales forecasting, and prepare structured, clear reports that support managerial decisions

This textbook helps you think like a researcher: systematic, curious and careful with evidence. It is ideal for students who want to work in marketing departments, research agencies or analytical roles where understanding customers and markets is key.
