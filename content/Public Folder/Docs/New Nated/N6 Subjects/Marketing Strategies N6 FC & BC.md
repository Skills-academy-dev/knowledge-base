Building Plans that Win in Competitive Markets

In a competitive business environment, marketing success is not accidental – it is planned. Organisations that grow consistently understand their markets, choose clear target customers, set strong objectives and implement strategies that create real competitive advantage.

With the Marketing Strategies N6 textbook. it teaches you how to think like a strategist, not just a marketer. Learn practical frameworks for analysing the environment, developing long-term plans and aligning marketing actions with organisational goals.

When you study this textbook, you will learn how to:

Understand marketing as a management function: Explain the marketing management process and how marketing departments are organised and managed Plan strategically: Apply the strategic planning process and use planning models to build structured marketing plans Analyse markets and environments: Use tools such as SWOT analysis to evaluate internal strengths and external opportunities and threats Choose strategy tools and models: Apply portfolio and planning models such as BCG, GE and growth strategies for decision-making Develop B2B strategies: Understand industrial markets and formulate industrial (B2B) marketing strategies based on buyer behaviour and market structure Strategise for services: Recognise the unique nature of services and design strategies suited to service organisations Compete internationally: Understand international trade, foreign market environments, entry strategies, trade policies and the international marketing mix

This textbook is designed to develop strategic thinking and practical decision- making. With case studies and exercises, the Marketing Strategies N6 textbook helps you build the skills to formulate, implement and evaluate marketing strategies that support real business growth.
