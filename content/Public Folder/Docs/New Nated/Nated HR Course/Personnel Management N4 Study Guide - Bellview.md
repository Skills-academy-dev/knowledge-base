Now it’s time to start working, but before you start studying. First read the contents page to
see what topics we will be discussing. These are mirrored in your Study Guide. You will find
subject outcomes or learning outcomes in your study guide to help you understand each

study guide must be used together to develop full understanding of the concepts in this
subject. This means that the activities and exercises as well as examples and case
studies in the textbook are equally important and should be completed and studied.

INTRODUCTION
Personnel management is important for any business and is a big part of what HR does.
It's all about getting the right people into the right jobs and making sure they're happy and
doing well at work. When the staff does well, the company makes more money. Managers
play a big role in helping and teaching the staff, so the company can reach its goal of
making money. This intro gets you ready for a lesson that will teach you the basic and
more complicated parts of being a manager and running a business, getting future HR
managers ready to make the best use of their teams.
Unit 1.1: Basic management tasks
This unit looks at the basic jobs that managers do and the different types of managers in a
company. You'll learn about what managers at each level do, from the bosses at the top to
the leaders who work directly with the workers. Knowing these jobs and who does what is
important for anyone who wants to manage well and help their company do well.
Read pages 6—8 of your textbook for more information on the following:
• 1.1.1 Levels of management
• 1.1.2 Key management tasks
Unit 1.2: Additional management tasks
In this unit, we'll dive into the essential tasks that help managers lead their companies to
success. This includes:
• Decision-making
• Communication
• Motivation
• Coordination
• Delegation
Read pages 8—9 of your textbook for more information.

INTRODUCTION
In this module, we'll delve into organisations and the importance of managing people
effectively. We'll explore what an organisation is and why it exists, the impact of a
company's culture on its operations, how to handle changes within an organisation, and
what contributes to an organisation's success. This unit aims to give you a comprehensive
understanding of these key aspects, helping you to manage better and improve
organisational performance.
Unit 2.1: The organisation
In this unit, we'll explore what an organisation is: essentially, a team united by a common
objective, often to generate profit
Read pages 15 – 47 of your textbook for more information on the following:
• 2.1.1 Organisational concepts
• 2.1.2 Organisational structure and design
• 2.1.3 Formal and informal organisational structure
Unit 2.2: Organisational culture
In this unit, we'll delve into the concept of organisational culture, which can be thought of
as a company's personality. This encompasses all the rules, beliefs, and practices that are
shared across the company, influencing how everyone, from senior leaders to new
employees, behaves and interacts at work.
Read pages 47 - 50 of your textbook for more information on the following:
• 2.2.1 Variables influencing organisational culture
• 2.2.2 Factors determining organisational culture
• 2.2.3 Types of organisational culture
• 2.2.4 Cultural differences in the South African work environment

elements: organisational policy, organisational image and organisational change.

Unit 2.4: Organisational effectiveness
In this unit, we'll explore the goal of organisational effectiveness, which for many
companies means maximising profit and outshining their competitors. We'll delve into
strategies that businesses employ to excel in the market and how they aim for growth and
development within their industry. Understanding these strategies is key to comprehending

topics included vision and mission statements, values, goals, and the policies that guide
them.
You also explored how tasks are organised, how work is divided, and how authority is
managed. We talked about formal and informal structures.
Lastly, we discussed workplace culture and how it affects organisations. We looked at how

In this module, we focus on Human Resources (HR) management, a vital element of
organisational success. We'll see how HR has evolved from administrative duties to a
strategic role within businesses, and how modern trends like performance management

nurturing a company's workforce, ensuring it thrives in a competitive environment.

INTRODUCTION
In this module, we'll delve into job design, a strategy used to structure work in a way that
reduces dissatisfaction and monotony. We'll explore how critical job roles are in aligning
the right people with the right work and how HR's deep understanding of each role can lead
to greater employee satisfaction and productivity. We'll cover the essentials of crafting roles
that not only meet organisational goals but also provide personal growth and fulfilment for
employees. You'll learn how to analyse jobs, create detailed job descriptions and
specifications, and see how these processes contribute to overall workplace efficiency.
This knowledge is essential for anyone looking to pursue a career in HR.
Unit 4.1: Job analysis
In this unit, we will focus on job analysis, a vital process for understanding all positions
within an organisation.
