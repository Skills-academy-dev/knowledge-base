Summary

This module explained how research helps organisations make better decisions by

gathering information about employees and workplace trends. It highlighted two types of

research: basic research, which focuses on general knowledge like job satisfaction, and

applied research, which solves specific workplace problems like reducing staff turnover.

You also learned about tools such as surveys and SWOT analysis to collect and evaluate

data, as well as how to use statistics like averages and percentages to analyse trends.

Charts and graphs, like bar charts and pie charts, make the data easier to understand. By

using these tools, organisations can improve their work environment and achieve their

goals.

people in the right roles to achieve their goals. This module explores the key steps involved

Summary

This module explained how organisations find, select, and prepare the right people for jobs.

It focused on four main areas: recruitment, screening and selection, placement, and

induction. Recruitment involves attracting candidates through job adverts, policies, and

hiring methods. Screening and selection ensure the best person is chosen for the job

through interviews and tests. Placement assigns employees to roles that match their skills

and qualifications, including promotions and transfers. Finally, induction helps new

employees adjust to their roles and the organisation, improving their confidence and

performance.

By following these steps, organisations can build strong teams, support new employees,

and achieve their goals efficiently.
